# Bonsai — Concept Map

Concepts are numbered C1 to C30. Lessons are numbered L1 to L51.

## Core Concepts (in learning order)

1. **C1 Bonsai as technique** — any suitable tree kept small by technique, not by genetics (L1).
2. **C2 Indoor vs outdoor trees** — temperate trees need outdoor seasons; only tropicals tolerate indoor conditions (L2). Depends on: C1.
3. **C3 Reading a tree** — nebari, trunk line, taper, branches, apex and pot (L3). Depends on: C1.
4. **C4 Tools and safety** — cutters, wire tools, hygiene (L4).
5. **C5 Water transport** — xylem, transpiration pull, stomata, wilting (L5).
6. **C6 Energy budget** — photosynthesis as income, starch reserves, growth vs storage (L6). Depends on: C5.
7. **C7 Apical dominance** — auxin, back-budding, distributing vigour (L7). Depends on: C6.
8. **C8 Root biology** — root hairs, oxygen demand, mycorrhizae (L9). Depends on: C5.
9. **C9 CODIT** — trees wall off wounds instead of healing them (L10). Depends on: C6.
10. **C10 Dormancy and hardiness** — chilling requirement, roots less hardy than branches (L11). Depends on: C6, C8.
11. **C11 Substrate physics** — air-filled porosity, particle size, perched water (L13–L14). Depends on: C5, C8.
12. **C12 Watering by need** — checking the tree instead of following a schedule (L15). Depends on: C5, C11.
13. **C13 Nutrition** — N-P-K, micronutrients, CEC, pH and chlorosis (L16). Depends on: C8, C11.
14. **C14 Repotting** — timing at bud swell, root pruning, aftercare (L17). Depends on: C6, C8, C11.
15. **C15 Light and microclimate** — light needs by species, PPFD, grow lights (L18). Depends on: C6.
16. **C16 Winter protection** — methods by zone and plant type (L19). Depends on: C10.
17. **C17 Diagnosis** — linking symptom to mechanism to fix (L20 gate). Depends on: C5–C16.
18. **C18 Pruning** — maintenance vs structural, cut placement (L21). Depends on: C7, C9, C6.
19. **C19 Pinching and defoliation** — ramification, leaf reduction (L22). Depends on: C6, C7.
20. **C20 Wiring** — gauge, angle, anchoring, removal timing (L23). Depends on: C6.
21. **C21 Trunk development** — sacrifice branches, ground growing, taper (L25). Depends on: C6, C7.
22. **C22 Deadwood** — jin, shari, lifelines, lime sulfur (L26). Depends on: C5, C9.
23. **C23 Propagation for design** — grafting and air-layering (L27). Depends on: C5, C9.
24. **C24 Development planning** — one major stress per year (L28). Depends on: C18–C23.
25. **C25 Species calendars** — elm, juniper, maple, pine, ficus, azalea and crabapple (L29–L36). Depends on: C10, C16, C24.
26. **C26 Styles** — the classic forms and what each one communicates (L37). Depends on: C3.
27. **C27 Design principles** — visual weight, asymmetry, front, negative space, movement (L38–L39). Depends on: C3, C26.
28. **C28 Traditions** — bonsai, penjing, hòn non bộ, bunjae, Western naturalistic (L40–L43). Depends on: C26, C27.
29. **C29 Pests, disease and IPM** — identification, the disease triangle, the escalation ladder, chemical safety (L44–L46). Depends on: C12, C15, C17.
30. **C30 Sourcing and biosecurity** — imported pests, legal and ethical collecting (L47–L48). Depends on: C29.

## Dependencies

- C6 (energy budget) is the hub. Every technique in C18–C23 spends stored energy, so a tree's reserves set how much work it can take (L24, L28).
- C11 (substrate) depends on C5 and C8. Roots need oxygen, and in a shallow pot the perched water zone takes up a larger share of the soil depth, so coarse, airy particles keep roots alive.
- C18 (pruning) needs C7, because cutting the apex releases auxin control and drives back-budding. It also needs C9, because the cut must sit where the tree can compartmentalise it.
- C16 (winter protection) applies C10. Roots in a pot sit above ground and are exposed in a way that roots in the earth are not.
- C17 (the L20 gate) is cumulative over C5–C16. No technique lesson (C18 onward) starts until it is passed.
- C25 (species) recombines C10, C16 and C18–C23 per genus. The timing differences between conifers and deciduous trees all come from different growth cycles.
- C27 (design) depends on C3 (reading a tree) and C26 (styles). C28 then compares how different traditions weight those principles.
- C29 depends on C12 and C15 because most bonsai pest and disease outbreaks follow cultural stress: wet roots, low light, poor airflow.
- C30 depends on C29. You can't judge an import risk without knowing what pests look like.

## Prerequisite Topics

- High-school biology: photosynthesis and cell basics, needed for C5–C10.
- Basic chemistry vocabulary (ions, pH), needed for C13.
- Local climate or hardiness zone, needed for C10, C16 and C25.
- Basic gardening practice, needed for C12 and C14.
