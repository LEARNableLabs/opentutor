## Overall
Strong, well-sequenced plan that follows the causal chain, with good misconception handling and recurring case studies. Issues below are mostly fixable without restructuring; none is a blocker.

## Findings

**Coverage (minor)**
- Mount St. Helens 1980 is promised as a running case study (Modules 2–6) but appears in the lesson list only in L17 and L28. Kīlauea appears only in L15 and L23. Pinatubo is mentioned in L25, L26, L29 and L30, but the Plinian eruption itself is never taught as an event. Add case-study callbacks to the lesson concepts or content (e.g. Pinatubo in L14 or L16, Kīlauea in L10 or L13).
- Exit criterion 8 and the teaching notes cover the contrast between volcanic and human CO2. L30 lists it, but the CO2 lesson (L24) never sets up the quantities (the ~1% figure). Fine, but make sure it is actually taught.
- Laki 1783 is flagged in the misconceptions (VEI vs impact) but is not a concept in any lesson. Put it in L14 or L30.
- Phreatic and phreatomagmatic eruptions (hydrovolcanism) are absent. Many deadly events (Ontake 2014, Whakaari 2019) are phreatic, and they matter for forecasting because they have few precursors. Add them to L14 or L25.
- Submarine volcanism and volcano-ice interaction beyond lahars get no coverage. Both are low priority.

**Sequencing (minor)**
- L3 (decompression, difficulty 3) comes before the magma-composition lessons. This is fine.
- Plan says ~31 lessons, but the JSON has 32. The review lessons sit at L6, 12, 18 and 26, while the plan and teaching notes say 'about 6, 12, 18, 25'. L25 is the probabilistic forecasting lesson (a difficulty peak), not a review. Reconcile the counts and review numbering in the plan.
- Module 5 has five lessons and carries two peak difficulty points. L22–L24 are rated 3 while the plan says Module 5 peaks at 4; only L25 is 4. Either re-rate or adjust the plan.
- The capstone is rated 5, but the plan says it ends 'with a level-4 capstone'. Align the two.
- The plan puts L9 as the viscosity difficulty gate, but L8 and L9 have no explicit predict-the-eruption drill before Module 3 other than the L12 review. That is acceptable.

**Breadth vs depth (minor)**
- Module 3 packs fragmentation, the style spectrum plus VEI, lava, columns and PDCs into 5 lessons, and L14 bundles four style labels, open vs closed degassing and VEI. It is dense for a 3–5 minute read. Consider moving VEI into L18 or splitting it off. The plan already notes a split option.
- L11 (differentiation, difficulty 4) is heavy for a 3–5 minute lesson and borders on the out-of-scope petrology. Keep it qualitative.

**Resources (minor)**
- Resources are mostly Wikipedia plus the USGS and Smithsonian portals. The plan itself notes the gap, but it is not filled. There is no Encyclopedia of Volcanoes, textbook, OpenLearn or IRIS link in any lesson, and few videos, even though the plan says most lessons should link visual media. Add USGS or YouTube footage to the real-world lessons (L15, L16, L17, L27).
- The L8 and L9 Giordano 2008 paper is research-level for most intermediate students. Provide the calculator or a plain-language summary instead.
- Several URLs (e.g. usgs.gov/volcanoes/mount-rainier) are unverified and may have moved. Spot-check them.
- L17's TITAN2D GitHub link is only a conceptual aid. Mark it optional.

**Exercises (minor)**
- The strategy is good (predict-the-eruption, estimation, data interpretation, decision scenarios). The open-ended decision scenario and teach-back need grading rubrics, since there is 'no single right answer'. The capstone's rubric should check for probability statements, timescales and an explicit alternative scenario.
- Estimation exercises ('how much SO2 cools the globe 0.5 °C') need given data, or students cannot do them at this level.

**Bias (minor)**
- The case studies are Americas-heavy and Western-institution heavy (USGS, Smithsonian). Indonesia (Tambora, Merapi) and the Philippines (Pinatubo) provide some balance. Consider mentioning local scientists, Indigenous knowledge and Hawaiian cultural context (Pele) in L5 and L15, and the role of local communities in evacuation (L29).

**Pedagogy (minor)**
- Plan-notes alignment is good. One inconsistency is that the plan says that 'wet clothes drying' is the flux-melting analogy, while the teaching notes use 'salt melting ice'. Pick one, the salt one being more accurate.
- The 'no more than two of same type in a row' rule holds. Teach-back appears at L21, which is early. By then the student has covered only fall vs flow in a brief way. Make sure L21 has the needed prerequisites (L16–L17). It does.
- Stating that Pinatubo was 'after about 500 years' (and L29's framing) should be verified (commonly ~500 years). Fine.

**teacher.md (minor)**
- No teacher.md content is included in the supplied material. The teaching notes are domain-specific (misconceptions, case studies, rabbit holes) and not generic, which is good. When teacher.md is generated, carry over the misconception corrections and the 'hold at L9/L13' rule.

## Verdict
No structural problem. Fix the count and numbering inconsistencies, the difficulty ratings, the missing phreatic/Laki coverage, and add visual and textbook resources. These are minor.
