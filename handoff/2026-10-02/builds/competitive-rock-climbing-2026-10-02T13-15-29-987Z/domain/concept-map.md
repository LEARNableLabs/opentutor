# Competitive Rock Climbing — Concept Map

All references use the lesson numbers from the canonical plan table. The separate concept-numbering scheme has been retired.

## Core Concepts (in learning order)

- **L1 Competition formats**: boulder, lead and speed; how rounds are structured.
- **L2 Boulder scoring**: tops, zones, attempts and points. Depends on: L1
- **L3 Footage rubric**: hold, move, crux, outcome; the setter as question-writer. Depends on: L1, L2
- **L4 Lead scoring, isolation and observation**; speed at overview level only. Depends on: L1
- **L5 Hold types and grip positions** (open hand, half crimp, full crimp)
- **L6 Footwork and body positioning**: hooks, drop knees, flags. Depends on: L5
- **L7 Dynamic comp moves**: dynos, coordination moves, run-and-jump, paddle. Depends on: L5, L6
- **L8 Centre of mass and force direction**; static vs dynamic movement. Depends on: L5-L7
- **L9 Review** of L5-L8
- **L10 Route-preview expertise and chunking**. Depends on: L5-L8
- **L11 Sequence, rest and clip planning**. Depends on: L4, L10
- **L12 Prediction task**: the saved answer key is reused in L20. Depends on: L10, L11
- **L13 Pacing and attempt economy**. Depends on: L2, L4, L11
- **L14 Setter intent**, style categories, women's and paraclimbing setting. Depends on: L3, L7, L10
- **L15 Review** of L10-L14
- **L16 Performance determinants**: a multi-factor model (Misconception 4). Depends on: L8
- **L17 Forearm pump**: occlusion, oxygenation, recovery. Depends on: L16
- **L18 Critical force**: F = CF + W'/t, equivalently F·t = CF·t + W'. Depends on: L17, plus algebra
- **L19 Fitting a CF line by hand**. Depends on: L18
- **L20 Physiological re-read of L12**. Depends on: L12, L17-L19
- **L21 Tendons, pulleys, slow adaptation, growth plates**. Depends on: L5 (grip positions), L16
- **L22 Safety gate**: pass = Track A eligible, fail = Track B. Depends on: L21
- **L23 Hangboard protocols (Track A only)**. Depends on: passing L22
- **L24 Max strength vs RFD; limit bouldering; campus board** (experienced adults only). Depends on: L16, L21, L22
- **L25 Endurance: ARC, intervals, 4x4s**. Depends on: L17-L19
- **L26 Track B climbing-based loading**. Depends on: L21, L22
- **L27 Review** of L16-L26
- **L28 Antagonists and shoulders**. Depends on: L16
- **L29 Periodisation and tapering**. Depends on: L23-L26
- **L30 Load and recovery monitoring (session RPE)**. Depends on: L29
- **L31 6-week block design**. Depends on: L22 (track), L23 or L26, L24, L25, L28-L30
- **L32 Injury red flags and referral**. Depends on: L21, L28
- **L33 Low energy availability and REDs**. Depends on: L30
- **L34 Review** of L28-L33, auditing the L31 block
- **L35 Isolation warm-up and observation**. Depends on: L4, L11
- **L36 Attempt strategy and arousal**. Depends on: L2, L13
- **L37 Capstone**: read, plan and pace a full final. Depends on: L10-L14, L17-L19, L35, L36
- **L38 Final cumulative review**

## Dependencies

- L2 and L4 (scoring) come before L13 (pacing) and L36 (attempt strategy), because tactics only make sense once you know what earns points.
- L5-L8 (vocabulary) come before L10-L14 (route reading), because you cannot read a route you cannot name.
- L17 (pump) comes before L18-L19 (CF), because CF formalises the sustainable/unsustainable boundary that pump makes you feel.
- L12 → L20: the same clip and the student's saved answer key are re-read through physiology. This is the bridge between reading and science.
- L21 → L22 → L23/L26: hard safety ordering. No hangboard content before the gate is passed. A fail routes the student to Track B (L26).
- L18-L19 → L25: endurance training is framed as raising CF and managing W'.
- L28-L30 → L31: the block design needs antagonist work, periodisation and monitoring.
- L30 → L33: monitoring signs overlap with low-energy-availability warning signs.

## Prerequisite Topics

- **High-school algebra** (slope, intercept, averaging): needed for L18, L19, L25
- **Regular indoor climbing experience**: needed for L5-L8 and for judging feasibility in L12 and L31
- **No anatomy or physiology background is assumed**: L17 and L21 build it from scratch.
