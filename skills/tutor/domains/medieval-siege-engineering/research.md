# Medieval Siege Engineering — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

1. **Physics and Mechanics of Siege Engines**
   - Torsion engines (ballista, springalds)
   - Traction engines (mangonels, traction trebuchets)
   - Counterweight engines (trebuchet evolution)
   - Energy transfer, projectile motion, structural forces

2. **Design and Construction**
   - Material selection (timber types, rope, metal components)
   - Scaling problems and structural integrity
   - Assembly and disassembly for transport
   - Siege warfare logistics

3. **Tactical Applications**
   - Offensive siege tactics (breaching, suppression, psychological warfare)
   - Defensive countermeasures
   - Siege duration and attrition
   - Combined arms coordination

4. **Historical Development**
   - Ancient origins (Greek, Roman, Chinese)
   - Medieval innovations (9th-15th century)
   - Technological transfer and regional variations
   - Decline with gunpowder artillery

5. **Primary Sources and Archaeological Evidence**
   - Contemporary manuscripts and treatises
   - Iconographic evidence
   - Archaeological finds
   - Experimental archaeology and reconstruction

## Key Concepts for Curriculum

1. **Fundamental Physics**: Projectile motion, energy conversion, mechanical advantage, structural stress
2. **Material Science**: Medieval timber properties, rope construction, metal working limits
3. **Scale and Engineering Constraints**: Scaling laws, transport logistics, construction time
4. **Tactical Integration**: When to use which engine, combined siege operations
5. **Historical Context**: Why certain innovations emerged when they did
6. **Experimental Method**: How modern historians test historical claims through reconstruction
