# Distillation — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks & Academic

- **"The Alcohol Textbook" (5th ed.) by Jacques, Lyons, and Kelsall** — Industry-standard reference covering fermentation science, distillation theory, and quality control. Comprehensive but accessible. Best for: deep dives into fermentation biochemistry and industrial processes.
  - https://www.nottingham.ac.uk/biosciences/documents/alcohol-textbook-5th-edition.pdf

- **"Whisky: Technology, Production and Marketing" (2nd ed.) by Inge Russell et al.** — Detailed chemistry and engineering of whisky production. Excellent on malting, distillation equipment, and maturation. Best for: lessons 17, 23-24.
  - https://www.elsevier.com/books/whisky/russell/978-0-12-401735-1

- **"Distillation: Principles and Practices" by Stichlmair & Fair** — Chemical engineering textbook on separation processes. Rigorous treatment of VLE, column design, and theoretical plates. Best for: lessons 7, 9-10 (theory-heavy sections).
  - https://www.wiley-vch.de/de/fachgebiete/chemie/distillation-978-0-471-26303-8

- **MIT OpenCourseWare: 10.32 Separation Processes** — Free lectures, problem sets, and notes on distillation, including McCabe-Thiele method and phase equilibria. Best for: lessons 7, 10.
  - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/10-32-separation-processes-spring-2005/

- **Chem LibreTexts: Distillation** — Open-access chemistry resource with clear explanations of simple/fractional distillation, azeotropes, and Raoult's law. Best for: lessons 3-5.
  - https://chem.libretexts.org/Bookshelves/Organic_Chemistry/Book%3A_Organic_Chemistry_Lab_Techniques_(Nichols)/05%3A_Distillation

### Industry & Practical

- **The Artisan Distiller Knowledge Base** — Practical articles on congeners, cut points, equipment selection, and spirit-specific techniques. Written for craft distillers; bridges theory and practice. Best for: lessons 11-12, 17-22.
  - https://www.artisan-distiller.com/distillation-knowledge-base

- **Distilling Knowledge Center (Kentucky Spirit LLC)** — Technical library with articles on fermentation, distillation science, and troubleshooting. Best for: practical applications across all modules.
  - https://www.kentuckyspirit.com/knowledge-center

- **American Distilling Institute (ADI) Resources** — Educational materials, conference proceedings, and industry standards. Requires membership for full access but has free introductory content. Best for: industry perspective on lessons 8-9, 17-22.
  - https://distilling.com/resources/

## Supplementary (for engagement)

### Videos & Channels

- **Still It (YouTube)** — Chemistry-focused home distilling channel with detailed explanations of cuts, congeners, and equipment. Excellent production quality. Best for: lessons 11-16.
  - https://www.youtube.com/c/StillIt

- **Clawhammer Supply (YouTube)** — Engineering and process videos on building stills, fermentation troubleshooting, and distillation runs. Best for: lessons 8-9, practical application.
  - https://www.youtube.com/c/ClawhammerSupply

- **How to Drink: The Science of Spirits** — Bartender-focused but includes chemistry explanations of flavor, aging, and production methods. Entertaining and accessible. Best for: lessons 12, 17-22 (spirit-specific).
  - https://www.youtube.com/c/howtodrink

- **MIT OCW 10.213J Chemical and Biological Reaction Engineering** — Lectures on thermodynamics and phase equilibria. Rigorous but clear. Best for: lesson 7 (phase diagrams).
  - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/10-213j-chemical-and-biological-reaction-engineering-spring-2006/video_galleries/video-lectures/

- **Master Distiller (Discovery Channel)** — Reality competition show; less educational but good for showing real-world distillation challenges and sensory evaluation. Best for: engagement, not theory.
  - https://www.discovery.com/shows/master-distiller

### Interactive Tools

- **PhET Interactive Simulations: States of Matter** — Molecular-level simulation of phase changes. Visualize boiling, condensation, and vapor pressure. Best for: lesson 1.
  - https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulations/states-of-matter

- **ChemCollective Virtual Lab: Distillation** — Simulated distillation runs with adjustable parameters. Practice without equipment. Best for: lessons 3, 11.
  - http://chemcollective.org/activities/autograded/107

- **Ethanol-Water VLE Calculator** — Online tool to generate T-x-y diagrams and calculate compositions. Best for: lessons 5, 7.
  - https://www.chemours.com/en/resource-library/ethanol-water-equilibrium-calculator

- **Proof Calculator (TTB formula)** — Convert between ABV, proof, and density. Practical tool for lessons 17-18.
  - https://www.ttb.gov/spirits/bam/chapter4.pdf

### Code & Simulations

- **Distillation Column Simulator (Python)** — GitHub repo with McCabe-Thiele implementation and column design tools. For programming-inclined students. Best for: lesson 10.
  - https://github.com/CalebBell/thermo

- **Aspen HYSYS / ChemCAD tutorials** — Industrial process simulation software; free student licenses available. Overkill for most students but powerful for lesson 10 (advanced calculation).
  - https://www.aspentech.com/en/products/engineering/aspen-hysys

## Books (Broader Context)

- **"On Food and Cooking" by Harold McGee** — Chapter on alcohol and fermentation provides flavor chemistry context. Accessible writing; pairs well with lessons 12-16 (congeners).
  - https://www.amazon.com/Food-Cooking-Science-Lore-Kitchen/dp/0684800012

- **"Proof: The Science of Booze" by Adam Rogers** — Pop-science book on distillation, fermentation, and aging. Engaging introduction to the topic; good supplementary reading.
  - https://www.hmhbooks.com/shop/books/Proof/9780547897912

- **"The Drunken Botanist" by Amy Stewart** — Chemistry and botany of spirit ingredients (grains, agave, botanicals). Best for: lessons 17, 20-22 (spirit-specific).
  - https://www.algonquinbooks.com/book/the-drunken-botanist/

## Academic Papers (Deep Dives)

- **"Congener Profiling of Distilled Spirits"** (Journal of Analytical Toxicology, 2007) — Comprehensive analysis of volatile compounds in whiskey, vodka, rum, gin. Best for: lesson 12.
  - https://academic.oup.com/jat/article/31/8/462/750541

- **"Chemistry of Whisky Maturation"** (Journal of Agricultural and Food Chemistry, 2005) — Research on wood-derived compounds and oxidation products during aging. Best for: lesson 23.
  - https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/jf048715c

- **"Ester Formation During Fermentation"** (Journal of Food Science, 2004) — Biochemical pathways for fruity flavor compounds. Best for: lesson 15.
  - https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/jf049347w

- **"Agave Fructans and Fructooligosaccharides"** (Journal of Agricultural and Food Chemistry, 2009) — Chemistry of agave hydrolysis in tequila production. Best for: lesson 22.
  - https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/jf901742b

## Unexpected Connections

- **Perfume Distillation** — Essential oil extraction shares principles with spirit distillation (vapor extraction, fractionation). Interesting cross-domain application. Best for: lesson 21 (gin).
  - https://www.fragrantica.com/news/The-Art-of-Distillation-in-Perfumery-5392.html

- **Petroleum Refining** — Crude oil distillation uses same principles (fractional distillation, theoretical plates) at industrial scale. Shows universality of separation science.
  - https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/oil-and-petroleum-products/refining-crude-oil.php

- **Desalination** — Distillation for freshwater production; similar thermodynamics, different application. Best for: lesson 7 (VLE).
  - https://www.usgs.gov/special-topics/water-science-school/science/desalination

- **Lavoisier's Wine Experiments** — Historical chemistry; early quantitative work on distillation and composition. Best for: historical context in lesson 1.
  - https://www.chemistryworld.com/opinion/antoine-lavoisier/3008191.article

## Regulatory & Safety Resources

- **TTB (Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau)** — US regulations on distillation, labeling, and production. Legal context for lessons 17-18 (proof, spirit definitions).
  - https://www.ttb.gov/spirits

- **OSHA Distillery Safety Guidelines** — Flammability, ventilation, and hazard management. Important safety context for lessons 3, 8.
  - https://www.osha.gov/distilleries

- **Methanol Safety Data (NIH)** — Toxicology and exposure data; addresses methanol fears in lesson 14.
  - https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/Methanol
