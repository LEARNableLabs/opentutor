# Distillation — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Fundamentals of Distillation
- Vapor-liquid equilibrium and phase diagrams
- Simple vs. fractional distillation
- Raoult's law and ideal/non-ideal mixtures
- The ethanol-water azeotrope (95.6% ABV limit)

### 2. Chemistry of Fermentation
- Yeast biochemistry (Saccharomyces cerevisiae)
- Sugar to ethanol conversion (glycolysis pathway)
- Substrate selection (grain, grapes, sugarcane, agave)
- Temperature and pH control

### 3. Distillation Engineering
- Pot stills vs. column stills
- Reflux ratio and theoretical plates
- Heads, hearts, and tails separation
- Heat management and energy efficiency

### 4. Congener Chemistry
- Volatile organic compounds beyond ethanol
- Esters, aldehydes, fusel alcohols
- Flavor compound retention and removal
- Sulfur compounds and off-flavors

### 5. Spirit-Specific Processes
- Whiskey: malting, mashing, barrel aging chemistry
- Vodka: high-proof distillation and filtration
- Rum: molasses chemistry and tropical aging
- Gin: botanical extraction and vapor infusion
- Tequila/Mezcal: agave chemistry and cooking

### 6. Maturation Chemistry
- Wood chemistry (lignin, cellulose, hemicellulose breakdown)
- Oxidation and esterification reactions
- Angel's share (evaporation)
- Temperature cycling effects

## Key Sources

### Academic Resources
- **"The Alcohol Textbook" by Jacques et al.** — Industry standard reference covering fermentation, distillation, and quality control
- **"Whisky: Technology, Production and Marketing" by Inge Russell** — Comprehensive chemistry and engineering
- **MIT OpenCourseWare: Chemical Engineering Thermodynamics** — Phase equilibria and separation processes
- **UC Davis Viticulture & Enology** — Academic program with distillation modules

### Industry & Technical
- **Distilling Knowledge Center (KSL)** — Technical library for craft distillers
- **American Distilling Institute** — Educational resources and workshops
- **Artisan Spirit Magazine** — Practical chemistry articles

### Interactive & Visual
- **ChemCollective Virtual Lab** — Distillation simulations
- **PhET Interactive Simulations** — Phase change demos

### Historical & Cultural
- **"The Chemistry of Beer" (ACS)** — Overlapping fermentation science

## Available Resources

- **Textbooks**: Mix of chemical engineering (separation processes) and food science (flavor chemistry)
- **Video**: Strong maker/DIY community on YouTube, some academic channels
- **Tools**: Virtual distillation labs, phase diagram calculators, proof calculators
- **Data**: TTB regulations, flavor compound databases, yeast strain libraries
- **Community**: Homebrewing/distilling forums, craft distiller associations

## Learning Gaps for Intermediate Students

- **Prerequisites assumed**: General chemistry (stoichiometry, equilibrium), organic chemistry basics (functional groups), some thermodynamics
- **Common gaps**: Phase diagrams, vapor pressure concepts, organic synthesis/reactions
- **Pedagogical approach**: Start with observable phenomena (boiling), build to theory (VLE), then apply to specific spirits
