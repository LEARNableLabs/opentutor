# Systems Biology and Biological Networks — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Network Foundations
- Graph theory for biological networks
- Network topology metrics (degree, clustering, path length)
- Scale-free and small-world properties
- Network motifs (feed-forward loops, feedback loops)

### 2. Types of Biological Networks
- Gene regulatory networks (GRNs)
- Metabolic networks
- Protein-protein interaction networks (PPINs)
- Signaling networks and cascades

### 3. Mathematical Modeling
- Ordinary differential equations (ODEs) for dynamics
- Boolean networks and logical modeling
- Stochastic models
- Flux balance analysis (FBA) for metabolism

### 4. Network Dynamics and Control
- Feedback and feedforward regulation
- Robustness and fragility
- Bistability and oscillations
- Adaptation and homeostasis

### 5. Network Inference and Analysis
- Reverse engineering from data
- Clustering and module detection
- Centrality and importance measures
- Comparative network analysis

### 6. Applications
- Drug target identification
- Disease network medicine
- Synthetic biology design
- Personalized medicine

## Key Sources

### Foundational Textbooks
- **Uri Alon, "An Introduction to Systems Biology"** — standard reference, covers motifs, design principles, ODE modeling
- **Edda Klipp et al., "Systems Biology: A Textbook"** — comprehensive, mathematical
- **Albert-László Barabási, "Network Science"** — network theory foundation

### Online Courses
- **MIT 7.32x Systems Biology** (edX/MIT OCW) — Jeff Gore, covers core principles
- **Stanford CS374 Systems Biology** — Russ Altman
- **Coursera: Network Analysis in Systems Biology** — Icahn School of Medicine

### Research Articles
- Uri Alon (2007) "Network motifs: theory and experimental approaches" — seminal motifs paper
- Barabási & Oltvai (2004) "Network biology: understanding the cell's functional organization"
- Kitano (2002) "Systems Biology: A Brief Overview" — foundational perspective

### Tools and Databases
- **Cytoscape** — network visualization and analysis platform
- **KEGG** — metabolic pathway database
- **STRING** — protein-protein interaction database
- **BioModels** — curated mathematical models
- **SBML** — Systems Biology Markup Language standard
