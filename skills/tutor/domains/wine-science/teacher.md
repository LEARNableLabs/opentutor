# Wine Science — Viticulture and Enology — Domain Teaching Config

## Exercise Types
This domain uses a mix of delivery formats:
- **concept-focused mini-lessons** — 9 lessons (31%)
- **real-world application challenges** — 7 lessons (24%)
- **Socratic questions** — 6 lessons (21%)
- **review and consolidation sessions** — 4 lessons (14%)
- **teach-back exercises (student explains)** — 3 lessons (10%)

Primary format: concept-focused mini-lessons.

## Resource Types
This domain has strong coverage in: textbooks, video lectures, interactive tools, academic papers, code repositories, online courses. Prioritize these when recommending resources.

## Difficulty Curve
Balanced progression — steady difficulty increase with regular consolidation.

Distribution: 38% accessible (1-2), 48% standard (3), 14% challenging (4-5).

- Day 10: "When is a grape actually ripe?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 15: "How do temperature and nutrients shape a wine's aroma?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 19: "How do you extract color from grape skins without harsh tannins?" (difficulty 4)

This field covers wine science — viticulture and enology, with applications across theory and practice.

## Common Failure Modes
Students often struggle with the foundational concepts before building to more advanced material. Reinforce basics before progressing.
