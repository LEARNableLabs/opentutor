# Film Photography Chemistry — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks & Technical References

- **The Film Developing Cookbook** by Bill Troop & Steve Anchell (2nd ed.)
  - Comprehensive coverage of developer formulations and chemistry theory
  - Perfect for intermediate level: explains mechanisms without excessive math
  - Strong focus on aesthetic outcomes of different formulations
  - https://www.amazon.com/Film-Developing-Cookbook-Formulas-Cookbook/dp/0240803043

- **Way Beyond Monochrome** by Ralph W. Lambrecht & Chris Woodhouse (2nd ed.)
  - Advanced treatment of zone system, sensitometry, and chemistry
  - Excellent chapters on developer chemistry and toning
  - Includes digital negative techniques for alternative processes
  - https://www.routledge.com/Way-Beyond-Monochrome/Lambrecht-Woodhouse/p/book/9781138228368

- **The Darkroom Cookbook** by Steve Anchell (4th ed.)
  - Deep dive into printing chemistry and formulations
  - Extensive toning recipes and alternative processes
  - Good intermediate-to-advanced resource
  - https://www.routledge.com/The-Darkroom-Cookbook/Anchell/p/book/9781138919891

- **Alternative Photographic Processes** by Brady Wilks & Christina Z. Anderson
  - Comprehensive guide to cyanotype, platinum/palladium, gum bichromate, etc.
  - Strong chemistry explanations for each process
  - Modern techniques with historical context
  - https://www.routledge.com/Alternative-Photographic-Processes/Wilks-Anderson/p/book/9781138054172

- **The Chemistry of Photography** by Derek McQuarrie (out of print but excellent)
  - University-level chemistry textbook focused on photographic processes
  - Rigorous treatment of silver halide theory and development mechanisms
  - Available used or through library systems

### Technical Documentation

- **Ilford Technical Data Sheets**
  - Professional documentation for all Ilford products
  - Excellent explanations of variable contrast, toning, archival processing
  - https://www.ilfordphoto.com/technical-information

- **Kodak Professional Technical Publications**
  - Historical archive of technical bulletins (many still relevant)
  - Deep coverage of sensitometry, grain structure, spectral sensitivity
  - https://www.kodak.com/en/motion/page/technical-information

- **Digital Truth Photo / Massive Dev Chart**
  - Comprehensive database of film/developer combinations
  - Great for pattern analysis: see how different developers behave across films
  - https://www.digitaltruth.com/devchart.php

### Interactive Tools

- **Photographic Chemistry Calculator**
  - Online tool for calculating developer dilutions, push/pull times
  - Helps visualize temperature/time relationships
  - http://www.digitaltruth.com/devchart.php (includes calculator tools)

- **The Massive Dev Chart Mobile App**
  - Searchable database of development times
  - Timer with temperature compensation
  - iOS and Android
  - https://www.digitaltruth.com/mobile/

- **Analyzer Light Meter Simulator**

  - Connects chemistry choices (N+1/N-1) to exposure strategy
  - https://apps.apple.com/us/app/analyzer/id1050812610

## Unexpected Connections (for wild cards)

### Cross-Discipline Links

- **Photochemistry and Solar Energy**
  - Silver halide photochemistry relates to dye-sensitized solar cells
  - Both involve electron transfer from excited states
  - Research paper: "From Photography to Solar Energy Harvesting" (Nature Chemistry)

- **Nanoparticle Science**
  - Silver grain structure is nanoscale materials science
  - Toning creates nanoparticle composites (silver selenide, etc.)
  - Connection to modern nanomaterials research

- **Conservation Science**
  - Archival chemistry connects to broader field of materials degradation
  - Paper conservation, textile preservation use similar chemistry concepts
  - Link to materials science and archaeological chemistry

- **Food Chemistry**
  - Maillard reaction (browning) shares chemistry with silver image formation
  - Both are redox reactions producing color
  - Developer oxidation resembles food spoilage mechanisms

- **Medical Imaging**
  - Film radiography uses same silver halide chemistry
  - Mammography film optimization requires understanding grain structure
  - Historical connection between medical and pictorial photography

- **Forensic Science**
  - Chemical analysis of photographs for authentication and dating
  - Silver mirroring and degradation as forensic markers
  - Connection to analytical chemistry and art forensics

- **19th Century Industrial Chemistry**
  - Photographic chemistry development paralleled industrial chemical engineering
  - Collodion process contributed to explosive and medical technology
  - Historical lens on chemistry's role in industrialization

### Books Outside Photography

- **The Disappearing Spoon** by Sam Kean (chemistry history)
  - Chapter on silver's unique properties illuminates photographic chemistry
  - Accessible chemistry storytelling style

- **Stuff Matters** by Mark Miodownik (materials science)
  - Chapter on photographic film explains grain structure beautifully
  - Connects to broader materials science concepts

- **The Secret Lives of Color** by Kassia St. Clair
  - Prussian blue chapter connects to cyanotype chemistry
  - Color history contextualizes toning and alternative processes
