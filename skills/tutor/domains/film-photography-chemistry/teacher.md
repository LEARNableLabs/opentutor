## Common Failure Modes
1. **"The developer creates the image"**
   - **Why**: Students confuse amplification with creation. They think development is like printing a digital file.
   - **Correction**: Emphasize that exposure creates the latent image; development merely makes it visible by selective reduction. Use the metaphor: exposure writes in invisible ink, developer applies the revealer.

2. **"All developers are basically the same"**
   - **Why**: Recipe-focused teaching obscures functional differences.
   - **Correction**: Contrast compensating developers (self-limiting, edge effects, sharpness) with solvent developers (uniform, smooth tonality, forgiving). Show side-by-side examples of the same negative developed in Rodinal vs D-76.
