# Science of Learning — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Foundations of Learning Science
- Cognitive psychology of learning and memory
- Memory systems (working, long-term, semantic, episodic)
- Information processing models
- Neuroscience of learning

### 2. Metacognition
- Metacognitive knowledge (knowing about knowing)
- Metacognitive regulation (monitoring and control)
- Judgments of learning (JOLs)
- Feeling of knowing (FOK)
- Metacognitive calibration

### 3. Evidence-Based Study Strategies
- **Retrieval practice** — testing effect, active recall
- **Spaced repetition** — distributed practice, optimal spacing algorithms
- **Interleaving** — mixing topics vs. blocking
- **Elaboration** — connecting new to known, self-explanation
- **Dual coding** — combining verbal and visual
- **Concrete examples** — grounding abstract concepts

### 4. Self-Regulated Learning (SRL)
- Planning and goal-setting
- Strategy selection
- Self-monitoring
- Self-evaluation
- Adaptive help-seeking

### 5. Desirable Difficulties
- Challenges that slow initial learning but boost retention
- Generation effect
- Testing effect
- Contextual interference
- Variation in practice

### 6. Illusions and Biases
- Illusions of competence (fluency, familiarity)
- Dunning-Kruger effect
- Overconfidence in learning
- Rereading and highlighting pitfalls

### 7. Transfer and Expertise
- Near vs. far transfer
- Development of expertise
- Deliberate practice
- Building mental models
