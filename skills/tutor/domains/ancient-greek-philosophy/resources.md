# Ancient Greek Philosophy — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Complete Texts and Translations

- **Internet Classics Archive (MIT)** — http://classics.mit.edu/
  - Full translations of Plato's dialogues, Aristotle's works, Pre-Socratic fragments
  - Free, accessible, searchable
  - Best for: quick access to complete texts

- **Perseus Digital Library** — https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/
  - Greek texts with English translations side-by-side
  - Detailed word-by-word tools for language learners
  - Best for: serious textual study, comparing translations

- **Loeb Classical Library** — https://www.loebclassics.com/ (subscription)
  - Gold standard Greek-English parallel texts
  - Excellent scholarly introductions and notes
  - Best for: authoritative translations with critical apparatus

### Pre-Socratics

- **Curd, Patricia (ed.). "A Presocratics Reader"** — standard anthology
- **McKirahan, Richard. "Philosophy Before Socrates"** — accessible introduction with fragments
- **Kirk, Raven, Schofield. "The Presocratic Philosophers"** — comprehensive scholarly reference
- **Online fragments** — http://www.iep.utm.edu/presoc/ and individual SEP entries

### Plato

- **Cooper, John (ed.). "Plato: Complete Works"** — standard modern edition (Hackett)
- **Recommended dialogues for this level:**
  - Early: *Euthyphro*, *Apology*, *Crito* (Socratic method, trial, death)
  - Middle: *Meno* (recollection), *Phaedo* (Forms, soul), *Republic* (Forms, justice, cave), *Symposium* (love, beauty)
  - Late: *Parmenides* (self-critique of Forms — advanced)
- **Online** — https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/plato/ for overview, then classics.mit.edu for full texts

### Aristotle

- **Barnes, Jonathan (ed.). "The Complete Works of Aristotle"** — standard edition (Revised Oxford Translation)
- **Recommended works for this level:**
  - *Categories*, *On Interpretation* (logic — selections)
  - *Physics* (selections on causes, change)
  - *Metaphysics* (Book I, selections from VII-IX on substance — advanced)
  - *Nicomachean Ethics* (complete, especially Books I-II, X on virtue and eudaimonia)
  - *Politics* (selections on political nature, Book I)
- **Accessible translations:** Terence Irwin (*Nicomachean Ethics*), C.D.C. Reeve (*Politics*, *Metaphysics*)
- **Online** — https://classics.mit.edu/Browse/browse-Aristotle.html

### Hellenistic Philosophy

- **Epictetus. "Enchiridion" (Handbook)** — https://classics.mit.edu/Epictetus/epicench.html
  - Short, practical Stoic manual; perfect for beginners
- **Marcus Aurelius. "Meditations"** — Stoic personal reflections
- **Epicurus. "Letter to Menoeceus"** — https://classics.mit.edu/Epicurus/menoec.html
  - Summary of Epicurean ethics; concise and accessible
- **Long, A.A. & Sedley, D.N. "The Hellenistic Philosophers"** — comprehensive source collection with commentary
- **Inwood, Brad & Gerson, Lloyd. "Hellenistic Philosophy: Introductory Readings"** — more accessible anthology

## Secondary Sources (for context and interpretation)

### Comprehensive Overviews

- **Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy** — https://plato.stanford.edu/
  - Peer-reviewed, authoritative articles on every major figure and concept
  - Regularly updated
  - Best single resource for intermediate students

- **Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy** — https://iep.utm.edu/
  - More accessible than SEP, still scholarly
  - Good for first overviews

### Introductory Books

- **Guthrie, W.K.C. "A History of Greek Philosophy" (6 volumes)** — comprehensive scholarly history
- **Barnes, Jonathan. "Early Greek Philosophy"** — excellent Pre-Socratics introduction
- **Annas, Julia. "Ancient Philosophy: A Very Short Introduction"** — concise overview (Oxford VSI series)
- **Shields, Christopher. "Ancient Philosophy: A Contemporary Introduction"** — accessible but rigorous
- **Taylor, C.C.W. (ed.). "Routledge History of Philosophy, Vol. 1: From the Beginning to Plato"**
- **Long, A.A. "Hellenistic Philosophy"** — standard introduction to Stoics, Epicureans, Skeptics

### Topic-Specific Monographs

- **Reeve, C.D.C. "Philosopher-Kings"** — Plato's political philosophy
- **Vlastos, Gregory. "Socrates: Ironist and Moral Philosopher"** — influential Socratic studies
- **Fine, Gail. "Plato on Knowledge and Forms"** — epistemology and metaphysics
- **Lear, Jonathan. "Aristotle: The Desire to Understand"** — accessible Aristotle overview
- **Nussbaum, Martha. "The Fragility of Goodness"** — Greek ethics, luck, and tragedy
- **Hadot, Pierre. "What is Ancient Philosophy?"** — philosophy as way of life
- **Inwood, Brad. "The Cambridge Companion to the Stoics"** — essays on Stoicism

## Unexpected Connections and Cross-Disciplinary Links

### Mathematics and Philosophy

- **Plato's Academy and geometry** — "Let no one ignorant of geometry enter"
- **Pythagoreanism** — mathematics as key to reality
- **Resources:** Marcus Giaquinto, "The Search for Certainty: A Philosophical Account of Foundations of Mathematics"

### Literature and Philosophy

- **Greek tragedy and philosophy** — Sophocles, Euripides explore philosophical themes
- **Nussbaum, Martha. "The Fragility of Goodness"** — philosophy and tragedy
- **Plato's dialogues as literature** — dramatic settings, character development

### Science and Philosophy

- **Aristotle's biology** — extensive empirical work on animals
- **Atomism and modern physics** — Democritus as proto-scientist?
- **Resources:** Armand Marie Leroi, "The Lagoon: How Aristotle Invented Science"

### Psychology and Philosophy

- **Plato's tripartite soul** — proto-psychology
- **Aristotle on emotions** (*Rhetoric*, *Nicomachean Ethics*)
- **Stoic cognitive therapy** — precursor to CBT
- **Resources:** Robertson, Donald. "How to Think Like a Roman Emperor" (Marcus Aurelius and CBT)

### Politics and Philosophy

- **Athenian democracy and Socrates' trial** — philosophy vs. democracy
- **Plato's *Republic* as political theory**
- **Aristotle's empirical study of constitutions**
- **Resources:** Lane, Melissa. "The Birth of Politics: Eight Greek and Roman Political Ideas and Why They Matter"

### Modern Stoicism Movement

- **Stoicon conferences** — https://modernstoicism.com/
- **Daily Stoic** — Ryan Holiday's popularization
- **Stoic Week** — annual global event
- **Resources:** Pigliucci & Lopez, "A Handbook for New Stoics"

## Curated Reading Lists

### For This Curriculum

**Essential primary readings (in order):**
1. Plato, *Apology* (Socratic method, trial)
2. Plato, *Crito* (civil disobedience)
3. Plato, *Meno* (virtue, recollection)
4. Plato, *Republic* Book VII (Allegory of the Cave)
5. Plato, *Symposium* (love, beauty)
6. Aristotle, *Nicomachean Ethics* Books I-II (virtue ethics, golden mean)
7. Epictetus, *Enchiridion* (Stoicism)
8. Epicurus, *Letter to Menoeceus* (Epicureanism)

**Essential secondary readings:**
1. Stanford Encyclopedia entries on each major figure (as introduction)
2. Annas, "Ancient Philosophy: A Very Short Introduction" (overview)
3. Nussbaum, "The Fragility of Goodness" (excerpts on tragedy and philosophy)
4. Long & Sedley, "The Hellenistic Philosophers" (selections)

### For Going Deeper

- Original texts with scholarly commentaries
- Guthrie's 6-volume history
- Specialized monographs on bottleneck concepts (e.g., Fine on Forms, Shields on Aristotle's substance)
- Learn ancient Greek to read originals
