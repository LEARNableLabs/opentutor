## Common Failure Modes
1. **"Gluten is a single molecule that forms when you add water to flour"**
   - **Why students think this**: "Gluten development" sounds like chemical synthesis
   - **Correction**: Gluten proteins (gliadin and glutenin) already exist in flour; water enables their movement so they can align into networks. Kneading organizes existing proteins, doesn't create new ones.

2. **"Kneading makes dough stronger by beating it into submission"**
   - **Why students think this**: Physical effort seems to correlate with structure
   - **Correction**: Kneading aligns proteins mechanically, like combing tangled hair. Over-kneading can damage networks, not strengthen them further.

3. **"High-protein flour always makes better bread"**
