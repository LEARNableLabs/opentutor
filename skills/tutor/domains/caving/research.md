# Research Summary — Caving and Vertical Rope Techniques

## Topic Overview

Caving vertical rope techniques (Single Rope Technique, or SRT) are the core skills for safely accessing deep cave systems. At intermediate level, students already understand basic caving safety and equipment but need systematic training in rope work, ascending/descending systems, and emergency procedures.

## Major Subtopics

1. **Equipment and Rigging** — rope selection, harnesses, ascenders, descenders, anchor systems, rigging standards
2. **Ascending Systems** — frog system, ropewalker, Mitchell system, efficiency and body mechanics
3. **Descending Techniques** — rappel devices (rack, bobbin, stop), speed control, changeovers
4. **Changeovers and Transitions** — rope-to-rope transfers, passing knots and rebelays, deviation work
5. **Rescue and Self-Rescue** — prusik escape, hauling systems, ascending injured, contingency planning
6. **Advanced Rigging** — traverse lines, guided rappels, complex multi-pitch systems
7. **Standards and Safety Culture** — NSS standards, NFPA rope specs, accident analysis, decision-making

## Key Sources

### Primary Textbooks
- **On Rope: North American Vertical Rope Techniques** (NSS, multiple editions) — the definitive manual for SRT in North America
- **Alpine Caving Techniques** (Marbach/Tourte) — European perspective, more alpine-influenced
- **Vertical Caving** (NSS Vertical Section publications)

### Organizations and Standards
- **National Speleological Society (NSS)** — publishes standards, training materials, accident reports
- **NCRC (National Cave Rescue Commission)** — rescue training curriculum
- **Vertical Section (NSS)** — specialized vertical caving techniques
- **NFPA** — rope and equipment standards

### Educational Resources
- NSS Convention vertical workshops (annual, hands-on training)
- Regional grotto training programs (local caving clubs)
- Petzl and other manufacturers' technical documentation
- Cave rescue training courses (NCRC Level 1-4)

### Video and Interactive
- Vertical training videos from NSS and regional grottos
- Petzl Professional YouTube channel (rope access techniques, many applicable to caving)
- Friars Hole caving videos (vertical systems in context)

## Available Resources

- **NSS website** — standards documents, bibliography, grotto finder
- **On Rope book** — available through NSS bookstore and PDF archives
- **Petzl technical notices** — detailed rigging diagrams and safety specs
- **CavingUK forums** — technique discussions, European vs. American differences
- **Bob & Bob Productions** — classic instructional videos

## Learning Progression for Intermediate Students

Intermediate students typically:
- Have been on 2-5 vertical trips under supervision
- Can ascend and descend simple drops (<100 ft) with basic systems
- Understand rope safety but lack fluency in changeovers and edge work
- Need practice in efficiency, complex systems, and emergency procedures

The curriculum should emphasize:
1. Building fluency and efficiency (moving from conscious competence to unconscious competence)
2. Understanding *why* techniques work (physics, ergonomics, failure modes)
3. Decision-making and judgment (when to rig certain ways, reading rope condition)
4. Self-sufficiency (self-rescue, rigging without instruction)
