# Oceanography — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

Oceanography divides into four main disciplines:

1. **Physical Oceanography** — ocean circulation, currents, waves, tides, heat transport, wind-driven and thermohaline circulation, Ekman layers
2. **Chemical Oceanography** — ocean chemistry, salinity, nutrients, carbonate chemistry, ocean acidification, biogeochemical cycles
3. **Biological Oceanography** — marine ecosystems, productivity, food webs, plankton dynamics, marine life zones
4. **Geological Oceanography** — seafloor mapping, plate tectonics, sediments, ocean basin formation, marine geology

## Available Educational Resources

The field has excellent open educational resources:
- MIT OCW provides full course materials with lecture notes
- Multiple interactive simulators for ocean currents and seafloor mapping
- Real-time ocean data platforms (NOAA, RSMAS)
- Free online courses (Coursera, Khan Academy)
- Strong visual and simulation-based learning opportunities

## Research Coverage

For intermediate level, the curriculum should:
- Balance all four subdisciplines (physical, chemical, biological, geological)
- Emphasize physical oceanography first (foundation for understanding circulation)
- Integrate real-world data and current events (climate change, ocean acidification)
- Leverage interactive tools for complex 3D concepts (currents, bathymetry)
- Connect to current research and societal relevance
