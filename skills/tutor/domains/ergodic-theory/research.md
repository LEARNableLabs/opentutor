# Ergodic Theory — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

1. **Measure-Preserving Transformations** — foundational framework; invariant measures, examples (circle rotations, Arnold cat map, shift spaces)
2. **Recurrence Phenomena** — Poincaré Recurrence Theorem, return times, Kac's formula
3. **Ergodicity & Mixing** — ergodic decomposition, weak mixing, strong mixing, unique ergodicity
4. **Birkhoff's Ergodic Theorem** — pointwise and mean ergodic theorems, applications to time averages
5. **Entropy Theory** — measure-theoretic entropy (Kolmogorov-Sinai), topological entropy, variational principle
6. **Spectral Theory** — unitary operators on L², spectral characterization of mixing
7. **Applications** — number theory (continued fractions, equidistribution), statistical mechanics (Boltzmann hypothesis), dynamical systems

### Prerequisites
- Real analysis and measure theory (Lebesgue integration, sigma-algebras, convergence theorems)
- Point-set topology (metric spaces, compactness)
- Basic functional analysis (Lᵖ spaces, bounded operators) — helpful but not essential
- Linear algebra — for spectral theory applications

### Connections
- **Dynamical Systems** — ergodic theory provides long-term statistical behavior of systems
- **Probability Theory** — laws of large numbers as special cases of ergodic theorems
- **Number Theory** — equidistribution, continued fractions, Diophantine approximation
- **Statistical Mechanics** — Boltzmann ergodic hypothesis, equilibrium states
- **Information Theory** — entropy as information rate
