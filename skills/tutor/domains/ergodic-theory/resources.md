# Ergodic Theory — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

- **Peter Walters, "An Introduction to Ergodic Theory"** (Springer, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 79) — the gold standard graduate textbook. Comprehensive, rigorous, well-motivated. Covers measure-preserving systems through entropy. Perfect for intermediate learners with solid measure theory background.

- **Karma Dajani & Charlene Kalle, "A First Course in Ergodic Theory"** (Routledge, 2022) — designed for students with minimal prerequisites. Gentler than Walters; good for filling gaps. Available: https://www.routledge.com/A-First-Course-in-Ergodic-Theory/Dajani-Kalle/p/book/9780367226206

- **Omri Sarig's Lecture Notes** (Weizmann Institute) — detailed, modern treatment from recurrence through entropy. Excellent for self-study. Free PDF: https://www.weizmann.ac.il/math/sarigo/sites/math.sarigo/files/uploads/ergodicnotes.pdf

- **Curtis McMullen's Course Notes** (Harvard) — concise, geometric perspective. Connects ergodic theory to dynamics and Teichmüller theory. Free PDF: https://people.math.harvard.edu/~ctm/papers/home/text/class/notes/ergodic/course.pdf

- **Maryam Mirzakhani's Lecture Notes** (Berkeley Math 248) — notes from her course, focusing on connections to geometry. Clear exposition. Free PDF: https://math.berkeley.edu/~fengt/ergodic_theory.pdf

- **Ohio State University Math 7221/7222 Syllabi** — detailed two-semester course outlines with weekly topics and problem sets. Syllabi: 
  - Part I: https://math.osu.edu/sites/default/files/courses/MATH_7221.02_Syllabus.pdf
  - Part II: https://math.osu.edu/sites/default/files/courses/MATH_7222.02_Syllabus.pdf

### Interactive Tools

- **Ergodicity Economics Interactive Apps** — Ole Peters' site includes interactive coin toss simulations demonstrating ergodicity breaking. Excellent for building intuition about time vs ensemble averages. Explore: https://ergodicityeconomics.com/

- **Python/SAGE notebooks for symbolic dynamics** — compute orbits, visualize shift spaces, compute invariant measures. (Students can code their own or find examples in course repos.)

- **Desmos/GeoGebra for circle rotations** — visualize orbits wrapping around the circle, plot histograms of visit frequencies, see equidistribution in action. Search: "circle rotation ergodic" on GeoGebra.

- **Arnold cat map visualizations** — many online applets show the stretching, folding, and return to initial image after period. Search: "Arnold cat map visualization."

### People (to look up)

- **Pioneers:** Henri Poincaré (recurrence), George Birkhoff (ergodic theorem), Andrey Kolmogorov (entropy), Vladimir Arnold (cat map), John von Neumann (mean ergodic theorem)

- **Modern researchers:** Maryam Mirzakhani (ergodic theory on Teichmüller space), Marina Ratner (rigidity theorems), Hillel Furstenberg (multiple recurrence, diophantine approximation), Curtis McMullen (dynamics and geometry), Omri Sarig (thermodynamic formalism), Bryna Kra & Bernard Host (nonconventional ergodic theorems), Anatole Katok (smooth ergodic theory)

- **Expository writers:** Peter Walters (textbook author), Karl Petersen (textbook author), Karma Dajani & Charlene Kalle (accessible textbook), Cesar Silva (survey articles)

### Unexpected Connections

- **Economics and decision theory** — ergodicity economics (Ole Peters, Gerd Gigerenzer) shows how ergodic vs non-ergodic processes fundamentally change optimal decisions. Challenges expected utility theory.

- **Number theory** — Gauss map ergodicity explains digit frequencies in continued fractions; equidistribution theorems connect to Diophantine approximation.

- **Information theory** — Kolmogorov-Sinai entropy measures information production rate; Shannon entropy is a special case.

- **Statistical mechanics** — Boltzmann ergodic hypothesis claims time averages equal ensemble averages for physical systems.

- **Combinatorics** — Furstenberg correspondence principle translates combinatorial problems (e.g., Szemerédi's theorem on arithmetic progressions) into ergodic-theoretic statements.

- **Quantum mechanics** — Koopman-von Neumann formulation presents classical mechanics in a Hilbert space framework analogous to quantum theory.

- **Machine learning** — ergodic theorems underlie convergence guarantees for stochastic gradient descent and reinforcement learning algorithms.

## Advanced Textbooks (for going deeper)

- **Karl Petersen, "Ergodic Theory"** (Cambridge Studies in Advanced Mathematics) — advanced, proof-heavy, comprehensive. For students moving toward research.

- **Manfred Einsiedler & Thomas Ward, "Ergodic Theory with a view towards Number Theory"** (Springer GTM 259) — emphasizes connections to number theory; includes homogeneous dynamics and Ratner's theorems.

- **Ricardo Mañé, "Ergodic Theory and Differentiable Dynamics"** (Springer) — smooth ergodic theory, hyperbolic systems, Pesin theory.

- **Anatole Katok & Boris Hasselblatt, "Introduction to the Modern Theory of Dynamical Systems"** (Cambridge) — encyclopedic; smooth dynamics, entropy, hyperbolic theory.
