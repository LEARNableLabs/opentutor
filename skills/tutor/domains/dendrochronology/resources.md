# Dendrochronology — Resources

## Primary Textbooks

### For Intermediate Students:

- **"Dendroecology: Principles and Practice" by James H. Speer** (2010)  
  The modern standard for learning dendrochronology. Covers core techniques, statistics, and applications with a practical, accessible approach. Perfect for intermediate students who want to *do* dendro, not just read about it.  
  [Springer](https://www.springer.com/gp/book/9781402106781)

- **"An Introduction to Tree-Ring Dating" by Marvin A. Stokes and Terah L. Smiley** (1968/1996)  
  Classic foundational text. Short, clear, focused on crossdating fundamentals. Dated but the core principles remain essential. Free PDF often available through university libraries.  
  [University of Arizona Press](https://uapress.arizona.edu/)

- **"Tree Rings and Climate" by Harold C. Fritts** (1976)  
  The definitive work on dendroclimatology. Dense and technical, but chapters 1-5 are accessible and cover ring formation, crossdating, and standardization beautifully. Reference this for deep understanding; don't try to read cover-to-cover at intermediate level.  
  [Classic reprint available](https://www.elsevier.com/books/tree-rings-and-climate/fritts/978-0-12-268450-0)

### Supplementary:

- **"Principles of Dendrochronology" by Susan L. Nash** (accessible online course notes)
- **"Dendrochronology in North America" edited by M.K. Hughes et al.** — regional focus, great for site selection insights

## Software and Tools

### Essential (Free):

- **COFECHA** — Statistical crossdating validation. The industry standard. Windows/Mac/Linux.  
  [Download from LTRR](https://www.ltrr.arizona.edu/software.html)  
  [COFECHA manual](https://www.ltrr.arizona.edu/webhome/dmeko/cofecha.pdf)

- **ARSTAN** — Chronology building and standardization. Pairs with COFECHA.  
  [Download from LTRR](https://www.ltrr.arizona.edu/software.html)

- **dplR (Dendrochronology Program Library in R)** — Open-source R package. Modern, flexible, excellent for learning and publication-quality analysis.  
  [CRAN dplR page](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/dplR/index.html)  
  [Interactive dplR tutorial](https://andybunn.shinyapps.io/dplR_tutorial/)  
  [dplR book (free online)](https://opendendro.github.io/dplR-workshop/)

- **CooRecorder / CDendro** — Free measurement and crossdating software (Windows/Mac). Visual and intuitive.  
  [Cybis Elektronik](http://www.cybis.se/forfun/dendro/)

### Commercial:

- **WinDENDRO** — Professional measurement system with hardware integration. Used in many labs.  
  [Regent Instruments](http://www.regentinstruments.com/assets/windendro_about.html)

### Data Archives:

- **International Tree-Ring Data Bank (ITRDB)** — Thousands of chronologies worldwide. NOAA Paleoclimatology.

- **NOAA Paleoclimatology Tree-Ring Data**

## Cross-Disciplinary Connections

### Climate Science:

  Global paleoclimate synthesis using tree rings and other proxies  
  [PAGES 2k](http://www.pastglobalchanges.org/science/wg/2k-network/intro)

### Art and Cultural Heritage:

- **Dating musical instruments** — Stradivarius violins, historic keyboards

- **Colonial architecture** — Dating timbers in historic buildings

### Ecology:

- **Forest Dynamics** — Growth responses to competition, disturbance, climate change

## Unexpected Rabbit Holes

- **Tree Rings on Mars?** — Proposals to use growth rings in hypothetical Martian organisms or engineered plants for climate archives on other planets. (Speculative but fun!)

- **Dendrochronology of Bamboo** — Non-woody plants can show growth bands. Used in archaeological contexts in Asia.

- **Coral Banding as "Ocean Tree Rings"** — Cross-dating methods adapted to marine systems.

- **Ice Core / Tree Ring Synchronization** — Matching volcanic events in both archives to create globally synchronized paleoclimate timelines.

- **Forensics and Timber Tracking** — Using tree-ring patterns to trace illegal logging and timber origin.

- **The Dendrochronology of Wine** — Dating wine vintages by isotopic signatures in grape fermentation products (not the grapes themselves, but the barrels!).

## Learning Pathway Recommendations

### Phase 1 (Lessons 1-10): Foundation
Read: Stokes & Smiley intro chapters, Speer chapters 1-4  
Watch: University of Arizona lab tour videos  
Do: Install COFECHA/CDendro, explore ITRDB datasets visually

### Phase 2 (Lessons 11-20): Core Skills
Read: Speer chapters 5-8, Fritts chapters 3-5 (standardization)  
Watch: Tree-Ring Society webinars on crossdating and chronology building  
Do: Complete dplR interactive tutorial, run COFECHA on practice datasets, build a chronology in ARSTAN or dplR

### Phase 3 (Lessons 21-28): Applications and Advanced
Read: Speer chapters 9-12 (applications), selected papers from *Tree-Ring Research*  
Watch: Webinars on fire history, archaeology, or isotope methods (based on interest)  
Do: Design a mini-project, explore advanced tools (blue intensity, wood anatomy software)
