# Optics Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### Classical Optics
1. **Wave Optics** — electromagnetic wave theory, Maxwell equations for light, wave equation
2. **Interference** — Young's double slit, thin films, interferometers (Michelson, Fabry-Perot)
3. **Diffraction** — Huygens-Fresnel principle, single slit, gratings, Fraunhofer vs Fresnel regimes
4. **Polarization** — Jones vectors, Stokes parameters, birefringence, optical activity
5. **Coherence Theory** — temporal and spatial coherence, correlation functions, partial coherence

### Quantum Optics
6. **Quantization of Light** — photons, Fock states, field operators, commutation relations
7. **Coherent and Squeezed States** — minimum uncertainty states, quadrature operators
8. **Light-Matter Interaction** — absorption/emission, two-level atoms, Rabi oscillations, Jaynes-Cummings model
9. **Photon Statistics** — bunching/antibunching, HBT effect, g^(2) function
10. **Quantum Applications** — single photons, entanglement, quantum cryptography, quantum computing with light

## Key Sources

### Textbooks
- **Hecht, Optics (5th ed)** — comprehensive classical optics, excellent for wave theory, interference, diffraction
- **Born & Wolf, Principles of Optics** — authoritative classical reference, mathematical depth
- **Gerry & Knight, Introductory Quantum Optics** — accessible quantum treatment, good for intermediate students
- **Griffiths, Introduction to Quantum Mechanics** — chapter on light-matter interaction
- **Fox, Quantum Optics: An Introduction** — Oxford Master Series, bridges classical-quantum gap well
- **Loudon, The Quantum Theory of Light** — comprehensive quantum optics reference

### Online Courses
- **MIT OCW 8.03** — Vibrations and Waves (classical wave optics foundation)
- **MIT OCW 8.421** — Atomic and Optical Physics I (quantum optics, light-matter interaction)
- **MIT OCW 8.422** — Atomic and Optical Physics II (advanced quantum optics)
- **Stanford EE 282** — Quantum Optics and Quantum Information

### Interactive Resources
- **PhET Simulations** — wave interference, geometric optics, quantum wave interference
- **Optics simulators** — Mathematica demonstrations, Python optics libraries
- **Virtual labs** — interference patterns, polarization experiments
