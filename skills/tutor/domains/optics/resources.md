# Optics — Resources

## Primary Textbooks

### Classical Optics
- **Hecht, _Optics_ (5th edition, 2016)** — The standard undergraduate text. Comprehensive coverage of geometrical optics, wave optics, interference, diffraction, and polarization. Excellent figures and real-world examples. Best for lessons 1-13.
  - Publisher: [Pearson](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/optics/P200000007073)
  
- **Born & Wolf, _Principles of Optics_ (7th edition, 1999)** — Advanced classical reference. Mathematical rigor, comprehensive theory. Use for deeper dives into coherence theory and diffraction.
  - [Cambridge University Press](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/principles-of-optics/6A3C1D31C272E3E23640F88657F6AC1F)

### Quantum Optics
- **Gerry & Knight, _Introductory Quantum Optics_ (2005)** — Perfect for intermediate students. Covers quantization, coherent states, light-matter interaction, photon statistics. Best match for lessons 14-26.
  - [Cambridge University Press](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/introductory-quantum-optics/7853978B1A4C63E33B86F11A82C42C3C)
  
- **Fox, _Quantum Optics: An Introduction_ (Oxford Master Series, 2006)** — Concise, well-structured. Good for light-matter interaction and applications. Complements Gerry & Knight.
  - [Oxford University Press](https://global.oup.com/academic/product/quantum-optics-9780198566731)
  
- **Loudon, _The Quantum Theory of Light_ (3rd edition, 2000)** — Comprehensive quantum optics reference. More advanced than Gerry & Knight; use for rabbit holes.
  - [Oxford University Press](https://global.oup.com/academic/product/the-quantum-theory-of-light-9780198501770)

### Supporting Texts
- **Griffiths, _Introduction to Quantum Mechanics_ (3rd edition, 2018)** — Chapters on time-dependent perturbation theory and light-matter interaction. Prerequisite reference.
  - [Cambridge University Press](https://www.cambridge.org/highereducation/books/introduction-to-quantum-mechanics/990799CA07A83FC5312402AF6860311E)
  
- **Feynman Lectures, Volume II** — Chapters 18-20 on electromagnetism and light. Beautiful physical intuition.
  - [Free online](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/)
