# Astrobiology — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Origins of Life
- Prebiotic chemistry and the RNA world
- Hydrothermal vents and early Earth conditions
- Miller-Urey experiments and amino acid synthesis
- Metabolism-first vs. replication-first theories

### 2. Extremophiles and Life's Limits
- Thermophiles, psychrophiles, halophiles, acidophiles
- Tardigrades and cryptobiosis
- Deep biosphere and subsurface life
- Radiation resistance (Deinococcus radiodurans)

### 3. Planetary Habitability
- Habitable zone (HZ) concept and variations
- Tidal heating and subsurface oceans
- Atmospheric composition and greenhouse effects
- Water activity and liquid solvents beyond water

### 4. Exoplanet Science
- Transit method, radial velocity, direct imaging
- Characterizing exoplanet atmospheres
- Super-Earths, mini-Neptunes, hot Jupiters
- TESS, Kepler, and JWST observations

### 5. Solar System Targets
- Mars: methane detection, ancient water, Perseverance rover
- Europa and Enceladus: subsurface oceans, plumes
- Titan: organic chemistry, methane lakes
- Venus: phosphine controversy

### 6. Biosignatures
- Atmospheric biosignatures (O₂, O₃, CH₄, N₂O)
- Surface biosignatures (vegetation red edge)
- Disequilibrium chemistry
- False positives and abiotic mimics

### 7. SETI and Technosignatures
- Drake equation and Fermi paradox
- Radio SETI (Breakthrough Listen)
- Optical SETI and laser technosignatures
- Megastructures (Dyson spheres, transit anomalies)
