# Behavioral Economics — Resources

## Primary Textbooks

- **Kahneman, D. (2011). Thinking, Fast and Slow** — the definitive introduction. Accessible, example-rich.
- **Thaler, R. & Sunstein, C. (2008). Nudge** — choice architecture and policy applications.
- **Ariely, D. (2008). Predictably Irrational** — engaging experiments on everyday irrationality.

## Online Courses

- **Behavioral Economics (Coursera, Duke University)** — Dan Ariely's course
- **Behavioral Finance (Yale, Robert Shiller)** — OpenCourseWare lectures

## Key Papers

- **Kahneman & Tversky (1979). Prospect Theory: An Analysis of Decision under Risk** — foundational paper
- **Thaler (1980). Toward a Positive Theory of Consumer Choice** — mental accounting
- **Tversky & Kahneman (1981). The Framing of Decisions and the Psychology of Choice** — framing effects

## Video

- **Daniel Kahneman: The riddle of experience vs. memory (TED)** — 20 min intro
