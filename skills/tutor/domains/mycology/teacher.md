# Mycology — the hidden kingdom of fungi — Domain Teaching Config

## Exercise Types
This domain uses a mix of delivery formats:
- **concept-focused mini-lessons** — 11 lessons (33%)
- **real-world application challenges** — 8 lessons (24%)
- **Socratic questions** — 6 lessons (18%)
- **review and consolidation sessions** — 4 lessons (12%)

## Common Failure Modes
1. **"Fungi are just decomposers"**
   - **Why students think this:** Elementary and high school biology often reduce fungi to "decomposers" in food web diagrams
   - **How to correct:** Introduce symbiotic roles early (mycorrhizae in lesson 2, lichens in lesson 24); emphasize that many fungi are mutualists, parasites, or pathogens, not just decomposers

2. **"Mushrooms are the whole organism"**
   - **Why students think this:** Mushrooms are visible; mycelium is hidden underground
   - **How to correct:** Explicitly teach in lesson 4 that mycelium is the main body, fruiting bodies are temporary reproductive structures; use analogy of apple (fruiting body) vs. apple tree (mycelium)

3. **"Fungi photosynthesize like plants"**
   - **Why students think this:** Persistent plant-fungus confusion, reinforced by "plant" categories in everyday language
   - **How to correct:** Hammer home absorptive heterotrophic nutrition in lesson 1; show phylogenetic tree positioning fungi closer to animal
