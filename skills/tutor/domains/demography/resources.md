# Demography — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks

- **Weeks, John R.** — *Population: An Introduction to Concepts and Issues* (13th ed., 2023)
  - Standard intermediate textbook, accessible, strong real-world examples
  - Covers all core topics with policy focus
  - Good for this level
  - https://www.cengage.com/c/population-an-introduction-to-concepts-and-issues-13e-weeks/

- **Rowland, Donald T.** — *Demographic Methods and Concepts* (2003)
  - Practical focus on calculation and interpretation
  - Clear explanations of life tables, standardization, projection
  - Slightly dated but conceptually solid
  - https://global.oup.com/academic/product/demographic-methods-and-concepts-9780198752158

- **Preston, Samuel H., Patrick Heuveline, Michel Guillot** — *Demography: Measuring and Modeling Population Processes* (2000)
  - More technical, rigorous mathematical treatment
  - Good reference for life tables, stable population theory
  - Use selectively for advanced students
  - https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Demography%3A+Measuring+and+Modeling+Population+Processes-p-9781557864512

### Online Courses

- **Population Reference Bureau (PRB) — Education Resources**
  - Free lesson plans, teaching materials, data activities
  - https://www.prb.org/education/

- **PRB Population Handbook (7th edition)**
  - Free comprehensive guide to demographic concepts and measures
  - https://www.prb.org/resources/population-handbook/

## Supplementary Resources

### Interactive Visualizations

- **PopulationPyramid.net**
  - Population pyramids for every country, animated over time
  - Essential for lessons 4-5, 21-23 (age structure)
  - https://www.populationpyramid.net/

- **Our World in Data — Population & Demography**
  - Max Roser's comprehensive interactive charts
  - Fertility, mortality, life expectancy, age structure, migration
  - Exceptional data quality and visualization
  - https://ourworldindata.org/population-and-demography

- **UN Population Pyramids (interactive)**
  - Official UN data, all countries, historical and projected
  - https://population.un.org/wpp/Graphs/DemographicProfiles/

- **Gapminder World**
  - Animated scatter plots of population indicators over time
  - Great for showing demographic transition visually
  - https://www.gapminder.org/tools/

### Data Sources

- **UN World Population Prospects (WPP)**
  - The authoritative source for global population data and projections
  - Updated every 2-3 years, complete methodology documentation
  - https://population.un.org/wpp/

- **US Census Bureau — International Database**
  - Population data, pyramids, and projections for all countries
  - https://www.census.gov/data-tools/demo/idb/

- **US Census Bureau — Domestic Data**
  - Detailed US demographic data, vital statistics
  - https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial-census.html

- **Human Mortality Database (HMD)**
  - High-quality life tables for 40+ countries, long time series
  - Requires free registration
  - https://www.mortality.org/

- **Human Fertility Database (HFD)**
  - Age-specific fertility rates, long time series
  - Companion to HMD
  - https://www.humanfertility.org/

- **Demographic and Health Surveys (DHS)**
  - Household surveys from 90+ countries, fertility and health focus
  - https://dhsprogram.com/

- **Population Reference Bureau — World Population Data Sheet**
  - Annual snapshot of key indicators for all countries
  - https://www.prb.org/resources/2024-world-population-data-sheet/

### Hands-on Tools

- **Excel / Google Sheets**
  - Life table construction (lesson 11)
  - Cohort-component projection (lesson 26)
  - Calculating TFR from ASFR (lesson 14)
  - Templates: https://www.prb.org/resources/

- **R packages**
  - `demography` — demographic analysis and forecasting (Rob Hyndman)
  - `MortalityLaws` — parametric mortality models
  - `LexisPlotR` — Lexis diagrams for visualizing demographic data
  - CRAN: https://cran.r-project.org/

- **Python libraries**
  - `lifelines` — survival analysis (includes life table functions)
  - `statsmodels` — demographic rate calculations
  - `pandas` — data manipulation for demographic analysis

- **Spectrum / DemProj**
  - Professional demographic projection software (free)
  - Used by USAID and many national statistical offices
  - https://www.healthpolicyproject.com/pubs/4_DemProjOverviewNov2013.pdf

### Papers and Reports

- **Omran, Abdel R.** (1971) — "The Epidemiological Transition"

  - Complements lesson 10 and 24

- **Lee, Ronald** (2003) — "The Demographic Transition: Three Centuries of Fundamental Change"
  - Comprehensive review of demographic transition theory
  - For lesson 24
  - https://www.jstor.org/stable/3088359

- **Bongaarts, John & Rodolfo A. Bulatao (Eds.)** (2000) — *Beyond Six Billion*
  - National Academy of Sciences report on future population growth
  - For lesson 25-26 on projections
  - https://www.nap.edu/catalog/9828/beyond-six-billion-forecasting-the-worlds-population

## Unexpected Connections

### Demography + Economics
- **Demographic dividend** — East Asian growth driven by age structure
  - David Bloom et al. work: https://www.hks.harvard.edu/centers/cid/publications/faculty-working-papers/demographic-dividend-new-perspective

### Demography + Climate
- **Population scenarios in IPCC models** — SSPs (Shared Socioeconomic Pathways)
  - Population is a key driver of emissions
  - https://www.carbonbrief.org/explainer-how-shared-socioeconomic-pathways-explore-future-climate-change/

### Demography + Urban Planning
- **Population projections drive infrastructure needs** — schools, hospitals, transport
  - UN Habitat: https://unhabitat.org/

### Demography + Genetics
- **Effective population size** — genetic diversity depends on demographic history
  - Bottlenecks, founder effects
  - https://www.nature.com/scitable/knowledge/library/genetic-drift-and-effective-population-size-12523692/

### Demography + Machine Learning
- **Mortality forecasting with neural networks** — competing with Lee-Carter models
  - Recent literature on deep learning for demographic prediction

### Demography + History
- **Black Death demographic impact** — Europe lost 1/3 of population, changed labor markets
- **Columbian Exchange** — 90% indigenous American mortality, demographic collapse
- **Baby boom** — post-WWII fertility spike, shaped politics for 70+ years
