# Demography — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

1. **Core Demographic Measures** — crude rates, age-specific rates, standardization, life tables
2. **Mortality** — life expectancy, infant mortality, survival curves, epidemiological transition
3. **Fertility** — total fertility rate, replacement level, age-specific fertility, proximate determinants
4. **Migration** — types of migration, measurement challenges, demographic impact
5. **Population Structure** — age pyramids, dependency ratios, aging populations, sex ratios
6. **Population Composition** — race/ethnicity, education, labor force, household structure
7. **Population Dynamics** — balancing equation, growth rates, momentum, projection methods
8. **Demographic Transition** — theory, stages, variations across countries, second demographic transition
9. **Population Policy** — pronatalist vs antinatalist, China's one-child, migration policy
10. **Applied Demography** — census methods, survey design, vital registration, data quality

## Key Sources

### Academic/Textbook
- **Weeks, John R.** — *Population: An Introduction to Concepts and Issues* (standard intermediate text)
- **Preston, Heuveline, Guillot** — *Demography: Measuring and Modeling Population Processes* (more technical)
- **Rowland, Donald T.** — *Demographic Methods and Concepts* (practical focus)

### Data & Visualization
- **UN Population Division** — World Population Prospects, detailed data and projections
- **Population Reference Bureau (PRB)** — World Population Data Sheet, infographics
- **US Census Bureau** — American data, International Database
- **Our World in Data** — Max Roser's interactive charts on fertility, mortality, population
- **PopulationPyramid.net** — interactive population pyramids for all countries

### Course Materials
- **Berkeley Demography** — strong graduate program, some public lecture materials
- **Population Reference Bureau Education** — lesson plans and teaching resources
- **PRB webinars** — regular expert presentations on demographic topics

### Tools
- **Excel/Google Sheets** — life table construction, basic projections
- **R packages** — demography, popbio, LexisPlotR
- **Python** — lifelines, statsmodels for demographic analysis

## Resource Availability

Strong availability across:
- Textbooks (multiple good intermediate options)
- Interactive visualizations (population pyramids, fertility trends)
- Real-world data (UN, World Bank, census bureaus)
- Video content (PRB webinars, academic lectures)
- Hands-on tools (life table calculators, projection software)

## Teaching Considerations

- Highly visual subject — population pyramids, survival curves, fertility trends
- Real-world policy relevance — aging, migration, family planning
- Mathematical content is moderate — rates, ratios, basic exponential growth
- Data literacy opportunity — working with census data, vital statistics
- Strong current events connections — demographic dividend, aging crisis, migration debates
