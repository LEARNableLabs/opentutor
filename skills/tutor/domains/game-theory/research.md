# Game Theory — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

Game theory at the intermediate level encompasses:

1. **Strategic-Form Games** — normal form representation, dominant strategies, rationalizability
2. **Nash Equilibrium** — pure and mixed strategies, existence, computation, interpretation
3. **Sequential Games** — extensive form, backward induction, subgame perfection
4. **Repeated Games** — finite vs infinite repetition, folk theorems, cooperation emergence
5. **Bayesian Games** — incomplete information, types, Bayesian Nash equilibrium
6. **Applications** — auctions, voting, bargaining, market competition, evolutionary dynamics
7. **Algorithmic Game Theory** — mechanism design, computational aspects

## Pedagogical Notes

- Strong emphasis on **strategic thinking** and **mathematical modeling**
- Mix of **formal theory** (proofs, existence theorems) and **applied examples** (auctions, voting, markets)
- **Computation** matters: finding equilibria is often non-trivial
- Best learned through **worked examples** and **interactive exploration**
- **Real-world applications** crucial for motivation and retention
