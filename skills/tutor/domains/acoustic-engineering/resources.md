# Acoustic Engineering and Room Design — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks
- **Master Handbook of Acoustics (6th ed.)** by F. Alton Everest & Ken Pohlmann — comprehensive, accessible, industry-standard reference. Covers wave mechanics, room modes, absorption, diffusion, measurement. Perfect for intermediate students.
  - Publisher: McGraw-Hill
  - https://www.mhprofessional.com/

- **Sound Reproduction: The Acoustics and Psychoacoustics of Loudspeakers and Rooms (3rd ed.)** by Floyd Toole — evidence-based approach to speaker-room interaction. Essential for understanding boundary loading, SBIR, and the limits of EQ.
  - Publisher: Routledge
  - https://www.routledge.com/Sound-Reproduction-The-Acoustics-and-Psychoacoustics-of-Loudspeakers-and-Rooms/Toole/p/book/9781138921368

- **Acoustics and Psychoacoustics (5th ed.)** by David Howard & Jamie Angus — bridges physical and perceptual aspects. Good for understanding why measurements matter to listening.
  - Publisher: Routledge
  - https://www.routledge.com/Acoustics-and-Psychoacoustics/Howard-Angus/p/book/9781138859876

- **Acoustic Absorbers and Diffusers: Theory, Design and Application (3rd ed.)** by Trevor Cox & Peter D'Antonio — definitive reference on treatment design. Advanced but accessible chapters on porous, resonant, and diffusive treatments.
  - Publisher: CRC Press
  - https://www.crcpress.com/

### Academic Courses (Open Access)
- **MIT 2.067: Acoustics and Sensing** (Spring 2015) — covers wave equation, impedance, radiation, room acoustics. Lecture notes, problem sets, and some video.
  - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-067-acoustics-and-sensing-spring-2015/

- **Salford Acoustics Web Resources** — introductory modules on wave behavior, resonance, room acoustics from University of Salford's renowned acoustics program.
  - https://www.acoustics.salford.ac.uk/acoustics_world/

### Standards and Technical Documents
- **ISO 3382-1:2009** — Measurement of room acoustic parameters (RT60, EDT, clarity, etc.). Defines professional measurement practice.
  - https://www.iso.org/standard/40979.html

- **IEC 60268-16** — Sound system equipment - Part 16: Objective rating of speech intelligibility. Useful for understanding STI and other intelligibility metrics.
  - https://www.iec.ch/

### Interactive Tools and Software
- **Room EQ Wizard (REW)** — free, industry-standard measurement and analysis software. Runs on Windows, Mac, Linux. Essential tool.
  - https://www.roomeqwizard.com/

- **Room Mode Calculator** (Hunecke) — calculates axial, tangential, and oblique modes for rectangular rooms. Visualizes modal distribution.
  - https://www.hunecke.de/en/calculators/room-eigenmodes.html

- **RT60 Calculator** (Sengpiel Audio) — Sabine equation calculator with absorption coefficient database for common materials.
  - https://www.sengpielaudio.com/calculator-RT60.htm

- **Wavelength Calculator** (Sengpiel Audio) — converts frequency to wavelength and vice versa. Simple but essential for sanity checks.
  - https://www.sengpielaudio.com/calculator-wavelength.htm

- **Bob Gold's Room Mode Calculator** — visualizes room modes and suggests optimal dimensions based on various room ratio theories.
  - https://www.bobgolds.com/Mode/RoomModes.htm

- **EASE / EASE Focus** (commercial) — professional room acoustic prediction software. Free demo available; educational licenses offered.
  - https://ease.afmg.eu/

- **Odeon** (commercial) — geometric room acoustics simulator. Widely used in architectural acoustics. Educational licenses available.
  - https://odeon.dk/

### Impulse Response Libraries (for listening and analysis)
- **OpenAIR** — free impulse response library of real spaces: concert halls, churches, studios, stairwells. Download and import to REW or convolution reverb.
  - https://www.openairlib.net/

- **EchoThief** — free impulse responses of interesting spaces, released by AudioEase (makers of Altiverb). High quality.
  - https://www.echothief.com/

### Manufacturer Resources (technical, not marketing)
- **Genelec: Monitoring Setup and Acoustic Environment** — excellent guides on speaker placement, boundary loading, and room treatment. Free PDFs.
  - https://www.genelec.com/monitor-placement

- **Harman: Research Publications** — Floyd Toole and Sean Olive's research on loudspeaker performance, room interaction, and listener preferences. Freely available papers.
  - https://www.harman.com/innovation/research

- **GIK Acoustics: Learning Center** — practical articles on bass trapping, diffusion, and treatment placement. Commercial site but educational content is solid.
  - https://www.gikacoustics.com/

### Code and Computational Tools
- **pyroomacoustics** (Python library) — simulate room acoustics, compute RIRs (room impulse responses), design beamformers. Open source.
  - https://github.com/LCAV/pyroomacoustics

- **Acoustic Python** (NumPy-based) — tools for signal processing, acoustics calculations, and room analysis.
  - https://github.com/python-acoustics/python-acoustics

- **REW HouseCurve** — iOS app for smartphone-based room measurements. Simpler than full REW but useful for quick checks.
  - https://housecurve.com/

## People to Know (researchers, practitioners, educators)

### Researchers and Authors
- **F. Alton Everest** (1909-2005) — wrote *Master Handbook of Acoustics*, foundational text for practical room acoustics.
- **Floyd Toole** — Harman researcher, author of *Sound Reproduction*. Pioneer in evidence-based loudspeaker and room research.
- **Trevor Cox** — University of Salford, author of *Acoustic Absorbers and Diffusers* and popular science books on sound. Active science communicator.
- **Peter D'Antonio** — co-author of *Acoustic Absorbers and Diffusers*, founder of RPG Diffusor Systems. Invented many diffuser designs.
- **Manfred Schroeder** (1926-2009) — developed quadratic-residue diffusers (QRD), pioneered diffusion research. Number theorist turned acoustician.
- **Leo Beranek** (1914-2016) — author of *Acoustics*, *Concert Halls and Opera Houses*. Designed many famous concert halls (Tanglewood, Avery Fisher Hall).

### Practitioners and Educators
- **Ethan Winer** — audio engineer, acoustic treatment manufacturer (RealTraps), educator. Known for myth-busting and measurement focus.
- **Dennis Foley** — founder of Acoustic Fields, educator. Prolific video creator on practical acoustics.
- **Glenn Kuras** — GIK Acoustics, educator. Practical treatment design and placement guidance.
- **Nyal Mellor** — founder of Acoustic Frontiers, consultant. Blog and YouTube channel on evidence-based acoustic design.
- **Keith Holland** — University of Southampton, room acoustics researcher. Work on low-frequency modal control.

## Unexpected Connections (wild cards)

### Music Technology and Production
- **Convolution Reverb** — digital reverb plugins use recorded impulse responses. The "vintage plate reverb" in your DAW is literally an IR of a 1960s EMT plate.
  - Related: Altiverb (commercial plugin), many free IR libraries

### Architecture and Heritage
- **Acoustic Archaeology** — measuring impulse responses of ancient theaters, lost spaces, and historical performance venues. Bridges acoustics and cultural history.
  - Related: OpenAIR library, papers on Epidaurus theater measurements

### Psychoacoustics and Perception
- **The Cocktail Party Effect** — how humans isolate speech in noisy rooms. Related to early reflections and intelligibility metrics (STI, C50).
  - Related: Research by Albert Bregman on auditory scene analysis

### Mathematics and Signal Processing
- **Fourier Analysis** — room acoustics is a giant Fourier problem. Time-domain reflections become frequency-domain peaks.
  - Related: FFT algorithms, Cooley-Tukey, connection to quantum mechanics (wave equation)

### Automotive Acoustics
- **Active Noise Cancellation in Cars** — uses microphones and out-of-phase speakers to cancel engine noise. Related to room mode cancellation (but easier because space is smaller).
  - Related: Research on global quiet zones, local active control

### Environmental Acoustics
- **Underwater Acoustics** — similar wave behavior in different medium. Ocean thermoclines act like waveguides (analogous to room modes in 2D).
  - Related: SONAR, whale communication, submarine detection

### Philosophy of Measurement
- **What is "Good Sound"?** — is there objective "good" or only preference? Toole's research suggests some universal preferences (flat on-axis, smooth off-axis), but culture and genre matter.
  - Related: Circle of confusion (monitoring standards), studio sound aesthetics
