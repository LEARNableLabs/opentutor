# Research Summary: Acoustic Engineering and Room Design

## Major Subtopics

1. **Wave Mechanics and Sound Propagation** — fundamentals of sound waves, frequency, wavelength, phase, interference patterns in enclosed spaces
2. **Room Modes and Standing Waves** — axial, tangential, and oblique modes; modal density; critical frequencies; eigen-frequencies
3. **Reverberation and Time-Domain Behavior** — RT60, decay curves, early reflections vs late reverberation, Sabine/Eyring equations
4. **Sound Absorption** — porous absorbers, resonant absorbers, membrane absorbers; absorption coefficients across frequency
5. **Diffusion and Scattering** — specular vs diffuse reflection, QRD and PRD diffusers, spatial distribution of sound energy
6. **Speaker-Room Interaction** — boundary loading, speaker placement, listening position optimization, symmetry considerations
7. **Measurement and Analysis** — impulse response, frequency response, waterfall plots, phase analysis, calibration techniques
8. **Design Principles** — room ratios, volume calculations, treatment placement strategies, bass trapping, LEDE/RFZ concepts
9. **Active and Digital Correction** — parametric EQ, convolution, DSP room correction, limits of electronic solutions

## Key Sources

### Academic Courses
- **MIT 2.067 (Acoustics and Sensing)** — covers wave equation, impedance, radiation, room acoustics fundamentals
- **Aalto University Acoustics Courses** — specialized room acoustics track with measurement labs
- **University of Salford MSc Acoustics** — industry-focused room design modules

### Textbooks and Standards
- **Master Handbook of Acoustics (6th ed., F. Alton Everest & Ken Pohlmann)** — comprehensive, accessible treatment; industry standard
- **Acoustics and Psychoacoustics (David Howard & Jamie Angus)** — bridges physical and perceptual aspects
- **Sound Reproduction (Floyd Toole, 3rd ed.)** — loudspeaker-room interaction, evidence-based approach
- **IEC 60268-16** — standard for objective rating of speech intelligibility
- **ISO 3382** — measurement of room acoustic parameters

### Software and Tools
- **REW (Room EQ Wizard)** — free, industry-standard measurement and analysis
- **EASE / EASE Focus** — professional room acoustic prediction (commercial)
- **Odeon** — geometric room acoustics simulator
- **COMSOL / Finite Element Methods** — advanced wave-based simulation

### Video and Online Resources
- **Acoustic Insider (YouTube)** — practical treatment walkthroughs, measurement tutorials
- **Acoustic Fields (YouTube)** — deep dives into absorber/diffuser design and placement
- **Ethan Winer (YouTube + AES papers)** — myth-busting, measurement-driven approach
- **Audio Engineering Society (AES) papers** — peer-reviewed research archive

### Communities and Organizations
- **Audio Engineering Society (AES)** — standards, papers, conferences
- **Acoustical Society of America (ASA)** — research journals, technical committees
- **Gearspace Acoustics subforum** — practitioner knowledge exchange
- **AVS Forum Home Theater** — DIY community with measurement emphasis

## Available Resources

- **Interactive simulators**: None widely accessible for free; EASE and Odeon are commercial but have educational licenses
- **Measurement tools**: REW is free and runs on all platforms; requires audio interface and calibrated microphone
- **Calculators**: Free online calculators for room modes, RT60 prediction, absorption area
- **Open datasets**: Limited; some published impulse response libraries for known spaces (e.g., OpenAIR)
