# Mechatronics — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks
- **Introduction to Mechatronics and Measurement Systems** by Alciatore & Histand (McGraw-Hill, 5th ed.) — industry-standard textbook with practical focus, Arduino examples, and strong coverage of sensors/actuators. Excellent for intermediate learners. Includes MATLAB/Simulink integration.
- **Mechatronics: Electronic Control Systems in Mechanical and Electrical Engineering** by William Bolton (Pearson, 7th ed.) — comprehensive, slightly more theoretical. Strong control systems chapters.
- **Mechatronics: Principles and Applications** by Godfrey Onwubolu (Butterworth-Heinemann) — good systems integration emphasis, includes case studies from automotive and manufacturing.
- **Embedded Systems: Introduction to Arm Cortex-M Microcontrollers** by Jonathan Valvano — for students ready to move beyond Arduino to professional embedded systems.

### University Courses (Open Access)
- **MIT OCW 2.004: Dynamics and Control II** — [https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-004-dynamics-and-control-ii-spring-2008/](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-004-dynamics-and-control-ii-spring-2008/) — control systems with mechanical focus, includes MATLAB assignments
- **MIT OCW 2.12: Introduction to Robotics** — [https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-12-introduction-to-robotics-fall-2005/](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-12-introduction-to-robotics-fall-2005/) — mechatronics foundations with robotics applications
- **Stanford ME218: Smart Product Design** — project-based mechatronics (syllabus and resources available via web search)
- **UC Berkeley EECS C106A: Introduction to Robotics** — [https://ucb-ee106.github.io/106a-fa20site/](https://ucb-ee106.github.io/106a-fa20site/) — includes sensor fusion, control, and kinematics

### Online Tutorials & References
- **All About Circuits** — [https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/) — comprehensive electronics tutorials (sensors, motor drivers, power electronics)
- **SparkFun Learn** — [https://learn.sparkfun.com/](https://learn.sparkfun.com/) — beginner-friendly guides for sensors, communication protocols, and microcontrollers
- **Arduino Official Tutorials** — [https://www.arduino.cc/en/Tutorial/BuiltInExamples](https://www.arduino.cc/en/Tutorial/BuiltInExamples) — built-in examples and project hub
- **How to Mechatronics** — [https://howtomechatronics.com/](https://howtomechatronics.com/) — excellent project-based tutorials (encoders, motor control, IMUs)

### Interactive Tools
- **TinkerCAD Circuits** — [https://www.tinkercad.com/circuits](https://www.tinkercad.com/circuits) — browser-based Arduino simulator with sensors/actuators. Great for prototyping before building hardware.
- **Falstad Circuit Simulator** — [https://www.falstad.com/circuit/](https://www.falstad.com/circuit/) — real-time circuit simulation, excellent for understanding motor drivers, filters, and power electronics
- **MATLAB/Simulink** — [https://www.mathworks.com/products/simulink.html](https://www.mathworks.com/products/simulink.html) — industry-standard control system modeling and simulation. Free student license available.
- **Python Control Systems Library** — [https://python-control.readthedocs.io/](https://python-control.readthedocs.io/) — open-source alternative to MATLAB for control design
- **Gazebo Simulator** — [http://gazebosim.org/](http://gazebosim.org/) — physics-based robot simulation (integrates with ROS)
- **Webots** — [https://cyberbotics.com/](https://cyberbotics.com/) — another excellent robot simulator with realistic sensor/actuator models
- **OnShape** — [https://www.onshape.com/](https://www.onshape.com/) — cloud-based CAD for mechanical design (free for students)
- **KiCad** — [https://www.kicad.org/](https://www.kicad.org/) — open-source PCB design tool for custom electronics

### Code & Repositories
- **Arduino Libraries** — [https://www.arduinolibraries.info/](https://www.arduinolibraries.info/) — searchable database of Arduino libraries for sensors, actuators, and protocols
- **PlatformIO** — [https://platformio.org/](https://platformio.org/) — professional embedded development environment (supports Arduino, STM32, ESP32, etc.)
- **ROS (Robot Operating System)** — [https://www.ros.org/](https://www.ros.org/) — software framework for building complex robotic systems
- **GitHub: TKJ Electronics BalancingRobot** — [https://github.com/TKJElectronics/BalancingRobot](https://github.com/TKJElectronics/BalancingRobot) — well-documented self-balancing robot (Arduino, IMU, PID)
- **GitHub: SimpleFOC** — [https://github.com/simplefoc/Arduino-FOC](https://github.com/simplefoc/Arduino-FOC) — field-oriented control library for brushless motors
- **Hackster.io Mechatronics Projects** — [https://www.hackster.io/mechatronics/projects](https://www.hackster.io/mechatronics/projects) — community-contributed project tutorials

### Datasheets & Application Notes (Teach Students to Read These!)
- **Texas Instruments Motor Driver Guides** — [https://www.ti.com/motor-drivers/overview.html](https://www.ti.com/motor-drivers/overview.html) — comprehensive application notes on H-bridges, stepper drivers, etc.
- **Analog Devices Sensor Signal Conditioning** — [https://www.analog.com/en/technical-articles.html](https://www.analog.com/en/technical-articles.html) — deep dives on filtering, amplification, noise reduction
- **STMicroelectronics MEMS Sensors** — [https://www.st.com/en/mems-and-sensors.html](https://www.st.com/en/mems-and-sensors.html) — datasheets for accelerometers, gyroscopes, IMUs (many used in phones and drones)

## Unexpected Connections

### Mechatronics in Biology
- **Biomechatronics** — [https://www.media.mit.edu/groups/biomechatronics/overview/](https://www.media.mit.edu/groups/biomechatronics/overview/) — MIT lab designing prosthetics and exoskeletons
- **Insect-inspired robotics** — how cockroaches stabilize on rough terrain informs legged robot design
- **Muscle as actuator** — biological systems use compliant, distributed actuation instead of rigid motors

### Mechatronics in Art
- **Kinetic sculpture** — artists like Theo Jansen (Strandbeest) use mechatronic principles for wind-powered walking sculptures
- **Interactive installations** — mechatronics enables art that responds to human presence (e.g., Random International's "Rain Room")

### Mechatronics in Music
- **Robotic instruments** — [Compressorhead](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3RBSkq-_St8) (robot band), [Animusic](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hyCIpKAIFyo) (animated mechatronic instruments)
- **Haptic music** — using force-feedback controllers to "feel" sound

### Mechatronics in Agriculture
- **Precision agriculture** — autonomous tractors, drone crop monitoring, robotic harvesters
- **Soft grippers for delicate crops** — picking strawberries without bruising

### Mechatronics in Space
- **Mars rovers** — extreme mechatronics (control with 20-minute communication delay, unstructured terrain, radiation-hardened electronics)
- **Satellite reaction wheels** — precision attitude control using motor flywheels

### Mechatronics in Medicine
- **Surgical robots** (da Vinci system) — teleoperated precision mechatronics
- **Continuous glucose monitors** — closed-loop insulin delivery (artificial pancreas)
- **Ventilators** — pressure/flow control systems that became critical during COVID-19
