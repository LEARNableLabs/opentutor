# Stained Glass — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks & Manuals

- **Randy Wardell & Judy Huffman, "The Complete Book of Stained Glass"** — Comprehensive coverage of both lead came and copper foil techniques, well-suited for intermediate learners. Excellent diagrams and step-by-step photography. Published by Dover, widely available.

- **Anita & Seymour Isenberg, "How to Work in Stained Glass"** (3rd Edition) — Classic reference text covering both traditional and modern methods. Strong on technical details and troubleshooting. Good for students who want deep technical understanding.

- **Michael Johnston, "Advanced Stained Glass Techniques"** — Covers 3D construction, hybrid techniques, and professional finishing. Best used as supplementary reading for lessons 16-22.

- **Vicki Payne, "The Ultimate Guide to Lead Came"** — Specialized resource for lead came mastery. Excellent for lessons 6-11 if students want extra depth.

### Online Courses & Platforms

- **Delphi Glass Resource Center** (https://www.delphiglass.com/resource-center) — Free tutorials, technique videos, and buying guides. Intermediate-friendly and professionally produced.

- **Craftsy/Bluprint Stained Glass Courses** (https://www.craftsy.com/browse/glass-art) — Structured video courses on both techniques. Subscription-based but high production quality.

- **Anything in Stained Glass** (https://www.anythinginstainedglass.com) — Educational site with technique library, pattern vault, and project walkthroughs.

## People to Follow

### Contemporary Artists

- **Judith Schaechter** — Contemporary narrative stained glass, pushing technical and artistic boundaries. Study her work for advanced composition and unconventional subject matter.

- **Tom Krepcio** — Architectural stained glass installations, strong on structural innovation and large-scale work.

- **Narcissus Quagliata** — German-based artist known for innovative kiln-formed and traditional stained glass hybrids.

### Educators & YouTube Creators

- **Randy Wardell** — Author and instructor, produces educational DVDs and books
- **Vicki Payne** — Instructor specializing in lead came technique
- **Jim Houser** — Advanced 3D copper foil construction techniques

### Historical Figures

- **Louis Comfort Tiffany (1848-1933)** — Inventor of copper foil technique, revolutionized American decorative glass
- **John La Farge** — Tiffany's contemporary and rival, pioneered opalescent glass use
- **Frank Lloyd Wright** — Architect who designed geometric stained glass as integral architecture

## Unexpected Connections

### Cross-Discipline Links

- **Electronics & Circuit Boards** — PCB design and stained glass pattern making share surprising similarities (trace routing, component spacing, structural integrity). Students with electronics background may find transfer learning opportunities.

- **Architecture & Structural Engineering** — Large stained glass windows face the same engineering challenges as curtain walls: wind load, thermal expansion, seismic considerations. Connect to architectural engineering for students interested in installations.

- **Chemistry & Materials Science** — Glass composition, flux chemistry, patina reactions, and lead metallurgy connect to chemistry curricula. Great for STEM students.

- **Art Conservation & Restoration** — Medieval window restoration combines art history, chemistry, structural engineering, and craft. Career pathway for students interested in heritage preservation.

- **Optics & Physics** — Light transmission, refraction, color mixing, and dichroic effects connect to physics. Use stained glass as a hands-on optics laboratory.

- **Computational Design & Generative Art** — Algorithmic pattern generation and parametric design for stained glass connect to creative coding and digital fabrication.

### Therapeutic & Mindfulness Applications

- **Art Therapy** — Stained glass is widely used in therapeutic settings (trauma recovery, PTSD treatment, mindfulness programs). Repetitive cutting and grinding can be meditative.

- **Maker Culture & Hackerspaces** — Many makerspaces have stained glass studios. Community learning and shared equipment models.

### Environmental & Sustainability

- **Glass Recycling & Upcycling** — Scrap glass reuse in mosaic, frit projects, or kiln forming. Sustainability-minded students can explore zero-waste approaches.

- **Architectural Daylighting** — Stained glass as passive solar design element (light control, heat management, privacy). Connect to green building and sustainable architecture.

## Safety Resources

- **Lead Safety Data Sheets** — OSHA guidelines for lead handling, ventilation, and cleanup
  - https://www.osha.gov/lead

- **Flux & Patina Safety** — Chemical safety information for common acids and bases used in finishing
  - Included in SGAA safety guidelines

- **Soldering Ventilation** — Proper fume extraction and workspace setup
  - Detailed in "The Complete Book of Stained Glass" appendix

## Historical Context Resources

- **"The Art of Stained Glass" by Virginia Chieffo Raguin** — Academic survey of stained glass from medieval to contemporary, excellent for understanding the medium's cultural significance.

- **"Tiffany's Glass Mosaics" by Alastair Duncan** — Deep dive into Tiffany Studios' methods and business, connects craft to industrial design history.

- **"The Art and Science of Glass" (Corning Museum publication)** — Chemistry, physics, and history of glass materials and techniques.
