# Sound Design for Film and Games — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks

- **The Sound Effects Bible** by Ric Viers
  - Comprehensive guide to recording, editing, and designing sound effects
  - Practical, intermediate-friendly
  - Covers both field recording and studio techniques
  - https://www.ricviers.com/

- **Designing Sound** by Andy Farnell
  - Procedural audio and synthesis approach using Pure Data
  - Advanced concepts, excellent for synthesis lessons
  - Free PD patches included
  - https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262014410/designing-sound/

- **The Foley Grail** by Vanessa Theme Ament
  - Definitive Foley art handbook
  - Performance techniques and studio setup
  - Intermediate to advanced
  - https://www.routledge.com/The-Foley-Grail-The-Art-of-Performing-Sound-for-Film-Games-and-Animation/Ament/p/book/9781138855939

- **Game Audio Implementation** by Richard Stevens and Dave Raybould
  - Practical Wwise and FMOD workflows
  - Bridges sound design and technical implementation
  - Essential for game audio module
  - https://www.routledge.com/Game-Audio-Implementation-A-Practical-Guide-Using-the-Unreal-Engine/Stevens-Raybould/p/book/9781138068919

- **Sound Design: The Expressive Power of Music, Voice and Sound Effects in Cinema** by David Sonnenschein
  - Aesthetic and narrative approach to film sound
  - Less technical, more conceptual
  - Great for understanding "why" behind decisions
  - https://www.mwp.com/products/sound-design

- **The Location Sound Bible** by Ric Viers
  - Field recording and location sound techniques
  - Equipment guides and practical workflows
  - Complements Sound Effects Bible
  - https://www.ricviers.com/

### Unexpected Connections (Rabbit Holes)

- **Musique concrète** — Pierre Schaeffer's experimental music pioneered sound design techniques
  - Listen: Pierre Schaeffer's "Études de Bruits" (1948)
  - Connection: Creative sound manipulation before it was "sound design"

- **Acoustic ecology** — R. Murray Schafer's concept of "soundscape"
  - Read: "The Soundscape: Our Sonic Environment and the Tuning of the World"
  - Connection: Worldbuilding through environmental audio

- **Psychoacoustic research** — Academic studies on perception
  - Read: "Psychoacoustics: Facts and Models" by Hugo Fastl and Eberhard Zwicker
  - Connection: Scientific basis for sound design decisions

- **Field Recording as Art** — Chris Watson, Bernie Krause
  - Listen: "El Tren Fantasma" (Chris Watson), "The Great Animal Orchestra" (Bernie Krause)
  - Connection: Recording as creative composition, not just capture

- **Synthesizer history** — Bob Moog, Don Buchla, and the birth of synthesis
  - Watch: "I Dream of Wires" documentary
  - Connection: Understanding synthesis philosophy helps sound design

- **Acoustics and architecture** — How spaces shape sound
  - Read: "Acoustic Absorbers and Diffusers" by Trevor Cox
  - Connection: Recording environment fundamentals

- **Disability and accessibility in game audio** — Audio-only games, subtitles, visual sound cues
  - Explore: "The Last of Us Part II" accessibility features
  - Connection: Inclusive design creates better experiences for everyone
