# Sound Design for Film and Games — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Acoustic Fundamentals
- Psychoacoustics (perception, masking, spatial hearing)
- Frequency, amplitude, phase relationships
- Room acoustics and reverberation

### 2. Recording Techniques
- Microphone types and polar patterns
- Field recording for location sound and SFX
- Recording dialogue and ADR
- Foley recording

### 3. Post-Production Workflow
- DAW fundamentals (Pro Tools, Reaper, Nuendo)
- Sound editing techniques
- Dialogue editing and cleanup
- Sound effects layering and design
- Mixing for film (stems, deliverables)

### 4. Game Audio Implementation
- Middleware (FMOD, Wwise)
- Interactive music systems
- Adaptive mixing and real-time DSP
- 3D audio and spatialization
- Event-driven sound design

### 5. Creative Sound Design
- Synthesis for sound design
- Creative processing and mangling
- Building signature sounds
- Worldbuilding through audio

### 6. Professional Practice
- Spotting sessions and collaboration
- File organization and metadata
- Delivery formats and standards
- Differences between linear and interactive media

## Key Educational Sources

### Textbooks and Books
- **The Sound Effects Bible** by Ric Viers — comprehensive SFX creation guide
- **Designing Sound** by Andy Farnell — procedural audio and synthesis approach
- **The Foley Grail** by Vanessa Theme Ament — Foley art techniques
- **Game Audio Implementation** by Richard Stevens — practical Wwise/FMOD workflows
- **Sound Design: The Expressive Power of Music, Voice and Sound Effects in Cinema** by David Sonnenschein
