# Intellectual Property — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

1. **Trade Secrets** — Protection of confidential business information, UTSA (Uniform Trade Secrets Act), DTSA (Defend Trade Secrets Act), common law protections, reasonable measures, misappropriation
2. **Patents** — Utility patents, design patents, patentability requirements (novelty, non-obviousness, utility), patent prosecution, claims drafting, infringement analysis, defenses
3. **Copyright** — Originality, fixation, scope of protection, fair use doctrine, DMCA, derivative works, work-for-hire
4. **Trademarks** (secondary but relevant) — Distinctiveness, likelihood of confusion, dilution
5. **IP Strategy** — Choosing appropriate protection, portfolio management, licensing, enforcement, international considerations

## Key Sources Found

### University Courses
- **Stanford Law School** — Introduction to Intellectual Property (LAW 4005), taught by Mark Lemley, covers trade secrets, patents, copyrights, and trademarks with no prerequisites
- **University of Pennsylvania Law School** — Coursera specialization on IP law with hands-on projects (trademark strategy, patent analysis, copyright issues)
- **Vermont Law School** — Spring 2025 syllabus available covering comprehensive IP law

### Textbooks
- **"Intellectual Property in the New Technological Age: 2019"** by Menell, Lemley, and Merges (UC Berkeley/Stanford) — widely used survey textbook
- **"Intellectual Property: Law & the Information Society"** by Boyle and Jenkins — open casebook with foundational cases and concepts
- **"Intellectual Property: The Law of Trademarks, Copyrights, Patents, and Trade Secrets"** by Bouchoux — comprehensive overview with practical tools
