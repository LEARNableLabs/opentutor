# Competitive Rock Climbing — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks and Training Manuals

- **Training for Climbing (3rd Edition)** by Eric J. Hörst — Comprehensive training science for climbers, covering energy systems, periodization, fingerboard protocols, mental training. Intermediate-friendly with practical programs. ISBN: 978-0762746927
  - Website: https://www.trainingforclimbing.com
  - Best for: systematic training approach, program templates, exercise library

- **The Rock Climber's Training Manual** by Michael L. Anderson and Mark L. Anderson — Advanced periodization approach based on block training. Dense but systematic. Best for intermediate climbers ready for structured programs.
  - Website: https://rockclimberstrainingmanual.com
  - Best for: periodization models, assessment protocols, long-term planning

- **9 out of 10 Climbers Make the Same Mistakes** by Dave MacLeod — Technique and mental training focus. Accessible, practical, focuses on movement efficiency and psychological skills.
  - Website: https://www.davemacleod.com
  - Best for: route reading, movement efficiency, fear management

- **Performance Rock Climbing** by Dale Goddard and Udo Neumann — Sport science foundation for climbing. More academic but comprehensive.
  - Best for: physiology background, energy systems, training principles

### Research and Academic Sources

- **Journal of Sports Sciences** — Climbing physiology and biomechanics research
  - Search: "rock climbing physiology", "climbing training adaptations"
  - Access: https://www.tandfonline.com/toc/rjsp20/current

- **International Journal of Sports Physiology and Performance** — Applied research on climbing performance
  - Search: "sport climbing", "bouldering performance"
  - Access: https://journals.humankinetics.com/view/journals/ijspp/ijspp-overview.xml

- **Frontiers in Psychology (Sport Psychology section)** — Mental training and flow state research
  - Search: "climbing psychology", "fear climbing", "flow state athletes"
  - Access: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/sections/movement-science-and-sport-psychology

- **University research groups:**
  - University of Chichester (UK) — climbing physiology lab
  - Bangor University (UK) — climbing performance research
  - University of Denver — competition climbing analysis

## Unexpected Connections (Rabbit Holes)

### Cross-Discipline Training Insights

- **Track and Field Periodization** — Soviet sports science (Verkhoshansky, Bompa) that climbing periodization models draw from
  - Book: *Periodization Training for Sports* by Tudor Bompa

- **Rock Climbing and Physics** — Lever arms, center of mass, friction
  - Video series: "Physics of Rock Climbing" (various YouTube creators)

- **Motor Learning Research** — How skill acquisition works, applies to route reading and technique development
  - Book: *Motor Learning and Performance* by Richard Schmidt and Tim Lee

### Climbing Culture and History

- **Competition Climbing Evolution** — How competition formats changed route reading and training
  - Documentary: *The Wall* (Netflix, 2018) — climbing Olympics journey
  - Book: *The Climbing Wall Association Handbook*

- **Free Solo Psychology** — Extreme risk management, flow state under pressure (distinct from competition but related mental skills)
  - Film: *Free Solo* (National Geographic, 2018)
  - Research: Search "flow state extreme sports"

### Training Science Deep Dives

- **Tendon Biology and Mechanotransduction** — How tissues adapt to loading
  - Research: Search "Keith Baar tendon adaptation"
  - Article: "Stress relaxation and targeted nutrition to treat patellar tendinopathy" (Baar lab)

- **Heart Rate Variability and Recovery** — HRV as readiness metric
  - App: HRV4Training — https://www.hrv4training.com
  - Research: Search "HRV training adaptation"

### Nutrition for Climbing

- **Performance Nutrition** — Fueling training and competition (not covered in main curriculum but important)
  - Book: *The Paleo Diet for Athletes* (general sports nutrition)
  - Lattice articles on climbing-specific nutrition

- **Weight Management for Climbers** — Complex topic, can be helpful or harmful
  - Resource: Lattice article "Body Composition for Climbers" (evidence-based, non-disordered approach)
