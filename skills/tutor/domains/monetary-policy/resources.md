# Monetary Policy and Central Banking — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks

- **Mishkin, "The Economics of Money, Banking, and Financial Markets"** (13th ed., 2021)
  - URL: https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/economics-of-money-banking-and-financial-markets-the/P200000005924
  - Coverage: Comprehensive undergraduate treatment, strong on institutions and policy
  - Why it's good: Intermediate level, excellent balance of theory and real-world applications, updated for post-2008 policy
  - Relevant chapters: 9-19 (Central Banking, Tools, Transmission, Frameworks)

- **Walsh, "Monetary Theory and Policy"** (4th ed., 2017)
  - URL: https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262035811/monetary-theory-and-policy/
  - Coverage: Graduate-level formal treatment, DSGE foundations
  - Why it's good: Authoritative technical reference for students ready for more formalism
  - Use selectively: Reference for Taylor Rule derivation, time-consistency theory, optimal policy

- **Cecchetti & Schoenholtz, "Money, Banking, and Financial Markets"** (6th ed., 2021)
  - URL: https://www.mheducation.com/highered/product/money-banking-financial-markets-cecchetti-schoenholtz/M9781260226782.html
  - Coverage: Modern intermediate text, strong on financial stability
  - Why it's good: More accessible than Mishkin, excellent on post-crisis unconventional policy

### Open Courses

- **MIT OpenCourseWare 14.02: Principles of Macroeconomics**
  - URL: https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/14-02-principles-of-macroeconomics-spring-2023/
  - Relevant modules: Lectures 9-12 on monetary policy, money, and banking
  - Video lectures + problem sets + exams

- **Khan Academy: Macroeconomics — Monetary Policy**
  - URL: https://www.khanacademy.org/economics-finance-domain/ap-macroeconomics/monetary-fiscal-policy-ap
  - Coverage: Accessible video series on money creation, Fed tools, transmission
  - Why it's good: Visual explanations, bite-sized videos, good for review or struggling students

- **Federal Reserve Online Courses**
  - URL: https://www.federalreserveeducation.org/resources/
  - Interactive modules on Fed structure, monetary policy tools, economic indicators
  - Especially useful: "The Fed Explained" video series

- **IMF Online Learning: Monetary Policy Analysis and Forecasting**
  - URL: https://www.imf.org/en/Capacity-Development/Training/ICDTC/Courses/FPP
  - Advanced technical training materials, some publicly available
  - Good for students wanting practitioner perspective

### Interactive Tools and Data

- **FRED (Federal Reserve Economic Data)**
  - URL: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/
  - Essential resource: time series data on policy rates, inflation, unemployment, balance sheets
  - Key series to bookmark:
    - Federal Funds Rate: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/FEDFUNDS
    - Fed Balance Sheet: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/WALCL
    - CPI Inflation: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/CPIAUCSL
    - PCE Inflation: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/PCEPI
    - Unemployment Rate: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/UNRATE
    - 10-Year Treasury: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/GS10

- **Atlanta Fed's Taylor Rule Utility**
  - URL: https://www.atlantafed.org/cqer/research/taylor-rule
  - Interactive calculator showing prescribed vs. actual policy rates
  - Excellent for lesson 18 (Taylor Rule)

- **New York Fed's Yield Curve**
  - URL: https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/yield-curve
  - Real-time yield curve visualization
  - Use to discuss transmission (how policy rate affects long rates) and inversion signals

- **Bank of England's Inflation Calculator**
  - URL: https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/monetary-policy/inflation/inflation-calculator
  - Shows cumulative inflation over time
  - Good for illustrating the importance of price stability

- **Trading Economics: Central Bank Policy Tracker**
  - URL: https://tradingeconomics.com/country-list/interest-rate
  - Compare policy rates across countries in real time

### Research and Working Papers

- **NBER Monetary Economics Working Papers**
  - URL: https://www.nber.org/programs-projects/projects-and-centers/monetary-economics
  - Cutting-edge research, many papers are accessible to intermediate students
  - Especially useful for current debates (NGDP targeting, NIRP, CBDCs)

- **BIS Working Papers and Quarterly Reviews**
  - URL: https://www.bis.org/list/publs/index.htm
  - International perspective on central banking
  - Quarterly Review has excellent non-technical summaries of policy issues

- **Federal Reserve Board Working Papers (FEDS)**
  - URL: https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/feds/
  - Staff research on transmission, policy effectiveness, etc.

- **ECB Working Papers**
  - URL: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/pub/research/working-papers/html/index.en.html
  - European perspective, strong on unconventional policy and NIRP

## Code and Simulations

- **FRB/US Model (Eviews files and documentation)**
  - URL: https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/us-models-about.htm
  - The Fed's main forecasting model
  - Advanced students can explore policy simulations

- **IRIS Toolbox for MATLAB**
  - URL: https://iristoolbox.codeplex.com/
  - Build and simulate DSGE models
  - Requires MATLAB proficiency

- **dynare (DSGE modeling software)**
  - URL: https://www.dynare.org/
  - Open-source platform for DSGE models
  - Community includes many central bank models

- **Python for Monetary Policy Analysis**
  - FRED API: https://github.com/mortada/fredapi
  - Pull FRED data directly into Python for analysis
  - Great for data-driven projects

## Unexpected Connections

- **Monetary Policy and Inequality**
  - Does QE worsen wealth inequality? Debate between Saez/Zucman vs. Bernanke
  - ECB research: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/pub/economic-bulletin/articles/2021/html/ecb.ebart202102_01~c2fbaa9de5.en.html

- **Central Banking and Climate Change**
  - Network for Greening the Financial System (NGFS): https://www.ngfs.net/en
  - Should central banks "tilt" asset purchases toward green bonds?

- **Cryptocurrency and Central Banks**
  - Does Bitcoin compete with fiat money? CBDCs as a response?
  - BIS Innovation Hub: https://www.bis.org/about/bisih.htm

- **Monetary Policy in Space Colonies**
  - Thought experiment: how would you design monetary policy for a Mars colony?
  - Connects to optimal currency area theory, Hayek's denationalization of money

- **Central Banking and Behavioral Economics**
  - Akerlof & Shiller, "Animal Spirits": https://press.princeton.edu/books/paperback/9780691142333/animal-spirits
  - How do central banks manage irrational exuberance and panic?

- **Political Economy of Central Banking**
  - Why did the U.S. resist a central bank until 1913? History of the Fed's creation
  - Ron Paul's "End the Fed" vs. Bernanke's defense: ideological extremes illuminate the debate
