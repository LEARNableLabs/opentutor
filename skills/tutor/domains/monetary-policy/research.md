# Monetary Policy and Central Banking — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Central Bank Fundamentals
- Role and mandate of central banks (price stability, employment, financial stability)
- Independence and accountability
- Central bank balance sheets and operations
- Historical evolution of modern central banking

### 2. Tools of Monetary Policy
- Interest rate policy (policy rates, corridor systems)
- Open market operations (OMOs)
- Reserve requirements
- Quantitative easing and unconventional tools
- Forward guidance and communication

### 3. Transmission Mechanisms
- Interest rate channel
- Credit channel
- Exchange rate channel
- Asset price channel
- Expectations channel
- Time lags and policy effectiveness

### 4. Policy Frameworks and Strategies
- Inflation targeting
- Dual mandate vs single mandate
- Price level targeting vs inflation targeting
- Nominal GDP targeting
- Taylor Rule and policy reaction functions

### 5. Challenges and Contemporary Issues
- Zero lower bound and negative interest rates
- Financial stability vs price stability tradeoffs
- Climate change and central banking
- Digital currencies (CBDCs)
- Global coordination and spillovers

## Teaching Approach for Intermediate Level

At intermediate level, students should:
- Understand the theoretical foundations (IS-LM, AS-AD, New Keynesian models)
- Analyze real-world policy decisions and their outcomes
- Work with data from FRED and other sources
- Read and critique actual central bank policy statements
- Compare different policy frameworks across countries

Balance formal models with real-world applications. Use recent policy episodes (2008 crisis, COVID-19, 2021-2023 inflation) as case studies.
