# Lockpicking — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

1. **Lock Mechanics & Engineering** — Pin tumbler locks, wafer locks, disc detainer locks, lever locks, combination locks, high-security mechanisms
2. **Picking Techniques** — Single pin picking (SPP), raking, bumping, impressioning, decoding, bypassing
3. **Tools & Materials** — Picks, tension wrenches, rakes, bump keys, shims, decoders, improvised tools
4. **Security Assessment** — Vulnerability analysis, attack vectors, security ratings, physical security integration
5. **Locksport Culture** — Competitions, community organizations (TOOOL), ethics, legal frameworks
6. **Advanced Topics** — High-security locks (Medeco, ASSA, BiLock), automotive locks, safes, electronic hybrids

## Key Educational Resources

### Foundational Texts

- **MIT Guide to Lock Picking** (Ted the Tool, 1991) — Classic free educational resource covering fundamentals, techniques, exercises, and legal considerations. Available at: https://www.lysator.liu.se/mit-guide/MITLockGuide.pdf and https://archive.org/details/MITLockGuide
- **Practical Lock Picking** (Deviant Ollam, 2nd ed.) — Industry-standard manual covering basic to advanced techniques, written by a renowned security educator. Available at: https://sciencedirect.com/book/9781597499897/practical-lock-picking
- **LSI Guide to Lockpicking** — Professional training resource
- **Easy Pickings** (Charles Edward Remington III, 1992) — Self-teaching manual

## Educational Approach Notes

- Strong emphasis on **hands-on practice** with real locks
- **Visual learning** crucial (cutaway locks, animations, video demonstrations)
- **Progressive difficulty** from simple padlocks to high-security cylinders
- **Ethical framework** essential — locksport code of conduct, legal awareness
- **Mechanical intuition** development through tactile feedback
- **Security mindset** cultivation — thinking like both attacker and defender
