# Olive Oil — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Cultivars and Geography
- Global olive varieties (500+ cultivars, focus on major commercial varieties)
- Mediterranean terroir and its impact on flavor
- Major producing regions: Spain, Italy, Greece, Tunisia, California
- Cultivar-specific characteristics (Arbequina, Koroneiki, Picual, Frantoio, etc.)
- Traditional vs. modern cultivation practices

### 2. Chemistry and Composition
- Fatty acid profile (oleic, linoleic, palmitic acids)
- Phenolic compounds and antioxidants (hydroxytyrosol, oleuropein, tyrosol)
- Volatile compounds and aroma chemistry
- Chlorophyll and color compounds
- Free fatty acids and quality indicators
- Oxidation mechanisms and shelf life

### 3. Sensory Analysis
- IOC (International Olive Council) panel testing methodology
- Organoleptic characteristics: fruitiness, bitterness, pungency
- Common defects: rancid, fusty, muddy sediment, winey-vinegary
- Quality grading: extra virgin, virgin, lampante
- Professional tasting protocol and vocabulary
- Sensory wheel and flavor descriptors

### 4. Processing and Quality
- Harvest timing and ripeness index
- Extraction methods: cold press, centrifugation, modern continuous systems
- Quality degradation factors
- Storage and packaging
- Adulteration detection

## Key Sources

### Academic and Technical
- UC Davis Olive Center — research publications on chemistry and quality
- International Olive Council (IOC) — standards and testing methods
- Journal of Agricultural and Food Chemistry — peer-reviewed olive oil research
- European Journal of Lipid Science and Technology
- *Olive Oil: Chemistry and Technology* by Boskou (textbook)

### Educational Resources
- UC Davis Olive Oil Chemistry course materials
- Olive Oil Times — industry news and educational content
- University of Jaen (Spain) — olive science programs
- COOC (California Olive Oil Council) — certification training materials
- Olive Wellness Institute — chemistry and health research

### Practical/Sensory Training
- IOC panel leader training manuals
- *The Olive Oil Source* — tasting guides and cultivar profiles
- Oleology courses and certification programs
- Professional tasting kits and sensory standards

## Research Gaps for This Curriculum
- Need more intermediate-level chemistry resources (most are either pop-science or highly technical)
- Limited interactive tools for sensory training (mostly requires physical kits)
- Few comprehensive resources that bridge all three areas (cultivars, chemistry, sensory)
