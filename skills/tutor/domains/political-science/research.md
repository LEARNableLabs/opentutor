# Political Science — Institutions and Voting Theory
## Research Summary

### Major Subtopics

1. **Political Institutions**
   - Legislatures: structure, function, comparative systems (unicameral vs. bicameral)
   - Executive systems: presidential vs. parliamentary models
   - Judicial independence and constitutional review
   - Federal vs. unitary systems
   - Electoral system design and consequences

2. **Social Choice and Voting Theory**
   - Preference aggregation fundamentals
   - Voting paradoxes (Condorcet, cyclical majorities)
   - Arrow's impossibility theorem
   - Gibbard-Satterthwaite theorem
   - Fairness criteria (Condorcet criterion, IIA, monotonicity)

3. **Electoral Systems**
   - Plurality and majority systems
   - Proportional representation (party list, STV)
   - Mixed systems (MMP, parallel voting)
   - Approval voting and score voting
   - Ranked-choice voting (instant runoff)

4. **Strategic Voting and Game Theory**
   - Strategic vs. sincere voting
   - Median voter theorem
   - Duverger's Law
   - Coalition formation
   - Agenda-setting power

5. **Comparative Political Institutions**
   - Veto players and policy stability
   - Institutional performance metrics
   - Democracy indices
   - Institutional reform and path dependence
