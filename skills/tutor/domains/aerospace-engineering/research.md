# Aerospace Engineering — Flight and Propulsion Research

## Major Subtopics

### Aerodynamics
- Subsonic and supersonic flow regimes
- Airfoil theory and wing design
- Boundary layers and viscous flows
- Lift and drag generation mechanisms
- Compressible flow effects

### Propulsion Systems
- Air-breathing engines (turbojets, turbofans, ramjets)
- Rocket propulsion (chemical, electric)
- Thrust generation and performance metrics
- Thermodynamic cycles (Brayton, Rankine)
- Propulsion-airframe integration

### Flight Mechanics
- Aircraft stability and control
- Performance analysis (range, endurance, climb)
- Aeroacoustics and noise reduction
- Hypersonic flight considerations

## Key Educational Resources

### University Courses

**MIT OpenCourseWare:**
- 16.100 Aerodynamics (Fall 2005) — subsonic potential flows, viscous flows, boundary layers, airfoil and wing theory, supersonic/hypersonic flows
- 16.50 Introduction to Propulsion Systems (Spring 2012) — air-breathing and rocket engines, functional requirements, engineering constraints
- 16.885J Aircraft Systems Engineering — comprehensive systems-level approach
- Video lectures available on YouTube for 2023 offerings

**Stanford Online:**
- AA283 Aircraft and Rocket Propulsion — design and performance of airbreathing and rocket engines
- Course textbook by Brian J. Cantwell available online

### Textbooks and Primary Sources
- *Aircraft and Rocket Propulsion* by Brian J. Cantwell (Stanford)
- MIT course catalogs showing prerequisite chains (Calculus II, Physics II, differential equations for propulsion courses)

### Video Content
- MIT 16.100 Aerodynamics 2023 lectures on YouTube
- MIT Private Pilot Ground School lectures on airplane aerodynamics
- MIT OCW video galleries for Aircraft Systems Engineering

### Interactive Tools and Simulators
- NASA Glenn Research Center interactive simulations — browser-based Java applets for investigating jet and turbine engine thrust production
- FlightStream (Altair/Siemens) — panel method CFD for rapid aerodynamic analysis with aeroacoustics toolbox
- AirShaper — aerodynamic efficiency analysis with propeller performance and propulsion-airframe interaction
- Stallion 3D — high-fidelity RANS CFD for aircraft, UAV, eVTOL, rockets
- AeroToy (GitHub) — real-time fluid-rigid-body simulator for experimentation

### Key Concepts Identified
- Thrust-to-weight and power-to-weight ratios
- Specific impulse (Isp) for propulsion performance
- Mach number effects and critical Mach
- Reynolds number and flow regime transitions
- Nozzle design and expansion ratios
- Combustion efficiency and stoichiometry

## Available Educational Pathways
The research shows a clear intermediate-level pathway: starting with fundamental aerodynamics (potential flow, boundary layers), progressing through airfoil/wing theory, then compressible flow effects, followed by propulsion fundamentals (thermodynamic cycles), and concluding with specific engine types and advanced integration topics. MIT and Stanford curricula consistently follow this arc, which allows building from fluid mechanics foundations to complex propulsion systems.
