# Urban Planning and City Science — Resources

## Primary Sources (Textbooks and Core References)

### Foundational Books

- **"The Death and Life of Great American Cities"** by Jane Jacobs (1961)
  - Classic critique of modernist planning; still relevant
  - Emphasizes observation, diversity, mixed-use, human scale
  - Good for: understanding criticisms of top-down planning
  - Level: Accessible to intermediate readers
  - [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Death-Life-Great-American-Cities/dp/067974195X)

- **"Cities for People"** by Jan Gehl (2010)
  - Human-centered urban design principles
  - Visual, practical, evidence-based
  - Good for: public space design, pedestrian experience
  - Level: Very accessible, lots of diagrams
  - [Island Press](https://islandpress.org/books/cities-people)

- **"The Triumph of the City"** by Edward Glaeser (2011)
  - Economic perspective on urbanization
  - Data-driven argument for density and cities
  - Good for: understanding agglomeration economics
  - Level: Intermediate, some economics background helpful
  - [Penguin Random House](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/202620/triumph-of-the-city-by-edward-glaeser/)

- **"Happy City"** by Charles Montgomery (2013)
  - Behavioral science and urban design
  - How city design affects well-being
  - Good for: connecting design to human outcomes
  - Level: Accessible journalism, well-researched
  - [FSG Books](https://us.macmillan.com/books/9780374534882/happycity)

### Technical and Academic

- **"Introduction to Urban Science"** edited by Luis M.A. Bettencourt and others (2021)
  - Computational and quantitative approaches
  - Covers network analysis, scaling laws, modeling
  - Good for: data-driven urbanism lessons (16-19)
  - Level: Advanced intermediate, some math
  - [MIT Press](https://mitpress.mit.edu/books/introduction-urban-science)

- **"Urban Design: Street and Square"** by Cliff Moughtin (2003)
  - Physical design principles and typologies
  - Good for: understanding built form
  - Level: Intermediate, design-focused
  - [Routledge](https://www.routledge.com/Urban-Design-Street-and-Square/Moughtin-Cuesta-Sarris-Signoretta/p/book/9780750657167)

- **"The Image of the City"** by Kevin Lynch (1960)
  - Foundational work on urban legibility and wayfinding
  - Concepts: paths, edges, districts, nodes, landmarks
  - Good for: cognitive mapping and urban form
  - Level: Accessible, influential
  - [MIT Press](https://mitpress.mit.edu/books/image-city)
