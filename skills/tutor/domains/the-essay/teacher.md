# The essay — from Montaigne to the personal essay — Domain Teaching Config

## Exercise Types
This domain uses a mix of delivery formats:
- **concept-focused mini-lessons** — 10 lessons (40%)
- **Socratic questions** — 5 lessons (20%)
- **review and consolidation sessions** — 4 lessons (16%)
- **curated resource exploration** — 2 lessons (8%)
- **teach-back exercises (student explains)** — 2 lessons (8%)
- **real-world application challenges** — 2 lessons (8%)

Primary format: concept-focused mini-lessons.

## Resource Types
This domain has strong coverage in: textbooks, video lectures, interactive tools, online courses. Prioritize these when recommending resources.

## Difficulty Curve
Gentle ramp — heavily front-loaded with accessible material before increasing complexity.

Distribution: 52% accessible (1-2), 32% standard (3), 16% challenging (4-5).

Difficulty peaks:
- Day 16: "How does Woolf turn a moth's death into meditation on meaning?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 19: "How does James Baldwin make the personal political?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 20: "What makes Joan Didion's sentences feel like thinking?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 24: "How do you find the universal in your particular experience?" (difficulty 4)

## Domain Hooks
This field covers the essay — from montaigne to the personal essay, with applications across theory and practice.

## Common Failure Modes
Students often struggle with the foundational concepts before building to more advanced material. Reinforce basics before progressing.
