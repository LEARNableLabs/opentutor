# CNC Machining — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks

- **"CNC Programming Handbook" by Peter Smid** (3rd Edition, 2008)
  - Industry bible for G-code and CNC fundamentals
  - Comprehensive coverage of programming techniques
  - Good for intermediate level — assumes some technical background
  - https://www.amazon.com/CNC-Programming-Handbook-Third-Comprehensive/dp/0831133473

- **"Fundamentals of Modern Manufacturing" by Mikell Groover** (7th Edition, 2020)
  - Covers all manufacturing processes including machining theory
  - Excellent material science foundation for machinability
  - Used in university manufacturing courses
  - https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Fundamentals+of+Modern+Manufacturing

- **"Metal Cutting Theory and Practice" by David A. Stephenson & John S. Agapiou** (3rd Edition, 2016)
  - Deep dive into cutting mechanics and tool wear
  - Research-level treatment of chip formation and forces
  - Best resource for understanding material science of machining
  - https://www.routledge.com/Metal-Cutting-Theory-and-Practice/Stephenson-Agapiou/p/book/9781574446593

- **"CAM Express" by Stephen F. Krar** (2009)
  - Practical CAM software training
  - Good introduction to toolpath strategies
  - Somewhat dated software-wise but principles remain
  - https://www.amazon.com/CAM-Express-Stephen-F-Krar/dp/0831134011

### Academic Courses (Free Online)

- **MIT 2.008 Design and Manufacturing II**

  - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-008-design-and-manufacturing-ii-spring-2004/

### Interactive Tools & Software

- **Autodesk Fusion 360** (Free for hobbyists/students)
  - Full CAD/CAM platform
  - Industry-standard for learning CAM
  - Excellent tutorials and community
  - https://www.autodesk.com/products/fusion-360/free-trial

- **CAMotics** (Free, open-source)
  - G-code simulator and visualizer
  - Verify toolpaths without a machine
  - Cross-platform
  - https://camotics.org/

- **G-Wizard Calculator** (Commercial, free trial)
  - Feeds and speeds calculator
  - Tool deflection analysis
  - Material database
  - https://www.gwizardfree.com/

- **HSMAdvisor** (Commercial)
  - Advanced cutting parameter optimization
  - Integrates with CAM software
  - https://www.hsmadvisor.com/

- **Machinist's Calculator** (Mobile apps)
  - Quick reference for shop math
  - Speeds/feeds, tapping, angles
  - Various apps available for iOS/Android
