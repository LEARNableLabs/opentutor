# Medical imaging — from X-rays to MRI — Domain Teaching Config

## Exercise Types
This domain uses a mix of delivery formats:
- **concept-focused mini-lessons** — 8 lessons (31%)
- **Socratic questions** — 5 lessons (19%)
- **real-world application challenges** — 5 lessons (19%)
- **review and consolidation sessions** — 3 lessons (12%)
- **teach-back exercises (student explains)** — 3 lessons (12%)
- **curated resource exploration** — 2 lessons (8%)

Primary format: concept-focused mini-lessons.

## Resource Types
This domain has strong coverage in: textbooks, video lectures, interactive tools, academic papers, code repositories, online courses. Prioritize these when recommending resources.

## Difficulty Curve
Balanced progression — steady difficulty increase with regular consolidation.

Distribution: 38% accessible (1-2), 38% standard (3), 23% challenging (4-5).

Difficulty peaks:
- Day 7: "How does CT turn X-rays into 3D slices?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 15: "Why do protons spin in a magnetic field?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 16: "What makes different tissues bright or dark in MRI?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 17: "How does MRI know where signals come from?" (difficulty 5)
- Day 19: "How do fast MRI techniques speed things up?" (difficulty 4)

## Domain Hooks
These are fascinating connections to drop in when students show interest or when a concept needs extra motivation.

## Common Failure Modes
Students often struggle with the foundational concepts before building to more advanced material. Reinforce basics before progressing.

## Vocabulary
Key terms for this domain: electromagnetic radiation, X-ray production, tissue attenuation, photoelectric effect, Compton scattering, attenuation coefficients, spatial resolution, focal spot size, geometric unsharpness, detector resolution, radiation dose, ALARA principle, effective dose, dose limits, scatter radiation (and 78 more).
