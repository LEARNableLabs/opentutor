# Algebraic Topology — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Homotopy Theory
- Paths and homotopy equivalence
- The fundamental group and higher homotopy groups
- Fibrations and cofibrations
- Homotopy groups of spheres
- The long exact sequence of a fibration

### 2. Homology Theory
- Simplicial homology (triangulations and simplicial complexes)
- Singular homology (functorial approach)
- Cellular homology (CW complexes)
- Homological algebra foundations (chain complexes, exact sequences)
- Mayer-Vietoris sequence and computational techniques

### 3. Cohomology Theory
- Singular cohomology
- Universal coefficient theorem
- Cup product and ring structure
- Cohomology of manifolds
- Poincaré duality

### 4. Applications
- Classification of surfaces
- Fixed point theorems (Brouwer, Lefschetz)
- Vector bundles and characteristic classes
- Computational topology and data analysis

## Key Sources

### Primary Textbooks
- **Allen Hatcher - Algebraic Topology** (Cornell)
  Free PDF available at https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/AT.pdf
  The standard modern reference, balancing geometric intuition with rigorous proofs
  
- **Robert M. Switzer - Algebraic Topology: Homotopy and Homology** (Springer)
  https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-61923-6
  Systematically covers generalized homology theories

- **Tammo tom Dieck - Algebraic Topology** (EMS Press)
  https://ems.press/books/etb/57
  Two-part structure: intro material then advanced topics (bordism, K-theory)

- **Alexander Kupers - Lectures on Algebraic Topology** (U Toronto)
  https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/people/kupers/wp-content/uploads/sites/50/algtop-2020.pdf
  Modern lecture notes with clear exposition

- **Clara Löh - Algebraic Topology** (U Regensburg)
  https://loeh.app.uni-regensburg.de/teaching/topologie1_ws1819/lecture_notes.pdf
  Comprehensive lecture notes from winter 2018/19

### Video Lectures
- **N.J. Wildberger - Algebraic Topology: A Beginner's Course** (UNSW)
  https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL41FDABC6AA085E78
  Calm, experienced lecturer with smooth introduction
  
- **MIT OpenCourseWare - Algebraic Topology I**
  https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-905-algebraic-topology-i-fall-2016/
  Covers singular homology, CW complexes, cohomology, Poincaré duality

- **Free Video Lectures - Algebraic Topology** (UNSW)
  https://freevideolectures.com/course/2721/algebraic-topology
  N.J. Wildberger's full course from 2010

- **Anthony Bosman - Algebraic Topology** (Andrews University)
  Uses Hatcher's textbook with excellent video production

- **Pierre Albin - Algebraic Topology** (University of Illinois)
  Great lecturer with good video quality, also uses Hatcher

### Computational & Applied Resources
- **Michael Robinson - Computational Algebraic Topology** (American University)
  Computational focus for applied work
  
- **Preparatory videos for Applied Algebraic Topology**
  http://homepage.math.uiowa.edu/~idarcy/COURSES/TDA/TDAprelectures.html
  Connections to topological data analysis

### Additional Course Materials
- **Tim Perutz - Algebraic Topology I** (UT Austin)
  https://web.ma.utexas.edu/users/perutz/PDF_files/AlgTop.pdf
  Lecture notes covering fundamental group and core homology

- **Robert Ghrist - Homology: An Applied Perspective**
  https://www2.math.upenn.edu/~ghrist/preprints/HAD.pdf
  Applications perspective (sensor networks, data analysis)

## Available Resources

### Textbooks
All major textbooks accessible (Hatcher free online, Springer/EMS via libraries)

### Video Content
Multiple full course sequences available on YouTube and OCW platforms, ranging from beginner-friendly (Wildberger) to rigorous graduate level (MIT)

### Interactive Tools
Limited interactive visualizations for algebraic topology (more available for point-set topology); computational tools exist for homology calculations but require programming background

### Community
Active Mathematics Stack Exchange community for questions, many researchers maintaining course pages with notes and problem sets
