# Research — Organic Chemistry: Reaction Mechanisms

## Major Subtopics

1. **Electron flow and arrow pushing** — curved arrow notation, nucleophiles, electrophiles, formal charges
2. **Substitution mechanisms** — SN1, SN2, factors affecting mechanism (nucleophile, substrate, leaving group, solvent)
3. **Elimination mechanisms** — E1, E2, Zaitsev's rule, stereochemistry
4. **Addition to alkenes** — electrophilic addition, Markovnikov's rule, carbocation stability
5. **Aromatic substitution** — electrophilic aromatic substitution (EAS), directing effects, mechanism patterns
6. **Carbonyl chemistry** — nucleophilic acyl substitution, nucleophilic addition, aldol reactions
7. **Oxidation and reduction** — mechanism perspective on redox, hydride transfer
8. **Radical mechanisms** — radical halogenation, stability, selectivity
9. **Rearrangements** — carbocation rearrangements, hydride/alkyl shifts, pinacol rearrangement

## Key Sources

### Textbooks
- **Clayden, Greeves, Warren — Organic Chemistry (2nd ed.)** — exceptional mechanistic focus, widely used for intermediate students
- **Klein — Organic Chemistry as a Second Language** — skill-building workbook, excellent for arrow pushing practice
- **Anslyn & Dougherty — Modern Physical Organic Chemistry** — advanced, for deeper mechanistic theory

### Online Courses
- **MIT OCW 5.12 Organic Chemistry I** — lecture videos, problem sets, exams
- **Khan Academy Organic Chemistry** — visual explanations of mechanisms, good for review
- **UC Irvine OpenChem** — full lecture series by Prof. James Nowick

### Interactive Tools
- **ChemDraw/MarvinSketch** — drawing mechanisms
- **MolView** — 3D visualization of intermediates
- **Master Organic Chemistry** — blog with mechanism walkthroughs and cheat sheets

### Video Resources
- **The Organic Chemistry Tutor (YouTube)** — clear step-by-step mechanism breakdowns
- **Professor Dave Explains (YouTube)** — conceptual overviews
- **Crash Course Organic Chemistry** — engaging introductions to mechanism types

### Practice Resources
- **ChemLibreTexts Organic Chemistry** — open textbook with practice problems
- **Organic Chemistry Portal** — reaction database, named reaction mechanisms
- **Chemistry Stack Exchange** — community Q&A for specific mechanism questions

## Available Resources

- **Full syllabi** — MIT, UC Berkeley, Stanford organic chemistry courses all available online
- **Problem sets** — ACS organic chemistry exams, textbook problem banks
- **Visualization** — 3D molecular viewers, reaction coordinate diagrams, molecular orbital tools
- **Community** — active forums, tutoring platforms, study groups on Discord/Reddit

## Target Student Level: Intermediate

Assumes students have completed:
- General chemistry (bonding, thermodynamics, kinetics)
- Basic organic chemistry (nomenclature, functional groups, stereochemistry)
- Some exposure to Lewis structures and resonance

Will prepare students for:
- Advanced synthesis planning
- Reaction prediction and problem-solving
- Graduate-level mechanistic analysis
