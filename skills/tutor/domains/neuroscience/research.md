# Research Summary — Neuroscience: From Neurons to Cognition

## Major Subtopics Identified

1. **Cellular & Molecular Neuroscience**
   - Neuron structure and cell biology
   - Ion channels and membrane potential
   - Action potential generation and propagation
   - Synaptic transmission and neurotransmitters

2. **Neural Circuits & Systems**
   - Neural network organization
   - Sensory systems (vision, hearing, olfaction, touch)
   - Motor systems and movement control
   - Autonomic nervous system

3. **Cognitive Neuroscience**
   - Learning and memory mechanisms
   - Attention and executive function
   - Language and communication
   - Emotion and motivation

4. **Computational Neuroscience**
   - Neural coding and information processing
   - Mathematical models (Hodgkin-Huxley, cable theory)
   - Network dynamics and plasticity

### University Courses
- **MIT OCW 9.01 Introduction to Neuroscience** — Comprehensive coverage from cellular to systems neuroscience
  - Textbook: Bear, Connors & Paradiso "Neuroscience: Exploring the Brain" (3rd ed)
  - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/9-01-introduction-to-neuroscience-fall-2007/

- **MIT OCW HST.131 Introduction to Neuroscience** — Medical perspective, molecular to cognitive

- **MIT OCW 9.29J Computational Neuroscience** — Mathematical foundations
  - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/9-29j-introduction-to-computational-neuroscience-spring-2004/

### Interactive Tools & Simulators
- **Neuronify** — Mobile/desktop app for building and exploring neural circuits (Android, iOS, Windows, Mac, Linux)
  - No programming required, plug-and-play neural network construction
  - https://www.eneuro.org/content/4/2/ENEURO.0022-17.2017

- **TensorFlow Playground** — Neural network visualization
  - https://playground.tensorflow.org/

- **NEST Desktop** — Web-based GUI for neuronal network modeling
  - https://ebrains.eu/data-tools-services/modelling-simulation/network-level-simulation

- **Neural Circuit Visualizer** — Full-scale circuit simulation (hippocampus focus)
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-44588-0

- **The Virtual Brain** — Personalized brain network models
  - https://ebrains.eu/data-tools-services/modelling-simulation

## Notes for Curriculum Design

- **Intermediate level requires**: Basic biology knowledge, some chemistry (especially for ion channels), comfort with graphs and quantitative thinking
- **Strengths of available resources**: Excellent interactive tools (Neuronify, NEST), comprehensive MIT OCW coverage, good video support from Khan Academy
- **Gaps to address**: Need to bridge cellular mechanisms to cognitive functions smoothly; computational aspects require careful scaffolding
- **Recommended progression**: Start with neuron biology → circuits → sensory/motor systems → higher cognition
