# Photochemistry — Resources

## Primary Textbooks (for lesson content)

### Comprehensive Graduate-Level
- **Modern Molecular Photochemistry of Organic Molecules** (Turro, Ramamurthy, Scaiano, 2010) — The definitive reference for organic photochemistry. Encyclopedic coverage of mechanisms, photophysics, and applications. Best for looking up specific reactions and detailed mechanisms.
- **Photochemistry of Organic Compounds: From Concepts to Practice** (Klán & Wirz, 2009) — Excellent balance of theory and practice with strong experimental focus. Good for understanding how photochemical reactions are actually performed in the lab.

### Intermediate-Level (Perfect for This Course)
- **Principles of Molecular Photochemistry: An Introduction** (Turro, Scaiano, Ramamurthy, 2009) — Accessible introduction with clear explanations of photophysics. Ideal primary text for this level.
- **Photochemistry** (Gilbert & Baggott, 1991) — Concise, well-organized primer. Out of print but available in libraries; excellent for core concepts.

### Inorganic and Coordination Chemistry Focus
- **Photochemistry and Photophysics of Coordination Compounds** (Balzani, Campagna, 2007) — Essential for understanding metal-based photocatalysts and photosensitizers. Good supplement for photoredox lessons.

### Physical Chemistry Context
- **Molecular Quantum Mechanics** (Atkins & Friedman) — Chapter on electronic spectroscopy provides quantum mechanical foundation
- **Physical Chemistry** (Atkins & de Paula) — Standard text; photochemistry sections provide broader context

## Unexpected Connections (for Wild Cards)

### Vision and Neuroscience
- **Retinal photoisomerization** in rhodopsin — fastest photochemical reaction known (~200 fs)
- Connection to conical intersections and ultrafast dynamics
- Resources: Stuart et al., "How fast is the rhodopsin isomerization?" (J. Phys. Chem. B)

### Atmospheric Chemistry and Climate
- **Ozone photochemistry** — Chapman cycle, ozone layer depletion
- **Photochemical smog formation** — NOx and VOC photochemistry
- Resources: NOAA atmospheric chemistry resources

### Bioluminescence and Chemiluminescence
- **Firefly luciferase** — bioluminescence mechanism
- **Glow sticks** — chemiluminescence applications
- Resources: "Bioluminescence: Chemical Principles and Methods" (Shimomura)

### Art Conservation
- **Photo-degradation of pigments** — why paintings fade
- **UV-induced fluorescence** for art authentication
- Resources: Getty Conservation Institute publications

### Quantum Information
- **Nitrogen-vacancy centers in diamond** — photophysics for quantum sensing
- **Quantum dots** — semiconductor photophysics meets nanotechnology
- Resources: Awschalom et al., "Quantum technologies with optically interfaced solid-state spins"

### Archaeology and Dating
- **Optically stimulated luminescence** (OSL) dating
- Photophysics applied to geochronology
- Resources: Aitken, "An Introduction to Optical Dating"

### Marine Biology
- **Coral fluorescence** — photoprotection mechanisms
- **Deep-sea bioluminescence** — chemiluminescence in extreme environments
- Resources: Gruber et al., "Coral fluorescence" (Science)
