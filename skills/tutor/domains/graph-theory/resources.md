# Graph Theory — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks
- **"Graph Theory and Additive Combinatorics: Exploring Structure and Randomness"** by Yufei Zhao (Cambridge University Press, 2023, ISBN: 9781009310949) — Modern treatment emphasizing extremal and probabilistic methods, perfect for intermediate learners who want rigor with motivation. Covers Szemerédi's regularity lemma, pseudorandom graphs, and graph limits.
- **Top 7 Graph Algorithm Books** — Curated guide to graph algorithms texts covering classical and modern approaches
  - https://memgraph.com/blog/top-7-graph-algorithm-books

## People to Follow

### Researchers and Educators
- **Yufei Zhao (MIT)** — Extremal combinatorics, graph regularity lemmas, additive combinatorics. Author of the standard modern textbook.
- **László Lovász** — Combinatorial optimization, graph limits, probabilistic methods. Turing Award winner.
- **Paul Erdős** — Foundational work in random graphs, Ramsey theory, extremal graph theory (historical, deceased 1996, but his problems still drive the field)
- **Fan Chung Graham** — Spectral graph theory, random graphs, internet mathematics
- **Robin Thomas** — Graph minors, structural graph theory, four color theorem alternate proof

### Practitioners and Applications
- **Network scientists** working on social networks, biological networks, recommendation systems
- **Algorithm designers** at Google, Facebook, Amazon working on graph databases and search

## Unexpected Cross-Discipline Connections

### Biology
- **Protein-protein interaction networks** — graph theory models cellular pathways
- **Phylogenetic trees** — evolutionary relationships as tree structures
- **Neural connectivity graphs** — brain networks and graph-theoretic measures of integration

### Chemistry
- **Molecular graphs** — atoms as vertices, bonds as edges; study molecular properties via graph invariants
- **Chemical reaction networks** — pathways represented as directed graphs

### Social Sciences
- **Social network analysis** — friendship graphs, influence propagation, community detection
- **Epidemiology** — disease spread on contact networks

### Computer Science
- **Compiler optimization** — register allocation as graph coloring
- **Database query optimization** — join operations as graph matching
- **Web search** — PageRank as eigenvector of the web graph
- **Recommendation systems** — collaborative filtering via bipartite matching

### Operations Research
- **Transportation networks** — shortest paths, max flow for routing
- **Supply chain optimization** — network flow for logistics
- **Project scheduling** — PERT charts as directed acyclic graphs

### Physics
- **Lattice models** — graphs embedded in space, percolation theory
- **Quantum computing** — graph states, entanglement graphs

### Art and Design
- **Graph drawing aesthetics** — visual clarity via planarity and embedding algorithms
- **Origami mathematics** — crease patterns as planar graphs, rigid foldability

## Wild Cards

- **The Seven Bridges of Königsberg** — Euler's 1736 paper that founded graph theory. Historical context makes the subject come alive.
- **Graph theory in music** — analyzing harmonic progressions as directed graphs, rhythm patterns as cycle structures
- **Game theory and graphs** — Nash equilibria on network games, evolutionary dynamics on graphs
- **Map coloring puzzles** — recreational mathematics leading to deep theorems (four color theorem)
- **Graph isomorphism in cryptography** — hard problems used for zero-knowledge proofs
- **Knot theory and graph theory** — connections via planar graphs and embeddings on surfaces
