# Research Summary — Cryptozoology as Pseudoscience

## Major Subtopics

1. **Demarcation Problem** — Philosophy of science, Popper's falsifiability, distinguishing science from pseudoscience
2. **Scientific Method Applied to Cryptozoology** — Hypothesis testing, burden of proof, extraordinary claims requiring extraordinary evidence
3. **Classic Cryptozoological Cases** — Bigfoot/Sasquatch, Loch Ness Monster, Mokele-mbembe, Yeti, Chupacabra, Patterson-Gimlin film
4. **Cognitive Biases & Psychology** — Confirmation bias, pareidolia, pattern recognition, motivated reasoning, appeal to mystery
5. **Evidence Evaluation** — Eyewitness testimony, photographic evidence, anecdotal vs. empirical evidence, hoaxes
6. **Historical Context** — Victorian-era "discoveries," colonial exploration narratives, coelacanth as false analogy
7. **Media & Cultural Impact** — Reality TV (Finding Bigfoot, MonsterQuest), documentaries, tourism, economic incentives
8. **Boundary Cases** — Real discoveries (okapi, mountain gorilla), extinction vs. survival, legitimate zoology vs. crypto
