# Plasma Physics — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Fundamentals & Plasma Parameters
- Plasma definition and occurrence in nature
- Debye shielding and plasma frequency
- Collisionality and coupling parameters
- Temperature and density regimes

### 2. Single Particle Motion
- Motion in uniform fields (E×B drift, gradient drift)
- Magnetic mirrors and adiabatic invariants
- Particle trapping and loss mechanisms

### 3. Kinetic Theory
- Velocity distribution functions
- Vlasov equation and collisionless plasmas
- Fokker-Planck equation for collisional systems
- Landau damping

### 4. Fluid Models
- Moment equations (continuity, momentum, energy)
- Magnetohydrodynamics (MHD)
- Two-fluid theory
- Closure problem

### 5. Waves & Instabilities
- Electromagnetic waves in plasmas
- Electrostatic waves (Langmuir, ion acoustic)
- MHD waves (Alfvén, magnetosonic)
- Plasma instabilities (streaming, gradient-driven)

### 6. Applications
- Magnetic confinement fusion (tokamaks, stellarators)
- Space plasmas (solar wind, magnetospheres)
- Industrial plasmas (etching, deposition)
- Astrophysical plasmas
