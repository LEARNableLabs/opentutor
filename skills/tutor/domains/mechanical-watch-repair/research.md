# Mechanical Watch Repair — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

1. **Watch Anatomy & Movement Types** — Understanding components, mechanical vs. automatic, movement calibers
2. **Tools & Workspace** — Essential tools, magnification, workspace organization, safety
3. **Disassembly Techniques** — Proper sequencing, documentation, component handling
4. **Cleaning & Lubrication** — Ultrasonic cleaning, watch oils, proper application techniques
5. **Movement Reassembly** — Reverse sequencing, alignment, verification
6. **Timing & Regulation** — Using timing machines, beat adjustment, rate adjustment
7. **Common Repairs** — Mainspring replacement, balance staff pivoting, jewel replacement
8. **Escapement Work** — Swiss lever escapement, pallet stones, escape wheel
9. **Balance & Hairspring** — Poising, truing, hairspring manipulation
10. **Complications** — Calendar mechanisms, chronographs (modular)
11. **Troubleshooting** — Diagnostic approaches, common failures, systematic testing

## Key Educational Sources

### Professional Organizations
- **AWCI (American Watchmakers-Clockmakers Institute)** — Offers structured courses including WATCH 190, 200, 230, 240, and 320 covering modern automatics, balance staffing, timing, escapements, and modular chronographs
- **NAWCC (National Association of Watch and Clock Collectors)** — Community forums with practical repair discussions

### Primary Textbooks
- **"Maintaining and Repairing Mechanical Watches" by Mark W. Wiles (2016)** — Modern comprehensive guide covering basics, how watches work, servicing/cleaning, common repairs, and different mechanism types. Written by practitioner with 20+ years experience
- **"Practical Watch Repairing" by Donald de Carle** — Classic reference, still in print, based on sound practical experience
- **Wisconsin Institute of Horology Home Study Course** — 23 lesson booklets covering filing, lathe work, pivoting, jeweling, and all aspects of watch repair
- **Chicago School of Watchmaking "Master Watchmaking" Course (1950s)** — Comprehensive with illustrations on mainsprings, jeweling, timing, balance wheels, hairsprings

### Online Courses & Video Resources
- **Watch Repair Tutorials (YouTube)** — Free lessons for beginners to advanced, first 20 videos cover basic service using ST36/ETA 6497 movement
- **watchFIX.com** — High-definition video courses on tools and modern repair techniques
- **LearnWatchmaking.com** — Online video courses including Watchmaking 101 and vintage chronograph courses
- **AWCI Distance Learning** — Live online classes via GoToMeeting, max 5 participants per session
- **Class Central** — Aggregates 100+ horology courses and expert-led YouTube tutorials

### Interactive Tools
- **Bartosz Ciechanowski's Mechanical Watch Article** (ciechanow.ski/mechanical-watch) — Interactive exploded view of ticking mechanical movement, can rotate and inspect every component
- **Fredrik F. Ellertsen's Exploded View** — Additional interactive visualization

### Community Resources
- **WatchUSeek Forums** — Practical repair discussions and service guides
- **Repairwat.ch** — Curated educational resource recommendations
