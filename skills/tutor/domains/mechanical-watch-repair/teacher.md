## Common Failure Modes
1. **"Watches are just small clocks with the same principles"**
   - **Why students think this**: Both keep time using mechanical oscillators
   - **Correction**: Watches face unique challenges — portability requires working in all positions (positional variance in timekeeping), compact size creates different lubrication challenges, and wrist motion adds complexity (automatic winding, shock protection). Use positional timing comparisons to demonstrate.

2. **"More lubrication is safer — it prevents wear"**
   - **Why students think this**: In automotive/industrial mechanics, generous lubrication is common
   - **Correction**: Watch oil is applied in *microscopic* quantities. Excess oil migrates via capillary action, contaminates the escapement, and gums up over time. Show examples of over-oiled movements and the resulting problems. One drop on a needle tip lubricates multiple pivot points.
