## Common Failure Modes
1. **"Primitive societies" are simple or frozen in time** — Students often treat non-industrial societies as evolutionary relics. Counter: Emphasize that all contemporary societies are equally evolved and complex; "simple technology" ≠ "simple social organization." Use examples like Australian Aboriginal kinship systems (incredibly complex) or Melanesian exchange networks.

2. **Kinship = biological family** — Students default to their own cultural model (usually bilateral/nuclear family). Counter: Start with radical examples (Nayar, where biological fathers have no social role; Trobriand, where fathers aren't seen as biological contributors) to break the biology assumption. Then build up the logic of unilineal descent.
