# Sociology of Technology — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

1. **Theoretical Frameworks**
   - Social Construction of Technology (SCOT)
   - Actor-Network Theory (ANT)
   - Technological determinism vs. social shaping
   - Sociotechnical systems theory
   - Large Technical Systems (LTS)

2. **Historical Studies**
   - Technology and industrialization
   - Gender and technology
   - Technology and power relations
   - Case studies: bicycles, telephones, bridges, nuclear power

3. **Contemporary Issues**
   - Platform capitalism and digital labor
   - Algorithmic governance and surveillance
   - Technology and inequality
   - Environmental technology and sustainability
   - AI ethics and automation

4. **Methods and Approaches**
   - Ethnography of technology use
   - Discourse analysis
   - Historical analysis
   - Comparative case studies

## Level Assessment

For **intermediate** learners:
- Assumes basic sociology concepts (socialization, institutions, power)
- Some exposure to social theory helpful but not required
- No technical background needed, but examples from computing/engineering domains
- Comfortable with abstract theoretical frameworks
- Can analyze case studies critically
