# Sociology of Technology — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

**Foundational Textbooks:**
- **Bijker, Hughes, Pinch (eds.) - *The Social Construction of Technological Systems*** (MIT Press, 1987) — The SCOT bible. Essential for lessons 3, 6, 11. [MIT Press](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262517607/the-social-construction-of-technological-systems/)
- **Wiebe Bijker - *Of Bicycles, Bakelites, and Bulbs*** (MIT Press, 1995) — The definitive SCOT case study book. Core for lesson 6. [MIT Press](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262522274/of-bicycles-bakelites-and-bulbs/)
- **Langdon Winner - *The Whale and the Reactor*** (University of Chicago Press, 1986) — "Do Artifacts Have Politics?" is in here. Lesson 2. [UChicago Press](https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/chicago/W/bo5961206.html)
- **Donald MacKenzie & Judy Wajcman (eds.) - *The Social Shaping of Technology*** (Open University Press, 2nd ed. 1999) — Comprehensive reader. Good overview. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Social-Shaping-Technology-Donald-MacKenzie/dp/0335199135)
- **Bruno Latour - *Reassembling the Social*** (Oxford, 2005) — ANT manifesto. Dense but essential for lessons 7-8. [Oxford UP](https://global.oup.com/academic/product/reassembling-the-social-9780199256044)

**Contemporary Works:**
- **Shoshana Zuboff - *The Age of Surveillance Capitalism*** (PublicAffairs, 2019) — Core for lesson 21. [Website](https://shoshannazuboff.com/book/about/)
- **Ruha Benjamin - *Race After Technology*** (Polity, 2019) — Essential for lesson 22. [Website](https://www.ruhabenjamin.com/race-after-technology)
- **Nick Srnicek - *Platform Capitalism*** (Polity, 2016) — Short, sharp analysis for lesson 23. [Wiley](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Platform+Capitalism-p-9781509504862)
- **Sasha Costanza-Chock - *Design Justice*** (MIT Press, 2020) — Participatory design framework for lesson 25. [MIT Press](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262043458/design-justice/)
- **Geoffrey Bowker & Susan Leigh Star - *Sorting Things Out*** (MIT Press, 1999) — Classification and standards. Lesson 17. [MIT Press](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262522953/sorting-things-out/)

**Classic Papers:**
- **Langdon Winner - "Do Artifacts Have Politics?"** (1980) — [PDF available](https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~beki/cs4001/Winner.pdf) | [JSTOR](https://www.jstor.org/stable/20024652)
- **Trevor Pinch & Wiebe Bijker - "The Social Construction of Facts and Artifacts"** (1984) — Foundational SCOT paper. [JSTOR](https://www.jstor.org/stable/285956)
- **Michel Callon - "Some Elements of a Sociology of Translation"** (1984) — Scallops and fishermen. ANT classic. [PDF](http://www.bruno-latour.fr/sites/default/files/P-67%20ACTOR-NETWORK.pdf)
- **Thomas Hughes - "The Evolution of Large Technical Systems"** (1987) — LTS framework. [JSTOR](https://www.jstor.org/stable/3105173)

## Unexpected Connections (for wild cards)

- **Architecture and STS** — Buildings as frozen politics. See: Rem Koolhaas, *Delirious New York*; Beatriz Colomina on media and architecture
- **Music Technology** — Synthesizers, sampling, DAWs. Who gets to make music? See: *Perfecting Sound Forever* (Greg Milner)
- **Food Technology** — From microwaves to molecular gastronomy. Gender and domestic tech. See: Ruth Schwartz Cowan, *More Work for Mother*
- **Sports Technology** — From sneakers to performance analytics. Bodies and enhancement. See: Prosthetics in sports, Oscar Pistorius case
- **Game Studies** — Virtual worlds as sociotechnical systems. See: T.L. Taylor, *Play Between Worlds*
- **Disability Studies and Technology** — Assistive tech, universal design. See: Sara Hendren, *What Can a Body Do?*
- **Forensics and Technology** — How evidence technologies construct truth. See: Sheila Jasanoff, *Science at the Bar*
- **Climate Technology** — Geoengineering, solar panels, carbon capture. Whose technology for whose planet? See: Sheila Jasanoff & Sang-Hyun Kim, *Dreamscapes of Modernity*
