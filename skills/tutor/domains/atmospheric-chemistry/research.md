# Atmospheric Chemistry and Air Quality — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Atmospheric Structure and Composition
- Vertical structure (troposphere, stratosphere, mesosphere)
- Major and trace gases
- Residence times and mixing ratios
- Global atmospheric budgets

### 2. Chemical Kinetics and Photochemistry
- Gas-phase reaction mechanisms
- Photodissociation and photostationary states
- Radical chemistry (OH, HO2, NO3)
- Temperature and pressure dependence

### 3. Tropospheric Chemistry
- Ozone formation and destruction
- NOx-VOC chemistry
- Urban vs. rural chemistry
- Oxidation capacity

### 4. Stratospheric Chemistry
- Chapman cycle
- Catalytic ozone destruction (ClOx, NOx, HOx)
- Polar stratospheric clouds
- Montreal Protocol chemistry

### 5. Aerosols and Particulate Matter
- Formation mechanisms (nucleation, condensation, coagulation)
- Size distributions and composition
- Health and climate impacts
- PM2.5 vs PM10

### 6. Emission Sources and Transport
- Anthropogenic vs. biogenic emissions
- Emission inventories
- Atmospheric transport and dispersion
- Long-range transport

### 7. Air Quality Monitoring and Modeling
- Measurement techniques
- Air Quality Index (AQI)
- Chemical transport models
- Satellite observations

### 8. Climate-Chemistry Interactions
- Greenhouse gases and radiative forcing
- Aerosol-cloud interactions
- Feedback mechanisms
- Chemistry-climate models

## Pedagogical Notes

This topic combines fundamental chemistry, environmental science, and public health. At intermediate level:
- Assume general chemistry background (kinetics, thermodynamics)
- Build up from molecular mechanisms to global scales
- Balance theory with real-world air quality applications
- Use visualizations heavily (satellite data, concentration maps, reaction mechanisms)
- Connect to daily experience (AQI, smog, climate news)
