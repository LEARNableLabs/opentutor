# Paleontology and the History of Life — Resources

## Primary Textbooks

- **Foote, M. & Miller, A.I. (2007). *Principles of Paleontology*** — Standard undergraduate text. Rigorous treatment of methods, taphonomy, phylogenetics. Best for intermediate-advanced students who want quantitative depth.
  
- **Cowen, R. (2013). *The History of Life*** — Narrative-driven survey from origin of life to present. Excellent for big-picture understanding. Accessible to intermediate students.

- **Prothero, D.R. (2013). *Bringing Fossils to Life*** — Comprehensive intro with superb illustrations. Covers both methods and major groups. Good balance of accessibility and rigor.

- **Benton, M.J. (2014). *Vertebrate Palaeontology*** — Authoritative text on vertebrate evolution. Advanced but indispensable for tetrapods, dinosaurs, mammals.

## Interactive Tools & Databases

- **OneZoom Tree of Life** — https://www.onezoom.org/
  - Interactive phylogeny of all life (~2.3 million species)
  - Zoom from bacteria to humans, see evolutionary relationships
  - Beautiful visualization for teaching tree-thinking

- **TimeTree** — http://www.timetree.org/
  - Database of divergence times (molecular clock + fossil calibration)
  - Search for any two organisms, get time since common ancestor

- **Paleobiology Database (PBDB)** — https://paleobiodb.org/
  - Massive database of fossil occurrences (>1.5 million records)
  - Navigator tool for exploring diversity through time
  - Useful for quantitative projects

- **Fossilworks** — http://www.fossilworks.org/
  - PBDB data in accessible interface
  - Search by taxonomy, location, time period

- **DeepTree Project** — http://www.phytools.org/Rbook/
  - Phylogenetic visualizations and tools
  - R-based for computational students

- **Earth Viewer** — https://www.hhmi.org/biointeractive/earthviewer
  - Interactive globe showing plate tectonics, climate, atmosphere through time
  - From Howard Hughes Medical Institute (HHMI)

## Code & Computational Resources

- **Paleobiology Database R package** — https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/paleobioDB/
  - Access PBDB data programmatically
  - For students with R skills who want to do diversity analyses

- **strap (phylogenetic trees in R)** — https://github.com/graemetlloyd/strap
  - Plot geological timescales with phylogenies
  - Publication-quality figures

- **FossilSim (R package)** — https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/FossilSim/
  - Simulate fossil data under various preservation models
  - Teaches taphonomic bias quantitatively

## Key Papers (Accessible to Intermediate Students)

- **Alvarez et al. (1980). "Extraterrestrial cause for the Cretaceous-Tertiary extinction."** *Science* 208(4448): 1095-1108.
  - Original K-Pg impact paper. Historical importance.

- **Daeschler, E.B., Shubin, N.H., & Jenkins, F.A. (2006). "A Devonian tetrapod-like fish and the evolution of the tetrapod body plan."** *Nature* 440: 757-763.
  - Tiktaalik discovery. Exemplary transitional fossil paper.

- **Xu, X. et al. (2014). "An integrative approach to understanding bird origins."** *Science* 346(6215): 1253293.
  - Feathered dinosaurs and bird origins. Synthesis paper.

- **Shubin, N., Tabin, C., & Carroll, S. (2009). "Deep homology and the origins of evolutionary novelty."** *Nature* 457: 818-823.
  - Evo-devo perspective on major transitions. Integrates fossils with molecular data.

## Popular Science Books (for Depth & Engagement)

- **Shubin, N. (2008). *Your Inner Fish*** — Engaging narrative on tetrapod evolution and human anatomy's fish origins.

- **Brusatte, S. (2018). *The Rise and Fall of the Dinosaurs*** — Modern synthesis by active researcher. Covers latest discoveries.

- **Gould, S.J. (1989). *Wonderful Life*** — Burgess Shale and the role of contingency. Classic argument about evolution's unpredictability.

- **Fortey, R. (1997). *Life: A Natural History of the First Four Billion Years*** — Readable deep-time narrative by trilobite expert.

- **Switek, B. (2010). *Written in Stone*** — Evolution's history through transitional fossils. Accessible, well-illustrated.

- **Quammen, D. (2018). *The Tangled Tree*** — Horizontal gene transfer, microbial evolution, tree of life revision. Modern perspective.

## Unexpected Connections (Cross-Discipline)

- **Paleontology ↔ Climate Science** — Past warm periods (PETM, Cretaceous) are natural experiments for greenhouse Earth. Foram isotopes, ice cores, tree rings = climate fossils.

- **Paleontology ↔ Astrobiology** — What counts as life? How do we recognize biosignatures? Stromatolites on Mars?

- **Paleontology ↔ Conservation** — Sixth mass extinction debate. What can recovery timescales from past events tell us? (Millions of years, not decades.)

- **Paleontology ↔ Genomics** — Ancient DNA from Neanderthals, Denisovans, mammoths. When do we have DNA (< 1 Ma) vs. only morphology?

- **Paleontology ↔ Philosophy of Science** — Historical sciences test hypotheses about unrepeatable events. How do we build converging evidence?
