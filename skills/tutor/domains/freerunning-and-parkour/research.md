# Research Summary — Freerunning and Parkour Biomechanics

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Biomechanical Foundations
- Force production and absorption
- Joint kinematics and kinetics
- Center of mass dynamics
- Ground reaction forces
- Momentum and impulse in parkour movements

### 2. Landing Mechanics
- Impact forces and shock absorption strategies
- Roll mechanics and energy dissipation
- Precision landing biomechanics
- Lower extremity joint loading
- Landing technique progression

### 3. Explosive Movements and Vaulting
- Plyometric mechanics in parkour
- Vault biomechanics (speed, kong, reverse, dash)
- Upper body force transmission
- Take-off mechanics and optimization
- Horizontal and vertical power generation

### 4. Movement Efficiency and Flow
- Economy of motion
- Transition mechanics between obstacles
- Spatial awareness and proprioception
- Movement adaptation and problem-solving
- Flow state and motor learning

### 5. Strength and Conditioning
- Parkour-specific strength requirements
- Functional mobility and flexibility
- Connective tissue adaptation
- Progressive overload in parkour training
- Energy systems for freerunning

### 6. Injury Prevention and Recovery
- Common parkour injuries and mechanisms
- Joint stress and overuse patterns
- Injury prevention strategies
- Movement screening and assessment
- Recovery protocols for high-impact training

### 7. Training Methodology
- Progressive difficulty scaling
- Skill acquisition models for complex movements
- Periodization for parkour athletes
- Deliberate practice in movement training
- Performance metrics and feedback

## Key Academic Sources

- Journal of Applied Biomechanics (parkour landing studies)
- Sports Biomechanics journal
- Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research
- Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise
- Human Movement Science journal

## Educational Resources Available

### Academic Programs
- Sports biomechanics courses from university kinesiology departments
- Movement science and motor learning curricula

### Practitioner Resources
- Parkour coaching certifications and training manuals
- Movement culture educational content (Ido Portal, MovNat)
- Biomechanics analysis from strength and conditioning organizations

### Video and Interactive Content
- Parkour tutorial channels with biomechanical breakdowns
- Slow-motion analysis videos
- Force plate and motion capture demonstrations
- Coaching breakdown series from established parkour organizations

## Notable Gaps

- Limited peer-reviewed research on freerunning-specific movements (flips, twists)
- Most biomechanics research focuses on landing rather than full movement sequences
- Minimal longitudinal data on training adaptations
- Need to bridge academic biomechanics with practitioner knowledge
