# Freerunning and Parkour — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Academic Journals
- **Journal of Applied Biomechanics** — https://journals.humankinetics.com/view/journals/jab/jab-overview.xml — peer-reviewed biomechanics research, including parkour landing studies and force analysis
- **Sports Biomechanics** — https://www.tandfonline.com/toc/rspb20/current — specialized biomechanics journal with movement analysis
- **Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research** — https://journals.lww.com/nsca-jscr/pages/default.aspx — training methodology and performance optimization research

### Research Databases
- **PubMed Central (PMC)** — https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/ — free full-text archive of biomedical and life sciences literature. Search "parkour biomechanics," "landing mechanics," "plyometric training"
- **Google Scholar** — https://scholar.google.com/ — broad academic search including biomechanics theses and conference papers

### Books
- **"Biomechanical Basis of Human Movement" by Joseph Hamill and Kathleen M. Knutzen** — comprehensive biomechanics textbook covering force, motion, and joint mechanics
- **"Parkour Strength Training" by Ryan Ford** — https://www.amazon.com/Parkour-Strength-Training-Overcome-Obstacles/dp/1937715302 — practical strength and conditioning for parkour athletes
- **"The Parkour & Freerunning Handbook" by Dan Edwardes** — https://www.amazon.com/Parkour-Freerunning-Handbook-Dan-Edwardes/dp/0061348007 — foundational techniques and training progressions

### Educational Websites
- **Physiopedia** — https://www.physio-pedia.com/ — free physiotherapy resource with excellent biomechanics articles (kinetic chain, tendon adaptation, landing mechanics)
- **Khan Academy Physics** — https://www.khanacademy.org/science/physics — forces, momentum, energy foundations
- **Parkour Visions** — https://parkourvisions.org/ — Seattle-based organization with training resources, research, and coaching certification

## Unexpected Connections

### Cross-Disciplinary Inspiration
- **Biomimicry in parkour** — Study animal locomotion (cats, primates, kangaroos) for movement insights
- **Dance biomechanics** — Contact improvisation and contemporary dance share force management principles
- **Martial arts** — Falling techniques, ukemi, and breakfalling from judo/aikido inform roll mechanics
- **Rock climbing** — Route reading, movement economy, and grip strength training transfer directly
- **Gymnastics** — Tumbling biomechanics and spatial awareness training methods

### Related Fields
- **Occupational biomechanics** — Manual handling and ergonomics research on safe lifting and impact absorption
- **Automotive crash testing** — Impact biomechanics and injury mechanisms research
- **Prosthetics and orthotics** — Spring-mass locomotion models inform understanding of elastic energy storage
- **Space physiology** — Microgravity adaptation research reveals fundamental movement principles

### Pop Culture and Media
- **"The Science of Parkour" podcast** — interviews with athletes and researchers
- **Parkour documentaries** — "Jump London," "Jump Britain," "Parkour: The Nature of Challenge"
- **Video games** — Mirror's Edge, Assassin's Creed (simplified but engaging movement models)

## Equipment and Tools

### Video Analysis
- **Smartphone slow-motion camera** — 120+ fps for technique review
- **Tripod or mount** — stable footage for accurate analysis
- **Kinovea or Hudl Technique** — free/affordable video analysis apps

### Training Equipment
- **Force plates** — if accessible through university/lab for ground reaction force measurement
- **Contact mats** — measure contact time and reactive strength index
- **Tendo unit or GymAware** — velocity-based training for power development measurement

### Wearables
- **Accelerometers** (smartphone apps or dedicated devices) — measure impact forces during training
- **Heart rate monitors** — track cardiovascular demand of training sessions
- **Jump mats** — measure vertical jump height for power assessment
