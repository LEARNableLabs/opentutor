# Tilings and Tessellations — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks

- **Grünbaum, B. & Shephard, G. C. (1987). *Tilings and Patterns*** — The comprehensive reference. Graduate level but accessible chapters. Covers everything from Archimedean tilings to aperiodic systems.
  - ISBN: 978-0716711933

- **Senechal, M. (1995). *Quasicrystals and Geometry*** — Connects mathematical tilings to physical quasicrystals. Excellent for intermediate students interested in applications.
  - ISBN: 978-0521575416

- **Baake, M. & Grimm, U. (2013). *Aperiodic Order, Vol 1: A Mathematical Invitation*** — Modern treatment with rigorous foundations. Strong on substitution systems and spectral theory.
  - https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/aperiodic-order/
  - ISBN: 978-0521869928

### Online Courses

- **MIT OpenCourseWare: 18.735 Double Affine Hecke Algebras** (relevant modules on symmetry)
  - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/mathematics/

- **Stanford Engineering Everywhere: Geometry lectures**
  - https://see.stanford.edu/

- **Tilings Encyclopedia** (U. Bielefeld)
  - https://tilings.math.uni-bielefeld.de/
  - Interactive database of substitution tilings with visualization tools

### Papers (foundational)

- **Penrose, R. (1974). "The role of aesthetics in pure and applied mathematical research." *Bull. Inst. Math. Appl.* 10:266-271**
  - Original Penrose tiling paper

- **Berger, R. (1966). "The undecidability of the domino problem." *Memoirs AMS* 66**
  - Proof that domino problem is undecidable

- **Shechtman, D. et al. (1984). "Metallic Phase with Long-Range Orientational Order and No Translational Symmetry." *Phys. Rev. Lett.* 53:1951**
  - Discovery of quasicrystals (Nobel Prize 2011)
  - https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.53.1951

- **Smith, D. et al. (2023). "An aperiodic monotile" *arXiv:2303.10798***
  - The hat monotile discovery
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.10798

## Unexpected Connections

### Cross-disciplinary

- **Islamic geometric art** — Girih tiles at Darb-i Imam (1453) show near-Penrose patterns
  - https://www.islamicart.com/main/geometry/tilings.html
  - Paper: Lu & Steinhardt (2007), *Science* 315:1106

- **Music and tilings** — Rhythmic patterns as 1D tilings
  - Connection to Euclidean rhythms and African drumming

- **Number theory** — Sturmian sequences and continued fractions
  - Fibonacci word as 1D aperiodic tiling

- **Dynamical systems** — Tiling spaces as topological dynamical systems
  - Ergodic theory and pattern statistics

- **Computer graphics** — Procedural texture generation with Wang tiles
  - Video game applications

### Physics connections

- **Soft matter physics** — Self-assembly of quasicrystalline phases
- **Photonic quasicrystals** — Optical materials with forbidden bandgaps
- **Mathematical biology** — Phyllotaxis and Fibonacci spirals in plants
