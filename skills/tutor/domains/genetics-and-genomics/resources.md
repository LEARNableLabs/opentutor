# Genetics and Genomics — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks
- **Genetics: Analysis of Genes and Genomes (9th ed.)** by Daniel Hartl and Elizabeth Jones — comprehensive intermediate genetics text with excellent problem sets. Covers classical and molecular genetics thoroughly.
- **Introduction to Genomics (3rd ed.)** by Arthur Lesk — accessible introduction to genomics, sequencing technologies, and bioinformatics. Good for the genomics modules.
- **Molecular Biology of the Cell (7th ed.)** by Alberts et al. — chapters on genetics, gene expression, and genome organization provide molecular detail. Advanced but authoritative.
- **Principles of Population Genetics (4th ed.)** by Hartl and Clark — for the population genetics module. More mathematical but conceptually clear.

### Online Courses
- **MIT OpenCourseWare 7.03 (Genetics)** — full course materials including lectures, problem sets, exams. Excellent for classical and molecular genetics.
  https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-03-genetics-fall-2004/
- **MIT OCW 7.91J (Foundations of Computational and Systems Biology)** — covers genomics, sequencing, and bioinformatics. More computational.
  https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-91j-foundations-of-computational-and-systems-biology-spring-2014/
- **Coursera: Genomic Data Science Specialization** (Johns Hopkins) — hands-on bioinformatics and data analysis
  https://www.coursera.org/specializations/genomic-data-science
- **edX: Introduction to Genomics** (UC San Diego) — solid survey of genomics concepts and tools
  https://www.edx.org/

### Institutional Resources
- **Nature Scitable: Genetics** — peer-reviewed educational articles on all major genetics topics. Free, high quality.
  https://www.nature.com/scitable/topic/genetics-5/
- **NCBI Education** — tutorials on databases, BLAST, genome browsers
  https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/learn/
- **Genome.gov (NHGRI)** — fact sheets, glossary, educational resources from the National Human Genome Research Institute
  https://www.genome.gov/

  https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/

  https://genome.ucsc.edu/
- **ClinVar** — database of genetic variants and their clinical significance
  https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/clinvar/

- **The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA)** — comprehensive cancer genomics data
  https://www.cancer.gov/tcga

  https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/snp/

## Unexpected Connections

### Cross-Disciplinary Links
- **Genetics and linguistics** — language evolution parallels genetic evolution
- **Forensic genetics** — DNA fingerprinting, genealogy databases solving cold cases
- **Art forgery detection** — using DNA from microbes in paint
- **Microbiome and health** — the "second genome" we carry
- **Ancient DNA archaeology** — rewriting human migration history
- **Conservation genetics** — genetic rescue of endangered species
- **Astrobiology** — could life use a different genetic code?
- **Quantum biology** — quantum effects in DNA mutation?

### Societal Connections
- **Direct-to-consumer genetic testing** — 23andMe, ancestry.com and privacy concerns
- **Gene drives** — potential to eradicate diseases (malaria) or invasive species
- **Genetic discrimination** — GINA law and employment/insurance issues
- **Biobanks and consent** — Henrietta Lacks, UK Biobank, ethical frameworks
- **Agricultural genomics** — feeding the world vs. GMO debates
- **Extinction revival** — woolly mammoth de-extinction projects

- **Epigenetic inheritance** — evidence for trans-generational effects
