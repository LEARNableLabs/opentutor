# Quantum Mechanics — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

1. **Quantum Foundations** — Wave-particle duality, postulates, measurement problem, superposition
2. **Mathematical Formalism** — Hilbert spaces, operators, eigenvalues, commutators, uncertainty principle
3. **One-Dimensional Systems** — Infinite/finite wells, harmonic oscillator, barriers, tunneling
4. **Three-Dimensional Systems** — Spherical coordinates, angular momentum, hydrogen atom, orbital structure
5. **Spin & Statistics** — Intrinsic angular momentum, Pauli matrices, identical particles, fermions/bosons
6. **Approximation Methods** — Time-independent/time-dependent perturbation theory, variational method, WKB
7. **Applications** — Selection rules, atomic structure, fine/hyperfine structure, scattering theory

## Key Educational Resources

### University Courses & Lecture Notes

- **MIT OpenCourseWare 8.04 (Quantum Physics I)** — https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-04-quantum-physics-i-spring-2016/
  - Comprehensive intro covering wave mechanics, Schrödinger equation, 1D & 3D systems
  - Video lectures by Allan Adams with problem sets and exams
  
- **MIT OpenCourseWare 8.05 (Quantum Physics II)** — https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-05-quantum-physics-ii-fall-2013/
  - Advanced topics: perturbation theory, scattering, identical particles
  - Taught by Barton Zwiebach with full video lectures

- **Stanford Physics 430 Lecture Notes (J. Greensite)** — https://stanford.edu/~oas/SI/QM/papers/QMGreensite.pdf
  - Comprehensive PDF notes covering foundations to advanced topics

### Textbooks

- **David J. Griffiths, "Introduction to Quantum Mechanics" (2nd ed., Pearson Prentice Hall, 2004)**
  - The gold standard for intermediate QM, clear exposition with excellent problems
  - YouTube lecture series available covering Griffiths content
  
- **Ramamurti Shankar, "Principles of Quantum Mechanics" (2nd ed., Plenum Press, 1994)**
  - More mathematical rigor, excellent for students with strong linear algebra background
  - Companion Yale OpenCourses video lectures on YouTube

### Video Lectures

- **MIT OCW 8.04 Video Lectures** — https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-04-quantum-physics-i-spring-2013/video_galleries/lecture-videos/
  - Full semester of lectures by Allan Adams, highly praised teaching
  
- **Griffiths QM YouTube Series** — https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLKxxVVdhQNB6CRn3f12Ro64hOL5BQV39d
  - Systematic coverage following the Griffiths textbook structure

### Interactive Tools & Simulators

- **PhET Quantum Simulations** (University of Colorado Boulder)
  - **Quantum Measurement** — https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulations/quantum-measurement
    - 2-level systems, measurement effects on quantum states
  - **Quantum Wave Interference** — https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulations/quantum-wave-interference
    - Double-slit experiment, wave-particle duality visualization
  - **Quantum Tunneling** — https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulations/quantum-tunneling
    - Wave packet behavior through barriers
  
- **QuVis (Quantum Visualization Project)** — https://www.physport.org/methods/QuVis
  - Collection of research-based interactive simulations for QM concepts
  - Free download with problem sets, advanced high school to undergraduate level

## Key Researchers & Practitioners

- **Allan Adams** (MIT) — Exceptional pedagogy, creator of popular MIT 8.04 lectures
- **Barton Zwiebach** (MIT) — String theory, advanced quantum topics
- **Leonard Susskind** (Stanford) — Theoretical Minimum series, accessible explanations
- **J.J. Sakurai** (deceased, but textbook remains influential) — Advanced QM standard reference

## Cross-Disciplinary Connections

- **Quantum Computing** — Qubit formalism, gate operations, quantum algorithms
- **Quantum Chemistry** — Molecular orbitals, computational methods
- **Condensed Matter Physics** — Band structure, superconductivity, quantum Hall effect
- **Quantum Information Theory** — Entanglement, Bell inequalities, quantum cryptography
- **Mathematical Physics** — Functional analysis, group theory, representation theory
