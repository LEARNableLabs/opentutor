# Lie Groups and Lie Algebras — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Foundation: Matrix Groups and Smooth Manifolds
- Classical matrix groups (GL, SL, O, SO, U, SU, Sp)
- Topological and smooth structure of Lie groups
- Group actions, homogeneous spaces, cosets

### 2. Lie Algebras and the Exponential Map
- Tangent space at the identity, Lie bracket
- Exponential map: algebra → group
- Baker-Campbell-Hausdorff formula
- Relationship between Lie group homomorphisms and Lie algebra homomorphisms

### 3. Representation Theory
- Linear representations of groups and algebras
- Irreducible representations, Schur's lemma
- Complete reducibility for compact/semisimple groups
- Weight spaces, highest weight representations
- Characters and character formulas

### 4. Structure Theory
- Solvable and nilpotent Lie algebras
- Engel's theorem, Lie's theorem
- Radical and semisimple decomposition
- Killing form and Cartan's criterion

### 5. Classification Theory
- Root systems, Weyl groups
- Cartan subalgebras, root space decomposition
- Dynkin diagrams
- Classification of simple complex Lie algebras (A_n, B_n, C_n, D_n, E_6, E_7, E_8, F_4, G_2)

### 6. Applications
- Quantum mechanics (angular momentum, su(2))
- Gauge theory in particle physics (su(3), Standard Model)
- Differential geometry (symmetries of manifolds)
- Robotics (SO(3), SE(3))

## Target Audience Notes
At intermediate level, students should have:
- Linear algebra (eigenvalues, inner products, matrix groups)
- Multivariable calculus (partial derivatives, tangent spaces)
- Basic abstract algebra (groups, rings, vector spaces)
- Some exposure to topology (continuity, compactness) helpful but not essential

The curriculum should balance:
- Concrete matrix computations (accessible entry point)
- Geometric intuition (manifolds, symmetries)
- Algebraic structure (theorems of Engel, Lie, Cartan)
- Classification theory (root systems, Dynkin diagrams)
