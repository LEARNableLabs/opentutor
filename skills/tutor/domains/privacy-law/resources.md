# Privacy Law and Data Protection — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Legal Texts
- **GDPR Official Text** (https://gdpr-info.eu/) — article-by-article with recitals, searchable, well-formatted. Essential reference.
- **CCPA/CPRA Full Text** (https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa) — California Attorney General's official site with regulations and guidance
- **US State Privacy Law Tracker** (https://iapp.org/resources/article/us-state-privacy-legislation-tracker/) — comprehensive tracker of all US state privacy bills and laws
- **NIST Privacy Framework** (https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework) — risk-based approach to privacy; good for privacy engineering

### Regulatory Guidance
- **European Data Protection Board (EDPB)** (https://edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/general-guidance/guidelines-recommendations-best-practices_en) — authoritative GDPR guidance, binding opinions, FAQ
- **ICO (UK) Guidance** (https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/guide-to-data-protection/) — incredibly detailed, practical guidance on GDPR and UK DPA; often clearer than EDPB
- **CNIL (France) Resources** (https://www.cnil.fr/en/home) — French DPA with good English resources, GDPR tools, and enforcement decisions
- **FTC Privacy & Security Guidance** (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security) — business guidance, enforcement actions, Section 5 framework
- **FTC COPPA Rule** (https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule-six-step-compliance-plan-your-business) — detailed COPPA compliance guide

### Case Law and Enforcement
- **CJEU Case Law** (https://curia.europa.eu/en/content/juris/index.htm) — Court of Justice of the European Union decisions (search "GDPR" or "data protection")
- **EUR-Lex** (https://eur-lex.europa.eu/) — official EU legal database
- **EDPB Case Law Database** (https://edpb.europa.eu/legal-framework/case-law_en) — curated privacy case law
- **FTC Enforcement Actions** (https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases) — search for privacy/data security cases; includes consent decrees

## Books

### Comprehensive Textbooks (Intermediate)
- **Daniel Solove & Paul Schwartz, *Privacy Law Fundamentals*** — leading US privacy law textbook, updated regularly, covers both US and international
- **Paul Voigt & Axel von dem Bussche, *The EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR): A Practical Guide*** — GDPR compliance handbook
- **Woodrow Hartzog, *Privacy's Blueprint: The Battle to Control the Design of New Technologies*** — privacy by design from a legal scholar; excellent on tech-law intersection

### Accessible Introductions
- **Daniel Solove, *Understanding Privacy*** — short, readable overview of privacy as a concept
- **Julia Angwin, *Dragnet Nation*** — narrative on surveillance and data collection; engaging for students

### Advanced Academic
- **Helen Nissenbaum, *Privacy in Context: Technology, Policy, and the Integrity of Social Life*** — theoretical framework for privacy; influential
- **Shoshana Zuboff, *The Age of Surveillance Capitalism*** — critical analysis of data-driven business models
- **Omer Tene & Jules Polonetsky (eds), *The Oxford Handbook of Cyberspace and Data Law*** — comprehensive academic overview

## Tools & Interactive Resources

## Using These Resources in Teaching

**For lesson preparation**: Start with GDPR/CCPA official texts, then layer in regulatory guidance (EDPB, ICO, FTC). Use enforcement actions as case studies.

**For student exploration**: Point students to IAPP resources, ICO guidance (very readable), and tools like Blacklight or ToS;DR to analyze real websites.

**For technical depth**: Use ARX, Google DP library, and OpenMined when discussing anonymization and PETs.

**For current events**: Subscribe to IAPP Daily Dashboard and The Markup; weave news into lessons.

**For engagement**: Have students map data flows for an app they use, analyze cookie banners with Cookiebot, read actual GDPR articles (not summaries), and debate emerging issues using FPF or EFF perspectives.
