# Origami Mathematics — Resources

## Primary Textbooks (for lesson content)

### Core References
- **Origametry: Mathematical Methods in Paper Folding** by Thomas Hull (Cambridge University Press, 2020)  
  Comprehensive treatment of origami mathematics accessible to undergraduates. Covers axioms, constructions, flat-foldability, and design algorithms. The definitive modern reference for this curriculum.  
  https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/mathematics-of-origami/2491B6A2B09128C38186D3F15FE6FC0E

- **Geometric Folding Algorithms: Linkages, Origami, Polyhedra** by Erik D. Demaine and Joseph O'Rourke (Cambridge, 2007)  
  Algorithmic and computational emphasis. More advanced than Hull but essential for design module. Includes 60+ open problems.  
  https://www.gfalop.org/  
  https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.5555/1478766

### Supplementary Textbooks
- **Project Origami: Activities for Exploring Mathematics (2nd edition)** by Thomas Hull (A K Peters, 2012)  
  Hands-on activities suitable for intermediate learners. Good for kinesthetic reinforcement of concepts.

- **The Mathematics of Origami** (Cambridge)  
  Accessible with only high school geometry and trigonometry. Features over 160 color figures and 49 exercises.

## Cross-Disciplinary Connections

### To Abstract Algebra
Symmetry groups of origami models, polyhedral symmetry, group theory applications in modular origami

### To Computer Science
Computational complexity (NP-hardness of flat-foldability), undecidability results, algorithm design

### To Art
Mathematical art, works by Robert Lang, Erik Demaine, Tomoko Fuse — rigor and beauty combined

### To Physics
Folding dynamics, mechanical properties of folded structures, zero-gravity folding in space

## Open Problems & Research Frontiers

### From Demaine & O'Rourke
60+ unsolved problems in geometric folding listed in the textbook. Topics include:
- Curved crease origami
- 4D folding theory
- Rigid origami characterization
- Optimal folding sequences

### Active Research Areas
- Origami tessellations
- Self-folding materials
- Origami robotics
- Computational complexity of folding problems
