# Structural Engineering — Bridges and Skyscrapers: Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Structural Fundamentals
- Load types (dead, live, wind, seismic, thermal)
- Force analysis and equilibrium
- Stress, strain, and material behavior
- Structural safety factors and failure modes

### 2. Materials Science for Structures
- Steel properties and design
- Concrete and reinforced concrete
- Composite materials
- Material selection criteria

### 3. Bridge Engineering
- **Beam bridges** — simple spans, continuous beams, load distribution
- **Arch bridges** — compression structures, thrust management
- **Suspension bridges** — cable systems, anchorage, tower design
- **Cable-stayed bridges** — fan vs harp configurations, deck-cable interaction
- Bridge deck design and construction methods

### 4. Skyscraper Structures
- **Rigid frame systems** — moment-resisting frames
- **Tube structures** — framed tube, bundled tube, diagrid
- **Core and outrigger systems** — lateral load resistance
- **Damping systems** — tuned mass dampers, viscous dampers
- Foundation design for tall buildings
- Wind and seismic design considerations

### 5. Analysis and Design Methods
- Statics and structural analysis
- Finite element modeling
- Load path design
- Code requirements and safety
