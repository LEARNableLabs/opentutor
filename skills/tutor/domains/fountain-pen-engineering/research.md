# Fountain Pen Engineering — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Nib Geometry and Mechanics
- **Tine design** — slit width, tine flexibility, springback, pressure response
- **Nib materials** — stainless steel vs gold alloys, work hardening, fatigue resistance
- **Tipping materials** — iridium alloys, osmium, hard metal welding, polishing geometry
- **Feed design** — capillary channels, ink flow regulation, air exchange
- **Curvature and contact geometry** — sweet spot, line variation, nib face angle

### 2. Ink Chemistry and Fluid Dynamics
- **Formulation components** — dyes vs pigments, surfactants, biocides, pH buffers
- **Rheology** — surface tension, viscosity, shear-thinning behavior, capillary action
- **Compatibility** — nib corrosion, feed clogging, paper feathering, dry time
- **Color science** — lightfastness, water resistance, shading, sheen
- **Flow dynamics** — fountain vs dip vs ballpoint, evaporation, nib creep

### 3. Manufacturing and Quality Control
- **Stamping and grinding** — nib blank production, tipping welding, polishing sequences
- **Flow testing** — pressure drop measurement, line width calibration
- **Tolerances** — slit alignment precision, tine symmetry, feed channel consistency
- **Materials sourcing** — alloy composition standards, tipping material supply chain
