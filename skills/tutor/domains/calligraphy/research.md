# Calligraphy Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Foundations & Cross-Cultural Elements
- **Tools & Materials** — pens, brushes, nibs, inks, paper selection across traditions
- **Posture & Grip** — body mechanics, breathing, hand positions
- **Rhythm & Spacing** — temporal and spatial flow in letter formation
- **Layout Principles** — composition, hierarchy, white space

### 2. Western Calligraphy
- **Historical Scripts** — Roman capitals, Uncial, Carolingian, Gothic/Blackletter
- **Italic** — foundational hand, variations (formal, cursive, flourished)
- **Copperplate/Engrosser's** — pointed pen technique, shading, flourishing
- **Modern Western Hands** — Foundational, Johnston, Neuland
- **Illumination** — gilding, decoration, marginalia

### 3. Arabic Calligraphy
- **Classical Scripts** — Kufic (geometric), Naskh (reading), Thuluth (monumental), Diwani (Ottoman), Nastaliq (Persian)
- **Pen Craft** — qalam (reed pen) cutting and use
- **Proportional Systems** — dot-based measurement, ductus
- **Composition** — mirroring, stacking, ligatures, tashkil (diacritics)
- **Spiritual Dimension** — calligraphy as meditation and devotion

### 4. East Asian Calligraphy
- **Chinese Traditions** — oracle bone script, seal script, clerical, regular, running, cursive (草書 caoshu)
- **Brush Techniques** — four treasures of study, loading, pressure variation, bone method
- **Japanese Styles** — kaisho (block), gyosho (semi-cursive), sosho (cursive), kana systems
- **Korean Hangul Calligraphy** — geometric construction, modern adaptations
- **Seal Carving** — complementary art form

### 5. Contemporary & Comparative
- **Cross-Cultural Influence** — Islamic art in Europe, Western abstraction from East Asian aesthetics
- **Modern Applications** — branding, typography, street art, digital calligraphy
- **Experimental Practices** — asemic writing, calligraffiti, 3D calligraphy

## Key Sources

- **Roehampton University (UK)** — Calligraphy & Book Arts MA program
- **École Estienne (Paris)** — calligraphy and typography programs
- **SOAS University of London** — Arabic and Islamic calligraphy courses
- **China Academy of Art (Hangzhou)** — traditional Chinese calligraphy

### Museums & Archives
- **British Library** — extensive manuscript collections across all traditions
- **Metropolitan Museum of Art** — Islamic Art department, Asian Art department
- **Victoria & Albert Museum** — calligraphy and manuscript collections
- **Freer|Sackler Galleries (Smithsonian)** — Asian and Islamic art
- **Library of Congress** — Hebraic/Arabic manuscript collection

### Organizations
- **IAMPETH** (International Association of Master Penmen, Engrossers, and Teachers of Handwriting) — Western tradition resources, conventions
- **Society for Calligraphy (Los Angeles)** — workshops, publications
- **Friends of Calligraphy (San Francisco)** — educational programs
- **The Calligraphy and Lettering Arts Society (CLAS, UK)** — journal, workshops

### Textbooks & Manuals
- **Edward Johnston**, *Writing & Illuminating, & Lettering* (1906) — foundational Western text
- **Sheila Waters**, *Foundations of Calligraphy* — comprehensive modern guide
- **Hassan Massoudy**, *Calligraphy: The Rhythm of Writing* — Arabic tradition
- **Ou-yang Hsun**, *Structure of Regular Script Characters* — classical Chinese
- **Yujiro Nakata**, *The Art of Japanese Calligraphy* — comprehensive Japanese survey

### Digital Resources
- **Internet Archive** — digitized historical manuals and exemplars
- **Qalam.org** — online Arabic calligraphy school
- **Japanese Calligraphy (YouTube: Takumi)** — technique demonstrations
- **Schin Loong (YouTube)** — Western calligraphy tutorials
- **The Postman's Knock** — copperplate and modern calligraphy blog

### Interactive Tools
- **Silk (weavesilk.com)** — generative symmetry tool (Arabic composition practice)
- **MyScriptFont** — digitize handwriting
- **Procreate brushes** — calligraphy brush sets for iPad practice
- **Endless Paper app** — Chinese character practice
