# Neon Sign Fabrication — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Glass Chemistry and Properties
Borosilicate vs. lead glass, thermal expansion coefficients, annealing, glass composition for neon work.

### 2. Glass Tube Bending Techniques
Torch types (crossfire, ribbon burners), heating techniques, bending angles and radii, end sealing, tubulation, bombarder processing.

### 3. Noble Gas Physics
Ionization, atomic emission spectra, energy levels, collision processes, discharge characteristics of neon, argon, helium, krypton, xenon.

### 4. Gas Discharge and Electrical Systems
Glow discharge physics, cathode fall, positive column, current-voltage characteristics, transformer design, electrode materials.

### 5. Color Theory and Phosphors
Spectral lines of noble gases, mercury addition for UV generation, phosphor coatings, color mixing, coating application techniques.

### 6. Design and Installation
Pattern making, full-scale templates, blocking (mounting hardware), high-voltage safety, weatherproofing.
