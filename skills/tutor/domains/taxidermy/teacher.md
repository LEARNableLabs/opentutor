# Taxidermy — anatomy, preservation, and ethics — Domain Teaching Config

## Exercise Types
This domain uses a mix of delivery formats:
- **concept-focused mini-lessons** — 10 lessons (37%)
- **Socratic questions** — 5 lessons (19%)
- **real-world application challenges** — 4 lessons (15%)
- **review and consolidation sessions** — 4 lessons (15%)
- **curated resource exploration** — 2 lessons (7%)
- **teach-back exercises (student explains)** — 2 lessons (7%)

Primary format: concept-focused mini-lessons.

## Resource Types
This domain has strong coverage in: textbooks, video lectures, interactive tools, academic papers, code repositories, online courses. Prioritize these when recommending resources.

## Difficulty Curve
Balanced progression — steady difficulty increase with regular consolidation.

Distribution: 44% accessible (1-2), 41% standard (3), 15% challenging (4-5).

Difficulty peaks:
- Day 8: "How do salt-based and acid-based tans differ chemically?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 13: "Why is fleshing the most critical step for long-term preservation?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 15: "What makes eye placement the difference between lifelike and dead-looking?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 19: "How do you preserve and position wings without distorting feathers?" (difficulty 4)

## Domain Hooks
This field covers taxidermy — anatomy, preservation, and ethics, with applications across theory and practice.

## Common Failure Modes
Students often struggle with the foundational concepts before building to more advanced material. Reinforce basics before progressing.
