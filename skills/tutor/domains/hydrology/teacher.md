# Hydrology and water cycles — Domain Teaching Config

## Exercise Types
This domain uses a mix of delivery formats:
- **concept-focused mini-lessons** — 10 lessons (32%)
- **Socratic questions** — 6 lessons (19%)
- **real-world application challenges** — 6 lessons (19%)
- **review and consolidation sessions** — 5 lessons (16%)
- **teach-back exercises (student explains)** — 3 lessons (10%)
- **curated resource exploration** — 1 lessons (3%)
