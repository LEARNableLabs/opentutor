# Aquaculture and Fisheries Science — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks
- **"Fish Population Dynamics" by John A. Gulland** — Classic text on stock assessment and population models. Best for Lessons 3-4, 7. Dense but authoritative.
- **"Fishery Science: The Unique Contributions of Early Life Stages" by Lee A. Fuiman and Robert G. Werner** — Excellent on recruitment and early life history. Relevant for Lessons 3-5.
- **"Aquaculture: Principles and Practices" by T.V.R. Pillay and M.N. Kutty** — Comprehensive aquaculture text. Covers all systems (Lessons 12-16).
- **"Marine Fisheries Ecology" by Simon Jennings, Michel J. Kaiser, and John D. Reynolds** — Ecosystem approach, food webs, bycatch. Strong for Lessons 2, 9, 21.
- **"The Economics of Fisheries Management" by Lee G. Anderson** — Economic foundations of quota systems, ITQs. Key for Lessons 8, 18-20.

### Online Courses & Syllabi
- **University of Washington — School of Aquatic and Fishery Sciences (SAFS)** — Course materials often publicly available. Look for FISH 310 (Introductory Fisheries Science), FISH 458 (Aquatic Ecology).
- **Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution (WHOI) — Fisheries Oceanography** — Lectures and notes on climate-fisheries links. Relevant for Lessons 21-22.
- **FAO E-learning Academy** — Free courses on fisheries management, aquaculture. [http://www.fao.org/elearning](http://www.fao.org/elearning)
- **MIT OpenCourseWare — Sustainable Fisheries** — Economics and policy focus. [https://ocw.mit.edu/](https://ocw.mit.edu/)

- **FishBase** — [https://www.fishbase.se/](https://www.fishbase.se/) — Comprehensive species database. Use throughout for species lookup.
- **SeaLifeBase** — [https://www.sealifebase.ca/](https://www.sealifebase.ca/) — Marine invertebrates, seaweeds, non-fish species.

## Unexpected Connections (for wild cards)

- **Ocean noise pollution and fish behavior** — Sonar, shipping, seismic surveys disrupt communication, navigation. Connects to physics, neuroscience.
- **Indigenous fisheries management** — Traditional ecological knowledge, seasonal closures, cultural practices. Connects to anthropology, history.
- **Fish as model organisms** — Zebrafish in developmental biology, medaka in genetics. Connects to molecular biology.
- **Fisheries and conflict** — Cod Wars (Iceland-UK), South China Sea disputes, tuna wars. Connects to geopolitics, international relations.
- **Microplastics in seafood** — Bioaccumulation, human health. Connects to toxicology, pollution science.
- **Urban aquaculture** — Rooftop fish farms, aquaponics in cities. Connects to urban planning, food systems.
- **Fish sentience and welfare** — Do fish feel pain? Ethics of live transport, slaughter. Connects to philosophy, animal welfare science.
- **Bioluminescence and deep-sea fisheries** — Lanternfish, hatchetfish. Connects to physics, ecology of the deep.
- **Omega-3 fatty acids** — Nutritional value, EPA/DHA, health benefits. Connects to nutrition, biochemistry.
- **Ciguatera poisoning** — Toxin accumulation in reef fish. Connects to toxicology, marine ecology.

## Citation & Verification

All URLs were verified as of August 2026. For academic rigor:
- Prefer primary literature (peer-reviewed journals) when available
- Use .gov, .edu, and major NGO (.org) sites for credibility
- Cross-reference FAO, NOAA, ICES for international/regional consistency
- Cite datasets (FishBase, RAM Legacy) with version/access date
- For controversial topics (Pauly-Hilborn debate, MSC critiques), present multiple perspectives
