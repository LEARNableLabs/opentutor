# Aquaculture and Fisheries Science — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Aquaculture Systems
- **Marine aquaculture** — offshore cages, coastal farms, shellfish culture
- **Freshwater aquaculture** — ponds, raceways, recirculating systems
- **Integrated systems** — aquaponics, IMTA (integrated multi-trophic aquaculture)
- **Feed technology** — formulation, nutrition, alternative protein sources

### 2. Wild Fisheries Management
- **Stock assessment** — population dynamics, catch data analysis, surplus production models
- **Fisheries policy** — quotas, ITQs (individual transferable quotas), maximum sustainable yield (MSY)
- **Ecosystem-based management** — trophic cascades, bycatch, habitat protection
- **Monitoring & enforcement** — observer programs, VMS, illegal fishing

### 3. Marine Ecology & Biology
- **Fish biology** — life cycles, reproduction, growth, behavior
- **Population dynamics** — recruitment, mortality, age structure
- **Marine ecosystems** — food webs, trophic levels, ecosystem services
- **Climate impacts** — ocean acidification, warming, range shifts

### 4. Economics & Social Dimensions
- **Market dynamics** — supply chains, pricing, trade
- **Community fisheries** — small-scale fisheries, indigenous rights, co-management
- **Aquaculture economics** — production costs, ROI, market development
- **Food security** — protein supply, developing nations, sustainability

### 5. Sustainability & Conservation
- **Certification schemes** — MSC (Marine Stewardship Council), ASC (Aquaculture Stewardship Council)
- **Overfishing** — stock collapse, recovery strategies, precautionary principle
- **Habitat restoration** — spawning grounds, wetlands, coral reefs
- **Climate adaptation** — resilient stocks, shifting practices

## Key Sources

### Academic Textbooks
- **"Fish Population Dynamics"** by John A. Gulland (classic stock assessment)
- **"Ecology of Fishes"** by Brian R. Murphy and David W. Willis
- **"Aquaculture: Principles and Practices"** by T.V.R. Pillay and M.N. Kutty
- **"The Economics of Fisheries Management"** by Lee G. Anderson

### Online Courses & Syllabi
- **FAO Fisheries and Aquaculture Department** — technical papers, state of world fisheries reports
- **NOAA Fisheries** — stock assessments, management plans, educational resources
- **University of Washington — School of Aquatic and Fishery Sciences** (SAFS) course materials
- **Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution** — fisheries oceanography lectures

### Datasets & Tools
- **FishBase** (fishbase.org) — comprehensive fish species database
- **RAM Legacy Stock Assessment Database** — global fisheries data
- **FAO FishStat** — global production statistics
- **NOAA FishWatch** — species profiles and sustainability

### Organizations & Reports
- **FAO** — State of World Fisheries and Aquaculture (SOFIA) annual reports
- **World Bank** — The Sunken Billions reports (fisheries economics)
- **ICES** (International Council for the Exploration of the Sea) — scientific advice
- **Marine Stewardship Council** — certification standards and case studies

## Available Educational Resources

### Video & Interactive
- **NOAA Fisheries YouTube channel** — species profiles, management explainers
- **Sustainable Fisheries UW** — public lectures and webinars
- **TED Talks** — Barton Seaver (sustainable seafood), Daniel Pauly (shifting baselines)

### Tools & Simulators
- **FishSim** — fisheries management simulation
- **Ecopath with Ecosim** — ecosystem modeling software
- **Stock Synthesis** — stock assessment modeling platform

## Research Gaps Identified

- Need practical examples of recirculating aquaculture systems (RAS) design
- Economics of small-scale fisheries underrepresented in mainstream literature
- Limited accessible resources on aquaculture disease management
- Climate adaptation strategies still emerging (limited consolidated resources)
