# Science and Technology Studies (STS) — Resources

## Primary Textbooks

### Core Introductions
- **Sergio Sismondo, *An Introduction to Science and Technology Studies* (2nd ed., Wiley-Blackwell, 2010)**
  - Best single-volume introduction for intermediate learners. Covers all major frameworks clearly.
  - https://www.wiley.com/en-us/An+Introduction+to+Science+and+Technology+Studies%2C+2nd+Edition-p-9781405187657

- **Ulrike Felt et al., eds., *The Handbook of Science and Technology Studies* (4th ed., MIT Press, 2017)**
  - Comprehensive reference with chapters by leading scholars. Use as encyclopedia, not cover-to-cover.
  - https://direct.mit.edu/books/edited-volume/3933/The-Handbook-of-Science-and-Technology-Studies

- **David J. Hess, *Science Studies: An Advanced Introduction* (NYU Press, 1997)**
  - Slightly older but excellent for connecting STS to broader science studies.
  - https://nyupress.org/9780814735640/science-studies/

### Classic Texts (primary sources)
- **Wiebe E. Bijker, Thomas P. Hughes, Trevor Pinch, eds., *The Social Construction of Technological Systems* (MIT Press, 1987)**
  - Foundational SCOT text. Includes Bijker's bicycle case.
  - https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262517607/

- **Bruno Latour, *Science in Action* (Harvard University Press, 1987)**
  - Core ANT text. Accessible and provocative.
  - https://www.hup.harvard.edu/catalog.php?isbn=9780674792913

- **Bruno Latour & Steve Woolgar, *Laboratory Life* (Princeton University Press, 1979)**
  - Ethnographic study of a biology lab. Shows science-in-the-making.
  - https://press.princeton.edu/books/paperback/9780691028323/laboratory-life

- **Donna Haraway, "A Cyborg Manifesto" (1985)**
  - Short, influential essay. Essential feminist STS.
  - https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/arts/english/currentstudents/undergraduate/modules/fictionnow/manifestly_haraway/

- **Langdon Winner, "Do Artifacts Have Politics?" (Daedalus, 1980)**
  - Classic paper on embedded values. Very readable.
  - https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~beki/cs4001/Winner.pdf

- **Sheila Jasanoff, ed., *States of Knowledge: The Co-Production of Science and Social Order* (Routledge, 2004)**
  - Key work on regulatory science and co-production.
  - https://www.routledge.com/States-of-Knowledge-The-Co-production-of-Science-and-the-Social-Order/Jasanoff/p/book/9780415403283
