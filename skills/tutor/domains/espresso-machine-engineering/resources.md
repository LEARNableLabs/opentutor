# Espresso Machine Engineering — Resources

- **"The Physics of Filter Coffee" by Jonathan Gagné** (Coffee ad Astra) — while focused on filter, the extraction science and fluid dynamics apply to espresso. Available at https://coffeeadastra.com/book/

## Unexpected Connections

### From chemical engineering
- **Packed bed reactors** — coffee pucks are chemically reactive packed beds. Chemical engineering literature on flow through packed beds applies directly.
- **Supercritical fluid extraction** — used in decaffeination, shares principles with espresso extraction at lower pressures.

### From materials science
- **Coffee bean microstructure** — cell wall breakdown during roasting affects extractability. Links to food science and materials engineering.
- **Scale formation** — mineral deposition in boilers and groupheads is a corrosion engineering problem.

### From control theory
- **Adaptive control** — some modern machines use adaptive PID that learns optimal parameters over time. Links to robotics and aerospace control systems.
- **Multi-input multi-output (MIMO) systems** — pressure and temperature interact; control theory for MIMO systems applies.

### From data science
- **Machine learning for shot prediction** — some apps use ML to predict extraction outcomes from sensor data. Links to time-series analysis.
- **Computer vision for puck analysis** — research on using cameras to detect channeling and density variations in real-time.

### From history and anthropology
- **Italian espresso machine design** — mid-century industrial design, E61 grouphead as iconic engineering artifact
- **Third wave coffee movement** — how specialty coffee drove pressure profiling innovation and open-source machine development

## Academic Papers (for deep dives)

- **"Systematically Improving Espresso: Insights from Mathematical Modeling and Experiment"** — Melrose et al., Matter, 2020. Computational model of espresso extraction.
- **"The effect of bean origin and temperature on grinding roasted coffee"** — Uman et al., Scientific Reports, 2016. Particle size distribution research.
- **"Investigating shot brewing parameters on the sensory scores of espresso coffee"** — Angeloni et al., Coffee Science, 2019. Empirical taste studies.
- **"Extraction kinetics of coffee aroma compounds using a semi-automatic espresso machine"** — Gloess et al., Journal of Food Engineering, 2014. Extraction chemistry.

Search **Google Scholar** for "espresso extraction" or **Semantic Scholar** for coffee science papers with open access.
