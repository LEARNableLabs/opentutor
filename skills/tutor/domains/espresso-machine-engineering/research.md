# Espresso Machine Engineering — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Thermodynamics and Heat Transfer
- Boiler systems (single boiler, heat exchanger, dual boiler)
- Temperature stability and PID control
- Thermal mass and recovery time
- Heat loss and insulation

### 2. Pressure Systems
- Pump types (vibratory vs. rotary)
- Pressure profiling and flow control
- Pre-infusion dynamics
- Over-Pressure Valve (OPV) function

### 3. Extraction Chemistry
- Solubility curves and extraction kinetics
- Coffee bed physics (channeling, resistance)
- Grind distribution and particle size
- Water chemistry (TDS, hardness, alkalinity)

### 4. Flow and Hydraulics
- Flow rate vs. pressure relationship
- Basket geometry and flow distribution
- Showerscreen design
- Backpressure and restriction

### 5. Advanced Control Systems
- PID tuning for temperature control
- Flow rate profiling
- Pressure decay curves
- Shot timers and automation

## Key Sources

### Academic and Technical
- **MIT OpenCourseWare: Thermodynamics** — foundational heat transfer principles
- **Fluid Mechanics fundamentals** — pump hydraulics, flow resistance
- **SCA Coffee Brewing Handbook** — extraction science standards
- **Coffee Chemistry (academic papers)** — Illy et al., extraction kinetics research

### Practitioner Resources
- **Barista Hustle** — professional barista training, extraction theory
- **Coffee ad Astra by Jonathan Gagné** — data-driven espresso science blog
- **Home-Barista forums** — technical machine discussions
- **Decent Espresso blog** — pressure profiling research

### Video Education
- **James Hoffmann YouTube** — equipment reviews, extraction theory
- **Lance Hedrick YouTube** — technical espresso tutorials
- **Sprometheus YouTube** — machine modifications and engineering

## Available Learning Resources

- Interactive extraction calculators and brew ratio tools
- Pressure profiling software (Decent, Acaia apps)
- Temperature logging tools
- Refractometers for measuring TDS
- Real machine schematics and service manuals
- Simulation tools for extraction modeling

## Gaps and Challenges

- Much of the best research is scattered across blogs and forums rather than formal courses
- Engineering fundamentals (thermo, fluids) exist but need translation to coffee context
- Hands-on experimentation is crucial but hard to simulate remotely
- Equipment varies widely — need to teach principles that apply across machines
