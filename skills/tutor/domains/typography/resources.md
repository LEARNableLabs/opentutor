# Typography and Lettering — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Books
- **Thinking with Type** by Ellen Lupton — comprehensive introduction covering classification, composition, and systems. Perfect for intermediate learners who want structured progression. Covers both print and digital.
- **The Elements of Typographic Style** by Robert Bringhurst — the definitive reference on typographic principles. Dense but essential. Intermediate students should read selectively (focus on chapters 2, 4, 6, 7).
- **Practical Typography** by Matthew Butterick (https://practicaltypography.com/) — free online book. Opinionated, practical, immediately applicable. Great for students who learn by doing. Covers both legal writing and general design.
- **Detail In Typography** by Jost Hochuli — slim, focused book on spacing and micro-typography. Perfect companion for lessons 5-9.
- **The Art of Hand Lettering** by Shauna Lynn Panczyszyn — comprehensive lettering guide covering brush, pencil, and digital techniques. Good for lessons 19-23.
- **Lettering & Type** by Bruce Willen and Nolen Strals — creates custom type using lettering as a gateway. Excellent for the lettering-to-type-design bridge (lessons 23-24).

### Online Courses
- **Type@Cooper Public Workshop** (https://coopertype.org/) — condensed type design workshops from Cooper Union. Professional-level but accessible to serious intermediate students.
- **Skillshare Lettering Courses** (https://www.skillshare.com/browse/lettering) — wide variety of hand lettering and brush lettering tutorials. Filter for intermediate level.
- **Domestika Typography Courses** (https://www.domestika.org/en/courses/areas/13-calligraphy-typography) — high-quality video courses from working designers. Many focus on applied projects.
- **Learn Type Design** at Envato Tuts+ (https://design.tutsplus.com/categories/typography) — free tutorials covering both typography and type design.

### Educational Websites
- **Typography.com Blog** (https://www.typography.com/blog/) — articles and case studies from Hoefler&Co. Deep dives into type history, design decisions, and technical topics.
- **Google Fonts Knowledge** (https://fonts.google.com/knowledge) — free educational modules covering type basics, choosing type, and using type on the web.
- **Fonts In Use** (https://fontsinuse.com/) — real-world typography examples with typeface details. Essential for studying type in context.
- **Typewolf** (https://www.typewolf.com/) — web typography inspiration, trend analysis, and practical guides. Great for lessons 16-18 (pairing).
- **Typographica** (https://typographica.org/) — type reviews, specimen analysis, and design critique. More advanced/specialized.
- **I Love Typography** (https://ilovetypography.com/) — articles on type history, design, and culture.

## Supplementary (for engagement and exploration)

### Interactive Tools
- **Kern Type** (https://type.method.ac/) — game for practicing kerning. Essential for lesson 6. Students should aim for 90%+ scores.
- **Shape Type** (https://shape.method.ac/) — practice drawing letter shapes by adjusting bezier curves. Great for lessons 20 and 25.
- **Modular Scale** (https://www.modularscale.com/) — calculator for harmonious type scales using ratios. Use for lesson 7 (vertical rhythm).
- **Archetype** (https://archetypeapp.com/) — preview and compare typefaces in real layouts. Useful for pairing exercises (lessons 16-18).
- **Font Pair** (https://fontpair.co/) — Google Fonts pairing suggestions. Good starting point for lesson 17.
- **WhatFont** browser extension — identify fonts on websites. Useful for real-world analysis throughout the curriculum.

### Video Resources
- **The Futur YouTube Channel** — typography tutorials and design business content. Search for "typography" for specific episodes.
- **Skillshare** (https://www.skillshare.com/browse/lettering) — subscription service with hundreds of lettering and typography courses

## Unexpected Connections (Rabbit Holes)

### Psychology and Perception
- **Reading and Typography** — research on eye movements, reading speed, and comprehension as affected by typography
- **Gestalt Principles in Typography** — how proximity, similarity, and closure affect type composition
- **Emotional Response to Type** — studies on personality perception based on typeface choice

### Technology and Code
- **Variable Fonts Spec** (https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/CSS_Fonts/Variable_Fonts_Guide) — technical deep dive into variable font technology
- **OpenType Features** (https://helpx.adobe.com/fonts/using/open-type-syntax.html) — ligatures, alternates, small caps, and advanced typography
- **Font Performance** — web performance optimization with font loading strategies (FOUT, FOIT, font-display)

### Cultural and Historical
- **Non-Latin Typography** — Arabic, Chinese, Devanagari, Hebrew type design and cultural conventions
- **Blackletter and Historical Scripts** — Gothic, Uncial, Carolingian scripts and their influence
- **Type in Social Movements** — typography in propaganda, protest, and political messaging

### Business and Professional
- **Type Licensing** — understanding font EULAs, web fonts, and commercial use
- **Custom Type for Brands** — case studies of corporate typeface commissions (IBM Plex, Netflix Sans, etc.)
- **Type Foundries** — business models and economics of commercial type design

### Cross-Disciplinary
- **Typography in Motion** — film titles, kinetic typography, animated type
- **Generative Typography** — code-generated letterforms and computational design
- **Environmental Typography** — wayfinding, signage, architectural lettering
- **Typography and Accessibility** — universal design, inclusive typography, WCAG standards

## Ongoing Learning

### After Completing This Curriculum
- **Take a type design workshop** — Type@Cooper, TypeParis, or KABK TypeMedia (advanced)
- **Build a lettering portfolio** — 30-day lettering challenges, client projects
- **Study a type family deeply** — pick one typeface, read about its history, study all weights/styles
- **Contribute to open-source type** — Google Fonts accepts contributions, or contribute to existing projects
- **Start a side project** — design a display typeface, create lettering for local businesses, build a typographic website

### Communities to Join
- **Type Thursday** meetups — network with local type enthusiasts
- **TypeDrawers** — get feedback on type design work
- **Dribbble/Behance** — share lettering and typography projects
- **Instagram lettering community** — follow hashtags #lettering #typography #typedesign for inspiration and connections
