# Philosophy of Mind — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Core Textbooks

**David J. Chalmers - "The Conscious Mind: In Search of a Fundamental Theory" (1996)**
- Oxford University Press: https://global.oup.com/academic/product/the-conscious-mind-9780195117899
- PDF available: https://personal.lse.ac.uk/ROBERT49/teaching/ph103/pdf/Chalmers_The_Conscious_Mind.pdf
- What it covers: Hard problem of consciousness, easy problems, property dualism, substrate independence, zombie arguments, consciousness science foundations
- Why it's essential: Defines the hard problem; required reading for understanding contemporary consciousness debates; Chalmers is pro-computational but anti-materialist, giving both sides

**Andy Clark - "Mindware: An Introduction to the Philosophy of Cognitive Science"**
- What it covers: Computational theory of mind, connectionism, embodied cognition, extended mind
- Why it's good for intermediate: Accessible writing; bridges philosophy and cognitive science; used in multiple university courses
- Level fit: Perfect for intermediate — not too technical, but rigorous argument structure

**John Haugeland - "Mind Design II: Philosophy, Psychology, Artificial Intelligence"**
- Classic anthology combining philosophy papers with AI/cogsci readings
- Covers: Turing, Searle, Dennett, classical computationalism, connectionism
- Why valuable: Primary sources from key figures; sees debates from AI/cogsci angle

## Unexpected Cross-Discipline Connections

### Physics and Information Theory

**Information theory foundations**
- Shannon entropy connects to IIT's integrated information
- Thermodynamics and information (Landauer's principle): does consciousness require physical substrate that processes information?
- Quantum information: some consciousness theories invoke quantum mechanics (Penrose-Hameroff)

**Connection value**: Shows consciousness isn't just "soft" philosophy — mathematical and physical concepts apply

### Ethics and Moral Philosophy

**Consciousness and moral status**
- If consciousness matters morally (sentience), how do we identify it?
- Animal consciousness and vegetarianism/animal rights
- AI consciousness and AI rights/welfare
- Implications for abortion debates, brain death, disorders of consciousness

**Connection value**: Makes abstract debates concrete and practical; students care about ethical implications

### Meditation and Contemplative Science

**First-person investigation of consciousness**
- Buddhist philosophy of mind (no-self doctrine challenges Western assumptions)
- Meditation as systematic first-person science
- Francisco Varela's neurophenomenology: combining first-person and third-person methods
- Mindfulness and meta-awareness as access consciousness

**Connection value**: Bridges Eastern and Western philosophy; gives students experiential handle on consciousness concepts

### Linguistics and Semantics

**Meaning and intentionality**
- How do mental states have "aboutness" (intentionality)?
- Searle's Chinese Room is fundamentally about syntax vs semantics
- Compositionality of thought mirrors compositionality of language
- Does language create consciousness or vice versa?

**Connection value**: Natural for students with language background; connects to familiar territory

### Psychiatry and Neurology

**Disorders of consciousness**
- Vegetative state, minimally conscious state, locked-in syndrome
- Schizophrenia and agency (who is the author of thoughts?)
- Dissociative identity disorder and unity of consciousness
- Blindsight (V1 damage), neglect, anosognosia

**Connection value**: Real clinical cases that probe philosophical concepts; shows philosophy informs medical ethics

### Evolutionary Biology

**Evolution of consciousness**
- Why did consciousness evolve? What adaptive function does it serve?
- At what point in evolution did phenomenal consciousness emerge?
- Is consciousness an adaptation or spandrel/byproduct?
- Continuity vs discontinuity (are animals conscious?)

**Connection value**: Challenges epiphenomenalism (if consciousness does nothing, why evolve it?); connects to biology majors

### Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

**Modern AI as test cases**
- Do LLMs understand or just manipulate symbols? (Updated Chinese Room)
- Neural networks vs symbolic AI: different computational substrates
- Emergent behavior in complex systems
- Alignment problem: does it presuppose machine consciousness or agency?

**Connection value**: Extremely relevant to current technology; students have direct experience with ChatGPT/Claude; makes 1980s debates feel urgent and contemporary

### Mathematics and Logic

**Formal theories of computation**
- Turing machines, Church-Turing thesis: what is computation?
- Gödel's incompleteness theorems (Penrose uses these to argue against strong AI)
- Complexity theory: P vs NP, computational limits
- Formal semantics: model theory for intentionality

**Connection value**: Gives precise framework for "computational" in computational functionalism; satisfies mathematically-minded students

## Wild Cards (for advanced/interested students)

**Psychedelics and consciousness**
- How do psychedelics alter consciousness? What does this tell us about normal consciousness?
- Robin Carhart-Harris's REBUS model, entropic brain hypothesis
- Connection to IIT (psychedelics might increase phi)

**Consciousness in non-carbon life**
- Could silicon-based life be conscious?
- Could consciousness exist in radically different substrates (plasma, quantum fields)?
- Astrobiology and consciousness detection

**Time consciousness**
- How do we experience temporal flow?
- Specious present, time perception, temporal binding
- Connection to physics (block universe vs presentism)

**Social/collective consciousness**
- Can groups have phenomenal consciousness?
- Hive minds, collective intelligence
- Social functionalism: society as computational system

**Art and consciousness**
- Aesthetic experience and qualia
- Does art appreciation require consciousness?
- Can AI create art? Does it matter if AI is conscious?
