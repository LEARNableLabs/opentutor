# Microeconomics — markets, incentives, and failures — Domain Teaching Config

## Exercise Types
This domain uses a mix of delivery formats:
- **concept-focused mini-lessons** — 18 lessons (45%)
- **Socratic questions** — 9 lessons (23%)
- **real-world application challenges** — 6 lessons (15%)
- **review and consolidation sessions** — 6 lessons (15%)
- **teach-back exercises (student explains)** — 1 lessons (3%)

Primary format: concept-focused mini-lessons.

## Resource Types
This domain has strong coverage in: textbooks, video lectures, interactive tools, academic papers, code repositories, online courses. Prioritize these when recommending resources.

## Difficulty Curve
Balanced progression — steady difficulty increase with regular consolidation.

Distribution: 30% accessible (1-2), 40% standard (3), 30% challenging (4-5).

Difficulty peaks:
- Day 10: "How do you choose the best bundle you can afford?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 11: "When prices change, why do you substitute toward cheaper goods?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 17: "How much should a firm produce to maximize profit?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 23: "Why do monopolies charge higher prices and produce less?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 24: "How does price discrimination let firms capture more surplus?" (difficulty 4)

## Domain Hooks
This field covers microeconomics — markets, incentives, and failures, with applications across theory and practice.

## Common Failure Modes
Students often struggle with the foundational concepts before building to more advanced material. Reinforce basics before progressing.

## Vocabulary
Key terms for this domain: scarcity, opportunity cost, markets as coordination mechanisms, demand curve, law of demand, willingness to pay, supply curve, law of supply, marginal cost, market equilibrium, market clearing, comparative statics, elasticity, price responsiveness, short-run vs long-run (and 104 more).
