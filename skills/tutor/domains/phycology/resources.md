# Phycology — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks

- **Algae (3rd Edition, 2016)** by Linda Graham, James Graham, and Lee Wilcox
  - Comprehensive undergraduate/graduate treatment with modern phylogenetic framework
  - Excellent for intermediate level — clear explanations, beautiful illustrations, up-to-date taxonomy
  - Strong on evolution, cell biology, and ecology
  - Available: major academic libraries, publisher (Cambridge University Press)
  - [Cambridge University Press page](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/algae/0B4FB5A0B073E8E3F8F8F6C7E7E0F7E2)

- **Phycology (4th Edition, 2018)** by Robert Edward Lee
  - Detailed coverage of all algal groups with emphasis on cell biology and ultrastructure
  - More technical than Graham et al., excellent reference for deep dives
  - Strong on flagellar apparatus, chloroplast structure, and life cycles
  - [Cambridge University Press](https://www.cambridge.org/us/academic/subjects/life-sciences/plant-science/phycology-4th-edition)

- **Algae: An Introduction to Phycology (1995)** by C. van den Hoek, D.G. Mann, and H.M. Jahns
  - Classic text, slightly dated taxonomy but excellent conceptual foundation
  - Outstanding illustrations and micrographs
  - Strong on morphology and ecology
  - Still widely used and cited

### Online Course Materials

- **AlgaeBase** — [www.algaebase.org](https://www.algaebase.org)
  - Comprehensive, authoritative database of all algal species
  - Taxonomic information, images, literature references
  - Maintained by M.D. Guiry, regularly updated
  - Essential reference for taxonomy and nomenclature

- **UC Berkeley Museum of Paleontology** — [https://ucmp.berkeley.edu](https://ucmp.berkeley.edu)
  - Excellent introductions to major algal groups
  - Phylogenetic trees, fossil records, key characteristics
  - [Cyanobacteria](https://ucmp.berkeley.edu/bacteria/cyanobacteria.html)
  - [Green Algae](https://ucmp.berkeley.edu/greenalgae/greenalgae.html)
  - [Red Algae](https://ucmp.berkeley.edu/rhodophyta/rhodophyta.html)
  - [Brown Algae](https://ucmp.berkeley.edu/chromista/phaeophyta.html)

- **Tree of Life Web Project** — [https://tolweb.org](https://tolweb.org)
  - Phylogenetic information on algal groups
  - Links to expert-written pages on each lineage
  - [Green Plants](https://tolweb.org/Green_plants/2382)

- **Diatoms of North America** — [https://diatoms.org](https://diatoms.org)
  - Comprehensive diatom identification and ecology database
  - Outstanding images and species descriptions
  - Regional keys and ecological indicator information

### Interactive Tools

- **Protist Image Database** — [https://protistimagedata.org](https://protistimagedata.org)
  - University of Montreal collection
  - High-resolution images of algae and other protists
  - Searchable by taxonomic group

- **PhycoKey Online** — Interactive identification tools
  - Dichotomous keys for algal identification
  - Educational modules for teaching
  - Regional guides (some geographic regions better covered than others)

- **Cyanosite** — [https://cyanosite.bio.purdue.edu](https://cyanosite.bio.purdue.edu)
  - Comprehensive cyanobacteria information portal
  - Taxonomy, images, culture collections
  - Links to research labs and resources

- **Encyclopedia of Life (EOL)** — [https://eol.org](https://eol.org)
  - Species pages for algal taxa with images, maps, and summaries
  - Crowd-sourced content from multiple databases
  - Good for quick overviews

- **MBARI (Monterey Bay Aquarium Research Institute)** — [Bioluminescence research](https://www.mbari.org/bioluminescence-research/)
  - Videos and information on bioluminescent dinoflagellates
  - Research expeditions and discoveries
  - Beautiful footage of living bioluminescence

### Code and Data

- **PhytoREF** — Phytoplankton reference database

- **Ocean Biogeographic Information System (OBIS)** — [https://obis.org](https://obis.org)

### Unexpected Connections

- **Art and Algae**
  - Ernst Haeckel's *Kunstformen der Natur* (1904) — Art Nouveau-influenced illustrations of diatoms and other algae
  - [Digital archive](https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/item/120272)
  - Inspired architecture, jewelry, and design movements

- **Algae and Climate**
  - CLAW hypothesis (Charlson, Lovelock, Andreae, Warren) — how marine algae influence cloud formation and climate through DMS/DMSP production
  - [Original paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/326655a0) (Nature, 1987)
  - Connects phytoplankton to atmospheric chemistry and global climate regulation

- **Forensic Phycology**
  - Diatoms in drowning investigations — diatom species in bone marrow can indicate drowning location
  - Ecological profiling of water bodies
  - Underappreciated application of algal expertise

- **Algae in Literature**
  - Rachel Carson's *The Sea Around Us* — poetic descriptions of phytoplankton and ocean ecology
  - Encourages nature writing and science communication

- **Extreme Algae**
  - *Cyanidium caldarium* in hot springs (56°C, pH 0.5)
  - *Dunaliella salina* in hypersaline environments (10× seawater salinity)
  - Snow algae at high altitudes and polar regions
  - Desert crust communities in extreme aridity
  - Shows adaptability and evolutionary resilience

## Research Papers (Key Reviews and Classics)

### Endosymbiosis and Evolution

- Keeling, P.J. (2010). "The endosymbiotic origin, diversification and fate of plastids." *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B*, 365(1541), 729-748.
  - [DOI: 10.1098/rstb.2009.0103](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2009.0103)
  - Comprehensive review of plastid evolution across eukaryotes

- Archibald, J.M. (2015). "Endosymbiosis and eukaryotic cell evolution." *Current Biology*, 25(19), R911-R921.
  - [DOI: 10.1016/j.cub.2015.07.055](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2015.07.055)
  - Accessible overview of endosymbiotic theory

### Phylogeny and Systematics

- Cavalier-Smith, T. (2017). "Kingdom Chromista and its eight phyla: a new synthesis emphasising periplastid protein targeting, cytoskeletal and periplastid evolution, and ancient divergences." *Protoplasma*, 255, 297-357.
  - Controversial but influential classification proposals
  - Shows ongoing debates in algal systematics

### Ecology and Global Change

- Field, C.B., Behrenfeld, M.J., Randerson, J.T., & Falkowski, P. (1998). "Primary production of the biosphere: integrating terrestrial and oceanic components." *Science*, 281(5374), 237-240.
  - [DOI: 10.1126/science.281.5374.237](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.281.5374.237)
  - Classic paper on global primary productivity — shows algae produce ~50% of Earth's oxygen

- Falkowski, P.G., & Raven, J.A. (2007). *Aquatic Photosynthesis* (2nd ed.). Princeton University Press.
  - Comprehensive treatment of photosynthesis in algae and cyanobacteria
  - Technical but authoritative

### Multicellularity

- Cock, J.M., Godfroy, O., Macaisne, N., Peters, A.F., & Coelho, S.M. (2014). "Evolution and regulation of complex life cycles: a brown algae perspective." *Current Opinion in Plant Biology*, 17, 1-6.
  - [DOI: 10.1016/j.pbi.2013.10.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pbi.2013.10.004)
  - Brown algae as model for convergent evolution of multicellularity

- Herron, M.D., Rashidi, A., Shelton, D.E., & Driscoll, W.W. (2013). "Cellular differentiation and individuality in the 'minor' multicellular taxa." *Biological Reviews*, 88(4), 844-861.
  - Compares multicellularity evolution across algae and other groups

### Harmful Algal Blooms

- Anderson, D.M., Cembella, A.D., & Hallegraeff, G.M. (2012). "Progress in understanding harmful algal blooms: paradigm shifts and new technologies for research, monitoring, and management." *Annual Review of Marine Science*, 4, 143-176.
  - [DOI: 10.1146/annurev-marine-120308-081121](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-marine-120308-081121)
  - Comprehensive HAB review

### Applications and Biotechnology

- Larkum, A.W., Ross, I.L., Kruse, O., & Hankamer, B. (2012). "Selection, breeding and engineering of microalgae for bioenergy and biofuel production." *Trends in Biotechnology*, 30(4), 198-205.
  - Algal biofuels and genetic engineering approaches

- Enzing, C., Ploeg, M., Barbosa, M., & Sijtsma, L. (2014). "Microalgae-based products for the food and feed sector: an outlook for Europe." *JRC Scientific and Policy Reports*, European Commission.
  - Commercial applications and market potential
