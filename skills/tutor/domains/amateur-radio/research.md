# Amateur Radio Research — Propagation, Antenna Theory, and Protocols

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Radio Wave Propagation
- Electromagnetic wave fundamentals (frequency, wavelength, polarization)
- Propagation modes: ground wave, line-of-sight, skywave
- Ionospheric layers (D, E, F1, F2) and their characteristics
- Solar cycle effects on HF propagation
- Maximum usable frequency (MUF), critical frequency, skip zone
- Propagation prediction tools and models
- VHF/UHF propagation (tropospheric ducting, scatter modes)

### 2. Antenna Theory
- Antenna fundamentals: radiation resistance, efficiency, gain, directivity
- Resonance, impedance, and SWR
- Feedlines: coax, ladder line, impedance transformation
- Basic antenna types: dipole, vertical, loop, Yagi, quad
- Phased arrays and directivity
- Antenna modeling and simulation
- Practical considerations: height, ground effects, interactions

### 3. Operating Protocols and Modes
- Voice modes: AM, SSB (USB/LSB), FM
- CW (Morse code) operating procedures
- Digital modes: PSK31, FT8, RTTY, SSTV
- Contest operating techniques
- DXing and pileup management
- Emergency communications (ARES/RACES, nets)
- QSL cards and awards (DXCC, WAS, etc.)

## Research Gaps and Opportunities
- Modern propagation prediction integrating real-time space weather data
- Software-defined radio (SDR) changing how we approach antenna experimentation
- Digital modes evolving rapidly (FT8 revolutionized weak-signal work)
- Integration with computer networking (APRS, D-STAR, DMR)
