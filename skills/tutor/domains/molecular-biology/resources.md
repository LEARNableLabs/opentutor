# Molecular Biology: DNA to Protein — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks

- **Molecular Biology of the Cell** (Alberts et al., 6th edition)
  - https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK21054/
  - Gold standard textbook, comprehensive coverage, freely available online through NCBI
  - Excellent for intermediate learners: detailed but accessible
  - Chapter 6 (DNA to RNA), Chapter 7 (Protein synthesis)

- **Lehninger Principles of Biochemistry** (Nelson & Cox, 8th edition)
  - https://www.macmillanlearning.com/college/us/product/Lehninger-Principles-of-Biochemistry/p/1464126119
  - Strong on biochemical mechanisms and energetics
  - Chapter 26 (RNA metabolism), Chapter 27 (Protein metabolism)

- **Molecular Biology** (Weaver, 5th edition)
  - Detailed molecular mechanisms with experimental evidence
  - Good for understanding "how we know" what we know

### Online Textbooks & References

- **Nature Scitable: Genetics and Gene Regulation**
  - https://www.nature.com/scitable/topic/genetics-and-gene-regulation-6/
  - Free, peer-reviewed educational resource with excellent review articles
  - Topic pages on transcription, translation, gene expression

- **NCBI Bookshelf: Molecular Cell Biology** (Lodish et al.)
  - https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK21475/
  - Freely available online, comprehensive
  - Section IV (Central Dogma)

### Online Courses

- **MIT OCW 7.016: Introductory Biology**
  - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-016-introductory-biology-fall-2018/
  - Video lectures by Eric Lander and others
  - Lectures 7-12 cover molecular biology fundamentals

- **Khan Academy: Central Dogma (DNA to RNA to Protein)**
  - https://www.khanacademy.org/science/biology/gene-expression-central-dogma
  - Free, accessible video lessons with practice problems
  - Good for review or filling gaps

- **Coursera: Introduction to Genomic Technologies (Johns Hopkins)**
  - https://www.coursera.org/learn/introduction-genomics
  - Connects molecular biology to modern techniques

## Supplementary Resources

### Videos & Animations

- **iBiology**
  - https://www.ibiology.org/
  - World-class scientists explaining their research
  - Excellent video series on transcription, translation, RNA processing
  - Specific talks: "Ribosome Structure and Function" (Ramakrishnan), "Chromatin and Gene Regulation" (Henikoff)

- **Nature Video**
  - https://www.youtube.com/user/NatureVideoChannel
  - High-quality animations of molecular processes
  - Search for: "transcription animation", "ribosome structure"

- **DNA Learning Center (DNALC) 3D Animations**
  - https://www.dnalc.org/resources/3d/
  - Interactive 3D animations of molecular processes
  - Excellent for visualizing transcription and translation

- **Yourgenome from Wellcome Genome Campus**
  - https://www.yourgenome.org/
  - Engaging explanations and videos for molecular biology topics

- **WEHImovies** (Walter and Eliza Hall Institute)
  - https://www.youtube.com/user/WEHImovies
  - Beautiful molecular animations including "The Central Dogma" series

### Interactive Tools

- **RCSB Protein Data Bank (PDB)**
  - https://www.rcsb.org/
  - 3D structures of biomolecules
  - Key structures for this course:
    - DNA double helix: 1BNA
    - Nucleosome: 1AOI
    - RNA polymerase: 1I6V, 6ALF
    - Spliceosome: 3JB9
    - Ribosome: 4V6X, 4V9D
    - tRNA: 1EHZ

- **Molecule World**
  - https://molworldapp.com/
  - iOS/Android app for exploring PDB structures
  - Great for students to manipulate structures on their devices

- **Proteopedia**
  - https://proteopedia.org/
  - Collaborative 3D encyclopedia of proteins
  - Interactive scenes for teaching: ribosome, RNA polymerase, etc.

- **NCBI Structure Database**
  - https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/structure/
  - Molecular structure data integrated with literature

- **ExPASy: Bioinformatics Resource Portal**
  - https://www.expasy.org/
  - Tools for sequence analysis, translation, protein properties

### Code & Computational Resources

- **Biopython**
  - https://biopython.org/
  - Python tools for computational molecular biology
  - Useful for students who want to code: sequence manipulation, translation, etc.

- **Rosalind** (bioinformatics problems)
  - https://rosalind.info/
  - Learn bioinformatics through problem-solving
  - Problems on DNA, RNA, translation, sequence analysis

- **PyMOL**
  - https://pymol.org/
  - Molecular visualization software (free educational version)
  - Essential for serious structure viewing

- **UGENE**
  - http://ugene.net/
  - Free, open-source bioinformatics tools
  - Sequence alignment, ORF finding, in silico translation

### Databases & Reference

- **OMIM (Online Mendelian Inheritance in Man)**
  - https://www.omim.org/
  - Catalog of human genes and genetic disorders
  - Use for connecting mutations to disease (Lesson 21)

- **GenBank (NCBI)**
  - https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/genbank/
  - DNA sequence database
  - Can look up actual gene sequences

- **Ensembl**
  - https://www.ensembl.org/
  - Genome browser with gene annotations
  - Visualize gene structure, alternative splicing

- **UniProt**
  - https://www.uniprot.org/
  - Comprehensive protein sequence and function database

## Unexpected Connections (Wild Cards)

### Cross-Discipline Links

- **Cryptography and the Genetic Code**
  - Information theory applied to DNA sequences
  - Error-correcting codes vs. codon redundancy
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/nrg1348

- **Molecular Motors and Engineering**
  - RNA polymerase and ribosomes as nanomachines
  - Biomimetic motors inspired by biology
  - https://www.annualreviews.org/doi/10.1146/annurev-biochem-060614-034232

- **Music and Protein Sequences**
  - "DNA music": mapping sequences to musical notes
  - Pattern recognition in both domains
  - Example: "What does DNA sound like?" videos on YouTube

- **Archaeology and Ancient DNA**
  - Reading DNA from extinct species (mammoths, Neanderthals)
  - How DNA degrades over time
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/nature14736

- **Forensics and the Central Dogma**
  - DNA profiling, paternity testing
  - mRNA expression analysis at crime scenes
  - TV shows (CSI) vs. reality

- **Linguistics and Genetic Code**
  - Both are symbolic systems with grammar
  - Translation metaphor is literal
  - Chomsky's linguistics vs. biological translation

- **Quantum Biology and DNA**
  - Quantum tunneling in DNA mutations
  - Quantum coherence in electron transfer
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/1006.4053

## Experimental Techniques (for "How We Know")

### Classic Experiments

- **Meselson-Stahl** — DNA replication mechanism
  - https://www.nature.com/scitable/topicpage/meselson-and-stahl-dna-replication-is-473/

- **Nirenberg-Matthaei** — Cracking the genetic code with synthetic RNA
  - https://profiles.nlm.nih.gov/spotlight/jj/feature/doublehelix

- **Anfinsen** — Protein folding is determined by sequence
  - https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/1972/anfinsen/facts/

### Modern Techniques

- **Cryo-EM** — ribosome structure (recent revolution)
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/nmeth.4170

- **RNA-seq** — genome-wide transcription profiling
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/nrg2484

- **Ribosome profiling** — where ribosomes are translating
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/nprot.2012.086

- **ChIP-seq** — where transcription factors bind
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/nrg2641

- **CLIP-seq** — RNA-protein interactions
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/nprot.2014.084
