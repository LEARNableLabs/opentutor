# Oncology Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Cancer Biology Fundamentals
- Cell cycle dysregulation and checkpoint failures
- Oncogenes and tumor suppressor genes (TP53, RAS, MYC, BRCA1/2)
- DNA damage, repair mechanisms, and mutation accumulation
- Cellular immortalization and telomerase
- Apoptosis evasion

### 2. Tumor Microenvironment and Progression
- Hallmarks of cancer (Hanahan & Weinberg framework)
- Angiogenesis and VEGF signaling
- Invasion and metastasis cascade
- Cancer stem cells and heterogeneity
- Immune evasion and immunosuppression

### 3. Treatment Modalities
- **Surgery** — resection, margins, lymph node dissection
- **Radiation therapy** — mechanism, fractionation, side effects
- **Chemotherapy** — cytotoxic agents, combination therapy, scheduling
- **Targeted therapy** — kinase inhibitors, monoclonal antibodies
- **Immunotherapy** — checkpoint inhibitors, CAR-T, cancer vaccines

### 4. Drug Resistance and Recurrence
- Mechanisms of resistance (intrinsic vs acquired)
- Minimal residual disease
- Tumor evolution and clonal selection
- Liquid biopsies and monitoring

### 5. Clinical Practice
- Cancer staging (TNM system)
- Biomarkers and precision medicine
- Clinical trial design (phases I-IV)
- Survivorship and quality of life

## Pedagogical Notes

At **intermediate level**, students likely have:
- Basic biology (cell structure, DNA/RNA, protein synthesis)
- Some biochemistry (metabolism, signaling pathways)
- General understanding of disease mechanisms

They should be ready for:
- Mechanistic explanations of cancer hallmarks
- Introduction to clinical trial logic and drug classes
- Real-world treatment decision frameworks
- Basic molecular biology techniques (PCR, sequencing, immunohistochemistry)

We should **avoid**:
- Deep dive into structural biology of individual proteins
- Advanced pharmacokinetics and dosing calculations
- Statistical design of clinical trials (beyond conceptual understanding)
