# Risk Management and Actuarial Science — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Probability and Statistical Foundations
- Survival models and life tables
- Probability distributions (normal, exponential, Pareto, Weibull, gamma)
- Maximum likelihood estimation
- Hypothesis testing in insurance contexts

### 2. Life Contingencies
- Life tables and mortality modeling
- Present value of life insurance and annuities
- Premium calculation principles
- Reserves and policy values

### 3. Loss Models and Severity
- Individual and aggregate loss models
- Parametric distributions for insurance losses
- Policy modifications (deductibles, limits, coinsurance)
- Reinsurance structures

### 4. Frequency Models
- Poisson and negative binomial distributions
- (a,b,0) and (a,b,1) classes
- Zero-truncated and zero-modified models
- Exposure and rate-making

### 5. Risk Theory and Ruin
- Collective risk models
- Compound distributions
- Adjustment coefficient and ruin probability
- Lundberg's inequality

### 6. Credibility Theory
- Limited fluctuation credibility
- Greatest accuracy credibility (Bühlmann)
- Empirical Bayes methods
- Experience rating

### 7. Enterprise Risk Management (ERM)
- Risk identification and assessment
- Economic capital and solvency
- Risk governance frameworks
- Regulatory requirements (Solvency II, RBC)

### 8. Financial Mathematics
- Time value of money and interest theory
- Bond pricing and duration
- Portfolio immunization
- Asset-liability management

## Topic Scope for Intermediate Level

This curriculum assumes:
- **Prerequisites**: calculus (through multivariable), linear algebra, probability theory (through moment generating functions), basic statistics
- **Target**: Students preparing for professional exams (SOA/CAS track) or graduate study
- **Depth**: Rigorous but not measure-theoretic; balance theory with practical application
- **Applications**: Mix of life insurance, property/casualty, and enterprise risk management

## Content Strategy

The curriculum will:
1. Build from probability foundations to applied actuarial models
2. Interleave life and P&C content to show different applications
3. Emphasize computational skills (R/Python) alongside theory
4. Include real insurance datasets and case studies
5. Prepare students for professional exam material (SOA Exams P, FM, FAM, ALTAM)
6. Connect to modern topics (machine learning in insurance, climate risk)
