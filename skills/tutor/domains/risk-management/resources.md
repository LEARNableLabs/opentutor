# Risk Management and Actuarial Science — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks

- **Loss Models: From Data to Decisions** (Klugman, Panjer, Willmot)
  - Comprehensive coverage of loss distributions, frequency models, and aggregate losses
  - Standard reference for SOA exams
  - Excellent for lessons 12-19, 21-23
  - [Wiley page](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Loss+Models%3A+From+Data+to+Decisions%2C+5th+Edition-p-9781119523796)

- **Actuarial Mathematics for Life Contingent Risks** (Dickson, Hardy, Waters)
  - Modern treatment of life insurance mathematics
  - Clear explanations of APV, premiums, annuities, reserves
  - Perfect for lessons 7-11
  - [Cambridge University Press](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/actuarial-mathematics-for-life-contingent-risks/46DACC4BC2B666F3FE4DD0F5832BB88D)

- **Introduction to Ratemaking and Loss Reserving for Property and Casualty Insurance** (Brown, Gottlieb)
  - CAS-focused, practical approach to pricing
  - Strong on credibility theory (lessons 24-26)
  - [ACTEX Publications](https://www.actexmadriver.com/)

- **Risk Theory** (Kaas, Goovaerts, Dhaene, Denuit)
  - Rigorous treatment of ruin theory, adjustment coefficient
  - Advanced but essential for lessons 21-23
  - Springer publication

- **Foundations of Casualty Actuarial Science** (CAS)
  - Free online textbook from Casualty Actuarial Society
  - Comprehensive, exam-focused
  - [CAS website](https://www.casact.org/professional-education/education-programs)

### Course Materials

- **MIT OpenCourseWare: Probability and Statistics**
  - 18.05 Introduction to Probability and Statistics
  - Excellent for foundational review (lessons 1-6)
  - [MIT OCW](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/mathematics/18-05-introduction-to-probability-and-statistics-spring-2014/)

- **University of Connecticut Actuarial Science Program**
  - Publishes syllabi and practice problems
  - One of top-ranked actuarial programs in US
  - [UConn Actuarial](https://actuarial.uconn.edu/)

- **Heriot-Watt University Online Actuarial Science Materials**
  - Free resources for life contingencies and loss models
  - Well-structured, exam-aligned
  - [Heriot-Watt](https://www.hw.ac.uk/uk/schools/mathematical-computer-sciences/study-with-us/actuarial-science.htm)

### Books (Broader Context)

- **Against the Gods: The Remarkable Story of Risk** (Peter Bernstein)
  - History of risk and probability
  - Engaging, non-technical
  - Great motivation for lesson 1

- **The Black Swan** (Nassim Nicholas Taleb)
  - Critique of models, tail risk, uncertainty
  - Excellent for lesson 30 (emerging risks)

- **Thinking, Fast and Slow** (Daniel Kahneman)
  - Behavioral economics and probability biases
  - Relevant for understanding how people perceive risk

- **Fortune's Formula** (William Poundstone)
  - Kelly criterion, gambling, risk-taking
  - Connection to insurance pricing and capital allocation

## People (to follow/read)

### Classic Figures
- **Filip Lundberg** — pioneered ruin theory (lessons 21-22)
- **Hans Bühlmann** — credibility theory (lesson 25)
- **Jan Grandell** — risk theory and point processes
- **Harry Panjer** — Panjer recursion (lesson 18-19)

### Modern Researchers
- **Paul Embrechts** (ETH Zurich) — quantitative risk management, copulas
- **Thomas Mikosch** (University of Copenhagen) — heavy-tailed distributions
- **Edward Frees** (University of Wisconsin) — regression for actuaries, longitudinal data
- **Mary Hardy** (University of Waterloo) — financial economics for actuaries
- **Phelim Boyle** — options pricing, Monte Carlo methods in insurance

### Practitioners and Communicators
- **Dave Ingram** — enterprise risk management thought leader
- **Jim Guszcza** (Deloitte) — predictive analytics and behavioral economics for actuaries
- **Stuart Klugman** — co-author of Loss Models, former SOA research chair
