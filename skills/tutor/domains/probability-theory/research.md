# Probability Theory and Stochastic Processes — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### Probability Theory Foundations
- Probability spaces, measure theory basics
- Random variables and their distributions
- Expectation, variance, moments
- Conditional probability and independence
- Multivariate distributions
- Limit theorems (Law of Large Numbers, Central Limit Theorem)
- Convergence concepts (in probability, almost surely, in distribution)

### Discrete-Time Stochastic Processes
- Markov chains (discrete state space)
- Random walks
- Martingales and stopping times
- Renewal processes
- Branching processes (Galton-Watson trees)

### Continuous-Time Stochastic Processes
- Poisson processes
- Continuous-time Markov chains
- Birth-and-death processes
- Brownian motion (Wiener process)
- Stochastic calculus basics (Itô integral)

### Applications and Advanced Topics
- Queueing theory
- Stationary processes
- Ergodic theory basics
- Applications in finance, physics, biology

## Key Sources

### Textbooks
- **Brémaud, "Probability Theory and Stochastic Processes"** (Springer) — comprehensive intermediate-level text with complete proofs, organized in three parts: probability theory, discrete-time processes, continuous-time processes
- **Yates, "Probability and Stochastic Processes"** — upper-undergraduate/graduate level, covers Poisson process, renewal, Markov chains, martingales, Brownian motion
- **Papoulis & Pillai, "Probability, Random Variables and Stochastic Processes"** (4th ed.) — classic reference
- **Miller & Childers, "Probability and Random Processes"** (2nd ed.) — accessible intermediate treatment

### Online Courses
- **MIT 6.262 Discrete Stochastic Processes** — video lectures on Markov chains, random walks, renewal processes. Full playlist on YouTube.
- **MIT 18.445 Introduction to Stochastic Processes** — Markov chains, random walks, martingales, Galton-Watson trees. Lecture notes and problem sets available on OCW.
- **MIT 18.S096 Topics in Mathematics with Applications in Finance** — includes lectures on stochastic processes and Brownian motion
- **Stanford STATS217 Introduction to Stochastic Processes I** — covers Markov chains, Poisson processes, birth-and-death processes

### Interactive Tools
- **Seeing Theory** (seeing-theory.brown.edu) — award-winning interactive visualizations for probability distributions, compound probability, inference (built with D3.js)
- **Learn Math Class** — interactive probability function visualizers for 9 distributions, tree diagrams, Venn diagrams
- **esheets.io probability visualization tool** — dependent/conditional probability with balls-in-box model

## Level Assessment

For **intermediate** students, the curriculum should:
- Assume comfort with basic probability (sample spaces, conditional probability, basic distributions)
- Assume calculus proficiency (derivatives, integrals, sequences/series)
- Build measure-theoretic intuition without full rigor
- Emphasize computational skills alongside theory
- Connect to applications (random walks, queueing, finance)
- Include simulation/visualization exercises
