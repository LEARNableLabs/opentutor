# Astrophotography — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Night Sky Fundamentals
- Celestial mechanics and sky motion
- Light pollution and dark sky sites
- Moon phases and planetary visibility
- Sky planning tools and apps

### 2. Equipment and Setup
- Camera bodies (DSLR, mirrorless, dedicated astronomy cameras)
- Lenses and telescopes for different targets
- Mounts (fixed tripod, star tracker, equatorial mount)
- Accessories (intervalometers, dew heaters, power supplies)

### 3. Wide-Field Astrophotography
- Milky Way imaging
- Nightscape composition
- Single-exposure techniques
- Panoramas and time-lapses

### 4. Tracked Deep-Sky Imaging
- Star trackers and polar alignment
- Exposure time and ISO optimization
- Dithering and field rotation
- Guided imaging basics

### 5. Image Acquisition
- Exposure theory (light frames, darks, flats, bias)
- Focus techniques (Bahtinov mask, live view)
- Framing and composition for deep-sky targets
- Session planning and weather monitoring

### 6. Image Processing
- Stacking workflows (DeepSkyStacker, Siril)
- Stretch and histogram adjustments
- Noise reduction and sharpening
- Color calibration and gradient removal
- Advanced techniques (HDR composites, star reduction)

### 7. Specialized Techniques
- Narrowband imaging (Ha, OIII, SII)
- Lucky imaging for planets
- Time-lapse assembly
- Satellite and ISS imaging

## Key Sources

### Tutorials and Courses
- **Lonely Speck** (lonelyspeck.com) — comprehensive Milky Way and nightscape tutorials by Ian Norman
- **AstroBackyard** (astrobackyard.com) — beginner to intermediate deep-sky imaging by Trevor Jones
- **Nebula Photos** (YouTube) — detailed processing workflows
- **Cuiv the Lazy Geek** (YouTube) — equipment reviews and beginner-friendly tutorials
- **Peter Zelinka** (YouTube) — advanced PixInsight processing

### Books
- **"The Deep-sky Imaging Primer"** by Charles Bracken — comprehensive DSO workflow
- **"Astrophotography"** by Thierry Legault — technical reference for serious imagers
- **"The Astrophotography Manual"** by Chris Woodhouse — practical techniques
- **"Making Every Photon Count"** by Steve Richards — exposure optimization

### Software Resources
- **DeepSkyStacker** (free) — stacking and calibration
- **Siril** (free) — Linux/Mac stacking alternative
- **PixInsight** (commercial) — industry-standard processing
- **Photoshop/GIMP** — post-processing
- **Stellarium** (free) — planetarium software for planning
- **PhotoPills** (mobile) — planning tool for Milky Way shots

### Communities and Forums
- **CloudyNights** (cloudynights.com) — largest astrophotography forum
- **r/astrophotography** — active Reddit community with monthly challenges
- **Astrobin** (astrobin.com) — image sharing with EXIF data

### Scientific Resources
- **NASA/JPL** — reference images and data
- **Sky & Telescope Magazine** — technique articles
- **Astronomy Magazine** — beginner-friendly content
