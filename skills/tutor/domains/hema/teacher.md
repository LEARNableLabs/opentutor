# Historical European Martial Arts (HEMA) — Domain Teaching Config

## Exercise Types
This domain uses a mix of delivery formats:
- **Socratic questions** — 8 lessons (32%)
- **concept-focused mini-lessons** — 5 lessons (20%)
- **teach-back exercises (student explains)** — 4 lessons (16%)
- **review and consolidation sessions** — 4 lessons (16%)
- **real-world application challenges** — 3 lessons (12%)
- **curated resource exploration** — 1 lessons (4%)

Primary format: Socratic questions.

## Resource Types
This domain has strong coverage in: textbooks, video lectures, interactive tools, academic papers. Prioritize these when recommending resources.

## Difficulty Curve
Balanced progression — steady difficulty increase with regular consolidation.

Distribution: 44% accessible (1-2), 48% standard (3), 8% challenging (4-5).

Difficulty peaks:
- Day 14: "Can longsword principles work with a dagger?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 23: "Can you design a training drill from a treatise passage?" (difficulty 4)

## Domain Hooks
This field covers historical european martial arts (hema), with applications across theory and practice.

## Common Failure Modes
Students often struggle with the foundational concepts before building to more advanced material. Reinforce basics before progressing.
