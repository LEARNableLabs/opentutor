# Knot Theory — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks

- **Colin Adams, "The Knot Book"** (AMS, 2004)
  - Coverage: Visual introduction, knot tables, basic invariants, polynomials
  - Why it's good: Most accessible text at intermediate level. Excellent diagrams, humor, physical intuition. Start here.
  - Available: https://www.ams.org/books/knot/

- **Charles Livingston, "Knot Theory"** (Cambridge Mathematical Library, 1993)
  - Coverage: Comprehensive treatment through Seifert surfaces, Alexander polynomial, surgery
  - Why it's good: Standard intermediate text. Rigorous but readable. Good exercises.
  - Available: University libraries, Cambridge University Press

- **W.B.R. Lickorish, "An Introduction to Knot Theory"** (Springer GTM 175, 1997)
  - Coverage: Polynomials, 3-manifolds, surgery, HOMFLY
  - Why it's good: More advanced treatment, connects knots to 3-manifold topology
  - Available: Springer, university libraries

### Interactive Tools

- **KnotPlot** (Rob Scharein)
  - https://knotplot.com/
  - Visualization and manipulation software. Essential for building intuition.
  - Free academic license available

- **Knot Atlas**
  - https://katlas.org/
  - Computational knot theory resource. Compute invariants, explore knot tables
  - Wiki-style collaborative resource

- **KnotInfo**
  - https://knotinfo.math.indiana.edu/
  - Comprehensive database of knot invariants. Look up any standard knot.
  - Includes tables, invariant values, diagrams

- **SnapPy** (Culler, Dunfield, et al.)
  - https://snappy.computop.org/
  - Python software for hyperbolic 3-manifolds. Compute hyperbolic structures of knot complements.
  - Install via pip: `pip install snappy`

## Unexpected Connections (for wild cards)

### Biology
- **DNA Topology**: DNA forms knots and links in cells; topoisomerases are enzymes that unknot DNA
  - Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.7624 (knots in DNA)
  - Real-world application of knot theory to molecular biology

### Physics
- **Quantum Field Theory**: Chern-Simons theory gives topological invariants; Witten's approach to Jones polynomial
  - Paper: Witten, "Quantum Field Theory and the Jones Polynomial" (1989)
  - Deep connection between topology and physics

### Chemistry
- **Molecular Knots**: Chemists can synthesize knotted molecules (trefoil, figure-eight)
  - Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.05316 (molecular knots)
  - Physical realization of abstract mathematical objects

### Computer Science
- **Computational Complexity**: Computing Jones polynomial is #P-hard; unknot recognition complexity unknown
  - Shows deep connections between topology and theoretical CS
  - Open problem: is unknot recognition in P?

### Art & Design
- **Celtic Knots**: Historical knotwork in art (Book of Kells, Norse art)
  - Most are actually *links* (multiple components) not knots
  - Beautiful visualization of mathematical concepts in medieval art

### Statistical Mechanics
- **Potts Model**: Jones polynomial arises from partition function of Potts model
  - Connection to statistical physics
  - Shows how topology encodes physical information

## Historical Resources

- **Lord Kelvin's Vortex Atom Theory** (1867)
  - Historical motivation: atoms as knotted vortices in ether
  - Wrong physics, but launched knot theory as a field

- **Peter Guthrie Tait's Knot Tables** (1870s-80s)
  - First systematic classification of knots
  - Some errors, but impressive computational feat
