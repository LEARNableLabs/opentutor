## Common Failure Modes
1. **"Low clouds mean low pressure systems"**
   - **Why students get this wrong**: They conflate cloud altitude with pressure system type. Low clouds (stratus, cumulus) are about boundary layer processes; low pressure systems are synoptic-scale circulation patterns.
   - **How to correct**: Emphasize that low clouds can form in anticyclones (morning fog in high pressure), and high clouds (cirrus) routinely precede low pressure systems. Cloud *type* and pressure *pattern* are related but distinct.

2. **"Pressure determines everything — once I read the pressure I can forecast"**
