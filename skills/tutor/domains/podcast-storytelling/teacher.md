# Podcast and Audio Storytelling — Domain Teaching Config

## Exercise Types
This domain uses a mix of delivery formats:
- **concept-focused mini-lessons** — 10 lessons (37%)
- **Socratic questions** — 6 lessons (22%)
- **real-world application challenges** — 4 lessons (15%)
- **review and consolidation sessions** — 4 lessons (15%)
- **curated resource exploration** — 2 lessons (7%)
- **teach-back exercises (student explains)** — 1 lessons (4%)

Primary format: concept-focused mini-lessons.

## Resource Types
This domain has strong coverage in: textbooks, video lectures, interactive tools, academic papers, code repositories, online courses. Prioritize these when recommending resources.

## Difficulty Curve
Balanced progression — steady difficulty increase with regular consolidation.

Distribution: 41% accessible (1-2), 41% standard (3), 19% challenging (4-5).

Difficulty peaks:
- Day 11: "What ethical responsibilities do you have to your interview subjects?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 14: "When does music enhance a story vs. manipulate the listener?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 16: "How do you layer sounds without creating sonic mud?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 20: "How do EQ and compression make voices sound professional?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 22: "How do you create consistent sound across multiple speakers and locations?" (difficulty 4)

## Domain Hooks
These are fascinating tangents that can enrich understanding but shouldn't derail the core curriculum. Drop them in as "bonus" exploration for curious students.

## Common Failure Modes
Students often struggle with the foundational concepts before building to more advanced material. Reinforce basics before progressing.

## Vocabulary
Key terms for this domain: audio-first thinking, sonic narrative, intimacy of voice, cold opens, stakes establishment, curiosity gaps, story arc, tension building, narrative payoff, scene construction, showing vs telling, temporal pacing, sonic signposting, narrative bridges, musical transitions (and 65 more).
