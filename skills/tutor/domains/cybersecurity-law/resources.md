# Cybersecurity Law and Policy — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks and Treatises

- **Cybersecurity Law (2nd ed, 2020)** by Jeff Kosseff — Comprehensive, accessible overview for technical and legal audiences. Covers CFAA, breach notification, privacy, surveillance. Excellent for intermediate students.
  - Publisher: Wiley (https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Cybersecurity+Law%2C+2nd+Edition-p-9781119517207)

- **Information Privacy Law (7th ed, 2021)** by Daniel Solove & Paul Schwartz — Standard privacy law casebook. Detailed but readable. Strong on constitutional foundations and comparative approaches.
  - Publisher: Wolters Kluwer

- **Computer Crime Law (5th ed, 2022)** (West Academic) — Authoritative treatise on CFAA, wire fraud, identity theft. Written for practitioners but accessible.

- **Prosecuting Computer Crimes Manual** (DOJ) — Free government resource. Practical guidance on federal computer crime statutes.
  - https://www.justice.gov/criminal-ccips/prosecuting-computer-crimes

## Unexpected Connections (Cross-Discipline Links)

### Economics and Game Theory
- **Cyber insurance markets** — moral hazard, adverse selection, risk pricing
- **Bug bounty economics** — incentive design, disclosure timing games
- Reading: Gordon & Loeb model for security investment

### International Relations
- **Cyber norms and international law** — UN GGE, Tallinn Manual on cyber warfare
- **Digital sovereignty movements** — China's CSL, Russia's data localization, EU strategic autonomy
- Reading: Cyber Command's "defend forward" strategy documents

### Philosophy and Ethics
- **Privacy as autonomy vs privacy as secrecy** — philosophical foundations
- **Dual-use technology ethics** — security tools used for surveillance
- Reading: Helen Nissenbaum's "Privacy in Context"

### History
- **Clipper Chip and the first crypto wars (1990s)** — historical precedent for encryption backdoors
- **ECHELON and pre-9/11 surveillance** — context for FISA evolution
- Reading: Steven Levy's "Crypto"

### Sociology
- **Surveillance capitalism** — Shoshana Zuboff's work on data extraction
- **Privacy paradox** — stated preferences vs revealed preferences in data sharing
- Reading: Zuboff, "The Age of Surveillance Capitalism"

### Computer Science
- **Differential privacy** — formal privacy guarantees, Apple's implementation
- **Homomorphic encryption** — compute on encrypted data (GDPR by design?)
- **Blockchain and smart contracts** — GDPR right to erasure vs immutability
- Reading: Dwork & Roth, "The Algorithmic Foundations of Differential Privacy"
