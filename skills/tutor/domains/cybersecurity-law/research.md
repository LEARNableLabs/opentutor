# Cybersecurity Law and Policy — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Privacy Law and Regulation
- GDPR (EU General Data Protection Regulation)
- CCPA/CPRA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
- HIPAA (health data)
- COPPA (children's data)
- Sector-specific privacy frameworks

### 2. Data Breach Law
- Breach notification requirements (state and federal)
- Duty of care and negligence
- Class action litigation patterns
- FTC enforcement actions

### 3. Computer Crime and Fraud
- Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA)
- Wire Fraud Act
- Economic Espionage Act
- State computer crime statutes
- Unauthorized access and exceeding authorization

### 4. Surveillance and National Security
- Fourth Amendment and digital searches
- FISA and foreign intelligence surveillance
- Third-party doctrine
- Encryption backdoors debate
- Stored Communications Act (SCA)

### 5. Intellectual Property in Cyberspace
- DMCA (Digital Millennium Copyright Act)
- Reverse engineering defenses
- Trade secret protection
- Patent issues in security research

### 6. International Law and Jurisdiction
- Cross-border data transfers
- Mutual legal assistance treaties (MLATs)
- Cybercrime Convention (Budapest Convention)
- Conflict of laws
- Safe harbor and adequacy frameworks

### 7. Regulatory Compliance
- Financial sector (GLBA, PCI-DSS)
- Critical infrastructure (CISA)
- Industry-specific standards
- State laws and fragmentation

### 8. Incident Response and Legal Obligations
- Reporting obligations
- Legal holds and preservation
- Attorney-client privilege in IR
- Third-party liability

## Pedagogical Approach for Intermediate Level

At intermediate level, students likely have:
- Basic legal literacy OR technical security background (but not necessarily both)
- Understanding of fundamental privacy concepts
- Awareness of major data breaches and incidents

This curriculum balances:
- Legal frameworks and statutory interpretation
- Policy debates and competing interests
- Practical compliance and risk management
- International and comparative perspectives
- Real-world case studies and incidents

Focus on WHY laws exist, HOW they interact, and WHEN they apply — not just WHAT they say.
