# Philosophy of Science — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Foundations & Methodology
- Scientific method and its justification
- Induction vs. deduction
- Observation and theory-ladenness
- Demarcation problem (science vs. pseudoscience)

### 2. Major Schools of Thought
- Logical positivism (Vienna Circle)
- Critical rationalism (Popper)
- Historical/sociological turn (Kuhn, Lakatos, Feyerabend)
- Scientific realism vs. instrumentalism
- Naturalized epistemology

### 3. Confirmation & Evidence
- Bayesian confirmation theory
- Hypothesis testing
- Underdetermination of theory by evidence
- Problem of induction (Hume)

### 4. Scientific Change & Progress
- Paradigm shifts (Kuhn)
- Research programmes (Lakatos)
- Scientific revolutions
- Progress vs. relativism debate

### 5. Explanation & Laws
- Covering law model (Hempel)
- Causal explanation
- Laws of nature
- Idealization and models

### 6. Contemporary Issues
- Values in science
- Science and society
- Reduction and emergence
- Philosophy of specific sciences (physics, biology, social sciences)
