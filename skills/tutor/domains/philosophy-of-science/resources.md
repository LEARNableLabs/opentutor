# Philosophy of Science — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Core Textbooks

- **Peter Godfrey-Smith, *Theory and Reality: An Introduction to the Philosophy of Science*** (2003)
  - Best introductory textbook for intermediate learners. Clear, engaging, historically grounded. Covers all major schools.
  - ISBN: 978-0226300634
  - https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/chicago/T/bo3640847.html

- **Alex Rosenberg, *Philosophy of Science: A Contemporary Introduction*** (4th ed., 2020)
  - Comprehensive, analytically rigorous, covers contemporary debates. Good for students who want formalism.
  - ISBN: 978-0415891776
  - https://www.routledge.com/Philosophy-of-Science-A-Contemporary-Introduction/Rosenberg-McIntyre/p/book/9780367196578

- **James Ladyman, *Understanding Philosophy of Science*** (2001)
  - Excellent on scientific realism and the semantic view of theories. More advanced sections for rabbit holes.
  - ISBN: 978-0415221573
  - https://www.routledge.com/Understanding-Philosophy-of-Science/Ladyman/p/book/9780415221573

- **Alan Chalmers, *What Is This Thing Called Science?*** (4th ed., 2013)
  - Accessible, question-driven approach. Good for students coming from science backgrounds.
  - ISBN: 978-0335264827
  - https://www.mheducation.co.uk/what-is-this-thing-called-science-9780335264827-emea

### Canonical Primary Texts (excerpts recommended)

- **Karl Popper, *The Logic of Scientific Discovery*** (1959)
  - Chapters on falsificationism and demarcation. Dense but essential for understanding Popper.
  - https://archive.org/details/the-logic-of-scientific-discovery

- **Thomas Kuhn, *The Structure of Scientific Revolutions*** (1962)
  - Chapters on normal science, paradigms, and revolutions. Most influential work in 20th-century philosophy of science.
  - https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/chicago/S/bo13179781.html

- **Carl Hempel, *Philosophy of Natural Science*** (1966)
  - Short, clear introduction to logical empiricist views. Covering law model, confirmation, explanation.
  - https://archive.org/details/philosophyofnatu00hemp

- **W.V.O. Quine, "Two Dogmas of Empiricism"** (1951)
  - Classic essay critiquing the analytic/synthetic distinction. Short, foundational.
  - https://www.ditext.com/quine/quine.html

- **Imre Lakatos, *The Methodology of Scientific Research Programmes*** (1978)
  - Sophisticated defense of falsificationism via "research programmes." For advanced rabbit holes.
  - https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/methodology-of-scientific-research-programmes/FC9F24AAAA10C22E3B91E84630DAEF39

## People to Follow (Contemporary Philosophers of Science)

### Major Figures (Historical)

- **Karl Popper** (1902-1994) — Falsificationism, critical rationalism
- **Thomas Kuhn** (1922-1996) — Paradigms, scientific revolutions
- **Carl Hempel** (1905-1997) — Logical empiricism, covering law model
- **W.V.O. Quine** (1908-2000) — Holism, naturalized epistemology
- **Imre Lakatos** (1922-1974) — Research programmes
- **Paul Feyerabend** (1924-1994) — Epistemological anarchism

## Unexpected Connections (for wild cards)

- **Science and Art**: How does scientific representation relate to artistic representation? See work by Catherine Elgin and Roman Frigg on models.

- **Science Fiction as Philosophy**: Thought experiments in SF often explore philosophy of science themes. See *The Three-Body Problem* (Liu Cixin) for underdetermination, *Contact* (Sagan) for SETI and evidence.

- **Magic and Science**: Historical connections between alchemy and chemistry, astrology and astronomy. Shows how demarcation has evolved. See work by historians Lawrence Principe and William Newman.

- **Indigenous Knowledge Systems**: How do non-Western knowledge systems relate to Western science? Challenges universalist assumptions. See work by Sandra Harding and Helen Verran.

- **Music and Mathematical Platonism**: The philosophy of mathematics (realism vs. formalism) parallels debates in philosophy of science. See work by Mark Balaguer.

- **Legal Reasoning and Scientific Inference**: How courts evaluate scientific evidence. Daubert standard, expert testimony. Philosophy of science applied to law. See Susan Haack's work.
