# Translation Studies — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### Theoretical Foundations
- **Translation theory and history** — evolution from equivalence-based approaches (Nida, Catford) to functionalist and cultural turns (Vermeer, Nord, Snell-Hornby)
- **Descriptive Translation Studies (DTS)** — Toury's norms, Holmes' map, polysystem theory (Even-Zohar)
- **Linguistic approaches** — contrastive analysis, equivalence frameworks, semantic vs. pragmatic translation
- **Cultural and sociological turns** — ideology, power, postcolonial translation, translation as rewriting

### Applied Translation
- **Literary translation** — style, voice, poeticity, narrative techniques
- **Technical and specialized translation** — terminology management, subject-matter expertise, consistency
- **Audiovisual translation** — subtitling, dubbing, accessibility (SDH, audio description)
- **Localization** — software, websites, games, multimedia, cultural adaptation beyond text

### Professional Practice
- **Translation technologies** — CAT tools (Trados, MemoQ), TM systems, MT post-editing
- **Translation competence** — PACTE model, translator training, decision-making processes
- **Quality assessment** — error typologies, revision strategies, metrics (DQF, MQM)
- **Ethics and professional standards** — confidentiality, cultural mediation, translator visibility

## Key Sources Discovered

### University Syllabi
- **Middlebury Institute (MIIS)** — Overview of Translation Studies (concepts, methods, metalanguage)
- **UT Dallas** — Cross-cultural communication and negotiation techniques
- **UNLV** — Theory of Translation (comparative analysis of multiple translations)

### Textbooks and Portals
- **Jeremy Munday's "Introducing Translation Studies"** — The definitive introductory textbook used on courses worldwide, with extensive online resources (quizzes, flashcards, videos, journal articles)
- **Routledge Translation Studies Portal** — Interactive companion websites, glossaries, podcasts, videos by featured authors
- **"Translating Texts" by Baer & Mellinger** — Coursebook on translation and text formation

### Video Lectures
- **Universitat Rovira i Virgili** — PhD-level lectures by Anthony Pym ("Exploring translation theories"), Franz Pöchhacker ("Developing Interpreting Studies"), and other scholars
- **transLectures project** — EU-funded tools for automatic transcription and translation of educational videos

### Tools and Platforms
- **Class Central** — 770+ free and certified Translation Studies online courses
- **Wordly, Microsoft Translator for Education** — Live translation and captions for education
- **GlobalizeEd** — Multimodal translation system preserving speaker identity
