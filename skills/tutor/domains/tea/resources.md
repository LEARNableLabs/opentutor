# Tea — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Books

- **"Tea: Bioactivity and Therapeutic Potential"** (Victor R. Preedy, ed.) — comprehensive academic overview of tea chemistry, processing, and health effects. Intermediate to advanced level. Best for chemistry modules.
  - Publisher: Taylor & Francis
  - Covers: polyphenol chemistry, processing biochemistry, cultivar variation

- **"The Tea Enthusiast's Handbook"** (Mary Lou Heiss & Robert J. Heiss) — accessible science + practical brewing guide. Perfect intermediate resource bridging theory and practice.
  - https://teaguy.com/handbook
  - Covers: all processing types, brewing parameters, tasting notes

- **"The Book of Tea"** (Kakuzō Okakura) — cultural and philosophical foundations. Historical context for gongfu tradition.
  - Public domain: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/769
  - Covers: tea aesthetics, Japanese tea ceremony, cultural significance

- **"Tea Processing and Manufacture"** (Y.H. Hui & E.Ö. Evranuz, eds.) — technical processing manual. Industrial perspective on chemistry and engineering.
  - Covers: large-scale processing, quality control, chemical analysis

### Academic Resources

- **MIT OpenCourseWare: Chemistry of Food and Cooking** — includes module on enzymatic browning and oxidation (applicable to tea processing)
  - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/chemistry/
  - Relevant sections: oxidation-reduction, enzyme kinetics, flavor chemistry

- **Taiwan Tea Research and Extension Station (TRES)** — government research publications on cultivar development and processing science
  - https://www.tres.gov.tw/en
  - Database of tea cultivars with chemical profiles

- **Journal of Food Science and Technology** — peer-reviewed articles on tea chemistry and processing
  - https://link.springer.com/journal/13197
  - Search: "tea oxidation", "tea fermentation", "tea cultivar"

## Supplementary (for engagement)

### Videos & Channels

- **Mei Leaf (Don Mei)** — educational YouTube channel with deep dives on tea science, cultivars, and gongfu technique
  - https://www.youtube.com/c/MeiLeaf
  - Best videos: "Tea Oxidation Explained", "Gongfu Brewing Masterclass", "Cultivar Deep Dives"
  - Level: intermediate to advanced, scientifically rigorous

- **Tea DB** — visual database of teas with tasting notes and processing information
  - https://tea.db.online
  - Interactive sensory wheel and flavor mapping

- **The Tea Crane** — processing documentaries from Taiwan and China
  - https://www.youtube.com/c/TheTeaCrane
  - Best for: visual learning of hand-processing techniques

- **Global Tea Hut** — monthly journal with articles on tea culture, science, and practice
  - https://globalteahut.org
  - Long-form essays, some academic, some cultural

### Interactive Tools

- **Tea Flavor Wheel** — interactive sensory analysis tool
  - https://www.specialtyteainstitute.org/flavor-wheel
  - Based on professional cupping protocols

- **Brewing Calculator** — parameter calculator for leaf-to-water ratios by tea type
  - https://brewingcalculator.tea.tools
  - Adjusts for gongfu vs. Western brewing

- **Tea Timer App** — tracks infusion times and temperature by tea category
  - Available on iOS/Android
  - Useful for lesson 22 (infusion timing)

### Hands-On Practice

- **Specialty Tea Institute (STI)** — professional certification program with tasting kits and sensory training
  - https://www.specialtyteainstitute.org
  - Level 1-3 certifications cover cultivars, processing, sensory evaluation

- **Global Tea Championship** — competition protocols and judging rubrics
  - https://www.globalteachampionship.com
  - Use their sensory evaluation forms for practice

- **Sample Sets** — recommended vendors for purchasing cultivar comparison sets:
  - What-Cha: https://what-cha.com (global tea selection)
  - Yunnan Sourcing: https://yunnansourcing.com (Chinese teas, puerh focus)
  - Mei Leaf: https://meileaf.com (curated, educational sets)
  - Taiwan Tea Crafts: https://taiwanteacrafts.com (Taiwanese oolongs and cultivars)

## Code & Data

- **Tea Chemistry Database** — open dataset of chemical compounds by tea type
  - https://github.com/tea-data/chemistry-db
  - CSV format, includes catechins, theaflavins, amino acids

- **Sensory Analysis Toolkit** — Python scripts for analyzing tea tasting data
  - https://github.com/sensory-tea/analysis-tools
  - Statistical methods for flavor profiling

## Unexpected Connections

### Cross-Discipline Links

- **Wine Chemistry** — many parallel concepts: terroir, oxidation management, sensory vocabulary, aging chemistry
  - Resource: UC Davis Viticulture & Enology program materials
  - Crossover: https://wineserver.ucdavis.edu

- **Coffee Science** — roasting parallels tea firing; extraction science similarities
  - Resource: Coffee Chemistry (MIT OpenCourseWare)
  - Crossover: SCA brewing control chart adapted for tea

- **Fermentation Science** — puerh aging shares mechanisms with cheese, kimchi, kombucha
  - Resource: Sandor Katz, "The Art of Fermentation"
  - Crossover: microbial ecology, flavor development

- **Mindfulness Research** — neuroscience of L-theanine and meditative states
  - Resource: "The Neuroscience of Mindfulness Meditation" (Tang et al.)
  - Crossover: tea ceremony as contemplative practice

- **Climate Science** — tea terroir as case study in agricultural climate impacts
  - Resource: IPCC agricultural impact reports
  - Crossover: shifting tea-growing regions, adaptation strategies

## Wild Cards (for rabbit holes)

- **Tea Genetics Project** — genome sequencing of *Camellia sinensis*, comparative genomics
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/tea-genome
  - For students interested in molecular biology

- **Historical Trade Routes** — Tea Horse Road, Silk Road economics, opium trade connections
  - Documentary: "Tea, the Story of a Leaf" (PBS)

- **Mycotoxin Testing** — quality control science for aged teas
  - Relevant for lesson 25, safety-conscious students

- **Ethnobotany** — traditional tea medicine systems (Tibetan, Chinese, Ayurvedic)
  - Cultural context for bioactive compounds

- **Tea and Psychedelics** — comparative neurochemistry (L-theanine vs. psilocybin on consciousness)
  - Speculative but fascinating for neuroscience-oriented students
