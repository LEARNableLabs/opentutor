# Tea — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Cultivar Science & Botany
- *Camellia sinensis* var. *sinensis* vs. var. *assamica* taxonomy
- Genetic diversity and modern breeding programs
- Terroir factors: altitude, soil chemistry, climate
- Phenolic compound expression by cultivar

### 2. Processing Chemistry
- Enzymatic oxidation and polyphenol oxidase activity
- Six major processing categories: white, green, yellow, oolong, black, dark
- Fermentation chemistry in dark teas (microbial vs. enzymatic)
- Kill-green (sha qing) techniques and their chemical effects
- Rolling, withering, firing parameters

### 3. Sensory Science
- Flavor compound chemistry (catechins, theaflavins, amino acids)
- Aromatic compound classes (terpenes, aldehydes, alcohols)
- Umami and L-theanine
- Astringency mechanisms (tannin-protein binding)

### 4. Gongfu Practice & Technique
- Vessel selection and heat retention
- Water temperature control and mineral content
- Leaf-to-water ratios by processing type
- Infusion timing and extraction curves
- Sensory evaluation methodology

### 5. Quality Assessment
- Grading systems (Chinese, Japanese, Indian standards)
- Visual, olfactory, and gustatory assessment
- Processing defects and their markers
- Storage and aging effects
