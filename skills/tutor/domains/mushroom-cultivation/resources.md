# Mushroom Cultivation — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks

- **Stamets, P. (2000). _Growing Gourmet and Medicinal Mushrooms_** — The definitive cultivation guide. Comprehensive substrate formulas, species-specific protocols, and troubleshooting. Essential for intermediate students. Covers 25+ species with detailed environmental parameters. ISBN: 978-1580081757  
  Available: [Fungi Perfecti](https://fungi.com/products/growing-gourmet-and-medicinal-mushrooms)

- **Chang, S.T. & Miles, P.G. (2004). _Mushrooms: Cultivation, Nutritional Value, Medicinal Effect, and Environmental Impact_** — Academic foundation for substrate chemistry, fungal physiology, and commercial systems. More technical than Stamets; excellent for understanding *why* protocols work. ISBN: 978-0849310430

- **Cotter, T. (2014). _Organic Mushroom Farming and Mycoremediation_** — Focus on outdoor cultivation, log culture, and large-scale production. Great for students interested in commercial scaling or regenerative agriculture. ISBN: 978-1603584555  
  Available: [Chelsea Green Publishing](https://www.chelseagreen.com/product/organic-mushroom-farming-and-mycoremediation/)

### University Extension Resources

- **Penn State Extension — Mushroom Cultivation**  
  https://extension.psu.edu/mushroom-cultivation  
  Free fact sheets on oyster, shiitake, and commercial cultivation. Excellent for species-specific parameters and troubleshooting guides.

- **Cornell Small Farms Program — Mushroom Production**  
  https://smallfarms.cornell.edu/projects/mushrooms/  
  Business planning guides, economics, and scaling advice for farmers entering mushroom production.

- **North Carolina State Extension — Shiitake Production**  
  https://content.ces.ncsu.edu/growing-shiitake-mushrooms  
  Detailed log cultivation and fruiting protocols specific to shiitake.

### Academic Papers (Open Access)

- **Sánchez, C. (2010). "Cultivation of Pleurotus ostreatus and other edible mushrooms." _Applied Microbiology and Biotechnology_**  
  https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7022788/  
  Review of oyster mushroom substrate options, supplementation strategies, and yield optimization.

- **Grimm, D. & Wösten, H.A.B. (2018). "Mushroom cultivation in the circular economy." _Applied Microbiology and Biotechnology_**  
  https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6274837/  
  Substrate waste streams, sustainability metrics, and agricultural waste conversion.

## Supplementary (for engagement)

### Video Channels

- **FreshCap Mushrooms (YouTube)**  
  https://www.youtube.com/c/FreshCapMushrooms  
  High-quality tutorials on substrate prep, sterile technique, and fruiting chamber design. Excellent production values, beginner-friendly but technically accurate. Recommended episodes: "How to Pasteurize Straw," "Building a Fruiting Chamber," "Grain Spawn Master Class."

- **Southwest Mushrooms (YouTube)**  
  https://www.youtube.com/c/SouthwestMushrooms  
  Focuses on shiitake log cultivation and outdoor methods. Great for students interested in low-tech, outdoor systems.

- **Mushroom Cultivation (YouTube — channel by "Arvin Fortuna")**  
  https://www.youtube.com/c/MushroomCultivation  
  Commercial-scale techniques, including bag-filling, sterilization systems, and climate-controlled fruiting rooms. Good for advanced students considering scaling.

### Interactive Tools

- **Mushroom Substrate Calculator**  
  https://grocycle.com/mushroom-substrate-calculator/  
  Calculate spawn-to-substrate ratios, moisture content, and supplementation amounts. Useful for lesson planning and experimentation.

- **SensorPush / Govee Environmental Monitors**  
  Smart temp/humidity sensors with data logging. Essential for students building fruiting chambers (lesson 16). Allows tracking of environmental parameters over time.  
  SensorPush: https://www.sensorpush.com/  
  Govee: https://us.govee.com/products/thermometer-hygrometer

- **CO₂ Meters**  
  https://www.co2meter.com/  
  Desktop CO₂ monitors (300-5000 ppm range) for diagnosing FAE issues and pinning failures. Recommended for intermediate students serious about optimization.

### Online Communities

- **Shroomery.org Forums**  
  https://www.shroomery.org/forums/  
  Largest mushroom cultivation forum. Extensive troubleshooting threads, cultivation logs, and species-specific subforums. Search before asking — most questions already answered. Key sections: "Cultivation" (general), "Gourmet & Medicinal" (food mushrooms).

- **r/MushroomGrowers (Reddit)**  
  https://www.reddit.com/r/MushroomGrowers/  
  Active community with photo-based troubleshooting. Good for contamination ID and real-time advice. Less technical depth than Shroomery but faster responses.

- **The Mushroom Cultivation Discord**  
  Real-time chat with experienced growers. Invite links rotate; search "mushroom cultivation discord" or ask in r/MushroomGrowers.

### Code & Data

- **Mushroom Cultivation Open Data**  
  https://github.com/topics/mushroom-cultivation  
  GitHub repos with environmental monitoring code (Arduino/Raspberry Pi), substrate calculators, and yield tracking tools. Good for students with programming background who want to automate.

- **OpenAg MushBox**  
  https://github.com/OpenAgInitiative/  
  MIT Media Lab's open-source mushroom growing chamber with environmental control. Advanced project for students interested in automation.

## People (researchers, practitioners to follow)

- **Paul Stamets** — Mycologist, author, founder of Fungi Perfecti. Pioneer in medicinal mushroom cultivation and mycomaterials. TED talks and books are accessible entry points.  
  Website: https://fungi.com/  
  TED Talk: "6 Ways Mushrooms Can Save the World"

- **Tradd Cotter** — Founder of Mushroom Mountain. Focus on outdoor cultivation, medicinal species, and mycoremediation. Author of _Organic Mushroom Farming and Mycoremediation_.  
  Website: https://mushroommountain.com/

- **William Padilla-Brown** — Urban mushroom cultivation advocate, pink oyster specialist. Focuses on low-tech methods accessible to beginners. Strong community-building ethos.  
  Instagram: @mycosymbiosis  
  YouTube: Various guest appearances on cultivation channels

- **Giuliana Furci** — Founder of Fungi Foundation, first female mycologist in Chile. Focuses on fungal conservation and biodiversity. Inspiring for students interested in ecological applications.  
  Website: https://fungi.foundation/

- **Merlin Sheldrake** — Author of _Entangled Life_. Not a cultivation expert, but excellent for big-picture fungal biology and ecological thinking. His book is perfect rabbit-hole material for curious students.  
  Book: _Entangled Life: How Fungi Make Our Worlds, Change Our Minds & Shape Our Futures_

## Unexpected Connections (cross-discipline links for wild cards)

- **Mycelium Leather (Fashion Tech)**  
  MycoWorks: https://www.mycoworks.com/  
  Bolt Threads (Mylo): https://www.boltthreads.com/technology/mylo/  
  Same substrate science and environmental control skills applied to biofabrication instead of food. Show students how cultivation knowledge transfers to materials engineering.

- **Ecovative (Mycelium Packaging & Building Materials)**  
  https://www.ecovative.com/  
  Growing mycelium as sustainable alternative to styrofoam and insulation. Excellent example of circular economy and bioengineering applications.

- **NASA Mushroom Research (Space Cultivation)**  
  https://www.nasa.gov/mission_pages/station/research/experiments/2382.html  
  Experiments growing mushrooms on ISS for waste recycling and fresh food production. Challenges with CO₂ management in microgravity. Fascinating edge case for environmental control principles.

- **Psilocybin Clinical Trials (Medical Research)**  
  Johns Hopkins Center for Psychedelic Research: https://hopkinspsychedelic.org/  
  Imperial College London: https://www.imperial.ac.uk/psychedelic-research-centre/  
  Growing medical interest in psilocybin mushrooms for depression, PTSD, addiction. Cultivation skills are foundation for emerging pharmaceutical/therapeutic industry.

- **Turkey Tail Polysaccharide Research (Cancer Adjuvant)**  
  https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4684115/  
  *Trametes versicolor* (turkey tail) PSK/PSP extracts used in cancer therapy in Japan. Show students that "medicinal mushroom" can mean FDA-relevant therapeutics, not just wellness supplements.

- **Wood Wide Web & Mycorrhizal Networks (Ecology)**  
  Suzanne Simard's research on forest mycorrhizal networks: https://suzannesimard.com/  
  Different from saprophytic mushrooms students are cultivating, but shows broader fungal ecological roles. Great for engagement when teaching fungal nutrition basics.

- **Bioremediation & Mycofiltration (Environmental Engineering)**  
  Using mushrooms to break down pollutants (oil spills, pesticides, heavy metals). Paul Stamets' work on mycorestoration.  
  Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XI5frPV58tY  
  Drop when teaching enzymatic breakdown of lignocellulose — same enzymes can target industrial pollutants.

## Courses & Workshops

  Search "mycological society [your city/state]" — most regions have clubs offering foraging trips, cultivation workshops, and networking. Great for finding local mentors and mushroom-growing community.
