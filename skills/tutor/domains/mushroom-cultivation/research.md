# Mushroom Cultivation — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Substrate Science
- **Substrate composition** — nutritional requirements (C:N ratio, minerals, vitamins), lignocellulose breakdown, supplementation strategies
- **Substrate preparation** — pasteurization vs. sterilization, moisture content optimization, pH adjustment
- **Spawn production** — grain spawn, sawdust spawn, liquid culture techniques
- **Substrate types** — straw, hardwood, manure-based, agricultural waste, casing layers

### 2. Environmental Control & Fruiting
- **Pinning triggers** — temperature shifts, light exposure, evaporation, CO₂ drops
- **Fruiting chamber design** — humidity control (85-95%), FAE (fresh air exchange), light cycles
- **Species-specific parameters** — oyster vs. shiitake vs. lion's mane vs. reishi requirements
- **Yield optimization** — flush management, biological efficiency calculations, contamination prevention

### 3. Mycelial Biology
- **Life cycle** — spore → germination → mycelium → primordia → fruiting body
- **Colonization dynamics** — growth rates, competitive exclusion, mycelial morphology
- **Metabolic processes** — enzymatic breakdown, secondary metabolite production

### 4. Practical Cultivation Systems
- **Low-tech methods** — log cultivation, straw rolls, bucket tek
- **Commercial systems** — bag culture, shelf systems, bottle cultivation
- **Monitoring & troubleshooting** — contamination ID (Trichoderma, Aspergillus, bacteria), environmental diagnostics

## Key Sources

### Academic
- **Stamets, P. (2000). _Growing Gourmet and Medicinal Mushrooms_** — comprehensive cultivation guide covering substrate formulas, environmental parameters, species-specific protocols
- **Chang, S.T. & Miles, P.G. (2004). _Mushrooms: Cultivation, Nutritional Value, Medicinal Effect, and Environmental Impact_** — academic foundation for substrate chemistry and fungal physiology
- **Kurtzman, R.H. (1997). "Mushroom Cultivation in the US" (_Mycologia_)** — commercial systems overview

### Educational Resources
- **Shroomery.org** — community wiki with substrate recipes, troubleshooting guides, cultivation logs
- **FreshCap Mushrooms (YouTube)** — video tutorials on sterile technique, substrate prep, fruiting chamber design
- **MycoWorks/Ecovative research** — mycelial material science applications (cross-disciplinary inspiration)
- **Cornell Mushroom Program** — extension resources, fact sheets on commercial cultivation

### Available Resources
- **Online courses** — Fungi Academy, North Spore courses, Mushroom Mountain workshops
- **Software tools** — environmental monitors (Govee, SensorPush), CO₂ meters, contamination image databases
- **Interactive** — substrate calculator tools, species comparison charts, troubleshooting flowcharts
