# Research Summary — Optical Fiber Communications

## Major Subtopics

1. **Light Propagation in Optical Fibers**
   - Ray optics and mode theory
   - Single-mode vs. multi-mode fibers
   - Fiber materials and fabrication
   - Attenuation and dispersion mechanisms

2. **Optical Components**
   - Light sources (LEDs, laser diodes, VCSELs)
   - Photodetectors (PIN, APD)
   - Optical amplifiers (EDFA, Raman, SOA)
   - Passive components (couplers, filters, isolators)

3. **Modulation and Multiplexing**
   - Intensity modulation / direct detection
   - Wavelength division multiplexing (WDM, DWDM)
   - Time and polarization division multiplexing
   - Digital modulation formats

4. **System Design and Performance**
   - Link power budget and rise time budget
   - Receiver sensitivity and noise
   - Bit error rate analysis
   - System limitations and trade-offs

5. **Advanced Topics**
   - Coherent detection and DSP
   - Nonlinear effects in fibers
   - Optical networks and switching
   - Next-generation systems (space-division multiplexing, quantum)

## Key Sources

### Textbooks
- **Gerd Keiser, "Optical Fiber Communications" (5th ed., McGraw-Hill)** — comprehensive coverage from fundamentals to systems, intermediate level
- **Govind P. Agrawal, "Fiber-Optic Communication Systems" (5th ed., Wiley)** — deeper dive into system design and advanced topics
- **John M. Senior, "Optical Fiber Communications: Principles and Practice" (3rd ed., Pearson)** — accessible introduction with good examples

### Academic Resources
- **MIT OpenCourseWare 6.011 - Introduction to Communication, Control, and Signal Processing** — includes optical communication modules
- **Stanford EE 348 - Fiber Optic Communication** — graduate-level course materials
- **IEEE Photonics Society Educational Resources** — tutorials, webinars, and short courses
- **OSA (Optica) tutorials and journals** — current research and review articles

### Online Courses and Videos
- **Coursera: "Optical Engineering" specialization** (University of Colorado Boulder)

- **YouTube: The Photonics Spotlight** — industry updates and technology deep-dives
