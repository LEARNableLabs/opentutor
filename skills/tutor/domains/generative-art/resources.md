# Generative and Computational Art — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Books

- **The Nature of Code** by Daniel Shiffman (https://natureofcode.com/book/) — foundational text on simulation, forces, particles, agents, cellular automata. Free online. Essential for intermediate students.

- **Generative Design** by Benedikt Gross, Hartmut Bohnacker, Julia Laub, Claudius Lazzeroni (http://www.generative-gestaltung.de/2/) — comprehensive Processing/p5.js examples across color, form, type, image processing. Great visual reference.

- **The Algorithmic Beauty of Plants** by Przemysław Prusinkiewicz and Aristid Lindenmayer (http://algorithmicbotany.org/papers/abop/abop.pdf) — definitive text on L-systems. Dense but beautifully illustrated.

- **Programming Design Systems** by Rune Madsen (https://programmingdesignsystems.com/) — focuses on color, shape, and composition from a design perspective. Excellent for color theory.

### Courses & Lecture Series

- **MIT OpenCourseWare: Introduction to Computational Thinking** — computational approach to problem-solving with visual examples

- **The Coding Train (Daniel Shiffman)** (https://thecodingtrain.com/) — hundreds of video tutorials on p5.js, Processing, and creative coding. Best resource for visual learners. See especially "Coding Challenges" and "Nature of Code" series.

- **ITP/IMA Courses at NYU** — syllabi often publicly available, covering creative coding, generative systems, and computational art

- **School for Poetic Computation** (https://sfpc.study/) — artist-led workshops and courses, emphasizes poetic and experimental approaches

### Academic Papers (for rabbit holes)

- **"Flocks, Herds, and Schools: A Distributed Behavioral Model"** by Craig Reynolds (1987) — foundational work on autonomous agents and flocking

- **"The Chemical Basis of Morphogenesis"** by Alan Turing (1952) — reaction-diffusion systems for pattern formation

- **"Toward a Global Theory of Form"** by Christopher Alexander — pattern language and generative systems in architecture/design

## Supplementary (for engagement)

### Interactive Tools & Platforms

- **p5.js Web Editor** (https://editor.p5js.org/) — instant browser-based coding environment, no setup required. Best for beginners and quick experiments.

- **OpenProcessing** (https://openprocessing.org/) — community platform for sharing and remixing sketches. Thousands of examples to study and fork.

- **Shadertoy** (https://www.shadertoy.com/) — fragment shader playground for GLSL. Advanced, but incredible for performance and visual effects.

- **Cables.gl** (https://cables.gl/) — node-based visual programming for WebGL. Great for students who prefer visual workflows.

- **Hydra** (https://hydra.ojack.xyz/) — live-coding video synthesis in the browser. Immediate visual feedback, very playful.

- **TouchDesigner** (https://derivative.ca/) — node-based environment for real-time generative visuals. Industry-standard for installations and live visuals.

- **CodePen** (https://codepen.io/search/pens?q=generative+art) — web playground with many generative art examples in p5.js, canvas, SVG

### Video Channels & Series

- **The Coding Train** (https://www.youtube.com/c/TheCodingTrain) — Daniel Shiffman's extensive tutorials on p5.js, Processing, machine learning for creative coding

- **Shiffman's Nature of Code videos** (https://thecodingtrain.com/tracks/the-nature-of-code-2) — video companion to the book

- **Sebastian Lague** (https://www.youtube.com/c/SebastianLague) — procedural generation, simulation, beautiful explanations of algorithms

- **Reducible** (https://www.youtube.com/c/Reducible) — computer science concepts with stunning visualizations (great for recursion, algorithms)

- **3Blue1Brown** (https://www.youtube.com/c/3blue1brown) — math visualizations that inspire generative thinking (Fourier transforms, differential equations)

### Code Repositories & Examples

- **Matt DesLauriers' workshop repos** (https://github.com/mattdesl) — canvas-sketch framework, tutorials on generative art for print/web

- **Generative Artistry tutorials** (https://generative-artistry.com/) — step-by-step recreations of classic generative algorithms (Schotter, Joy Division, Tiled Lines)

- **Tyler Hobbs' essays** (https://tylerxhobbs.com/essays) — deep writing on flow fields, color, aesthetics in generative art

- **Inconvergent (Anders Hoff)** (https://inconvergent.net/) — technical blog on differential growth, sand painting, algorithmic line work

- **Creative Applications Network** (https://www.creativeapplications.net/) — showcase of creative coding projects with technical breakdowns

- **OpenFrameworks examples** (https://openframeworks.cc/examples/) — C++ creative coding toolkit, more performant than Processing/p5.js

### Tools for Physical Output

- **AxiDraw pen plotter** (https://www.axidraw.com/) — consumer-grade pen plotter for turning generative art into physical drawings

- **vpype** (https://github.com/abey79/vpype) — Swiss-Army-knife CLI tool for plotter artwork (SVG optimization, hatching, layering)

- **Risograph printing** — two-color printing with beautiful texture, popular in generative art zines

- **Blotter.js** (https://blotter.js.org/) — text effects library, useful for generative typography

## Unexpected Connections (for wild cards)

- **Islamic geometric patterns** — rule-based tessellation systems, beautiful math (http://www.tilingsearch.org/)
- **Cymatics** — sound made visible through vibration and particles (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q3oItpVa9fs)
- **Origami and fold patterns** — Erik Demaine's work on computational origami
- **Barnsley fern** — iterated function systems for plant-like forms
- **Voronoi diagrams** — natural partitioning (cells, giraffe patterns, cracked earth)
- **Delaunay triangulation** — dual of Voronoi, useful for mesh generation
- **Fourier transforms** — decomposing images/audio into frequencies (epicycles, 3Blue1Brown)
- **Generative music** — Brian Eno, Eno's "Bloom" app, Tero Parviainen's work
- **Procedural generation in games** — No Man's Sky, Minecraft, spelunky level generation
- **Data visualization as art** — Nadieh Bremer, Shirley Wu, dear-data project
- **Weaving and textile patterns** — Jacquard loom as early programmable machine
- **Bonsai and pruning algorithms** — space colonization algorithm for tree generation
- **Traffic flow and swarm intelligence** — boids applied to urban planning
- **Crystal growth** — diffusion-limited aggregation (DLA)
