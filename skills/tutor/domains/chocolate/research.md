# Chocolate Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

1. **Cacao Genetics & Cultivation**
   - Theobroma cacao varieties (Criollo, Forastero, Trinitario)
   - Terroir and genetic expression
   - Climate requirements and disease resistance

2. **Post-Harvest Processing**
   - Fermentation biochemistry (yeasts, bacteria, acetic acid)
   - Drying and flavor precursor development
   - Enzymatic reactions and phenolic compounds

3. **Chemistry of Flavor**
   - Maillard reactions during roasting
   - Volatile compounds (pyrazines, aldehydes, esters)
   - Flavor profiling and sensory analysis

4. **Processing & Conching**
   - Winnowing and nibs
   - Grinding and particle size
   - Conching chemistry and texture development

5. **Crystallization Physics & Tempering**
   - Cocoa butter polymorphism (6 crystal forms)
   - Nucleation and crystal growth
   - Temperature curves and tempering methods

6. **Sensory Science**
   - Tasting methodology
   - Flavor wheels and descriptors
   - Quality assessment
