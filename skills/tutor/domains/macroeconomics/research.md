# Macroeconomics — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. National Income Accounting
Measurement frameworks (GDP, GNP, national accounts), real vs. nominal variables, price indices, limitations of GDP as a welfare measure.

### 2. Economic Growth
Long-run growth theory, Solow model, capital accumulation, technological progress, convergence, endogenous growth theory, growth accounting, productivity.

### 3. Business Cycles
Short-run fluctuations, aggregate demand and supply, consumption theory, investment, government spending, net exports, multiplier effects.

### 4. Labor Markets
Labor supply and demand, unemployment (types, measurement, natural rate), wage determination, job search, efficiency wages.

### 5. Inflation and Monetary Policy
Money supply and demand, quantity theory, inflation causes and costs, Phillips curve, central banking, monetary policy tools, inflation targeting.

### 6. Fiscal Policy
Government spending, taxation, budget deficits and debt, automatic stabilizers, discretionary policy, crowding out, ricardian equivalence.

### 7. Open Economy Macroeconomics
Exchange rates, balance of payments, international trade and capital flows, open economy IS-LM, Mundell-Fleming model.

### 8. Financial Markets
Asset pricing, interest rates, term structure, financial crises, credit markets, banking system.

## Key Sources

### Textbooks
- **Mankiw, "Macroeconomics"** (9th ed.) — standard intermediate text, clear exposition, policy-focused
- **Blanchard & Johnson, "Macroeconomics"** (7th ed.) — strong on European perspective, IS-LM framework
- **Jones, "Macroeconomics"** (4th ed.) — modern approach, growth emphasis
- **Acemoglu, Laibson & List, "Economics"** — recent, behavioral insights

### Online Courses
- **MIT OpenCourseWare 14.05 Intermediate Macroeconomics** — lecture notes, problem sets, exams
- **Khan Academy Macroeconomics** — video series covering basics to intermediate
- **Marginal Revolution University** — Tyler Cowen's development and macro courses
- **Core-Econ "The Economy"** — open-access textbook with interactive elements

### Data and Tools
- **FRED (Federal Reserve Economic Data)** — comprehensive macro data, graphing tools
- **World Bank Open Data** — international development statistics
- **OECD Statistics** — comparative macro data across countries
- **IMF Data** — global economic indicators

### Institutional Resources
- **Federal Reserve Education** — explainers on monetary policy, economic concepts
- **Bureau of Economic Analysis** — US national accounts methodology
- **Congressional Budget Office** — fiscal policy analysis and projections
