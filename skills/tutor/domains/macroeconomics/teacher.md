# Macroeconomics — growth, cycles, and policy — Domain Teaching Config

## Exercise Types
This domain uses a mix of delivery formats:
- **concept-focused mini-lessons** — 11 lessons (35%)
- **Socratic questions** — 7 lessons (23%)
- **real-world application challenges** — 5 lessons (16%)
- **review and consolidation sessions** — 4 lessons (13%)
- **teach-back exercises (student explains)** — 3 lessons (10%)
- **curated resource exploration** — 1 lessons (3%)

Primary format: concept-focused mini-lessons.

## Resource Types
This domain has strong coverage in: textbooks, video lectures, interactive tools, academic papers, code repositories, online courses. Prioritize these when recommending resources.

## Difficulty Curve
Balanced progression — steady difficulty increase with regular consolidation.

Distribution: 32% accessible (1-2), 42% standard (3), 26% challenging (4-5).

Difficulty peaks:
- Day 7: "Why hasn't growth slowed down despite diminishing returns?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 8: "Can innovation and ideas sustain endless growth?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 14: "What happens when prices and wages are sticky?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 18: "Why do wages sometimes exceed market-clearing levels?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 21: "Is there really a trade-off between inflation and unemployment?" (difficulty 4)

## Domain Hooks
This field covers macroeconomics — growth, cycles, and policy, with applications across theory and practice.

## Common Failure Modes
Students often struggle with the foundational concepts before building to more advanced material. Reinforce basics before progressing.

## Vocabulary
Key terms for this domain: GDP, national income accounting, GDP limitations, real vs. nominal GDP, price indices, deflators, unemployment rate, labor force participation, measurement issues, economic growth, cross-country income differences, convergence, capital stock, investment, depreciation (and 81 more).
