## Common Failure Modes
1. **"Glaze chemistry is about memorizing recipes"**
   - **Why students think this**: Most beginners use published recipes without understanding them; chemistry seems like advanced trivia
   - **How to correct**: Show two glazes with identical visual results but completely different oxide compositions. Emphasize that recipes are starting points, not endpoints — the chemistry lets you modify and troubleshoot.

2. **"Weight percentages tell you everything you need to know"**
   - **Why students think this**: Recipes are written in grams and percentages; seems complete
   - **How to correct**: Compare a recipe with 10% feldspar vs. 10% silica — same weight percentage, radically different chemical contribution. Molecular formula reveals what's actually happening.

3. **"Silica is the 'main ingredient' because it's usually the largest percentage"**
   - **Why students think this**: Silica often dominates by weight (40-60%)
   - **How to correct**: Show that in UMF, silica is typically 2-4
