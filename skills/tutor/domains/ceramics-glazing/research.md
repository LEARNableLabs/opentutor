# Ceramics Glazing Chemistry — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Oxide Chemistry Fundamentals
- The role of oxides in glaze formation (glass formers, fluxes, stabilizers)
- Molecular vs. weight percentage
- Unity Molecular Formula (UMF) system
- Limit formulas and oxide ratios

### 2. Glaze Components
- **Fluxes** (Na₂O, K₂O, CaO, MgO, Li₂O, ZnO, BaO, SrO, PbO)
- **Glass formers** (SiO₂, B₂O₃)
- **Stabilizers** (Al₂O₃)
- **Colorants and opacifiers** (transition metal oxides, tin oxide, zirconium)

### 3. Glaze Calculation and Testing
- Recipe-to-formula conversion
- Formula-to-recipe conversion
- Glaze calculation software (GlazeMaster, Insight, HyperGlaze)
- Systematic testing methods (line blends, triaxial blends)

### 4. Eutectic Systems and Melting Behavior
- Phase diagrams in ceramic systems
- Eutectic points and their significance
- Viscosity and surface tension
- Firing temperature ranges

### 5. Color Chemistry
- Chromophores (Cr, Co, Cu, Fe, Mn, Ni)
- Oxidation vs. reduction atmospheres
- Color modifiers and stabilizers
- Stains vs. raw colorants

### 6. Surface Effects and Crystallization
- Matte vs. glossy surfaces
- Crystal growth (calcium borate, zinc, titanium crystals)
- Crawling, crazing, and shivering
- Glaze fit and thermal expansion

### 7. Troubleshooting and Adjustment
- Defects and their causes
- Adjusting melting temperature
- Modifying surface quality
- Addressing application problems

## Key Educational Resources

### Textbooks and References
- **"Mastering Cone 6 Glazes"** by John Hesselberth & Ron Roy — comprehensive chemistry foundation for mid-range firing
- **"The Potter's Dictionary of Materials and Techniques"** by Frank & Janet Hamer — encyclopedic reference
- **"Glazes: Materials, Recipes, and Techniques"** by Richard Zakin — systematic approach to understanding glaze chemistry
- **"Understanding Glazes"** by Richard Eppler & Mimi Obstler — oxide chemistry depth

### Online Resources
- **Glazy.org** — open-source glaze database with calculation tools
- **DigitalFire** — extensive glaze chemistry articles and reference database
- **The Ceramic School** (YouTube) — video tutorials on glaze chemistry
- **Linda Arbuckle's glaze chemistry course materials** (University of Florida)

### Tools and Software
- **Insight/GlazeMaster** — industry-standard glaze calculation software
- **HyperGlaze** — free alternative for glaze calculation
- **Glazy** — web-based calculator with recipe sharing
- **GlazeChem** — mobile/desktop calculation app

## Prerequisites for Intermediate Level

Students should have:
- Basic chemistry knowledge (atomic structure, chemical bonding)
- Experience with ceramic materials and firing
- Familiarity with glaze application techniques
- Understanding of kiln operation and firing schedules

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this curriculum, students will be able to:
- Calculate Unity Molecular Formula from glaze recipes
- Predict glaze behavior from oxide composition
- Formulate original glazes for specific effects
- Troubleshoot common glaze defects chemically
- Adjust existing recipes for different firing conditions
- Use glaze calculation software effectively
- Design systematic testing protocols
