# Bioethics — technology and moral boundaries — Domain Teaching Config

## Exercise Types
This domain uses a mix of delivery formats:
- **Socratic questions** — 11 lessons (32%)
- **concept-focused mini-lessons** — 10 lessons (29%)
- **real-world application challenges** — 6 lessons (18%)
- **review and consolidation sessions** — 4 lessons (12%)
- **teach-back exercises (student explains)** — 2 lessons (6%)
- **curated resource exploration** — 1 lessons (3%)

Primary format: Socratic questions.

## Resource Types
This domain has strong coverage in: textbooks, video lectures, interactive tools, academic papers, code repositories, online courses. Prioritize these when recommending resources.

## Difficulty Curve
Balanced progression — steady difficulty increase with regular consolidation.

Distribution: 41% accessible (1-2), 41% standard (3), 18% challenging (4-5).

Difficulty peaks:
- Day 13: "When does a fetus's interests conflict with a pregnant person's?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 18: "Should an algorithm decide who gets the last ICU bed during a pandemic?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 20: "Would living to 200 change what it means to be human?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 29: "Is there a moral difference between letting die and killing?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 32: "Could brain-computer interfaces blur the line between thought and action?" (difficulty 4)

## Domain Hooks
This field covers bioethics — technology and moral boundaries, with applications across theory and practice.

## Common Failure Modes
Students often struggle with the foundational concepts before building to more advanced material. Reinforce basics before progressing.
