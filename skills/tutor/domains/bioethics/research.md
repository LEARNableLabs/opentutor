# Bioethics Research Summary

## Topic Scope

Bioethics at the intersection of technology and moral boundaries, covering:
- Genetic engineering (CRISPR, gene therapy, designer babies)
- Reproductive technologies (IVF, surrogacy, genetic screening)
- Artificial intelligence in healthcare (diagnosis, treatment, allocation)
- Human enhancement (cognitive, physical, lifespan extension)
- Digital health ethics (data privacy, surveillance, equity)
- End-of-life technologies (life support, physician-assisted death)
- Emerging challenges (synthetic biology, brain-computer interfaces, bioterrorism)

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Foundations
- Four principles framework (autonomy, beneficence, non-maleficence, justice)
- Consequentialism vs. deontology in medical contexts
- Rights-based approaches
- Virtue ethics and care ethics
- Public health ethics vs. clinical ethics

### 2. Genetic Technologies
- CRISPR ethics (somatic vs. germline editing)
- Gene therapy governance
- Genetic privacy and discrimination
- Preimplantation genetic diagnosis
- "Designer baby" debates

### 3. Reproductive Ethics
- Assisted reproductive technology access
- Surrogacy and commodification concerns
- Abortion ethics across frameworks
- Maternal-fetal conflicts
- Sex selection

### 4. AI & Healthcare
- Algorithmic bias in diagnosis
- Liability and transparency
- Doctor-AI collaboration
- Resource allocation algorithms
- Privacy vs. ML training data needs

### 5. Enhancement
- Therapy vs. enhancement distinction
- Cognitive enhancers (pharmacological, neural)
- Athletic enhancement
- Anti-aging interventions
- Distributive justice in enhancement access

### 6. Digital Health
- Electronic health records privacy
- Genetic data ownership
- Wearable surveillance
- Telehealth equity
- AI consent models

### 7. End-of-Life
- Defining death (brain death vs. cardiac)
- Life support withdrawal
- Physician-assisted death legality and ethics
- Palliative care access
- Advance directives

### 8. Emerging Frontiers
- Brain-computer interfaces
- Synthetic biology and artificial life
- Dual-use research (bioterrorism risk)
- Climate change and health justice
- Pandemic preparedness ethics

## Teaching Approach for Intermediate Level

- Students should have basic philosophy exposure (ethical frameworks)
- Balance theory (frameworks) with applied cases
- Use real-world controversies (He Jiankui CRISPR babies, COVID triage)
- Emphasize multi-stakeholder perspectives (patients, doctors, policymakers, industry)
- Encourage wrestling with dilemmas without clean answers
- Progressive depth: start with individual cases → systemic issues → policy design
