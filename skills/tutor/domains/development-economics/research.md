# Development Economics — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Poverty Measurement and Growth
- Poverty lines, multidimensional poverty indices
- Growth theory: Solow model, endogenous growth, poverty traps
- Inequality measurement (Gini, Lorenz curves, Theil index)

### 2. Human Capital
- Education: returns to schooling, enrollment decisions, quality vs. access
- Health: disease burden, nutrition, child mortality, healthcare access
- Intergenerational transmission of poverty

### 3. Household Economics
- Intra-household bargaining, gender dynamics
- Fertility decisions, child labor
- Migration and remittances

### 4. Markets and Institutions
- Credit markets: microfinance, savings, informal lending
- Labor markets: informality, agricultural labor, wage determination
- Land markets and property rights
- Institutions: governance, corruption, state capacity

### 5. Agriculture and Rural Development
- Technology adoption, fertilizer use, irrigation
- Risk and insurance, weather shocks
- Market access and price volatility

### 6. Policy and Experimental Methods
- Randomized controlled trials (RCTs) in development
- Program evaluation: conditional cash transfers, subsidies, infrastructure
- External validity and scaling up interventions
