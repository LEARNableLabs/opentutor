# Microbiology and Virology — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks

- **Brock Biology of Microorganisms** (Madigan, Bender, Buckley, Sattley, Stahl) — The gold standard for intermediate microbiology. Comprehensive, mechanistic, excellent figures. Available online through many university libraries.
  - https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/brock-biology-of-microorganisms/P200000006801

- **Medical Microbiology** (Murray, Rosenthal, Pfaller) — Clinical focus with strong fundamental microbiology and virology sections. Excellent for pathogenesis and host-microbe interactions.
  - https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK8423/ (older edition, free)

- **Molecular Biology of the Cell** (Alberts et al.) — Chapters on viruses, pathogens, and prokaryotic cell biology provide excellent mechanistic depth.
  - https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK21054/ (free online)

- **Principles of Virology** (Flint, Racaniello, Rall, Skalka, Enquist) — Comprehensive virology textbook, excellent for advanced students.
  - https://www.asmscience.org/content/book/10.1128/9781555819415

### Open Educational Resources

- **MIT OpenCourseWare: 7.014 Introductory Biology** — lectures on microbiology, genetics, and molecular biology
  - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-014-introductory-biology-spring-2005/

- **MIT OpenCourseWare: 7.342 Viruses and Cells** — advanced undergraduate seminar on viral biology
  - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-342-viruses-and-cells-fall-2020/

- **NCBI Bookshelf** — free medical and biology textbooks, including Medical Microbiology
  - https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/

- **Khan Academy: Biology** — bacteria, archaea, and virus modules with videos and practice
  - https://www.khanacademy.org/science/biology/bacteria-archaea
  - https://www.khanacademy.org/science/biology/biology-of-viruses

- **NCBI Virus** — viral genome database and analysis tools
  - https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/labs/virus/

- **MicrobeWiki** (Kenyon College) — student-edited encyclopedia of microbiology

- **IMG/M** (Integrated Microbial Genomes & Microbiomes) — comparative genomics platform

- **Human Microbiome Project** — data and resources on the human microbiome

## Unexpected Connections (for wild cards)

- **Architecture and biofilms** — How do bacteria build structural communities? Links to architecture, materials science
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/nrmicro.2016.94

- **Music and microbial growth** — Students have studied effects of music on bacterial growth (fun experimental design discussion)
  - Example: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6718013/

- **Art and extremophiles** — Artists use extremophile pigments (thermophiles, halophiles) in paintings
  - https://www.atlasobscura.com/articles/art-made-from-bacteria

- **Fermentation and food science** — cheese, beer, kimchi, sourdough all rely on microbial metabolism
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03532-4

- **Space microbiology** — microbes on the ISS, planetary protection, searching for life on Mars/Europa
  - https://www.nasa.gov/mission_pages/station/research/benefits/microbiology.html

- **Microbial forensics** — using microbiome signatures to identify individuals or locations
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/s41564-019-0651-4

- **Bioluminescence** — from fireflies to anglerfish to bacteria (Vibrio fischeri)
  - https://www.biointeractive.org/classroom-resources/bioluminescence

- **Biological computation** — using bacteria as logic gates and biosensors
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/s41589-020-0510-4

- **Microbial electricity** — bacteria that generate current (Geobacter, Shewanella), microbial fuel cells
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/s41579-019-0173-x

- **Symbiosis at vents** — deep-sea hydrothermal vent ecosystems powered by chemosynthetic bacteria
  - https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/facts/vents-bacteria.html
