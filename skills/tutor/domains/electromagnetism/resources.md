# Electromagnetism — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks

**Intermediate Level (Primary):**
- **Griffiths, "Introduction to Electrodynamics" (4th ed.)** — The gold standard for intermediate classical EM. Clear explanations, excellent problems, perfect balance of rigor and intuition. Covers Maxwell's equations through radiation. [Publisher](https://www.cambridge.org/highereducation/books/introduction-to-electrodynamics/2D2C1A88F496953FB6D39A4C69B86BB1)

- **Purcell & Morin, "Electricity and Magnetism" (3rd ed.)** — Beautiful treatment emphasizing special relativity. More conceptual than Griffiths. Excellent for understanding field transformations. [Publisher](https://www.cambridge.org/highereducation/books/electricity-and-magnetism/72C097F6B07C3A94D0CA8F0BC91A6B16)

- **Zangwill, "Modern Electrodynamics"** — Comprehensive modern treatment. More advanced than Griffiths but excellent reference. Strong on applications and numerical methods. [Publisher](https://www.cambridge.org/highereducation/books/modern-electrodynamics/E0E7A5901F7BF37004F194F3C1CB7DE4)

**Wave Optics & Photonics:**
- **Saleh & Teich, "Fundamentals of Photonics" (2nd ed.)** — Comprehensive photonics from wave optics through quantum. Covers fibers, lasers, nonlinear optics, photonic crystals. Excellent diagrams. [Wiley](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Fundamentals+of+Photonics%2C+3rd+Edition-p-9781119506874)

- **Hecht, "Optics" (5th ed.)** — Accessible wave optics, interference, diffraction, polarization. Great for building intuition. Less mathematical than Saleh & Teich. [Pearson](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/optics/P200000006086)

**Waveguides & Microwave:**
- **Pozar, "Microwave Engineering" (4th ed.)** — Industry standard for transmission lines, waveguides, resonators, S-parameters. Applied engineering perspective. [Wiley](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Microwave+Engineering%2C+4th+Edition-p-9780470631553)

**Advanced (Graduate-level reference):**
- **Jackson, "Classical Electrodynamics" (3rd ed.)** — The comprehensive graduate reference. Rigorous and complete. Covers special relativity, radiation, multipoles, waveguides in depth. [Wiley](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Classical+Electrodynamics%2C+3rd+Edition-p-9780471309321)

## People to Follow

- **James Clerk Maxwell** (1831-1879) — Unified E&M, predicted EM waves. Read his papers for historical context.
- **Heinrich Hertz** (1857-1894) — First to experimentally verify EM waves. "Hertz" frequency unit named after him.
- **Michael Faraday** (1791-1867) — Discovered induction, field concept. "Lines of force" visualization.
- **Oliver Heaviside** (1850-1925) — Reformulated Maxwell's equations into modern vector notation.

## Unexpected Connections (for wild cards)

### Electromagnetism × Biology
- **Electroreception in sharks** — Ampullae of Lorenzini detect electric fields for navigation. EM sensing in nature.
- **Photosynthesis** — Light-harvesting complexes use quantum coherence for energy transfer. EM waves powering life.
- **Magnetoreception in birds** — Migratory birds may navigate using quantum entanglement in cryptochrome proteins. EM-based biological compass.

### Electromagnetism × Music
- **Electric guitars** — Electromagnetic pickups convert string vibrations to current. Faraday's law in action.
- **Theremin** — Capacitance-based instrument. EM field interaction for music.
- **Acoustic resonance vs. EM resonance** — Helmholtz resonators vs. cavity resonators. Same math, different waves.

### Electromagnetism × Astronomy
- **Pulsars** — Rotating neutron stars emit EM radiation like lighthouses. Extreme EM environments.
- **Cosmic microwave background** — Relic EM radiation from Big Bang. Blackbody spectrum at 2.7 K.
- **Fast radio bursts** — Mysterious EM pulses from deep space. Active research area.
- **Black hole imaging (Event Horizon Telescope)** — Interferometry at mm wavelengths to resolve Schwarzschild radius.

### Electromagnetism × Art
- **Holography** — Interference pattern recording for 3D images. Dennis Gabor invented it (Nobel Prize).
- **Light art (James Turrell)** — Using EM spectrum (visible light) as artistic medium.
- **Structural color** — Butterfly wings use photonic crystals, not pigments. Nature's metamaterials.

### Electromagnetism × Climate
- **Greenhouse effect** — Infrared absorption by CO₂, H₂O. Molecular resonances in EM spectrum.
- **Remote sensing** — Satellites use EM spectrum (visible, IR, microwave) to monitor Earth.
- **Atmospheric optics** — Rainbows, halos, mirages all from EM wave interactions with atmosphere.

### Electromagnetism × Quantum Computing
- **Cavity QED for qubits** — Superconducting qubits coupled to microwave cavities. Circuit QED.
- **Photonic quantum computing** — Linear optics + photon counting for quantum gates.
- **Quantum communication** — Fiber-optic quantum key distribution. BB84 protocol.

### Electromagnetism × Medicine
- **MRI** — Nuclear magnetic resonance in strong EM fields. Imaging without ionizing radiation.
- **Optical coherence tomography** — Low-coherence interferometry for retinal imaging.
- **Laser surgery** — Photocoagulation, photodisruption using nonlinear absorption.
- **Optogenetics** — Using light to control neurons. EM waves controlling brain activity.

### Electromagnetism × Information Technology
- **Fiber optic internet** — Global communications via guided EM waves in glass.
- **Photonic integrated circuits** — Chips that route light instead of electrons.
- **Optical data storage** — CD/DVD/Blu-ray use interference and diffraction for bits.
- **Terahertz imaging** — Security scanning, materials analysis in "THz gap" of EM spectrum.

## Historical Milestones (Timeline)

- **1820** — Ørsted discovers connection between electricity and magnetism
- **1831** — Faraday discovers electromagnetic induction
- **1861-1862** — Maxwell publishes "On Physical Lines of Force" (early Maxwell equations)
- **1865** — Maxwell predicts EM waves travel at speed of light
- **1887** — Hertz experimentally verifies EM waves
- **1905** — Einstein's photoelectric effect paper introduces photon concept
- **1960** — Maiman demonstrates first working laser (ruby laser)
- **1966** — Kao and Hockham propose optical fibers for communications
- **1987** — Yablonovitch and John independently propose photonic crystals
- **2000** — Pendry proposes "perfect lens" using metamaterials with negative index
- **2019** — Event Horizon Telescope images black hole M87* using mm-wave interferometry
- **2022** — Nobel Prize to Aspect, Clauser, Zeilinger for quantum entanglement experiments

## Recommended Learning Path Through Resources

### Phase 1: Foundation (Lessons 1-5)
- **Read:** Griffiths Chapters 1-7 (vector calculus, electrostatics, magnetostatics, Maxwell's equations)
- **Watch:** MIT 8.02 Lectures 1-12
- **Interact:** PhET "Charges and Fields"
- **Derive:** Wave equation from Maxwell (Griffiths Section 9.2)

### Phase 2: Wave Phenomena (Lessons 6-12)
- **Read:** Griffiths Chapter 9 (EM waves), Hecht Chapters 3-4 (wave optics)
- **Watch:** 3Blue1Brown "EM waves visually explained"
- **Interact:** Falstad 3D EM wave sim, PhET "Wave Interference"
- **Explore:** UNSW polarization animations

### Phase 3: Matter Interactions (Lessons 13-18)
- **Read:** Griffiths Chapter 9.4-9.5 (waves in matter), Saleh & Teich Chapters 5-13 (lasers)
- **Watch:** MIT 8.03 Lectures on dispersion and group velocity
- **Interact:** PhET "Bending Light"
- **Dive deeper:** RP Photonics Encyclopedia entries on lasers, dispersion

### Phase 4: Guided Waves (Lessons 19-23)
- **Read:** Pozar Chapters 2-4 (transmission lines, waveguides, resonators)
- **Watch:** MIT 6.013 Lectures on waveguides
- **Interact:** Falstad waveguide mode visualizations
- **Tools:** Learn Smith chart via Microwaves101

### Phase 5: Photonics (Lessons 24-28)
- **Read:** Saleh & Teich Chapters 1, 8-11, 18-21 (fibers, photonic crystals, nonlinear, quantum)
- **Watch:** Lectures on photonic crystals, metamaterials (YouTube)
- **Interact:** MIT Photonic Bands software
- **Explore:** Recent papers on metamaterials, quantum optics (Optica journals)

## Further Reading (Beyond Curriculum)

### Books
- **Feynman, "Lectures on Physics Vol. II"** — Feynman's unique perspective on E&M
- **Landau & Lifshitz, "The Classical Theory of Fields"** — Graduate-level, emphasizes special relativity
- **Boyd, "Nonlinear Optics"** — Comprehensive treatment of χ⁽²⁾ and χ⁽³⁾ processes

- **Pozar, "Microwave Engineering"** — Waveguides and transmission lines
- **Haus, "Waves and Fields in Optoelectronics"** — Advanced photonics and waveguides

- Maxwell, "A Dynamical Theory of the Electromagnetic Field" (1865) — Original prediction of EM waves

- Pendry et al., "Magnetism from Conductors and Enhanced Nonlinear Phenomena" (1999) — Metamaterial with negative μ
- Smith et al., "Composite Medium with Simultaneously Negative Permeability and Permittivity" (2000) — First negative-index metamaterial
