# Electromagnetism — Research Summary

## Topic Scope

This curriculum covers classical electromagnetism from Maxwell's equations through modern photonics applications. The progression spans:

1. **Foundations** — Maxwell's equations in differential and integral form, vector calculus foundations
2. **Wave phenomena** — Electromagnetic wave propagation, polarization, interference, diffraction
3. **Material interactions** — Dielectrics, conductors, boundary conditions, dispersion
4. **Guided waves** — Waveguides, transmission lines, resonators
5. **Photonics** — Optical fibers, lasers, photonic crystals, quantum optics basics

## Major Subtopics

### Maxwell's Equations (Foundation)
- Gauss's law (electric and magnetic)
- Faraday's law of induction
- Ampère-Maxwell law
- Continuity equation and charge conservation
- Potentials (scalar and vector)

### Electromagnetic Waves
- Wave equation derivation from Maxwell
- Plane waves in vacuum and matter
- Energy and momentum in EM fields (Poynting vector)
- Polarization states (linear, circular, elliptical)
- Reflection and refraction (Fresnel equations)

### Wave Optics
- Interference (two-slit, thin films, interferometry)
- Diffraction (Fraunhofer and Fresnel regimes)
- Coherence and temporal/spatial coherence length
- Fourier optics fundamentals

### Guided Waves & Transmission
- TEM, TE, TM modes
- Rectangular and cylindrical waveguides
- Transmission line theory
- Impedance matching and S-parameters
- Resonant cavities

### Photonics Applications
- Optical fibers (single-mode, multi-mode, dispersion)
- Laser physics (gain, cavity, modes)
- Nonlinear optics (second-harmonic generation, parametric processes)
- Photonic crystals and metamaterials
- Quantum optics (photon statistics, entanglement basics)

## Prerequisites

Students at intermediate level should have:
- **Vector calculus** — div, grad, curl, Stokes' theorem, divergence theorem
- **Multivariable calculus** — partial derivatives, multiple integrals, line/surface integrals
- **Linear algebra** — matrices, eigenvalues, vector spaces
- **Ordinary differential equations** — first and second order, solutions to wave equations
- **Basic physics** — Mechanics, introductory E&M (electric fields, magnetic fields, circuits)

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this curriculum, students will be able to:
- Apply Maxwell's equations to solve boundary value problems
- Analyze electromagnetic wave propagation in various media
- Design and analyze waveguides and transmission lines
- Understand photonic device principles (fibers, lasers, crystals)
- Connect classical EM to modern applications in telecommunications and quantum technologies

## Pedagogical Notes

- **Visual-first approach** — EM is highly geometric; use field visualizations extensively
- **Build intuition before formalism** — Start with physical pictures, then derive mathematics
- **Connect to applications** — Link every concept to real technology (antennas, fiber optics, etc.)
- **Emphasize symmetry** — Maxwell equations are beautifully symmetric; highlight this
- **Bridge to quantum** — Touch on photon picture where appropriate (lasers, quantum optics)
