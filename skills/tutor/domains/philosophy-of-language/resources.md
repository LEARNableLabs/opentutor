# Philosophy of Language — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Essential Readings

- **Frege, G. "On Sense and Reference" (1892)**
  - https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/frege/
  - The foundational text distinguishing sense from reference
  - Good for intermediate students; can be challenging but rewarding

- **Russell, B. "On Denoting" (1905)**
  - https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/descriptions/
  - Classic theory of definite descriptions
  - Dense but essential; use SEP summary alongside original

- **Kripke, S. *Naming and Necessity* (1980)**
  - https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/rigid-designators/
  - The watershed text for causal theory and rigid designation
  - Originally lectures; readable and full of examples
  - Consider assigning Lecture I in full

- **Putnam, H. "The Meaning of 'Meaning'" (1975)**
  - https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/content-externalism/
  - Twin Earth thought experiment and division of linguistic labor
  - Long but accessible; can assign just sections 1-4

### Supplementary Primary Texts

- **Strawson, P.F. "On Referring" (1950)**
  - Critique of Russell on presupposition
  - https://philpapers.org/rec/STROR

- **Donnellan, K. "Reference and Definite Descriptions" (1966)**
  - Referential vs. attributive distinction
  - https://philpapers.org/rec/DONRAD

- **Kaplan, D. "Demonstratives" (1989)**
  - Character vs. content for indexicals
  - Technical but groundbreaking
  - https://philpapers.org/rec/KAPD

## Textbooks & Course Materials

### Primary Textbooks

- **Lycan, W. *Philosophy of Language: A Contemporary Introduction* (3rd ed., Routledge, 2018)**
  - Comprehensive, intermediate-friendly
  - Covers all major topics with clear explanations
  - Available: https://www.routledge.com/Philosophy-of-Language-A-Contemporary-Introduction/Lycan/p/book/9781138501317

- **Soames, S. *Philosophical Analysis in the Twentieth Century, Volume 2* (Princeton, 2003)**
  - Historical approach covering Frege through Kripke
  - Excellent for context and connections
  - Available: https://press.princeton.edu/books/paperback/9780691115733/philosophical-analysis-in-the-twentieth-century-volume-2

- **Martinich, A.P. & Sosa, D. (eds.) *The Philosophy of Language* (7th ed., Oxford, 2021)**
  - Anthology with original papers and helpful introductions
  - Good for assigning specific chapters
  - Available: https://global.oup.com/ushe/product/the-philosophy-of-language-9780190698317

### Online Courses

### People to Follow

- **Saul Kripke (1940-2022)** — Causal theory of reference, rigid designation, necessary a posteriori
  - Key work: *Naming and Necessity*

- **Hilary Putnam (1926-2016)** — Twin Earth, semantic externalism, division of linguistic labor
  - Key work: "The Meaning of 'Meaning'"

- **Gareth Evans (1946-1980)** — Refined causal theory, varieties of reference
  - Key work: *The Varieties of Reference*

- **Tyler Burge** — Social externalism, anti-individualism
  - Key work: "Individualism and the Mental"

- **David Kaplan** — Demonstratives, character vs. content, direct reference
  - Key work: "Demonstratives"

- **Nathan Salmon** — Millian theory of names, propositions
  - Key work: *Reference and Essence*

- **Scott Soames** — Reference, propositions, philosophy of language history
  - Key work: *Beyond Rigidity*

- **Michael Devitt** — Causal theory development, designation and denotation
  - Key work: *Designation*

### Code & Simulations

- **Prolog for Semantic Analysis**
  - Logic programming can model semantic theories
  - Tutorial: https://www.swi-prolog.org/
  - Use for implementing Russell's theory of descriptions

- **Python for Formal Semantics**
  - NLTK (Natural Language Toolkit): https://www.nltk.org/
  - Can implement compositional semantics
  - Good for modeling how meanings compose

- **Coq/Agda for Type Theory**
  - Advanced: type-theoretic semantics
  - Coq: https://coq.inria.fr/
  - Agda: https://wiki.portal.chalmers.se/agda/

## Unexpected Connections (for wild cards)

- **Linguistics and Generative Grammar**
  - How Chomsky's linguistic theories connect to semantic theory
  - https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/generative-grammar/

- **Cognitive Science and Concepts**
  - How psychological research on concepts relates to theories of meaning
  - https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/concepts/

- **Computer Science and Formal Languages**
  - Connections between natural language semantics and programming language semantics
  - Type theory, lambda calculus, denotational semantics

- **Philosophy of Mind**
  - Internalism/externalism debate extends to mental content
  - https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/content-externalism/

- **Metaphysics of Modality**
  - Possible worlds semantics and modal metaphysics
  - https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/possible-worlds/

- **Epistemology**
  - How semantic externalism affects epistemology (externalist epistemology)
  - Necessary a posteriori challenges traditional analytic/synthetic distinction

- **Philosophy of Science**
  - Natural kinds and scientific classification
  - Reference of theoretical terms
  - https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/natural-kinds/

- **Mathematics and Logicism**
  - Frege's project of reducing arithmetic to logic
  - Philosophy of mathematics and reference to abstract objects
  - https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/logicism/
