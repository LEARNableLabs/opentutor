# Topology Research — From Coffee Cups to Knot Invariants

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Point-Set Topology (Foundations)
- Topological spaces, open/closed sets, bases
- Continuity and homeomorphisms
- Connectedness and compactness
- Separation axioms (Hausdorff, normal spaces)

### 2. Surfaces and Classification
- The coffee cup = donut homeomorphism (genus 1)
- Euler characteristic and genus
- Classification of closed surfaces
- Orientability

### 3. Algebraic Topology
- Fundamental group and homotopy
- Covering spaces
- Simplicial and singular homology
- CW complexes

### 4. Knot Theory
- Knot diagrams and Reidemeister moves
- Knot invariants: Alexander polynomial, Jones polynomial, Vassiliev invariants
- Linking number
- Applications to DNA, quantum field theory

### 5. Geometric Topology
- Manifolds
- Triangulations
- Low-dimensional topology

## Research Strategy

Primary approach: Start with visual intuition (coffee cup = donut), build formal foundations (topological spaces), then branch into algebraic topology (fundamental group) and knot theory (invariants). Use interactive tools heavily for intermediate students to build geometric intuition before abstract proofs.
