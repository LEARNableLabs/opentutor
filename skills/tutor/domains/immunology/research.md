# Immunology — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Innate Immunity
- Pattern recognition receptors (PRRs), PAMPs, and DAMPs
- Complement system (classical, alternative, lectin pathways)
- Phagocytes, natural killer cells, inflammatory response
- Barrier functions and antimicrobial peptides

### 2. Adaptive Immunity Foundations
- Antigen recognition: specificity, diversity, memory
- B lymphocytes and antibody structure/function
- T lymphocytes and cell-mediated immunity
- Lymphoid organs and tissue organization

### 3. Molecular Immunology
- MHC class I and II molecules
- Antigen processing and presentation
- T cell receptor (TCR) structure and signaling
- B cell receptor (BCR) and immunoglobulin genetics

### 4. Cellular Effector Mechanisms
- CD4+ T helper cell subsets (Th1, Th2, Th17, Treg)
- CD8+ cytotoxic T lymphocytes
- Cytokine networks and signaling
- B cell activation, germinal centers, plasma cells

### 5. Immunological Memory & Regulation
- Memory T and B cells
- Vaccine design principles
- Immune tolerance (central and peripheral)
- Regulatory mechanisms

### 6. Clinical Immunology
- Hypersensitivity reactions (Types I-IV)
- Autoimmune diseases
- Primary and secondary immunodeficiencies
- Transplantation immunology
- Tumor immunology and immunotherapy

## Pedagogical Notes

Immunology at intermediate level assumes:
- Basic cell biology (cell types, organelles, basic signaling)
- Foundational biochemistry (proteins, DNA/RNA, basic metabolism)
- General biology concepts (evolution, genetics)

Immunology is particularly well-suited to:
- Visual learning (cell interactions, molecular structures)
- Mechanistic thinking (signal cascades, feedback loops)
- Clinical connections (disease mechanisms, therapies)
- Problem-solving (diagnostic reasoning, experimental design)

Key pedagogical challenges:
- Nomenclature overload (CD markers, cytokines, pathways)
- Interconnected concepts (hard to linearize learning)
- Multiple levels of abstraction (molecular → cellular → systemic)
- Rapidly evolving field (new discoveries, therapies)
