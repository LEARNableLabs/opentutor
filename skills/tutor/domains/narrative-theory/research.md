# Narrative Theory — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

1. **Classical Narratology** — Structuralist foundations (Genette, Todorov, Barthes, Greimas)
2. **Story vs. Discourse** — The fundamental distinction between what happened (fabula) and how it's told (syuzhet)
3. **Narrative Time** — Order, duration, frequency, analepsis, prolepsis
4. **Focalization** — Point of view, perspective, who sees vs. who speaks
5. **Voice and Narration** — Narrative levels, homo/heterodiegetic narration, reliability
6. **Character Theory** — Actants, character functions, flat vs. round characters
7. **Plot and Structure** — Narrative arcs, causality, emplotment
8. **Reader Response** — How audiences construct meaning, narrative gaps
9. **Transmedia Narratology** — Storytelling across media (film, games, serial formats)
10. **Postclassical Approaches** — Cognitive narratology, feminist narratology, unnatural narratives

### Key Textbooks

- **H. Porter Abbott**: *The Cambridge Introduction to Narrative* (Cambridge University Press)
  - Most frequently cited introductory text, clear and accessible

- **Mieke Bal**: *Narratology: Introduction to the Theory of Narrative* (University of Toronto Press, 1997)
  - Comprehensive systematic approach, influential in literary studies

- **Seymour Chatman**: *Story and Discourse: Narrative Structure in Fiction and Film* (Cornell University Press, 1978)
  - Classic text bridging literary and film narrative

- **Gerard Genette**: *Narrative Discourse: An Essay in Method* (1983)
  - Foundational structuralist work, introduced key vocabulary
  - Full text: https://dn790007.ca.archive.org/0/items/NarrativeDiscourseAnEssayInMethod/NarrativeDiscourse-AnEssayInMethod.pdf

- **David Herman** (ed.): *Narratologies: New Perspectives on Narrative Analysis*
  - Contemporary approaches beyond classical narratology

### Key Researchers and Practitioners

- **Gerard Genette** (1930-2018): French structuralist, developed systematic vocabulary for narrative analysis (focalization, narrative levels, mood, voice, time)
  - Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G%C3%A9rard_Genette
  - Literary Theory resource: https://literariness.org/2016/12/03/gerard-genette-and-structural-narratology/

- **Tzvetan Todorov**: Coined the term "narratology" in 1969
- **Vladimir Propp**: Analyzed folktale structure, morphology of the folktale
- **Roland Barthes**: Semiotics and narrative codes
- **Mieke Bal**: Contemporary narratology, feminist approaches
- **Monika Fludernik**: Cognitive narratology, natural narratives
- **Shlomith Rimmon-Kenan**: *Narrative Fiction: Contemporary Poetics*
- **Gerald Prince**: *Narratology: The Form and Functioning of Narrative*

## Coverage Gaps

- Most resources are text-focused (literary narratology); interactive storytelling and game narratology less represented
- Few beginner-friendly visual explanations of abstract concepts like focalization
- Limited open-access video lectures compared to STEM fields
- Practical analysis exercises are scattered across course materials

## Approach for Intermediate Level

At intermediate level, students should:
- Move beyond intuitive story understanding to systematic analysis
- Master core terminology (story/discourse, focalization, narrative levels, time manipulation)
- Apply frameworks to diverse texts (novels, films, games, serial narratives)
- Understand both classical (structuralist) and contemporary (cognitive, feminist, transmedia) approaches
- Develop analytical skills through close reading and framework application
