# Auction Theory and Mechanism Design — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

1. **Classical Auction Theory**
   - Single-item auctions (English, Dutch, first-price sealed-bid, Vickrey)
   - Revenue equivalence theorem
   - Bidder behavior and strategic equivalence

2. **Mechanism Design Fundamentals**
   - Incentive compatibility (dominant strategy, Bayesian)
   - Individual rationality
   - Revelation principle
   - Myerson's Lemma and characterization theorems

3. **Revenue Maximization**
   - Optimal auction design
   - Reserve prices
   - Virtual valuations
   - Bayesian mechanism design

4. **Advanced Mechanisms**
   - VCG mechanism
   - Multi-parameter settings
   - Combinatorial auctions
   - Sponsored search auctions

5. **Market Design Applications**
   - Spectrum auctions
   - Ad auctions
   - Matching markets with money
   - Real-world implementation challenges

## Assessment

This is a mature field with excellent educational resources. The Stanford CS364A/B courses and the Algorithmic Game Theory textbook provide the gold standard for intermediate learners. The material bridges economics and computer science, requiring comfort with probability, optimization, and algorithmic thinking.

Best pedagogical approach combines:
- Concrete auction examples (eBay, spectrum, ads)
- Formal mechanism design theory
- Computational and algorithmic perspectives
- Real-world implementation challenges
