# Color Theory — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Perception and Physics
- How light interacts with the eye (photons, wavelengths, receptors)
- Trichromatic theory and opponent process theory
- Color constancy and adaptation
- Color vision deficiencies

### 2. Color Systems and Models
- Additive color (RGB) vs. subtractive color (CMYK)
- Munsell color system (hue, value, chroma)
- HSL/HSB models
- CIE color spaces and standardization
- Perceptual uniformity

### 3. Color Relationships and Harmony
- Color wheel organization
- Complementary, analogous, triadic schemes
- Color contrast and simultaneous contrast
- Josef Albers' interaction of color
- Value and intensity relationships

### 4. Psychology and Culture
- Emotional associations with color
- Cultural variations in color meaning
- Color in branding and marketing
- Synesthesia and cross-modal associations

### 5. Practical Application
- Color in digital vs. print media
- Accessibility and color contrast ratios
- Color grading and correction
- Painting techniques and pigment mixing
- Design systems and color palettes

## Key Educational Resources Found

### University Courses
- **MIT OCW: Art of Color** (https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/es-298-art-of-color-spring-2005/pages/syllabus/) — Studio seminar covering principles of visual design, color theory, and the science of color (physics, neuroscience, chemistry, mathematics)
- **San Jose State: ART 14 Color** — Introduction covering physical, artistic, psychological, semiotic, and cultural perspectives
- **UT Dallas: ARTS 3311** — Color theories, properties, and applications across historical, symbolic, psychological, and cultural systems

### Interactive Tools
- **PhET Color Vision** (https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulations/color-vision) — Simulate RGB light mixing, wavelengths, filtering, additive color
- **Munsell Color Tool** (https://munsell.com/color-blog/color-theory-simulation-tool/) — Interactive learning for value and intensity through chip ordering
- **SimBucket** (https://www.simbucket.com/teaching-color-theory-with-simbucket-simulations/) — Five simulations for CMY painting, RGB addition, stage lighting
- **Swatchery** (https://swatchery.com/color-theory) — Free interactive tutorial covering harmonies, color wheel, HSB/RGB models, contrast
- **Horizon Lab Color Science Tutorials** (https://horizon-lab.org/colorvis/) — Visualization tools connected to "Foundations of Visual Computing" textbook

### Video Content
- **Khan Academy: Pixar in a Box - Color Science** (https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/pixar/color) — How color is determined by light physics and brain perception
- MIT OCW video lectures available through their platform

### Key Textbooks
- **Interaction of Color by Josef Albers** — Classic text on how color perception changes with context
- **The Elements of Color by Johannes Itten** — Condensed examination with colored illustrations and practical exercises
- **Color Theory by Patti Mollica** — Practical guide including psychology, pigment characteristics, color schemes
- **Cognition through Color by Jules Davidoff** — Modern neuropsychology perspective on color as perceptual building block

### Additional Resources
- **New York Public Library Color Theory Research Guide** (https://libguides.nypl.org/colortheory/perception) — Curated academic resources on perception and psychology
- **Behavioral Design Academy: Color Psychology** — Book on color in UX, design, and marketing

## Available Educational Formats
- Interactive simulators for hands-on RGB/CMY experimentation
- Studio-based exercises (Albers squares, color mixing)
- Video demonstrations of color phenomena
- Historical and theoretical readings
- Practical application in digital tools
- Scientific grounding in physics and neuroscience

## Research Approach Notes
For intermediate students, the curriculum should bridge conceptual understanding (why colors behave this way) with practical application (how to use color effectively). The material should start with accessible perceptual phenomena, build systematic understanding of color models, then apply to real-world design and art scenarios. Heavy emphasis on interactive exploration and hands-on exercises.
