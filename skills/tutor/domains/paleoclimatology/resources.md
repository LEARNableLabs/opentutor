# Paleoclimatology — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks (Intermediate Level)

- **Ruddiman, W.F. (2014)** — *Earth's Climate: Past and Future* (3rd edition)
  - Standard intermediate text, balances accessibility with rigor
  - Strong on Quaternary climate, Milankovitch theory
  - Covers both observational methods and modeling
  - https://www.whfreeman.com/earthsclimate

- **Bradley, R.S. (2015)** — *Paleoclimatology: Reconstructing Climates of the Quaternary* (3rd edition)
  - Comprehensive coverage of proxy methods
  - Excellent on dating techniques and uncertainty
  - Heavy on ice cores and tree rings
  - https://www.elsevier.com/books/paleoclimatology/bradley/978-0-12-386913-5

- **Cronin, T.M. (2010)** — *Paleoclimates: Understanding Climate Change Past and Present*
  - Integrates paleoclimate with modern climate science
  - Good on deep time events (PETM, Cretaceous)
  - Emphasizes applications to current climate change
  - Columbia University Press

### University Syllabi & Course Materials

- **MIT OCW 12.842** — Climate Physics and Chemistry
  - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/12-842-climate-physics-and-chemistry-fall-2008/
  - Lecture notes, problem sets, readings
  - Advanced undergraduate/graduate level

- **NOAA Paleoclimatology Education**
  - https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/paleoclimatology/education
  - Educational resources, lesson plans, data tutorials
  - Wide range of levels, excellent for self-study

- **University of Arizona Laboratory of Tree-Ring Research**
  - https://www.ltrr.arizona.edu/
  - Dendroclimatology courses, tutorials
  - Interactive tree ring dating exercises

- **ANDRILL Educational Resources**
  - https://www.andrill.org/education/
  - Antarctic drilling project materials
  - Videos, data, teaching modules on ice core science

### Review Papers & Key Publications

- **IPCC AR6 WG1 Chapter 2** — "Changing State of the Climate System"
  - https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/
  - Comprehensive review of paleo evidence for climate change
  - State-of-the-art synthesis, updated to 2021

- **Zachos et al. (2008)** — "Trends, Rhythms, and Aberrations in Global Climate 65 Ma to Present"
  - *Science* 292: 686-693
  - Iconic Cenozoic temperature curve
  - https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1059412

- **Petit et al. (1999)** — "Climate and atmospheric history of the past 420,000 years from the Vostok ice core, Antarctica"
  - *Nature* 399: 429-436
  - Classic ice core record showing glacial/interglacial cycles
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/20859

- **EPICA Community Members (2004)** — "Eight glacial cycles from an Antarctic ice core"
  - *Nature* 429: 623-628
  - Extends ice core record to 800 ka
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/nature02599

- **Lisiecki & Raymo (2005)** — "A Pliocene-Pleistocene stack of 57 globally distributed benthic δ18O records"
  - *Paleoceanography* 20: PA1003
  - LR04 stack: standard chronology for last 5 Ma
  - https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2004PA001071

### Interactive Tools & Visualizations

- **NOAA Paleo Data Viewer**
  - https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/maps/paleo/
  - Explore proxy records geographically
  - Download datasets, plot time series

- **NASA Earth Observatory**
  - https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/
  - Articles, images, data visualizations
  - Strong on climate change impacts and paleo context

- **Climate Reanalyzer** (University of Maine)
  - https://climatereanalyzer.org/
  - Real-time climate data and reanalysis products
  - Includes paleo model outputs (PMIP)

- **Vostok Ice Core Interactive**
  - https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/paleo-search/study/6080
  - Browse CO2, temperature, and dust data interactively
  - Downloadable datasets

- **EarthViewer** (HHMI BioInteractive)
  - https://www.biointeractive.org/classroom-resources/earthviewer
  - Reconstruct Earth's plate positions, atmosphere, climate over geologic time
  - Excellent for visualizing deep time changes

- **Orbital Variation Calculator**
  - https://climatemodels.uchicago.edu/orbits/
  - Compute insolation for any latitude/time based on orbital parameters
  - Visualize Milankovitch cycle impacts

### Code & Data Repositories

- **Pangaea** — Publishing Network for Geoscientific & Environmental Data
  - https://www.pangaea.de/
  - Searchable database of paleoclimate datasets
  - Standardized formats, DOIs for all datasets

- **NOAA Paleoclimatology Data Archive**
  - https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/paleoclimatology/paleoclimatology-data
  - Ice cores, tree rings, corals, sediments, etc.
  - Free download, well-documented

- **PRYSM** — Proxy System Modeling in Python
  - https://github.com/sylvia-dee/PRYSM
  - Forward models for climate proxies (ice cores, tree rings, corals, lake sediments)
  - Helps understand what proxies actually record

- **LiPD** — Linked Paleo Data
  - http://lipd.net/
  - Standardized data format for paleo records
  - R and Python utilities for analysis

- **Paleoclimate Modelling Intercomparison Project (PMIP)**
  - https://pmip.lsce.ipsl.fr/
  - Climate model outputs for past periods (LGM, mid-Holocene, Pliocene, etc.)
  - Data-model comparison resources

### Datasets (Key Archives)

- **EPICA Dome C Ice Core** — 800 ka Antarctic climate
  - https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/paleo-search/study/6080
  - CO2, δD (temperature proxy), dust, methane

- **NGRIP/GISP2 Ice Cores** — Greenland climate record
  - https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/paleoclimatology/ice-core
  - High resolution (annual) back to ~110 ka
  - Shows abrupt climate changes (Dansgaard-Oeschger events)

- **LR04 Benthic Stack** — Global ocean temperature/ice volume, 5 Ma
  - https://lorraine-lisiecki.com/stack.html
  - Standard chronology for Quaternary

- **International Tree Ring Data Bank (ITRDB)**
  - https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/paleoclimatology/tree-ring
  - Thousands of tree ring chronologies worldwide
  - Regional climate reconstructions

- **Ocean Drilling Program (ODP) / Integrated Ocean Drilling Program (IODP)**
  - http://www-odp.tamu.edu/ (ODP legacy site)
  - https://www.iodp.org/ (current program)
  - Deep-sea sediment cores, hundreds of sites globally

## Cross-Discipline Connections (Unexpected Rabbit Holes)

### Paleoclimate ↔ Astrobiology
- **Faint Young Sun Paradox** — How Earth stayed warm 4 Ga ago
  - https://www.lpi.usra.edu/science/kring/epo_web/impact_cratering/Earth/faint_young_sun.html
- **Snowball Earth and Oxygenation** — Life reshaping climate
  - https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1090110
- **Exoplanet habitability** — Using Earth's climate extremes as analogs
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/s41550-020-1237-3

### Paleoclimate ↔ Human Evolution
- **Precessional pacing of African climate** — Orbital forcing → lake level changes → hominin evolution
  - deMenocal, P. (2011) *Nature* 470: 146-148
- **Toba supereruption** — Volcanic winter vs. genetic bottleneck debate
  - https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1173146

### Paleoclimate ↔ Mass Extinctions
- **Chicxulub impact** — K-Pg extinction, impact winter
  - https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1909479116
- **Permian-Triassic extinction** — Largest extinction, linked to volcanism and climate
  - https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1256013
- **Ocean Anoxic Events** — OAEs and biotic crises in Mesozoic
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/nature08327

### Paleoclimate ↔ Art & Culture
- **Little Ice Age** — Breugel's winter paintings, Thames frost fairs
- **Year Without a Summer (1816)** — Tambora eruption → crop failures → Frankenstein written during dark summer
- **Climate and Civilizations** — Drought and Maya collapse, Akkadian Empire fall
  - https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1058775

### Paleoclimate ↔ Philosophy of Science
- **Uniformitarianism** — "Present is the key to the past" (Lyell)
- **Contingency vs. Determinism** — How predictable is climate evolution?
- **Models vs. Data** — What counts as evidence? How do we validate models of the past?

### Paleoclimate ↔ Economics
- **Social Cost of Carbon** — Paleo informs climate sensitivity → damage functions
- **Climate Risk** — Fat-tailed distributions, learning from rare events in paleo record
- **Intergenerational equity** — Committed warming from paleo perspective (ESS)

## Advanced Topics (Beyond This Curriculum)

For students wanting to go deeper:

- **Climate Sensitivity Estimation** — Bayesian methods, uncertainty quantification
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-020-2943-z

  - https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2018RG000654

  - https://www.annualreviews.org/doi/10.1146/annurev-earth-042711-105228

  - https://pmip.lsce.ipsl.fr/

  - Blaauw & Christen (2011) *Bayesian Analysis* 6(3): 457-474

### Classic Papers (Historical Interest)
- **Hays et al. (1976)** — "Variations in the Earth's Orbit: Pacemaker of the Ice Ages"
  - *Science* 194: 1121-1132
  - First definitive confirmation of Milankovitch theory
- **Broecker & Denton (1989)** — "The role of ocean-atmosphere reorganizations in glacial cycles"
  - *Geochimica et Cosmochimica Acta* 53: 2465-2501
  - Ocean circulation as key to abrupt change
- **Kennett et al. (1985)** — "Carbon isotopic evidence for rapid climate change at the termination of the Paleocene"
  - *Nature* 316: 708-711
  - Early PETM paper
