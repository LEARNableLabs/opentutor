# Antitrust and Competition Law — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks

- **Herbert Hovenkamp, *Federal Antitrust Policy: The Law of Competition and Its Practice*** (6th ed.) — The standard US antitrust textbook. Clear, economically sophisticated, comprehensive coverage of Sherman Act, Clayton Act, and FTC Act. Best for understanding US doctrine.
  - Publisher: [West Academic](https://www.westacademic.com/)

- **Phillip Areeda & Herbert Hovenkamp, *Antitrust Law: An Analysis of Antitrust Principles and Their Application*** (multi-volume treatise) — The definitive reference. Exhaustive. Use for deep dives on specific issues (predatory pricing, tying, essential facilities).
  - [Aspen Publishing](https://www.aspenpublishing.com/)

- **Richard Whish & David Bailey, *Competition Law*** (10th ed.) — Leading textbook for EU competition law. Covers Articles 101 and 102 TFEU, merger regulation, and comparative perspective. Essential for EU side.
  - [Oxford University Press](https://global.oup.com/)

- **Louis Kaplow, *Competition Policy and Price Fixing*** — Economic foundations of antitrust. Rigorous treatment of why price fixing is harmful and how to design optimal enforcement. For students comfortable with economics.
  - [Princeton University Press](https://press.princeton.edu/)

### Institutional Resources

- **FTC Competition Guidance** — [https://www.ftc.gov/advice-guidance/competition-guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/advice-guidance/competition-guidance)  
  Guides on single-firm conduct, competitor collaborations, intellectual property, health care. Written for practitioners but accessible.

- **DOJ Antitrust Division Resources** — [https://www.justice.gov/atr](https://www.justice.gov/atr)  
  Case filings, policy statements, merger guidelines, and speeches. Essential for understanding enforcement priorities.

- **European Commission DG Competition** — [https://ec.europa.eu/competition/](https://ec.europa.eu/competition/)  
  Decisions, guidelines, sector inquiries, and annual reports. See "Cases" section for major decisions (Google, Microsoft, Intel).

- **OECD Competition Division** — [https://www.oecd.org/competition/](https://www.oecd.org/competition/)  
  Policy roundtables, country reviews, and economic research. Best for comparative and international perspective.

- **International Competition Network (ICN)** — [https://www.internationalcompetitionnetwork.org/](https://www.internationalcompetitionnetwork.org/)  
  Recommended practices, agency handbooks, and working group reports. Good for seeing convergence/divergence across jurisdictions.

### Online Courses

- **Coursera: "Competition Law" (Lund University)** — [https://www.coursera.org/learn/competition-law](https://www.coursera.org/learn/competition-law)  
  EU-focused, covers cartels, abuse of dominance, and merger control. Good production quality, includes case studies.

- **edX: "EU Competition Law" (KU Leuven)** — [https://www.edx.org/course/eu-competition-law](https://www.edx.org/course/eu-competition-law)  
  Taught by leading European scholars. Focuses on Articles 101/102 and merger regulation.

- **MIT OpenCourseWare: "Industrial Organization and Competitive Strategy"** — [https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/sloan-school-of-management/15-013-industrial-organization-and-competitive-strategy-spring-2016/](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/sloan-school-of-management/15-013-industrial-organization-and-competitive-strategy-spring-2016/)  
  Not antitrust-specific, but provides economic foundations (oligopoly theory, entry barriers, predatory pricing). Lecture notes and problem sets.

### Case Law and Materials

- **Supreme Court Reporter** — [https://supreme.justia.com/](https://supreme.justia.com/)  
  Free access to all Supreme Court antitrust decisions. Search "Sherman Act," "Clayton Act," or specific cases (Leegin, Trinko, American Express).

- **EUR-Lex (EU case law)** — [https://eur-lex.europa.eu/](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/)  
  Official EU legal database. Search CJEU judgments and Commission decisions. Use case numbers from lesson resources.

- **FTC Case Database** — [https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings)  
  Full complaints, settlements, and administrative decisions. Includes expert reports and economic analysis.

- **DOJ Antitrust Case Filings** — [https://www.justice.gov/atr/antitrust-case-filings](https://www.justice.gov/atr/antitrust-case-filings)  
  Complaints, proposed final judgments, and competitive impact statements. See Google, AT&T/Time Warner, American Airlines/US Airways.

## Unexpected Connections

### Antitrust and IP

- Patent pooling and standard-setting organizations raise antitrust issues (licensing terms, FRAND commitments)
- DOJ/FTC joint IP guidelines: [https://www.justice.gov/atr/IPguidelines](https://www.justice.gov/atr/IPguidelines)
- Pharmaceutical "pay-for-delay" settlements (FTC v. Actavis)

### Antitrust and Labor Markets

- No-poach agreements between employers treated like horizontal cartels (DOJ prosecutions 2020+)
- Monopsony power in labor markets (lower wages, reduced employment)
- Non-compete clauses as antitrust issue (FTC proposed ban 2023)
- Resources: [https://www.ftc.gov/policy/advocacy-research/tech-at-ftc/2022/06/monopsony-labor-markets](https://www.ftc.gov/policy/advocacy-research/tech-at-ftc/2022/06/monopsony-labor-markets)

### Antitrust and Privacy

- Data portability and interoperability as competition remedies
- Privacy as quality dimension in competition analysis (Facebook/WhatsApp merger)
- Tension between data minimization (privacy) and data sharing (competition)
- GDPR and DMA interaction (EU)

### Antitrust and Environmental Sustainability

- Can competitors collaborate on green standards without violating antitrust? (EU Green Deal guidance)
- Should antitrust account for environmental externalities in efficiency analysis?
- Greenwashing and consumer deception (FTC Green Guides)
- Resources: [https://ec.europa.eu/competition/antitrust/sustainability.html](https://ec.europa.eu/competition/antitrust/sustainability.html)

### Antitrust and National Security

- CFIUS review vs. antitrust review of foreign acquisitions (US)
- EU foreign subsidies regulation
- Export controls and antitrust (semiconductors, critical minerals)
- 5G network equipment and security-driven market exclusions

### Antitrust and Inequality

- Debate over whether antitrust should pursue distributional goals (neo-Brandeisian view)
- Market power and wage stagnation linkage
- "Common ownership" hypothesis (BlackRock/Vanguard passive investing reduces competition)
- Resources: [https://equitablegrowth.org/research-paper/market-power-inequality/](https://equitablegrowth.org/research-paper/market-power-inequality/)

### Antitrust and Behavioral Economics

- Behavioral biases and consumer harm (e.g., default settings on platforms)
- Bounded rationality and switching costs as entry barriers
- Nudges and dark patterns as exclusionary conduct
- Resources: OECD roundtable on behavioral economics: [https://www.oecd.org/daf/competition/behavioural-insights.htm](https://www.oecd.org/daf/competition/behavioural-insights.htm)

## Recommended Reading Sequence

### For this curriculum (intermediate level):

1. **Start with**: Hovenkamp textbook (chapters on Sherman Act §1 and §2, merger control)
2. **Supplement with**: DOJ/FTC Horizontal Merger Guidelines and EU Merger Regulation for frameworks
3. **Deep dives**: Read full decisions for case study lessons (Microsoft, Google Shopping, Apple e-books, Staples/Office Depot)
4. **Current events**: Follow Competition Policy International and FTC/DOJ press releases
5. **Advanced topics**: OECD roundtables on algorithmic pricing, two-sided markets, and digital platforms

### Beyond this curriculum:

- **For economics deep dive**: Louis Kaplow, *Competition Policy and Price Fixing* + Massimo Motta, *Competition Policy: Theory and Practice*
- **For EU specialization**: Richard Whish & David Bailey + European Commission decisions database
- **For US litigation practice**: ABA Antitrust Section books on merger litigation, Hart-Scott-Rodino practice, expert testimony
- **For policy debates**: Lina Khan's "Amazon's Antitrust Paradox," Tim Wu's *The Curse of Bigness*, Herbert Hovenkamp's responses on SSRN
