# Permaculture Design — Domain Teaching Config

## Exercise Types
This domain uses a mix of delivery formats:
- **concept-focused mini-lessons** — 11 lessons (38%)
- **Socratic questions** — 6 lessons (21%)
- **review and consolidation sessions** — 5 lessons (17%)
- **real-world application challenges** — 4 lessons (14%)
- **teach-back exercises (student explains)** — 2 lessons (7%)
- **curated resource exploration** — 1 lessons (3%)

Primary format: concept-focused mini-lessons.

## Resource Types
This domain has strong coverage in: textbooks, video lectures, interactive tools, code repositories, online courses. Prioritize these when recommending resources.

## Difficulty Curve
Balanced progression — steady difficulty increase with regular consolidation.

Distribution: 48% accessible (1-2), 31% standard (3), 21% challenging (4-5).

Difficulty peaks:
- Day 13: "Where should water go on a sloped site?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 14: "How can earthworks transform a degraded landscape?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 18: "How do you design a plant guild?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 24: "How do you design from pattern to details?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 25: "Can you analyze this failed design and propose fixes?" (difficulty 5)

## Domain Hooks
This field covers permaculture design, with applications across theory and practice.

## Common Failure Modes
Students often struggle with the foundational concepts before building to more advanced material. Reinforce basics before progressing.

## Vocabulary
Key terms for this domain: earth care, people care, fair share, ethical framework, systems thinking, closed-loop systems, observation, natural patterns, protracted observation, pattern recognition, baseline data, phenology, water flow, catchment, hydrology (and 90 more).
