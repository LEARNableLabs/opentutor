# Permaculture Design — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Ethics & Principles
- Three core ethics (Earth Care, People Care, Fair Share)
- 12 design principles (Holmgren framework)
- Systems thinking and pattern recognition
- Observation and interaction with natural systems

### 2. Observation & Analysis
- Site analysis methodologies (climate, water, soil, sun, wind)
- Zone and sector planning
- Reading landscapes and microclimates
- Mapping techniques and data collection

### 3. Design Methodologies
- Scale of permanence (Yeomans framework)
- Design process: observation → boundaries → resources → analysis → design → implementation → maintenance
- Pattern languages and natural patterns
- Edge effect and succession planning

### 4. Water Systems
- Water harvesting and storage
- Swales, ponds, and earthworks
- Greywater systems
- Keyline design

### 5. Soil & Fertility
- Soil biology and food webs
- Composting systems
- No-dig gardening and sheet mulching
- Nutrient cycling

### 6. Plant Systems
- Guild design and stacking functions
- Perennial polycultures
- Food forests and forest gardens
- Seed saving and propagation

### 7. Animals & Integration
- Chicken tractors and mobile systems
- Integrated pest management
- Livestock as system elements
- Beneficial animal relationships

### 8. Built Environment
- Energy-efficient design
- Natural building materials
- Appropriate technology
- Infrastructure placement

## Key Sources

### Foundational Texts
- **Bill Mollison** — "Permaculture: A Designers' Manual" (comprehensive reference)
- **David Holmgren** — "Permaculture: Principles and Pathways Beyond Sustainability" (principle framework)
- **Toby Hemenway** — "Gaia's Garden" (accessible, practical)
- **Geoff Lawton** — Video courses and case studies (practical implementation)

### Educational Institutions
- **Permaculture Research Institute** — Online PDC courses, articles, case studies
- **Oregon State University** — Permaculture design resources
- **Aprovecho Research Center** — Appropriate technology and systems

### Online Resources
- Permaculture Principles (permacultureprinciples.com) — principle deep-dives
- Permaculture Research Institute (permaculturenews.org) — current practices, case studies
- YouTube: Geoff Lawton, Andrew Millison (OSU), Justin Rhodes
- iNaturalist, USDA Plants Database — species identification

### Tools & Software
- Google Earth / satellite imagery — site analysis
- Sun position calculators
- Climate data sources (NOAA, local weather stations)
- Soil testing labs

## Student Profile (Intermediate)

Assumed background:
- Basic ecology and natural systems understanding
- Some gardening or land management experience
- Familiarity with sustainability concepts
- Ready to learn formal design process and systems integration

Focus areas for intermediate:
- Design methodology and process
- Systems thinking and pattern recognition
- Practical techniques for common climates
- Real-world case study analysis
- Hands-on design projects
