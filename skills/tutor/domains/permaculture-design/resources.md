# Permaculture Design — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Foundational Textbooks
- **Bill Mollison — "Permaculture: A Designers' Manual"** (1988)  
  The comprehensive reference. Dense but authoritative. Covers all systems and climates. Best as lookup reference for intermediate students, not linear reading.

- **David Holmgren — "Permaculture: Principles and Pathways Beyond Sustainability"** (2002)  
  Articulates the 12 design principles framework. More philosophical and principle-focused than Mollison. Excellent for understanding *why* behind design decisions.  
  Website: https://www.permacultureprinciples.com/

- **Toby Hemenway — "Gaia's Garden"** (2009)  
  Most accessible introduction to permaculture design. Urban/suburban focus. Perfect for intermediate students wanting practical application. Covers guilds, soil building, water, and design process clearly.

- **Ben Falk — "The Resilient Farm and Homestead"** (2013)  
  Case study-based, cold climate focus. Strong on water systems, earthworks, and whole-site design. Shows real-world implementation and problem-solving.

### Online Courses & Educational Platforms
- **Permaculture Research Institute** — https://www.permaculturenews.org/  
  Extensive article archive, case studies, regional guides. Geoff Lawton's online PDC courses. Global examples.

- **Oregon State University — Ecampus Permaculture Resources**  
  Academic treatment of permaculture. Andrew Millison's work on water harvesting and regenerative agriculture.

- **Permaculture Principles** — https://www.permacultureprinciples.com/  
  David Holmgren's official site. Deep-dive into each of the 12 principles with examples, posters, and educational materials.

### Interactive Tools & Software

**Site Analysis**
- **SunCalc** — https://www.suncalc.org/  
  Sun position calculator for sector analysis. Essential for solar planning.

- **Google Earth**  
  Satellite imagery, terrain view, historical imagery for observing site changes over time.

- **USDA Web Soil Survey** — https://websoilsurvey.nrcs.usda.gov/  
  Detailed soil data for US sites. Critical for earthworks and plant selection.

- **NOAA Climate Data** — https://www.ncdc.noaa.gov/  
  Historical weather data, precipitation patterns, frost dates.

**Plant Selection & Guild Design**
- **USDA Plants Database** — https://plants.usda.gov/  
  Native and introduced plant database, hardiness zones, characteristics.

- **iNaturalist** — https://www.inaturalist.org/  
  Species identification, local biodiversity observation. Citizen science platform for site ecology.

- **PFAF (Plants For A Future)** — https://pfaf.org/  
  Edible and useful plants database. Searchable by function, climate, and characteristics. Essential for guild design.

- **Dave's Garden — Plant Files** — https://davesgarden.com/guides/pf/  
  Community-driven plant database with growing experiences and reviews.

**Design & Mapping**
- **QGIS** — https://qgis.org/ (free GIS software)  
  Professional mapping and spatial analysis. Overkill for small sites, valuable for larger properties.

- **Inkscape** — https://inkscape.org/ (free vector graphics)  
  Create clean site plans and design drawings.

- **SketchUp Free** — https://www.sketchup.com/  
  3D modeling for structures and landscape visualization.

### Books (Specialized Topics)

**Water Systems**
- **Brad Lancaster — "Rainwater Harvesting for Drylands and Beyond"** (series)  
  Comprehensive water harvesting reference. All climates, urban to rural.

- **P.A. Yeomans — "Water for Every Farm"**  
  Keyline design fundamentals. Historic but foundational for landscape water systems.

**Soil & Fertility**
- **Jeff Lowenfels & Wayne Lewis — "Teaming with Microbes"**  
  Soil food web explained accessibly. Practical applications for gardeners.

- **Elaine Ingham — Soil Food Web School**  
  Scientific foundation for soil biology and compost quality.

**Forest Gardens & Perennials**
- **Martin Crawford — "Creating a Forest Garden"**  
  Temperate forest garden design. Plant lists, guilds, management. UK-focused but broadly applicable.

- **Eric Toensmeier — "Perennial Vegetables" and "The Carbon Farming Solution"**  
  Edible perennials and climate mitigation through agriculture.

**Animals & Intensive Systems**
- **Joel Salatin — "You Can Farm" and "Salad Bar Beef"**  
  Rotational grazing, mobile animal systems, polyface farm model.

- **Sepp Holzer — "Sepp Holzer's Permaculture"**  
  Alpine permaculture, creative animal integration, terracing.
