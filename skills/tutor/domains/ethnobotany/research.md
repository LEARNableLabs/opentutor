# Ethnobotany — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Plant Domestication and Agricultural Origins
- Centers of origin (Vavilov centers)
- Coevolution of plants and human societies
- Traditional agricultural systems (milpa, terrace farming, agroforestry)
- Seed saving and crop diversity

### 2. Traditional Ecological Knowledge (TEK)
- Indigenous plant classification systems
- Folk taxonomy and nomenclature
- Knowledge transmission across generations
- TEK validation through scientific research

### 3. Medicinal Plants and Pharmacology
- Traditional healing systems (Ayurveda, Traditional Chinese Medicine, curanderismo)
- Bioactive compounds and drug discovery
- Ethnopharmacology methods
- WHO traditional medicine programs

### 4. Plants in Culture and Ritual
- Psychoactive plants and shamanic traditions
- Sacred plants and religious practices
- Plants in mythology and folklore
- Symbolic uses of plants

### 5. Food Systems and Nutrition
- Wild food plants and foraging
- Traditional food processing techniques
- Nutritional ecology
- Food sovereignty movements

### 6. Material Culture
- Fiber plants and textiles
- Dye plants
- Construction materials
- Tools and technology

### 7. Conservation and Biocultural Diversity
- Biocultural conservation approaches
- Indigenous land management
- Ethnobotanical gardens
- Benefit sharing and biopiracy

### 8. Contemporary Applications
- Economic botany
- Sustainable development
- Climate adaptation strategies
- Urban ethnobotany
