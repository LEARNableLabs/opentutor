# Braille — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Historical Development
- Louis Braille's life and invention (1809-1852)
- Pre-Braille tactile reading systems (Haüy, Barbier's night writing)
- Adoption struggles and standardization
- Evolution of unified English Braille code
- International variations and World Braille Council

### 2. The Braille Code System
- Six-dot cell structure and numbering
- Grade 1 vs Grade 2 (contracted) Braille
- Nemeth Code for mathematics
- Music Braille (Braille music notation)
- Eight-dot Braille for computer applications
- Unicode Braille patterns

### 3. Literacy and Education
- Braille literacy rates and challenges
- Teaching methodologies (whole-word vs phonics-based)
- Age-appropriate instruction
- Dual-media literacy (Braille + audio)
- Deafblind communication
- Braille vs audio debate

### 4. Modern Technology
- Refreshable Braille displays
- Braille embossers and translation software
- Screen readers with Braille output
- Braille notetakers and smart devices
- Cost and accessibility barriers
- Integration with mainstream technology

### 5. Tactile Design Principles
- Tactile graphics and raised-line drawings
- Wayfinding and environmental signage
- Tactile maps and diagrams
- Three-dimensional modeling
- Material selection and durability
- Testing and user feedback

### 6. Universal Design and Policy
- ADA requirements for Braille signage
- International accessibility standards
- Museum and public space accessibility
- Product labeling and packaging
- Advocacy and disability rights
- Future of Braille in a digital age

## Key Sources

### Primary Educational Resources
- **American Foundation for the Blind** (afb.org) — comprehensive history, literacy research, technology guides
- **Perkins School for the Blind** (perkins.org) — teaching resources, professional development
- **National Braille Press** (nbp.org) — Braille production, children's literacy
- **Braille Institute** (brailleinstitute.org) — adult education, instructional materials
- **Library of Congress, National Library Service** (loc.gov/nls) — standards, talking books and Braille

### Academic and Historical
- **UNESCO** — literacy statistics, international education policy
- **Royal National Institute of Blind People (RNIB)** — UK-based research and advocacy
- **Journal of Visual Impairment & Blindness** — peer-reviewed literacy research
- **APH (American Printing House for the Blind)** — educational products, ConnectCenter database

### Technical Standards
- **Unified English Braille (UEB)** specifications
- **Braille Authority of North America (BANA)** — code standards
- **World Braille Council** — international unification efforts

### Tactile Design
- **Lighthouse Guild** — environmental design, wayfinding
- **Tactile Graphics Image Library** (TGIL)
- **3D printing for accessibility** — research papers and maker community projects
