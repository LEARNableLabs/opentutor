# Epigenetics — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Foundational Mechanisms
- **DNA methylation** — CpG islands, methyltransferases (DNMTs), demethylation pathways
- **Histone modifications** — acetylation, methylation, phosphorylation, ubiquitination; writers, readers, erasers
- **Chromatin remodeling** — nucleosome positioning, ATP-dependent remodelers, chromatin accessibility
- **Non-coding RNAs** — microRNAs, long non-coding RNAs (lncRNAs), their regulatory roles

### 2. Regulatory Processes
- **Gene expression control** — transcriptional activation and silencing through epigenetic marks
- **Genomic imprinting** — parent-of-origin specific expression
- **X-chromosome inactivation** — dosage compensation mechanisms in mammals
- **Position effect variegation** — heterochromatin spreading and boundary elements

### 3. Development and Differentiation
- **Embryonic development** — epigenetic reprogramming, pluripotency maintenance
- **Cell fate decisions** — lineage commitment through chromatin states
- **Stem cell biology** — epigenetic regulation of self-renewal and differentiation
- **Cellular memory** — maintenance of cell-type-specific gene expression

### 4. Environmental Interactions
- **Environmental epigenetics** — diet, stress, toxins affecting epigenetic marks
- **Transgenerational inheritance** — epigenetic information across generations
- **Developmental origins of disease** — fetal programming and adult health outcomes

### 5. Disease and Therapeutics
- **Cancer epigenetics** — DNA hypermethylation, histone modification changes, chromatin remodelers as oncogenes
- **Neurological disorders** — Rett syndrome, Fragile X, epigenetics in learning and memory
- **Metabolic diseases** — Type 2 diabetes, obesity, epigenetic contributions
- **Epigenetic therapy** — DNMT inhibitors, HDAC inhibitors, drug development

### 6. Methods and Technologies
- **Genome-wide profiling** — ChIP-seq, ATAC-seq, bisulfite sequencing, WGBS
- **Single-cell epigenomics** — scATAC-seq, single-cell methylation analysis
- **CRISPR-based tools** — targeted epigenome editing (dCas9 fusions)
- **Computational approaches** — epigenome data analysis, machine learning applications

## Pedagogical Notes

For **intermediate** students:
- Assume basic molecular biology (DNA structure, transcription, translation)
- Build from mechanism to function to disease
- Emphasize experimental evidence and how we know what we know
- Connect to current research frontiers (single-cell, CRISPR editing)
- Include computational/data science aspects given modern genomics emphasis
- Address misconceptions about heritability and Lamarckian evolution
