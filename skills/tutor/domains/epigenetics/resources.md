# Epigenetics — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks
- **Allis et al., _Epigenetics_ (2nd Edition, Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory Press, 2015)** — The comprehensive reference for the field. Excellent for intermediate students who want depth on mechanisms. Covers all major topics with contributions from leading researchers.
  - https://www.amazon.com/Epigenetics-Second-Cold-Spring-Laboratory/dp/1936113597

- **Carey, _Epigenetics_ (Academic Cell, 2021)** — More accessible than Allis, good balance of mechanisms and applications. Strong on disease and therapeutics.
  - https://www.elsevier.com/books/epigenetics/carey/978-0-12-824050-4

- **Ptashne, _Genes & Signals_ (2002)** — Classic short book on gene regulation, foundational for understanding chromatin regulation.

### Review Articles
- **Berger et al., "An operational definition of epigenetics" (_Genes & Development_, 2009)** — Defines what counts as epigenetic, resolves terminological confusion.
  - https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2684276/

- **Allis & Jenuwein, "The molecular hallmarks of epigenetic control" (_Nature Reviews Genetics_, 2016)** — Modern synthesis of the field.
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/nrg.2016.59

- **Smith & Meissner, "DNA methylation: roles in mammalian development" (_Nature Reviews Genetics_, 2013)** — Comprehensive review of methylation biology.
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/nrg3354

- **Bannister & Kouzarides, "Regulation of chromatin by histone modifications" (_Cell Research_, 2011)** — Deep dive into histone marks and their functions.
  - https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3193420/

- **Cedar & Bergman, "Programming of DNA methylation patterns" (_Annual Review of Biochemistry_, 2012)** — How methylation patterns are established and maintained.
  - https://www.annualreviews.org/doi/10.1146/annurev-biochem-052610-091920

### Interactive Tools

#### Genome Browsers (visualize epigenetic data)
- **UCSC Genome Browser** — Gold standard for viewing ChIP-seq, methylation, ATAC-seq tracks. Essential for data literacy.
  - https://genome.ucsc.edu/
  
- **WashU Epigenome Browser** — Excellent for multi-track epigenome data visualization, good for comparing cell types.
  - https://epigenomegateway.wustl.edu/

- **Ensembl Genome Browser** — European alternative, includes regulatory build (predicted regulatory elements from ENCODE/Roadmap data).
  - https://www.ensembl.org/

- **IGV (Integrative Genomics Viewer)** — Downloadable tool for local visualization of epigenomic datasets.
  - https://software.broadinstitute.org/software/igv/

#### Databases
- **ENCODE Portal** — Comprehensive epigenomic datasets across cell types and assays. Search by gene, region, or experiment type.
  - https://www.encodeproject.org/

- **NIH Roadmap Epigenomics** — Reference epigenomes for 127 human tissues/cell types.
  - http://www.roadmapepigenomics.org/

- **IHEC Data Portal (International Human Epigenome Consortium)** — Global collection of reference epigenomes.
  - https://epigenomesportal.ca/ihec/

- **DiseaseMeth** — Database of disease-associated DNA methylation changes.
  - http://bio-bigdata.hrbmu.edu.cn/diseasemeth/

- **MethBase** — DNA methylation database across species.
  - http://smithlabresearch.org/software/methbase/

#### Molecule Viewers
- **RCSB PDB-101: Nucleosome** — 3D structure of nucleosome, visualize DNA wrapping around histones.
  - https://pdb101.rcsb.org/motm/7

- **Protein Data Bank: Chromatin Structures** — Search for chromatin remodeler structures, histone modifying enzymes.
  - https://www.rcsb.org/

### Code and Analysis Tools

#### R/Bioconductor Packages
- **ChIPseeker** — Annotation and visualization of ChIP-seq data.
  - https://bioconductor.org/packages/ChIPseeker/

- **methylKit** — Analysis of DNA methylation data from bisulfite sequencing.
  - https://bioconductor.org/packages/methylKit/

- **GenomicRanges** — Core infrastructure for genomic interval operations.
  - https://bioconductor.org/packages/GenomicRanges/

- **chromVAR** — Analyzing variability in chromatin accessibility.
  - https://bioconductor.org/packages/chromVAR/

#### Python Tools
- **deepTools** — Suite for NGS data analysis, excellent for ChIP-seq, ATAC-seq visualization (heatmaps, profiles).
  - https://deeptools.readthedocs.io/

- **PyMethylation** — DNA methylation analysis toolkit.
  - https://github.com/christacaggiano/pyMethylation

#### Web-based Analysis
- **Galaxy** — Web platform for genomic data analysis, includes epigenomics workflows.
  - https://usegalaxy.org/

- **Cistrome** — ChIP-seq analysis platform, integrates data from thousands of experiments.
  - http://cistrome.org/

### Unexpected Connections (Rabbit Holes)

#### Evolution and Comparative Epigenomics
- **Yoav Gilad Lab (UChicago)** — Comparative epigenomics across primates, regulatory evolution
  - https://giladlab.uchicago.edu/

- **Evolutionary origins of imprinting** — Why does parent-of-origin expression exist? Kinship theory (Haig hypothesis)
  - Haig D, "The kinship theory of genomic imprinting" (_Annual Review of Ecology and Systematics_, 2000)

#### Epigenetics in Non-model Organisms
- **Honeybee epigenetics** — Caste determination via nutrition and DNA methylation
  - https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2346549/

- **Plant epigenetics** — Extensive methylation, siRNAs, RdDM pathway
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/nrg2719

#### Neuroscience and Cognition
- **Sweatt Lab (Vanderbilt)** — Epigenetics of learning and memory
  - https://lab.vanderbilt.edu/sweatt-lab/

- **HDAC inhibitors for memory** — Drugs that enhance memory consolidation
  - https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2755056/

#### Philosophy and Ethics
- **The ethics of epigenetic information** — Should prenatal exposures be tracked? Implications for inequality?
  - Richardson S, "Transgenerational epigenetic inheritance: myths and mechanisms" (_Cell_, 2014)

- **Epigenetics and determinism** — Does epigenetics challenge genetic determinism or create a new form?
  - Meloni M, "How biology became social, and what it means for social theory" (_The Sociological Review_, 2014)

## Historical Papers (Foundations)

- **Riggs, "X inactivation, differentiation, and DNA methylation" (_Cytogenet Cell Genet_, 1975)** — Proposes methylation role in X-inactivation
- **Bird & Wolffe, "Methylation-induced repression—belts, braces, and chromatin" (_Cell_, 1999)** — Connects methylation to chromatin
- **Strahl & Allis, "The language of covalent histone modifications" (_Nature_, 2000)** — Introduces "histone code" hypothesis
- **Reik et al., "Genomic imprinting: parental influence on the genome" (_Nature Reviews Genetics_, 2001)** — Review of imprinting mechanisms
- **Lee, "The X-inactivation center and chromosome territories" (_J Cell Biochem_, 2000)** — Xist and nuclear organization
