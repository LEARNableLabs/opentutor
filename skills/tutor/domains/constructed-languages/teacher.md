## Common Failure Modes
1. **"All conlangs are like Esperanto"** — Students often assume all constructed languages are auxiliary languages designed for ease of learning. Reality: the vast majority are artistic (Tolkien, media conlangs) or experimental (Lojban, Ithkuil). Correct this early in Lesson 1-4 by showing the diversity of purposes and approaches.

2. **"Conlangs are inherently simpler than natural languages"** — Many assume constructed languages are "dumbed down." Counter-example: Lojban and Ithkuil are far more complex than most natural languages. Regularity ≠ simplicity. Address explicitly in Lesson 15 when introducing Lojban.

3. **"A posteriori means 'easy to learn'"** — Students confuse "based on existing languages" with "easy." Interlingua may look familiar to Romance speakers but is still a full language to learn. Clarify in Lessons 10-13 that familiarity helps but doesn't eliminate learning effort.
