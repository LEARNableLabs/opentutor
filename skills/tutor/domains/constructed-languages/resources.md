# Constructed Languages — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Books
- **"The Art of Language Invention: From Horse-Lords to Dark Elves, the Words Behind World-Building"** by David J. Peterson (2015) — written by the creator of Dothraki and High Valyrian. Comprehensive guide to creating languages for fiction, covering phonology, morphology, syntax, and worldbuilding integration. Best for Lessons 19-25.
  - [Publisher page](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/316309/the-art-of-language-invention-by-david-j-peterson/)

- **"In the Land of Invented Languages: Esperanto Rock Stars, Klingon Poets, Loglan Lovers, and the Mad Dreamers Who Tried to Build A Perfect Language"** by Arika Okrent (2009) — engaging history and cultural analysis of major conlangs. Accessible, funny, well-researched. Excellent for Lessons 1-4, 10-13.
  - [Publisher page](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/301834/in-the-land-of-invented-languages-by-arika-okrent/)

- **"The Language Construction Kit"** by Mark Rosenfelder (2010) — practical, detailed guide to building a language from scratch. Covers phonology, morphology, syntax, semantics, and historical change. Essential reference for Lessons 5-9, 23-25.
  - [Online version](https://zompist.com/kit.html)
  - [Extended version (book)](https://www.zompist.com/kitbook.html)

- **"Toki Pona: The Language of Good"** by Sonja Lang (2014) — official book for Toki Pona, covers philosophy, vocabulary, grammar, and usage. Primary source for Lessons 14, 16.
  - [Official site](https://tokipona.org)

- **"The Complete Lojban Language"** by John Cowan (1997) — comprehensive reference grammar for Lojban. Technical but authoritative. For advanced students or reference during Lesson 15.
  - [Free online version](https://lojban.org/publications/cll/cll_v1.1_book.pdf)

- **"Fundamento de Esperanto"** by L.L. Zamenhof (1905) — foundational document of Esperanto, historically important. For context in Lesson 10.
  - [Online version](https://bertilow.com/fundamento/)

## Unexpected Connections (for wild cards)

### Cross-Discipline Links
- **Music and Language**: Solresol (musical language where notes = morphemes)
  - [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solresol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solresol)

- **Mathematics and Language**: Ithkuil's morphophonological algorithm, Lojban's predicate logic foundations
  - Connection to formal semantics and lambda calculus

- **Computer Science**: Parsing algorithms, formal grammars, programming language design principles
  - Compare Lojban's PEG grammar to LALR parsers

- **Philosophy**: Sapir-Whorf hypothesis (linguistic relativity), Wittgenstein's language games, semantic primitives (Anna Wierzbicka's Natural Semantic Metalanguage)
  - Connection to Toki Pona's philosophical minimalism

- **Accessibility**: Blissymbolics used in AAC (augmentative and alternative communication)
  - [https://www.blissymbolics.org](https://www.blissymbolics.org)

- **Deaf Culture**: Gestuno (international sign language), relation to natural sign languages
  - Discussion of modality in language design

### Pop Culture Entry Points
- **Lord of the Rings** (films) — Sindarin and Quenya subtitles and dialogue
- **Game of Thrones / House of the Dragon** — Dothraki and High Valyrian
- **Avatar** (James Cameron) — Na'vi language
- **Star Trek** — Klingon (most developed alien language in sci-fi)
- **The Expanse** — Lang Belta (creole-based conlang)
