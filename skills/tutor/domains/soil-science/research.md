# Soil Science & Pedology — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Pedogenesis (Soil Formation)
- Five soil-forming factors (climate, organisms, relief, parent material, time)
- Weathering processes (physical, chemical, biological)
- Soil horizonation and profile development
- Time scales of soil formation

### 2. Soil Morphology
- Soil horizons (O, A, E, B, C, R)
- Color, texture, and structure
- Soil consistence and porosity
- Field description methods

### 3. Soil Classification
- USDA Soil Taxonomy (12 orders)
- WRB (World Reference Base)
- Diagnostic horizons and properties
- Soil series and families

### 4. Physical Properties
- Particle size distribution (sand, silt, clay)
- Soil structure and aggregation
- Water retention and movement
- Bulk density and porosity

### 5. Chemical Properties
- pH and buffering capacity
- Cation exchange capacity (CEC)
- Nutrient cycling (N, P, K, micronutrients)
- Organic matter and humus
- Redox reactions

### 6. Soil Biology
- Soil food web
- Microbial communities
- Macroorganisms (earthworms, arthropods)
- Rhizosphere processes

### 7. Soil Survey & Mapping
- Field techniques
- Remote sensing applications
- Digital soil mapping
- Soil information systems

### 8. Applied Soil Science
- Soil fertility and amendment
- Erosion and conservation
- Land use planning
- Environmental quality (contamination, remediation)

## Research Depth

This intermediate-level curriculum assumes:
- Basic chemistry and geology background
- Familiarity with scientific method
- Some environmental science exposure

Focus areas:
- Conceptual understanding of processes
- Practical field/lab skills
- Classification proficiency
- Applied problem-solving
