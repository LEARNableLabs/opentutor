# Soil Science & Pedology — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks
- **Brady, Nyle C. & Weil, Ray R. "The Nature and Properties of Soils" (15th edition, 2016)** — The standard comprehensive textbook. Covers all major topics with good balance of theory and application. Accessible for intermediate students. Available through university libraries and used book markets.

- **Schaetzl, Randall J. & Anderson, Sharon. "Soils: Genesis and Geomorphology" (2005)** — Excellent for understanding soil formation and landscape relationships. More detailed on pedogenesis than Brady & Weil. Good for students interested in the geological aspects.

- **Buol, S.W., Southard, R.J., Graham, R.C., & McDaniel, P.A. "Soil Genesis and Classification" (6th edition, 2011)** — Authoritative text on soil formation and USDA taxonomy. More technical than Brady & Weil; good reference for classification deep-dives.

- **White, Robert E. "Principles and Practice of Soil Science: The Soil as a Natural Resource" (4th edition, 2005)** — British perspective, more applied/practical orientation. Good complement to US-focused texts.

### Open Educational Resources
- **UC Davis SoilWeb** (https://casoilresource.lawr.ucdavis.edu/soilweb-apps/) — Interactive soil survey app with map interface. Essential tool for exploring real soil data.

- **USDA NRCS Soil Science Division** (https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/conservation-basics/natural-resource-concerns/soils) — Official resources, guides, manuals. Free access to Soil Survey Manual, Keys to Soil Taxonomy, and educational materials.

- **Soil Science Society of America** (https://www.soils.org/about-soils/) — "About Soils" section has accessible explanations of core concepts. Good for quick reference.

- **FAO Soils Portal** (https://www.fao.org/soils-portal/en/) — Global perspective, World Reference Base classification, international soil resources. Good for non-US contexts.

### University Course Materials
- **Cornell SOIL courses** — Cornell's Soil Science Department has some open materials. Check their department website for syllabi and lecture notes.

- **Iowa State University Agronomy** — Strong soil science program with educational resources.

- **UC Davis Land, Air and Water Resources** — Home of SoilWeb and other digital resources.

## Supplementary (for engagement)

### Videos & Channels
- **Soil Science Society of America YouTube** (https://www.youtube.com/@SoilScienceSocietyofAmerica) — Educational videos on various soil topics, ranging from basic to advanced.

- **Kiss the Ground (2020)** — Netflix documentary on soil health and regenerative agriculture. Good for motivation/context, though not a technical resource. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K3-V1j-zMZw (trailer)

- **Symphony of the Soil (2012)** — Documentary exploring soil as living ecosystem. More lyrical than Kiss the Ground. https://www.symphonyofthesoil.com/

- **University lecture series** — Many soil science courses are available on YouTube. Search "soil science lectures" + university name (e.g., UC Davis, Cornell, Texas A&M).

### Interactive Tools
- **USDA Web Soil Survey** (https://websoilsurvey.nrcs.usda.gov/) — Official tool for accessing detailed soil survey data for any location in the US. Essential for real-world soil investigation.

- **SoilWeb Earth** (https://casoilresource.lawr.ucdavis.edu/soilweb-apps/) — Google Earth interface for soil data. Visualize soils in 3D landscape context.

- **NRCS Soil Texture Calculator** (https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/resources/guides-and-instructions/soil-texture-calculator) — Interactive texture triangle tool.

- **SoilGrids** (https://soilgrids.org/) — Global soil information at 250m resolution from ISRIC. For international soil data.

- **iSDAsoil** (https://www.isda-africa.com/isdasoil/) — High-resolution soil data for Africa. Example of cutting-edge digital soil mapping.

### Code & Data Resources
- **aqp R package** (http://ncss-tech.github.io/aqp/) — Algorithms for Quantitative Pedology in R. For students interested in soil data analysis and visualization.

- **soilDB R package** (https://github.com/ncss-tech/soilDB) — Access NRCS soil databases from R. Programmatic access to US soil data.

- **NASIS database** — National Soil Information System used by NRCS. Advanced users can request access for detailed soil data.

- **SSURGO/STATSGO datasets** — Spatial soil data at different scales. Available through USDA Geospatial Data Gateway.

### People & Researchers to Follow
- **Hans Jenny** (historical) — Developed the CLORPT model of soil formation. Classic book: "The Soil Resource" (1980).

- **Rattan Lal** (Ohio State) — Leading researcher on soil carbon sequestration and climate change. Prolific author.

- **Christine Jones** — Australian soil ecologist focused on soil carbon and regenerative agriculture. Bridges science and practice.

- **Elaine Ingham** — Soil microbiologist known for work on soil food web. Controversial in some circles but influential in raising awareness of soil biology.

- **Dylan Warren** (UC Davis) — Active in digital soil mapping and soil survey modernization.

- **NRCS state soil scientists** — Many states have excellent communicators sharing soil information on social media and blogs.

### Podcasts & Blogs
- **The Soiled Diaries** — Podcast by soil scientists Jocelyn Lavallee and Amy Ganguli. Conversational, accessible.

- **Plant Proof Podcast** (select episodes on soil health) — Nutrition podcast that occasionally features soil scientists discussing food-soil connections.

- **NRCS blog** (https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/news) — News and stories related to soil conservation.

- **SoilWeb blog** (https://casoilresource.lawr.ucdavis.edu/blog/) — Technical blog about soil data, mapping, and digital tools from UC Davis group.

### Organizations & Communities
- **Soil Science Society of America (SSSA)** (https://www.soils.org/) — Professional society with publications, meetings, certification programs. Student memberships available.

- **National Cooperative Soil Survey (NCSS)** (https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/resources/guides-and-instructions/national-cooperative-soil-survey-ncss) — Partnership creating soil surveys. Get involved as volunteer or student.

- **International Union of Soil Sciences (IUSS)** (https://www.iuss.org/) — Global organization for soil science. World Congress every 4 years.

- **Soil Health Institute** (https://soilhealthinstitute.org/) — Applied research and outreach on soil health. Good for agricultural applications.

## Unexpected Connections

### Art & Soil
- **Soil as pigment** — Many natural pigments come from soils (ochres, umbers, siennas). Connection to art history and geology.
- **Land art** — Artists like Robert Smithson ("Spiral Jetty") work directly with earth materials.

### Archaeology & Soil
- **Soil micromorphology** — Thin sections of soil used to understand ancient human activities, settlement patterns, and environmental change.
- **Anthrosols** — Soils modified by human activity. Terra preta in Amazon, plaggen soils in Netherlands.

### Medicine & Soil
- **Antibiotics from soil** — Many antibiotics (streptomycin, tetracycline) discovered in soil bacteria (Streptomyces).
- **Geomedicine** — Study of relationships between geological environment and health. Selenium deficiency, arsenic contamination, etc.

### Wine & Terroir
- **Soil and wine character** — How soil properties influence grape quality and wine flavor. Limestone in Burgundy, slate in Mosel, volcanic soils in Sicily.
- **Viticulture resources** — Wine industry pays intense attention to soil; interesting applied context for pedology.

### Philosophy & Soil
- **Aldo Leopold** — "The land ethic" in "A Sand County Almanac" (1949). Soil as component of ecological community deserving ethical consideration.
- **Wendell Berry** — Farmer, poet, essayist on agriculture and soil stewardship. "The Unsettling of America" (1977).

### Literature & Soil
- **John Steinbeck, "The Grapes of Wrath"** — Dust Bowl migration. Soil degradation as central plot driver.
- **Kristin Kimball, "The Dirty Life"** — Memoir of starting a farm; learning about soil through practice.

### Music & Soil
- **Woody Guthrie, "Dust Bowl Ballads"** — Songs documenting ecological disaster of the 1930s.
- **Pete Seeger, "My Dirty Stream (The Hudson River Song)"** — Environmental folk music often references soil, erosion, and land degradation.

## Professional Development

### Certifications
- **Certified Professional Soil Scientist (CPSS)** — Professional credential from SSSA. Requires education + experience + exam.
- **Certified Professional Soil Classifier (CPSC)** — Specialized in soil classification and survey.

### Career Paths
- **Soil survey / NRCS** — Government positions mapping and classifying soils.
- **Environmental consulting** — Wetland delineation, contaminated site assessment, land use planning.
- **Agriculture** — Soil fertility consulting, precision agriculture, crop advising.
- **Research** — Universities, USDA-ARS, national labs, NGOs.
- **Education** — Extension, outreach, K-12 education (Project Learning Tree, etc.).
- **International development** — FAO, NGOs working on food security, sustainable agriculture, land degradation.

### Field Equipment & Methods
- **Munsell Soil Color Book** — Standard tool for soil color determination. Available from SSSA or geological supply companies.
- **Texture by feel** — Skill developed through practice. NRCS has guides and videos.
- **pH test kits** — Portable kits for field pH testing (e.g., Rapitest, LaMotte).
- **Soil augers and probes** — Bucket augers, push probes for sampling. See forestry supply catalogs.
- **Field guides** — "Field Book for Describing and Sampling Soils" (USDA-NRCS version 3.0, 2012).
