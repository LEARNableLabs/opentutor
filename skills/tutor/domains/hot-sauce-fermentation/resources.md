# Hot Sauce Fermentation — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Books


- **"The Art of Fermentation"** by Sandor Ellix Katz — Comprehensive cultural and practical guide to fermentation. Less technical than Noma, more philosophical. Great for understanding wild fermentation traditions. [https://www.wildfermentation.com/the-art-of-fermentation/](https://www.wildfermentation.com/the-art-of-fermentation/)


- **"The Pepper Trail: History and Recipes from Around the World"** by Jean Andrews — Historical and botanical deep dive. Excellent for understanding pepper varietals and regional fermentation traditions. [https://www.amazon.com/Pepper-Trail-History-Recipes-Around/dp/1574160184](https://www.amazon.com/Pepper-Trail-History-Recipes-Around/dp/1574160184)

### Academic & Technical


- **Journal of Food Science** — Regular papers on capsaicin chemistry, LAB fermentation kinetics, and hot sauce safety. Search "capsaicin fermentation" or "lactic acid pepper." [https://ift.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/17503841](https://ift.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/17503841)

- **Food Microbiology (journal)** — Deep dives into LAB species, succession dynamics, and safety. More advanced but critical for understanding the microbiology. [https://www.journals.elsevier.com/food-microbiology](https://www.journals.elsevier.com/food-microbiology)


### Online Courses & Educational Resources


- **Cultures for Health Learning Library** — Free tutorials on LAB fermentation, starter cultures, troubleshooting. Very beginner-friendly. [https://www.culturesforhealth.com/learn/](https://www.culturesforhealth.com/learn/)

- **MIT OpenCourseWare: Food Fermentation Microbiology** — Lecture notes and readings from MIT's food science program. Advanced but free. [https://ocw.mit.edu/](https://ocw.mit.edu/)

## Supplementary (for engagement)

### Videos & Channels

- **Brad Leone's "It's Alive" series** (Bon Appétit YouTube) — Episode on fermented hot sauce is a masterclass in accessible fermentation education. Entertaining and informative. [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UGjCeAbWKPo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UGjCeAbWKPo)




- **Tabasco Factory Tour** (virtual) — See industrial fermentation at scale. Three-year barrel aging process. [https://www.tabasco.com/visit-avery-island/](https://www.tabasco.com/visit-avery-island/)

### Interactive Tools & Calculators

- **MolView** — Visualize capsaicin, lactic acid, and other molecules in 3D. Great for understanding structure. [https://molview.org/](https://molview.org/)




- **Lactobacillus Growth Curve Simulator** — Model fermentation kinetics. More advanced. [https://www.aem.asm.org/simulators](https://www.aem.asm.org/simulators)

- **Hot Sauce Recipe Database** (community-maintained) — Hundreds of recipes with ratings and reviews. Good for comparative analysis. [https://www.hotsaucerecipes.com/](https://www.hotsaucerecipes.com/)

### Commercial Hot Sauce Case Studies

Study these for reverse-engineering exercises:

- **Tabasco Original Red Sauce** — Three-year barrel-aged, wild fermentation, minimal ingredients. Pure expression of time + terroir. [https://www.tabasco.com/](https://www.tabasco.com/)

- **Huy Fong Sriracha** — Short fermentation, high garlic, paste texture. Study in adjunct balance. [https://www.huyfong.com/](https://www.huyfong.com/)

- **Cholula** — Arbol and piquin peppers, vinegar-forward (not heavily fermented). Compare to Tabasco. [https://www.cholula.com/](https://www.cholula.com/)

- **Yellowbird** — Modern craft brand, organic, farmer's market aesthetic. Study in marketing + process. [https://www.yellowbirdfoods.com/](https://www.yellowbirdfoods.com/)

- **Marie Sharp's** — Habanero-carrot blend from Belize. Fruit adjunct case study. [https://mariesharpsusa.com/](https://mariesharpsusa.com/)

## Unexpected Connections

### Cross-Discipline Links

- **Kimchi fermentation** — Similar LAB process but with different vegetables and flavor profile. Study for comparison. [https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/kimchi](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/kimchi)

- **Sourdough starter** — Another LAB + wild yeast fermentation. Compare microbial succession. [https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/sourdough-starter-recipe](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/sourdough-starter-recipe)

- **Kombucha SCOBY** — Acetic acid fermentation vs. lactic acid. Contrast the biochemistry. [https://www.kombuchakamp.com/](https://www.kombuchakamp.com/)

- **Wine/beer fermentation** — Alcoholic vs. lactic fermentation. Same "create an environment" principle. [https://winemakermag.com/](https://winemakermag.com/)

- **Cheese aging** — Flavor development over time, competing microbes, safety through acidification. [https://www.cheesemaking.com/](https://www.cheesemaking.com/)

### Wild Cards



- **Industrial fermentation scaling** — How does Tabasco ferment 10,000 barrels at once? Process control challenges. [https://www.foodengineeringmag.com/](https://www.foodengineeringmag.com/)

## Pepper Sourcing

- **Farmer's markets** — Fresh, ripe peppers at peak season. Build relationships with growers.
- **Specialty seed companies** — Baker Creek, Johnny's Selected Seeds for heirloom varieties. [https://www.rareseeds.com/](https://www.rareseeds.com/)
- **Online pepper vendors** — Refining Fire Chiles for fresh and dried specialty peppers. [https://www.superhotchiles.com/](https://www.superhotchiles.com/)
- **Grow your own** — Ultimate control over variety and ripeness. See r/HotPeppers. [https://www.reddit.com/r/HotPeppers/](https://www.reddit.com/r/HotPeppers/)

## Starter Culture Sources (if not doing wild fermentation)

- **Cultures for Health LAB Culture** — Specific Lactobacillus strains. [https://www.culturesforhealth.com/](https://www.culturesforhealth.com/)
- **Whey from live yogurt** — DIY starter option. Contains Lactobacillus acidophilus.
