- **Socratic questions** — 4 lessons (15%)
- **real-world application challenges** — 4 lessons (15%)

## Difficulty Curve
Gentle ramp — heavily front-loaded with accessible material before increasing complexity.

Distribution: 54% accessible (1-2), 38% standard (3), 8% challenging (4-5).

Difficulty peaks:
- Day 10: "Why do collectors prize plate flaws and re-entries?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 15: "How can you tell if a rare stamp is genuine?" (difficulty 4)

## Domain Hooks
This field covers postal history and philately, with applications across theory and practice.

## Common Failure Modes
Students often struggle with the foundational concepts before building to more advanced material. Reinforce basics before progressing.
