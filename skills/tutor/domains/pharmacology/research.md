# Pharmacology — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Pharmacodynamics
How drugs interact with biological targets (receptors, enzymes, ion channels). Dose-response relationships, therapeutic index, agonists/antagonists.

### 2. Pharmacokinetics
ADME: Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism, Excretion. Half-life, bioavailability, steady-state concentration, clearance.

### 3. Receptor Theory
Receptor types (GPCR, ion channels, nuclear receptors), signal transduction, desensitization, drug-receptor binding kinetics.

### 4. Drug Classes by System
Cardiovascular (antihypertensives, anticoagulants), CNS (antidepressants, antipsychotics, anesthetics), antimicrobials, anti-inflammatory, endocrine.

### 5. Drug Development & Safety
Clinical trials, FDA approval process, adverse drug reactions, drug interactions, pharmacovigilance.

### 6. Advanced Topics
Pharmacogenomics, personalized medicine, drug resistance, biologics vs small molecules.

## Learning Approach for Intermediate Level

Students at this level should have basic chemistry (functional groups, acid/base) and biology (cell biology, basic physiology). The curriculum emphasizes:

1. **Mechanisms over memorization** — understand WHY drugs work, not just WHAT they do
2. **Clinical context** — every concept tied to real-world applications
3. **Quantitative reasoning** — dose calculations, PK/PD modeling
4. **Critical thinking** — drug design logic, predicting side effects from mechanisms
5. **Current topics** — biologics, gene therapy, resistance

Target: 28 lessons over 5 modules, building from foundational principles to clinical application.
