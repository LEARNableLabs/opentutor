# Pharmacology — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks

- **Goodman & Gilman's The Pharmacological Basis of Therapeutics** (14th ed.)
  - https://accesspharmacy.mhmedical.com/book.aspx?bookid=2189
  - Comprehensive reference, clinical focus, gold standard for mechanism + application
  - **Best for:** Detailed drug class information, clinical pharmacology, intermediate-advanced students
  - **Level match:** Excellent for intermediate; has depth to grow into

- **Katzung's Basic & Clinical Pharmacology** (15th ed.)
  - https://accesspharmacy.mhmedical.com/book.aspx?bookid=2988
  - Balance of basic science and clinical application, well-organized
  - **Best for:** Systematic learning, exam preparation, clinical correlations
  - **Level match:** Perfect for intermediate students

- **Rang & Dale's Pharmacology** (9th ed.)
  - https://www.elsevier.com/books/rang-and-dales-pharmacology/ritter/978-0-7020-7448-6
  - Strong mechanistic explanations, excellent diagrams, physiology integration
  - **Best for:** Understanding mechanisms, visual learners
  - **Level match:** Ideal for intermediate; mechanistic depth without overwhelming

### Open Educational Resources

- **StatPearls Pharmacology**
  - https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK544251/
  - Free, peer-reviewed, constantly updated, searchable by drug/topic
  - **Best for:** Quick references, specific drug lookups, clinical updates

- **MIT OpenCourseWare: 20.201 Mechanisms of Drug Actions**
  - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/20-201-mechanisms-of-drug-actions-fall-2013/
  - Lecture notes, problem sets, readings; molecular mechanisms focus
  - **Best for:** Quantitative PK/PD, signal transduction depth

- **Khan Academy Medicine: Pharmacology**
  - https://www.khanacademy.org/science/health-and-medicine/executive-systems-of-the-brain/drug-dependence
  - Free videos, accessible explanations, good for basics
  - **Best for:** Visual learners, review, foundational concepts

### Interactive Tools & Databases

- **DrugBank**
  - https://www.drugbank.com/
  - Comprehensive drug database: structures, mechanisms, targets, PK parameters
  - **How to use:** Look up specific drugs, explore drug-target relationships, compare drug classes

- **PharmGKB (Pharmacogenomics Knowledgebase)**
  - https://www.pharmgkb.org/
  - Curated database of gene-drug associations, variant effects on drug response
  - **How to use:** Explore CYP450 variants, clinical annotations, pathway diagrams
  - **Essential for:** Lesson 25 (pharmacogenomics), understanding individual variation

- **PubChem**
  - https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/
  - Chemical structures, properties, bioactivity data
  - **How to use:** Look up drug structures, compare chemical similarity, bioassay results

- **BindingDB**
  - https://www.bindingdb.org/
  - Database of measured binding affinities (Ki, Kd, IC50, EC50)
  - **How to use:** Compare drug potencies, explore SAR data
  - **Essential for:** Lesson 16 (binding affinity)

- **FDA Drug Database**
  - https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cder/daf/
  - Official drug approvals, labels, clinical trial info
  - **How to use:** Find FDA-approved indications, dosing, warnings
  - **Essential for:** Lesson 28 (drug development), understanding regulatory process

- **RxList**
  - https://www.rxlist.com/
  - Drug information, interactions checker, pill identifier
  - **How to use:** Clinical drug information, patient education materials

- **PK/PD Simulator (University of Florida)**
  - https://pkpdsim.boomer.org/
  - Interactive pharmacokinetic simulations (one-compartment, two-compartment models)
  - **How to use:** Visualize concentration-time curves, manipulate parameters (Vd, CL, ka)
  - **Essential for:** Lessons 9-11 (PK concepts)

### Code & Computational Tools

- **RxNorm (NLM)**
  - https://www.nlm.nih.gov/research/umls/rxnorm/
  - Standardized drug nomenclature, API for drug information
  - **Use for:** Data science projects, drug information retrieval

- **ChEMBL**
  - https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl/
  - Bioactivity database, API access, downloadable datasets
  - **Use for:** Computational pharmacology, machine learning projects

- **PK/PD modeling in R/Python**
  - R: `mrgsolve` package (https://github.com/metrumresearchgroup/mrgsolve)
  - Python: `PKPD` tools (https://github.com/pkpdapp-team/pkpdapp)
  - **Use for:** Simulating drug concentration profiles, custom PK/PD models

- **Molecular docking tutorials**
  - AutoDock Vina: http://vina.scripps.edu/
  - PyMOL: https://pymol.org/
  - **Use for:** Visualizing drug-receptor binding, structure-based design

## Unexpected Connections & Rabbit Holes

### Cross-Disciplinary Links

- **Economics: Patent cliffs and generic competition**
  - Why drug prices drop after patent expiry; pharmaceutical business models
  - **Resource:** https://www.fda.gov/drugs/development-approval-process-drugs/generic-drugs-questions-answers

- **Evolution: Antibiotic resistance as natural selection in real-time**
  - Fastest observable evolution; implications for drug design
  - **Resource:** https://www.cdc.gov/antibiotic-use/antibiotic-resistance.html

- **Chemistry: Chirality and enantiomers in drug action**
  - Thalidomide disaster, ibuprofen (one active enantiomer), why stereochemistry matters
  - **Resource:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6117795/

- **Engineering: Drug delivery systems (nanoparticles, controlled release)**
  - Liposomal doxorubicin, insulin pumps, depot injections
  - **Resource:** https://www.nature.com/subjects/drug-delivery

- **Data Science: Machine learning in drug discovery**
  - AlphaFold for protein structure prediction, generative models for drug design
  - **Resource:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03819-2 (AlphaFold paper)

- **History: From snake oil to clinical trials**
  - Elixir sulfanilamide disaster (1937), thalidomide (1950s-60s), why we have FDA
  - **Resource:** https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/fda-history

- **Psychology: Placebo effect and nocebo effect**
  - How expectation modulates drug response; clinical trial design implications
  - **Resource:** https://www.health.harvard.edu/mental-health/the-power-of-the-placebo-effect

### Wild Cards for Engagement

- **Why does caffeine withdrawal cause headaches?** — adenosine receptor upregulation (tolerance), then removal of antagonist
- **Can you overdose on water?** — hyponatremia, therapeutic index concept applied to nutrients
- **Why do some people get "Asian flush" from alcohol?** — ALDH2 polymorphism (pharmacogenomics in action)
- **How do truth serums work (or don't)?** — sodium pentothal (barbiturate), GABA receptor modulation, ethical/legal issues
- **Why does cheese + certain antidepressants = crisis?** — MAO inhibitors + tyramine → hypertensive crisis (drug-food interaction)

## How to Use These Resources

### For Daily Lessons
1. **Prepare:** Read textbook section (Katzung or Rang & Dale) before lesson
2. **Engage:** Watch Osmosis or Ninja Nerd video for visual reinforcement
3. **Deepen:** Explore DrugBank or PharmGKB for specific drugs/pathways
4. **Apply:** Use FDA database to find real clinical applications

### For Review Sessions
1. **Speed Pharmacology** videos for quick refreshers
2. **PK/PD simulator** to manipulate variables and see effects
3. **Practice problems** from MIT OCW problem sets

### For Going Deeper
1. **Primary literature** via PubMed (e.g., mechanism papers, clinical trials)
2. **Computational projects** using ChEMBL data
3. **Coursera courses** for structured deep-dives

### Red Flags (Resources to Avoid)
- **Outdated textbooks** (pre-2015) — pharmacology evolves rapidly
- **Non-peer-reviewed websites** (Dr. Google, unverified blogs) — misinformation risk
- **Overly simplified sources** (pop-sci articles without citations) — useful for engagement but verify with primary sources
