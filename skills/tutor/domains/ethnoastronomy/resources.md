# Ethnoastronomy — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks & Books
- **"Skywatchers" by Anthony Aveni** — comprehensive survey of archaeoastronomy across cultures, intermediate level, excellent for Americas focus
- **"The Oxford Handbook of Archaeoastronomy and Ethnoastronomy"** — academic reference covering methodology and case studies globally
- **"The Star Atlas Companion" by Philip Simpson** — cultural star lore from around the world, accessible
- **"Astronomy Across Cultures" edited by Helaine Selin** — multi-author volume with chapters on different cultural traditions
- **"Polynesian Navigation and Settlement of the Pacific" by Ben Finney** — definitive work on Polynesian wayfinding

### Academic Courses
- **Cornell University: Cultural Astronomy** — open syllabus available, covers multiple traditions
- **University of Arizona: Ethnoastronomy Research Group** — ongoing research and publications
- **MIT OpenCourseWare: Ancient Astronomy** — lecture notes and problem sets available

### Online Databases
- **World Archaeoastronomy Database** (https://www.worldarchaeoastronomy.com/) — searchable database of sites with astronomical alignments
- **International Astronomical Union: Astronomy and World Heritage** (https://www.iau.org/public/themes/cultural_astronomy/) — official IAU resources on cultural astronomy

## Supplementary (for engagement)

### Videos
- **Native Skies video series** — NSF-funded educational series on Indigenous astronomy, available on YouTube
- **Smithsonian Air & Space: Native Skies exhibit** — virtual exhibit tour and educational resources
- **"We Are Astronomers" documentary** — includes segments on Indigenous Australian astronomy
- **Hōkūle'a voyage documentaries** — multiple films on Polynesian voyaging canoe and navigation revival
- **"The Star Lore of Native America" lecture series** (YouTube) — von Del Chamberlain lectures

### Interactive Tools
- **Stellarium** (https://stellarium.org/) — free open-source planetarium with cultural skycultures (Aboriginal Australian, Polynesian, Maya, Chinese, Arabic, and many more)
- **Stellarium Web** — browser-based version, no installation needed
- **Google Sky** — accessible sky exploration tool
- **Paper astrolabe templates** — printable PDFs for building working astrolabes (astrolabes.org)
- **Star compass simulator** — interactive tools from Polynesian Voyaging Society website

### Code & Simulations
- **PyEphem** — Python library for astronomical calculations, useful for computing historical sky positions
- **SkyField** — modern Python astronomy library, can recreate ancient observations
- **NASA Horizons System** — web interface for planetary positions at any time in history

### Museums & Institutions
- **Adler Planetarium (Chicago)** — Indigenous Astronomy programs and resources
- **Smithsonian National Air & Space Museum** — Native Skies permanent exhibit
- **Griffith Observatory (Los Angeles)** — public programs on cultural astronomy
- **Polynesian Voyaging Society** (Hawaii) — Hōkūle'a education resources and navigator training
- **CSIRO Australia** — Indigenous astronomy outreach and research

### Academic Journals
- **Journal of Astronomical History and Heritage** — peer-reviewed, focus on cultural and historical astronomy
- **Archaeoastronomy: The Journal of Astronomy in Culture** — official journal of the field
- **Journal for the History of Astronomy** — historical focus, includes ethnoastronomy papers

## Unexpected Connections

### Cross-discipline Links
- **Cognitive science of pattern recognition** — why humans see faces/patterns in stars (pareidolia)
- **Archaeoacoustics** — sound properties of astronomical sites (Chichen Itza echo effects)
- **Ethnomathematics** — how cultures developed mathematical systems through astronomy
- **Navigation biology** — how birds use stars; parallels with human navigation
- **Climate and astronomy** — how Ice Age sky differed due to axial precession

### Modern Applications
- **Dark sky preservation** — Indigenous knowledge arguments for reducing light pollution
- **Space exploration ethics** — learning from diverse perspectives on cosmos
- **STEM education** — using cultural astronomy to engage diverse students
- **Decolonizing science** — recognizing multiple valid knowledge systems

### Art & Culture
- **Aboriginal art and astronomy** — emu egg carvings showing sky knowledge
- **Planetarium art installations** — contemporary artists using cultural sky themes
- **Science fiction** — Ted Chiang's "The Merchant and the Alchemist's Gate" (Islamic astronomy themes)
- **Music and the stars** — compositions inspired by cultural astronomy (R. Murray Schafer's "Music for Wilderness Lake")

## Tools for Validation

- **Archaeological site databases** — cross-reference alignment claims
- **Historical star catalogs** — Hipparcos, Yale Bright Star Catalog for positions
- **Precession calculators** — account for how sky positions change over millennia
