# Research Summary — Materials Science: Metamaterials and Smart Materials

## Major Subtopics

### Metamaterials
1. **Electromagnetic metamaterials** — negative refractive index, cloaking, perfect lensing
2. **Acoustic metamaterials** — sound manipulation, noise cancellation, focusing
3. **Mechanical metamaterials** — auxetic materials, pentamode structures, programmable stiffness
4. **Photonic metamaterials** — photonic crystals, bandgap engineering
5. **Design principles** — unit cell design, homogenization theory, scaling laws

### Smart Materials
1. **Shape memory alloys (SMAs)** — martensitic transformation, training, applications
2. **Piezoelectric materials** — direct/converse effects, sensors, actuators
3. **Electroactive polymers (EAPs)** — dielectric elastomers, ionic polymers
4. **Magnetorheological/electrorheological fluids** — field-responsive viscosity
5. **Self-healing materials** — capsule-based, vascular networks, intrinsic healing
6. **Phase-change materials** — thermal energy storage, optical switching

## Pedagogical Considerations

- **Balance theory and application**: Intermediate students need physical intuition, not just Maxwell's equations
- **Visual emphasis**: Metamaterials are highly visual — animations of wave propagation, unit cell deformations critical
- **Multi-scale thinking**: Unit cell → effective medium → macroscale device is core conceptual arc
- **Interdisciplinary**: Physics (waves, fields), chemistry (phase transitions, polymers), engineering (fabrication, testing)
- **Historical narrative**: Veselago's 1967 prediction → Pendry's 1999 designs → modern applications helps motivation

## Gaps and Challenges

- Many advanced resources assume graduate-level EM or solid mechanics
- Fabrication techniques are rapidly evolving (need 2024-2026 resources)
- Trade journals often proprietary (IEEE, APS behind paywalls)
- Best interactive tools require MATLAB or commercial FEA licenses
