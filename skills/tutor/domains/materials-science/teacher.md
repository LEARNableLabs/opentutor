# Materials Science — Metamaterials and Smart Materials — Domain Teaching Config

## Exercise Types
This domain uses a mix of delivery formats:
- **concept-focused mini-lessons** — 9 lessons (33%)
- **Socratic questions** — 6 lessons (22%)
- **real-world application challenges** — 4 lessons (15%)
- **teach-back exercises (student explains)** — 3 lessons (11%)
- **review and consolidation sessions** — 3 lessons (11%)
- **curated resource exploration** — 2 lessons (7%)

Primary format: concept-focused mini-lessons.

## Resource Types
This domain has strong coverage in: textbooks, video lectures, interactive tools, academic papers, code repositories, online courses. Prioritize these when recommending resources.

## Difficulty Curve
Balanced progression — steady difficulty increase with regular consolidation.

Distribution: 41% accessible (1-2), 44% standard (3), 15% challenging (4-5).

Difficulty peaks:
- Day 6: "Could we really build an invisibility cloak?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 8: "How would you design a perfect lens?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 12: "How can we design materials with zero shear modulus?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 25: "How do you simulate a metamaterial before building it?" (difficulty 4)

## Domain Hooks
This field covers materials science — metamaterials and smart materials, with applications across theory and practice.

## Common Failure Modes
Students often struggle with the foundational concepts before building to more advanced material. Reinforce basics before progressing.

## Vocabulary
Key terms for this domain: effective medium theory, unit cell design, emergent properties, permittivity, permeability, constitutive relations, dispersion, refractive index, Veselago's hypothesis, double negativity, stimuli-responsive, adaptivity, reversibility, split-ring resonators, LC circuits (and 82 more).
