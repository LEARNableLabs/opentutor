# Materials Science: Metamaterials and Smart Materials — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks

- **"Metamaterials: Physics and Engineering Explorations"** by Engheta and Ziolkowski (2006)
  - Comprehensive foundation for EM metamaterials
  - Good for intermediate students, balances theory and applications
  - https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/book/10.1002/0471784192

- **"Acoustic Metamaterials and Phononic Crystals"** by Deymier (2013)
  - Covers mechanical/acoustic metamaterials
  - More specialized but excellent for phononic concepts
  - https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-31232-8

- **"Shape Memory Alloys: Modeling and Engineering Applications"** by Lagoudas (2008)
  - Definitive text on SMAs
  - Advanced but chapters 1-3 accessible to intermediate students
  - https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-0-387-47685-8

- **"Encyclopedia of Smart Materials"** by Schwartz (2002)
  - Multi-volume reference, somewhat dated but comprehensive coverage
  - Good for browsing different material classes
  - https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/book/10.1002/0471216275

### Online Courses

- **MIT OCW 3.091SC "Introduction to Solid State Chemistry"** (Fall 2010)
  - Foundation for understanding material properties and phase transitions
  - Free video lectures, problem sets, exams
  - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-091sc-introduction-to-solid-state-chemistry-fall-2010/

- **MIT OCW 6-007 "Electromagnetic Energy: From Motors to Lasers"** (Spring 2011)
  - Covers EM fundamentals needed for metamaterial understanding
  - Accessible treatment of Maxwell's equations
  - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-007-electromagnetic-energy-from-motors-to-lasers-spring-2011/

- **Coursera "Materials Science: 10 Things Every Engineer Should Know"** (UC Davis)

  - Good prerequisite refresher
  - https://www.coursera.org/learn/material-science

- **edX "Nanotechnology: The Basics"** (Rice University)
  - Covers nanofabrication techniques relevant to metamaterials
  - https://www.edx.org/course/nanotechnology-the-basics

### Interactive Tools and Simulations

- **PhET Interactive Simulations** (University of Colorado Boulder)
  - Wave interference, bending light, capacitors (for understanding basic physics)
  - Free, browser-based, excellent visualizations
  - https://phet.colorado.edu/

- **COMSOL Multiphysics Gallery**
  - Metamaterial simulation examples (photonic crystals, cloaking, acoustic absorption)
  - Can download models if you have COMSOL license

- **Wolfram Demonstrations Project**
  - Split-ring resonator models, bandgap calculators, auxetic structure visualizations

  - https://demonstrations.wolfram.com/

- **Meep FDTD Tutorial**
  - Open-source electromagnetic simulation (MIT)
  - Python-based, good for learning computational methods
  - https://meep.readthedocs.io/en/latest/

- **GeoGebra: Wave and Optics Simulations**
  - Interactive tool for understanding wave propagation, Snell's law, interference
  - https://www.geogebra.org/

## Journals and Publications

### Key Papers (Historical and Review)

- **Veselago (1968)** "The electrodynamics of substances with simultaneously negative values of ε and μ"
  - Original theoretical prediction of negative index materials
  - Soviet Physics Uspekhi 10(4): 509-514

- **Pendry et al. (1999)** "Magnetism from conductors and enhanced nonlinear phenomena"
  - First practical proposal for negative permeability
  - IEEE Trans. Microwave Theory Tech. 47(11): 2075-2084

- **Shelby, Smith, Schultz (2001)** "Experimental verification of a negative index of refraction"
  - First experimental demonstration of negative refraction
  - Science 292(5514): 77-79
  - https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1058847

- **Schurig et al. (2006)** "Metamaterial electromagnetic cloak at microwave frequencies"
  - First experimental cloaking demonstration
  - Science 314(5801): 977-980
  - https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1133628

- **Bertoldi et al. (2017)** "Flexible mechanical metamaterials"
  - Comprehensive review of mechanical metamaterials
  - Nature Reviews Materials 2: 17066
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/natrevmats201766

- **Lakes (1987)** "Foam structures with a negative Poisson's ratio"
  - Pioneering work on auxetic materials
  - Science 235(4792): 1038-1040

- **White et al. (2001)** "Autonomic healing of polymer composites"
  - Original capsule-based self-healing demonstration
  - Nature 409: 794-797
  - https://www.nature.com/articles/35057232

## Unexpected Connections (wild cards)

### Cross-Discipline Links

- **Metamaterials and Music**
  - Acoustic metamaterials for concert hall design, noise-canceling architecture
  - "Sonic crystals" as public art installations
  - Example: https://www.dezeen.com/2019/07/15/acoustic-metamaterial-wall-hong-kong/

- **Origami Mathematics**
  - Rigorous mathematical theory (Maekawa's theorem, Kawasaki's theorem) underpins origami metamaterials
  - Connects to graph theory, differential geometry
  - Robert Lang's work bridges art and engineering: https://langorigami.com/

- **Biological Inspiration**
  - Butterfly wing nanostructures are natural photonic crystals
  - Mantis shrimp dactyl club is a natural composite with hierarchical structure (inspiration for impact-resistant metamaterials)
  - Spider silk as natural self-healing fiber
  - Paper: "Structural Biomaterials" by Julian Vincent

- **Fashion and Architecture**
  - 4D-printed clothing that responds to temperature/humidity (MIT Self-Assembly Lab)
  - Auxetic fabrics for better fit and comfort
  - Shape-memory alloy actuators in responsive building facades
  - Example: https://selfassemblylab.mit.edu/4d-printing

- **Metamaterials in Art**
  - Olafur Eliasson's installations using iridescent films (photonic structures)
  - Anish Kapoor's Vantablack (not a metamaterial but similar aesthetic)
  - Art-science collaborations exploring cloaking, invisibility, perception

- **Gaming and VR**
  - Haptic feedback using piezoelectric actuators and electroactive polymers
  - Adaptive game controllers with variable stiffness (magnetorheological fluids, SMAs)
  - Ultrasonic metamaterial lenses for mid-air haptics (e.g., Ultraleap technology)
  - https://www.ultraleap.com/

- **Space Exploration**
  - Deployable structures using shape memory polymers (solar sails, antennas)
  - Acoustic metamaterials for vibration isolation in satellites
  - NASA's use of auxetic materials for better packaging efficiency
  - Paper: "Shape Memory Alloys for Space Applications" (NASA)

- **Medical Devices**
  - Piezoelectric ultrasound transducers (both imaging and therapy)
  - SMA stents that expand at body temperature
  - Metamaterial lenses for improved MRI resolution
  - Self-healing hydrogels for tissue engineering
  - Paper: "Smart Biomaterials" — Nature Materials review

## Recommended Reading Order for Self-Study

1. **Start**: Engheta & Ziolkowski textbook (Chapters 1-3) for EM metamaterial foundations
2. **Parallel**: Watch Veritasium and Royal Institution videos for motivation
3. **Hands-on**: Try PhET simulations and Wolfram Demonstrations to build intuition
4. **Deep dive**: Read Shelby 2001 and Schurig 2006 papers (experimental milestones)
5. **Mechanical**: Read Bertoldi 2017 review for state-of-the-art mechanical metamaterials
6. **Smart materials**: Browse Schwartz encyclopedia for breadth, then focus on material class of interest
7. **Simulation**: Work through Meep tutorials if interested in computational design
8. **Advanced**: Explore research group websites and recent journal articles for cutting edge
