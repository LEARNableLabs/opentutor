# International Law and Human Rights — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Foundations of International Law
- History and development of international legal order
- Relationship between domestic and international law
- Principles of sovereignty, consent, and jurisdiction

### 2. Sources and Subjects of International Law
- Treaties, custom, general principles, judicial decisions
- States, international organizations, individuals, NGOs
- Treaty law and the Vienna Convention

### 3. International Human Rights Law
- Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR)
- International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights (ICCPR)
- International Covenant on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights (ICESCR)
- Regional human rights systems (European, Inter-American, African)
- Specific rights regimes (women, children, refugees, disabled persons)

### 4. International Humanitarian Law
- Geneva Conventions and Additional Protocols
- Law of armed conflict (jus ad bellum and jus in bello)
- Protection of civilians and combatants
- War crimes and crimes against humanity

### 5. Enforcement and Accountability
- International Court of Justice (ICJ)
- International Criminal Court (ICC)
- Human rights treaty bodies and special procedures
- Universal jurisdiction and extraterritorial obligations
- State responsibility and remedies

### 6. Contemporary Challenges
- Climate change and human rights
- Migration and refugee protection
- Business and human rights
- Digital rights and surveillance
- Atrocity prevention and R2P (Responsibility to Protect)

## Key Sources

### Academic Textbooks
- **Malcolm Shaw, "International Law" (9th ed., Cambridge)** — comprehensive treatise, standard reference
- **David Forsythe, "Human Rights in International Relations" (Cambridge)** — political science perspective
- **Antonio Cassese, "International Law" (Oxford)** — influential European perspective
- **Dinah Shelton, "Advanced Introduction to International Human Rights Law" (Elgar)** — accessible intermediate-level text

### Primary Legal Sources
- **UN Human Rights Office (OHCHR)** — treaty texts, reporting mechanisms, special procedures
- **UN Treaty Collection** — multilateral treaty database
- **ICJ Reports** — case law and advisory opinions
- **ICC Legal Texts** — Rome Statute, Elements of Crimes, Rules of Procedure

### Course Materials
- **Harvard Law School: International Human Rights Clinic** — case studies and advocacy materials
- **Yale Law School: Avalon Project** — historical documents in law and diplomacy
- **Coursera: "International Law in Action" (Leiden)** — video lectures and case studies
- **edX: "International Humanitarian Law" (Louvain)** — IHL fundamentals
- **UN Human Rights Learning resources** — thematic modules

### Interactive Resources
- **Human Rights Watch** — country reports, thematic research, advocacy campaigns
- **Amnesty International** — urgent actions, educational materials
- **ICRC Casebook** — IHL case studies and teaching materials
- **UN Treaty Body Database** — searchable state reports and recommendations
- **Refworld (UNHCR)** — legal and policy documents on forced displacement

### Video Resources
- **UN WebTV** — Human Rights Council sessions, treaty body reviews
- **ICC YouTube Channel** — trial proceedings, educational videos
- **Coursera/edX courses** — structured video lectures from Leiden, Louvain, Geneva Academy
- **TED Talks on human rights** — accessible entry points (Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie, Bryan Stevenson)

## Pedagogy Notes

- **Intermediate level** suggests familiarity with basic legal concepts and global governance structures
- Emphasis on **real cases and contemporary issues** to maintain engagement
- Balance **doctrinal foundations** (treaties, custom, principles) with **practical application** (case studies, advocacy)
- Use **primary sources** (treaty excerpts, case excerpts) alongside secondary analysis
- **Visual aids** helpful for institutional structures and procedural flowcharts
- **Comparative perspective** across regional systems enriches understanding of universality vs. cultural relativism debates
