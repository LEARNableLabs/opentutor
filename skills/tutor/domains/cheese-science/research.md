# Cheese Science Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Cheese Microbiology
- Starter cultures (LAB: Lactococcus, Lactobacillus, Streptococcus)
- Secondary cultures (Propionibacterium, Penicillium, Brevibacterium)
- Non-starter lactic acid bacteria (NSLAB)
- Microbial succession during aging
- Probiotics in cheese

### 2. Biochemistry of Cheese Aging
- Proteolysis: breakdown of casein proteins
- Lipolysis: breakdown of milk fats
- Glycolysis: lactose and lactate metabolism
- Flavor compound formation (volatile fatty acids, amino acid catabolism)
- Texture development

### 3. Terroir and Environmental Factors
- Milk composition and origin (pasture, breed, season)
- Cave/aging environment microbiology
- Regional microbial communities
- Environmental controls (temperature, humidity, air flow)
- Geographic Protected Designation of Origin (PDO)

### 4. Cheese Production Science
- Coagulation chemistry (rennet, acid)
- Curd processing and syneresis
- Salt and brine chemistry
- Surface treatments and rind development
- Standardization vs. artisanal methods

### 5. Quality and Safety
- Pathogen control (Listeria, E. coli, Salmonella)
- Pasteurization vs. raw milk
- Sensory evaluation methods
- Defect identification and prevention
- Shelf life and packaging

## Available Educational Resources

Strong resources exist for:
- General dairy science fundamentals
- Industrial cheese production
- Microbiology of fermented foods
- Sensory science

Gaps in public education:
- Advanced terroir microbiology (mostly in research papers)
- Comparative aging environments (limited to trade knowledge)
- Regional PDO specifications (scattered across regulatory bodies)
- Hands-on microbial identification (requires lab access)

## Research Notes

This curriculum focuses on the **science** of cheese, not production technique. Students will learn WHY cheese behaves as it does, not HOW to make it (though understanding mechanism informs practice). Intermediate level assumes basic microbiology (bacterial growth, fermentation) and chemistry (organic chemistry fundamentals, pH, enzymatic reactions).
