# Cardiology — The Heart as an Engineering System
## Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks

- **Guyton & Hall, *Textbook of Medical Physiology* (14th ed., 2020)**
  - Chapters 9-13 cover cardiac mechanics, hemodynamics, and regulation
  - Quantitative but accessible, excellent for intermediate learners
  - Gold standard medical physiology text
  - [Elsevier](https://www.elsevier.com/books/guyton-and-hall-textbook-of-medical-physiology/hall/978-0-323-59712-8)

- **Mohrman & Heller, *Cardiovascular Physiology* (9th ed., 2018)**
  - Concise, equation-rich, engineering-oriented approach
  - Perfect for intermediate students with physics background
  - ~200 pages, focused entirely on cardiovascular system
  - [McGraw Hill](https://www.mhprofessional.com/cardiovascular-physiology-ninth-edition-9781259922879-usa)

- **Berne & Levy, *Physiology* (7th ed., 2017)**
  - Chapters 12-18 cover cardiovascular system
  - More detailed than Guyton, includes cellular mechanisms
  - [Elsevier](https://www.elsevier.com/books/berne-and-levy-physiology/koeppen/978-0-323-39394-2)

- **Nichols & O'Rourke, *McDonald's Blood Flow in Arteries* (6th ed., 2011)**
  - Definitive hemodynamics reference
  - Advanced treatment of wave propagation, impedance, compliance
  - For deep dives into vascular mechanics
  - [CRC Press](https://www.routledge.com/McDonalds-Blood-Flow-in-Arteries-Sixth-Edition-Theoretical-Experimental/Nichols-ORourke-Vlachopoulos/p/book/9780340985014)

## Unexpected Connections (Wild Cards)

### Cross-Discipline Links

- **Electrical Engineering ↔ Cardiac Electrophysiology**
  - ECG = bioelectric signal processing
  - Pacemakers = embedded systems with feedback control
  - Defibrillators = chaos control theory

- **Fluid Mechanics ↔ Hemodynamics**
  - Cardiovascular system = complex pipe network with compliant walls
  - Turbulence in stenotic valves
  - Wave propagation and reflection (transmission line theory)

- **Control Theory ↔ Autonomic Regulation**
  - Baroreceptor reflex = negative feedback controller
  - RAAS = integral controller (slow, accumulating)
  - Heart rate variability = system noise and adaptability

- **Materials Science ↔ Cardiac Tissue Mechanics**
  - Myocardium = anisotropic viscoelastic composite
  - Valve leaflets = nonlinear elastic membranes
  - Stents = biocompatible structural materials

- **Evolutionary Biology ↔ Cardiac Design**
  - Why 4 chambers? Separation of oxygenated/deoxygenated blood enables endothermy
  - Scaling laws: heart mass ∝ body mass^0.98, heart rate ∝ body mass^-0.25
  - Giraffe cardiovascular adaptations for long-neck perfusion

- **Music & HRV**
  - Respiratory sinus arrhythmia = coupling between breathing and heart rate
  - Musicians with breath control show enhanced HRV
  - Rhythm perception and cardiac rhythm

- **Space Physiology ↔ Cardiovascular Deconditioning**
  - Microgravity → reduced blood volume, baroreceptor resetting
  - Orthostatic intolerance on return to Earth
  - Countermeasures: lower body negative pressure, exercise

## Recommended Learning Path

1. **Start with CVphysiology.com** — structured, comprehensive, free
2. **Supplement with Khan Academy videos** — visual reinforcement
3. **Practice ECG interpretation** — LITFL and Harvard ECG libraries
4. **Use PhET simulations** — hands-on manipulation builds intuition
5. **Read Mohrman & Heller** — for quantitative depth
6. **Explore PhysioNet datasets** — real data analysis for advanced learners
7. **Code a simple model** — MATLAB or Python cardiovascular simulation
8. **Read primary literature** — Circulation Research reviews for current understanding
