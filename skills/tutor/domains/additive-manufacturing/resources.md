# Additive Manufacturing — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks
- **"Additive Manufacturing Technologies" by Gibson, Rosen, Stucker** (3rd Edition, 2021) — Comprehensive coverage of all AM processes, materials, and applications. Strong on process physics and engineering. Best for intermediate learners who want depth without heavy math.
- **"3D Printing and Additive Manufacturing: Principles and Applications" by Chua, Leong, Lim** (5th Edition, 2017) — Broad overview with emphasis on design and applications. Good balance of theory and practice.
- **"Laser Additive Manufacturing: Materials, Design, Technologies, and Applications" edited by Brandt** (2017) — Deep dive on metal AM processes, particularly powder bed fusion and directed energy deposition. More advanced but excellent reference for Lessons 11-16.

### University Courses
- **MIT 2.S998: Additive Manufacturing** — https://ocw.mit.edu/ — Graduate course covering process fundamentals, materials, and design. Lecture notes and assignments available.
- **Penn State Center for Additive Manufacturing and Logistics** — https://www.lps.psu.edu/academic-programs/graduate-degrees/additive-manufacturing-and-design — Graduate certificate program with excellent pedagogical materials.
- **Georgia Tech PACE (Professional and Continuing Education) AM Certificate** — https://pe.gatech.edu/degrees/additive-manufacturing — Industry-focused professional education.

### Standards & Technical Documentation
- **ASTM F2792-12a: Standard Terminology for Additive Manufacturing Technologies** — https://www.astm.org/f2792-12a.html — Canonical definitions and process taxonomy.
- **ISO/ASTM 52900: Additive Manufacturing — General Principles — Fundamentals and Vocabulary** — https://www.iso.org/standard/69669.html — International standard terminology.
- **NIST Additive Manufacturing Benchmark Test Series (AMBench)** — https://www.nist.gov/ambench — Standardized test artifacts and measurement protocols for process validation.

### Industry Reports
- **Wohlers Report** — https://wohlersassociates.com/press-releases.html — Annual state-of-the-industry analysis. Comprehensive market data, technology trends, and case studies.
- **America Makes (NAMII) Project Reports** — https://www.americamakes.us/ — Publicly available research from the National Additive Manufacturing Innovation Institute.

## Supplementary (for engagement)

### Video Channels
- **CNC Kitchen** — https://www.youtube.com/c/CNCKitchen — Rigorous testing of FDM materials and parameters. Excellent empirical data on strength, layer adhesion, infill patterns. Use for Lessons 6-9.
- **Teaching Tech** — https://www.youtube.com/c/TeachingTech — FDM calibration, troubleshooting, and parameter optimization. Great for Lesson 10 (review/troubleshooting).
- **Maker's Muse** — https://www.youtube.com/c/MakersMuse — Design for AM principles, support strategies, orientation effects. Use for Lessons 17-21.
- **GE Additive Webinar Series** — https://www.ge.com/additive/ — Industrial metal AM applications, particularly aerospace. Excellent for Lesson 24 (LEAP engine fuel nozzle).
- **Desktop Metal** — https://www.desktopmetal.com/resources/webinars — Binder jetting process and applications. Useful for Lesson 15.

### Journal Articles (Open Access)
- **Additive Manufacturing (Elsevier)** — https://www.journals.elsevier.com/additive-manufacturing — Top-tier peer-reviewed research. Many articles on process-microstructure-property relationships.
- **3D Printing and Additive Manufacturing (Mary Ann Liebert)** — https://www.liebertpub.com/journal/3dp — Multidisciplinary journal covering materials, biology, electronics.
- **Journal of Manufacturing Science and Engineering (ASME)** — https://asmedigitalcollection.asme.org/manufacturingscience — Process modeling and optimization studies.

### Interactive Tools & Software
- **nTopology** — https://ntopology.com/ — Free educational license for lattice design and implicit modeling. Use for Lesson 19 (lattice structures).
- **Autodesk Fusion 360** — https://www.autodesk.com/products/fusion-360/ — Free for students/educators. Topology optimization built-in. Use for Lesson 18.
- **Materialise Magics** — https://www.materialise.com/en/industrial/software/magics — Industry-standard build preparation. Limited free trial.
- **PrusaSlicer** — https://www.prusa3d.com/page/prusaslicer_424/ — Open-source FDM slicer with excellent parameter visualization. Use for Lessons 6-7.
- **Simplify3D** — https://www.simplify3d.com/ — Commercial FDM slicer with advanced support and process control.

### Databases & Repositories
- **NIST Material Measurement Laboratory Data** — https://www.nist.gov/mml — Material properties for AM metals and polymers.
- **Granta MI (Ansys)** — https://www.ansys.com/products/materials — Materials database including AM-specific data.
- **MatWeb** — http://www.matweb.com/ — Free material property database with some AM materials.

### Code & Simulation
- **FLOW-3D AM** — https://www.flow3d.com/products/flow-3d-am/ — Melt pool simulation. Academic licenses available.
- **OpenFOAM** — https://www.openfoam.com/ — Open-source CFD, used for melt pool modeling research.
- **AM-Bench Benchmark Problems** — https://www.nist.gov/ambench/amb2018-02-description — Thermal modeling challenge problems with experimental validation data.

## Unexpected Connections

### Architecture & Construction
- **Contour Crafting** — Large-scale concrete 3D printing for building construction. Connects to Lessons 1-2 (scaling AM) and Lesson 17 (DfAM for civil engineering).
- **MX3D Bridge** — 3D-printed steel pedestrian bridge in Amsterdam. Combines metal AM with robotic deposition.

### Food
- **BeeHex Pizza Printer, Natural Machines Foodini** — Extrusion-based food printing. Same FDM physics (Lesson 6) applied to edible materials.

### Fashion & Textiles
- **Nervous System Kinematics Dress** — SLS-printed articulated fabric. Demonstrates design impossible with traditional textiles. Links to Lesson 19 (lattices as mechanical metamaterials).

### Energy
- **GE HArmonic Steam Turbine Blades** — Topology-optimized metal AM for power generation. Connects Lessons 18 and 24.
- **Siemens Gas Turbine Burners** — Complex internal cooling channels via AM. Impossible to manufacture conventionally.

### Biology & Biomimicry
- **Lattice structures mimicking bone trabecular architecture** — Used in orthopedic implants (Lesson 25) to match stiffness and promote osteointegration.
- **Shark skin-inspired drag reduction surfaces** — Textured surfaces via AM for fluid dynamics applications.

### Space Exploration
- **Made In Space (Redwire Space)** — ISS 3D printer for on-demand manufacturing in orbit. Discusses AM without gravity.
- **Relativity Space Terran 1 Rocket** — 95% 3D-printed rocket, largest metal AM structure. Extreme scaling of Lessons 11-14.
- **NASA Mars Habitat Challenge** — 3D printing structures from in-situ resources (regolith). Connects to materials (Lesson 3) and DfAM (Lesson 17).

### Art
- **Joshua Harker Sculptures** — SLS-printed impossibly intricate art pieces. Pure expression of geometric freedom.
- **Neri Oxman / MIT Media Lab** — Multifunctional, multi-material AM for architecture and wearables. Blurs art, design, and engineering.
