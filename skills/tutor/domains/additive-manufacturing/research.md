# Additive Manufacturing — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

1. **Fundamentals of Layer-by-Layer Fabrication** — thermodynamics, material phase transitions, heat transfer, solidification
2. **Process Technologies** — FDM/FFF, SLA/DLP, SLS, DMLS/SLM, binder jetting, material jetting, directed energy deposition
3. **Material Science** — polymers (thermoplastics, photopolymers), metals (titanium, aluminum, steel), ceramics, composites
4. **Design for Additive Manufacturing (DfAM)** — topology optimization, lattice structures, support strategies, part orientation
5. **Process Parameters & Control** — layer thickness, build speed, temperature profiles, laser/electron beam parameters
6. **Microstructure & Properties** — grain structure in metals, porosity, anisotropy, residual stress
7. **Post-Processing** — heat treatment, machining, surface finishing, support removal
8. **Quality Control & Metrology** — in-situ monitoring, CT scanning, dimensional accuracy, defect detection
9. **Applications & Case Studies** — aerospace (GE fuel nozzles), medical (implants, prosthetics), tooling, automotive

## Research Gaps Identified

- Limited open educational resources on **metal AM microstructure** at intermediate level
- Need more interactive simulations for **thermal modeling**
- Strong industry content but fewer **pedagogical progressions** for self-learners
- Excellent practitioner content (YouTube) but variable scientific rigor
