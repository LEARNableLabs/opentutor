# Plate Tectonics and Geodynamics — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Plate Tectonics Fundamentals
- Plate boundaries (divergent, convergent, transform)
- Plate motions and kinematics
- Historical development of plate theory
- Evidence from paleomagnetism, seafloor spreading, earthquake patterns

### 2. Mantle Dynamics
- Mantle convection mechanisms
- Thermal structure and heat transfer
- Plumes and hotspots
- Mantle rheology (viscosity, deformation)

### 3. Driving Forces
- Ridge push and slab pull
- Mantle drag
- Energy budget of plate motions
- Role of gravity and buoyancy

### 4. Tectonic Processes
- Subduction zone dynamics
- Continental rifting and breakup
- Mountain building (orogeny)
- Transform fault mechanics

### 5. Observational Methods
- GPS and geodesy
- Seismic tomography
- Heat flow measurements
- Satellite gravity and magnetic data

### 6. Numerical and Analog Modeling
- Convection simulations
- Plate reconstruction software
- Laboratory experiments on mantle materials

## Educational Pathways

For **intermediate level** (assumes basic geology background):
- Start with plate kinematics and boundary types (kinematic description)
- Progress to driving forces and mantle convection (dynamic explanation)
- Integrate quantitative methods (heat flow, force balance, scaling)
- Build to specific tectonic settings (subduction, rifting, etc.)
- Connect observations (GPS, seismology) to theory
- Optional advanced topics: non-Newtonian rheology, complex plate boundaries, geochemical cycles
