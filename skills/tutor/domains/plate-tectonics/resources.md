# Plate Tectonics and Geodynamics — Resources

## Primary Textbooks

### Comprehensive treatments
- **"Geodynamics" by Donald Turcotte and Gerald Schubert (3rd ed, 2014)** — The canonical quantitative textbook. Covers heat flow, rheology, convection, plate dynamics with full mathematical development. Advanced but essential reference. Intermediate students can read selected chapters (1-4, 6-7) for conceptual grounding and key equations.
  - Cambridge University Press
  - https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/geodynamics/

- **"The Solid Earth: An Introduction to Global Geophysics" by C.M.R. Fowler (2nd ed, 2004)** — Excellent balance of observation and theory. Strong on seismology, gravity, heat flow. More accessible than Turcotte & Schubert while maintaining rigor. Great for intermediate level.
  - Cambridge University Press
  - https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/solid-earth/

### Plate tectonics focused
- **"Plate Tectonics: An Insider's History of the Modern Theory of the Earth" edited by Naomi Oreskes (2001)** — Historical perspective with original papers by pioneers. Provides context for how the theory developed. Complements technical treatments.
  - Westview Press

- **"Plate Tectonics" by Peter Molnar (2015)** — Concise, modern synthesis at advanced undergraduate level. Emphasizes force balance and quantitative constraints.
  - Oxford University Press

## Interactive Tools and Visualizations

### Plate reconstructions
- **GPlates** — Open-source desktop software for interactive plate tectonic reconstructions. Reconstruct any time period, visualize motion paths, export data.
  - https://www.gplates.org/
  - Download: https://www.earthbyte.org/gplates-2-3-software-and-data-sets/

- **EarthByte Portal** — Web-based viewer for plate reconstructions, seafloor age grids, topography evolution
  - https://portal.earthbyte.org/
  - Interactive globe: https://www.earthbyte.org/webgl-globe-with-time-varying-data/

### Real-time data
- **UNAVCO GPS Velocity Viewer** — Visualize real-time and campaign GPS velocities globally

## Key Papers (Reviews and Syntheses)

### Plate driving forces
- **Forsyth & Uyeda (1975)** "On the relative importance of the driving forces of plate motion" — Classic analysis of slab pull vs. ridge push
  - *Geophysical Journal International*, 43(1), 163-200

- **Conrad & Lithgow-Bertelloni (2002)** "How mantle slabs drive plate tectonics" — Modern synthesis with numerical models
  - *Science*, 298(5591), 207-209

### Mantle convection
- **Davies (1999)** "Dynamic Earth: Plates, Plumes and Mantle Convection" — Book synthesizing convection theory and observations
  - Cambridge University Press

- **Schubert et al. (2001)** "Mantle Convection in the Earth and Planets" — Comprehensive reference work
  - Cambridge University Press

### Seismic tomography
- **Romanowicz (2003)** "Global mantle tomography: progress status in the past 10 years" — Review of imaging techniques and results
  - *Annual Review of Earth and Planetary Sciences*, 31, 303-328

### Subduction zones
- **Stern (2002)** "Subduction zones" — Overview of subduction processes and settings
  - *Reviews of Geophysics*, 40(4), 1012

- **Syracuse et al. (2010)** "The global range of subduction zone thermal models" — Compilation and analysis of thermal structure
  - *Physics of the Earth and Planetary Interiors*, 183(1-2), 73-90

## Unexpected Connections

### Plate tectonics and climate
- **CO₂ regulation via weathering-subduction cycle** — Plate tectonics acts as a planetary thermostat over million-year timescales
  - Walker et al. (1981) "A negative feedback mechanism for the long-term stabilization of Earth's surface temperature"

### Plate tectonics and life
- **Rare Earth hypothesis** — Plate tectonics may be essential for complex life (nutrient cycling, magnetic field, climate stability)
  - Ward & Brownlee (2000) "Rare Earth: Why Complex Life is Uncommon in the Universe"

### Solid-state physics and mantle rheology
- **Dislocation creep and diffusion creep** — Mantle deformation mechanisms come from materials science
  - Karato (2008) "Deformation of Earth Materials" — connects atomic-scale processes to plate tectonics

### Cosmology and planet formation
- **Why Earth has plate tectonics and Venus doesn't** — Role of water, planetary size, initial conditions
  - Valencia & O'Connell (2009) "Convection scaling and subduction on Earth and super-Earths"

### Archaeology and human migration
- **Tectonic history shapes human evolution and dispersal** — Rift valleys in East Africa, land bridges during glaciations
  - Bailey & King (2011) "Tectonics, volcanism, landscape structure and human evolution in the African Rift"
