# Psycholinguistics — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Speech Perception
How listeners segment continuous acoustic signals into words and phonemes. Includes categorical perception, phoneme restoration, McGurk effect, and the role of context in speech understanding.

### 2. Word Recognition
Processes of accessing meaning from spoken and written words. Covers lexical access, frequency effects, neighborhood effects, semantic priming, and models like cohort and TRACE.

### 3. Sentence Processing
Syntactic parsing, garden-path sentences, attachment preferences, working memory constraints, and incremental interpretation. Includes classic debates about syntax-first vs. constraint-based models.

### 4. Language Production
From intention to articulation: conceptualization, lexical selection, grammatical encoding, phonological encoding, and speech motor control. Includes speech error analysis and tip-of-the-tongue phenomena.

### 5. Reading Processes
Eye movements, lexical access in reading, dual-route models, skilled vs. beginning readers, dyslexia, and writing systems.

### 6. Bilingualism and Code-Switching
Lexical access in bilinguals, language control mechanisms, code-switching patterns, transfer effects, and cognitive consequences of bilingualism.

### 7. Language and the Brain
Neural substrates of language: Broca's area, Wernicke's area, language networks, aphasia syndromes, and neuroimaging methods (fMRI, ERP, MEG).

### 8. Psycholinguistic Methods
Reaction time experiments, eye-tracking, self-paced reading, cross-modal priming, ERP components (N400, P600), neuroimaging paradigms.

## Pedagogical Notes

At intermediate level, students should have:
- Basic cognitive psychology background (memory, attention)
- Some exposure to linguistics (phonetics, syntax basics)
- Comfort with experimental design concepts

The curriculum should:
- Balance theory and empirical methods
- Include classic phenomena (garden-path, priming) early
- Build from perception → comprehension → production
- Integrate neuroscience gradually (not front-loaded)
- Include hands-on experiments or demos where possible
