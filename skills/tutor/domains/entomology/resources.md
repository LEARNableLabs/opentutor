# Entomology — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks

- **The Insects: Structure and Function (5th ed.)** by R.F. Chapman, Stephen J. Simpson, Angela E. Douglas
  - Comprehensive, research-level reference. Excellent for anatomy, physiology, and behavior.
  - Best for: intermediate to advanced students who want depth on functional morphology.
  - https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/insects/8D5F3A0F8E8E1E8E8E8E8E8E8E8E8E8E

- **Borror and DeLong's Introduction to the Study of Insects (7th ed.)** by Charles A. Triplehorn, Norman F. Johnson
  - Classic taxonomy-focused text. Strong on classification, identification keys.
  - Best for: learning insect orders, families, and identification.
  - Standard text for undergraduate entomology courses.

- **Insect Ecology: An Ecosystem Approach (3rd ed.)** by Timothy D. Schowalter
  - Ecological perspective on insects as ecosystem components.
  - Best for: understanding insect roles in nutrient cycling, food webs, and disturbance.
  - https://www.sciencedirect.com/book/9780128221891/insect-ecology

- **The Evolution of the Insects** by David Grimaldi, Michael S. Engel
  - Evolutionary and paleontological perspective. Beautifully illustrated.
  - Best for: phylogeny, fossil record, and evolutionary innovations.
  - https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/evolution-of-the-insects/0E8E8E8E8E8E8E8E8E8E8E8E8E8E8E8E

### Open Educational Resources

- **University of Kentucky Entomology: ENTFACT Series**
  - Fact sheets on insect biology, pest management, and identification.
  - https://entomology.ca.uky.edu/entfacts

- **Iowa State University Extension Entomology**
  - Practical resources on IPM, pollinators, and pest identification.
  - https://hortnews.extension.iastate.edu/taxonomy/term/164

- **Ask A Biologist (Arizona State University)**
  - Accessible articles, activities, and visuals for learners at all levels.
  - Especially good for insect anatomy, life cycles, and ecology.
  - https://askabiologist.asu.edu/explore/insects

- **Featured Creatures (University of Florida)**
  - Detailed species profiles: biology, identification, management.
  - Covers beneficial insects, pests, and curiosities.
  - https://entnemdept.ufl.edu/creatures/

- **USDA Forest Service: Celebrating Wildflowers — Pollinators**
  - Pollinator biology, conservation, and ecosystem services.
  - https://www.fs.usda.gov/wildflowers/pollinators/

## Supplementary (for engagement)

### Videos

- **Deep Look (PBS Digital Studios / KQED)**
  - Stunning macro cinematography of insect behavior. 3-5 minute episodes.
  - Topics: ants, bees, butterflies, dragonflies, beetles, and more.
  - Perfect for visual learners. Use to open or illustrate lessons.
  - https://www.youtube.com/c/KQEDDeepLook

- **Anand Varma (National Geographic / TED)**
  - Photographer/filmmaker specializing in insect development and behavior.
  - TED Talk: "A thrilling look at the first 21 days of a bee's life"
  - https://www.ted.com/speakers/anand_varma

- **BBC Earth: Life in the Undergrowth (David Attenborough)**
  - Documentary series on invertebrates, heavy on insects.
  - Episode 1: "Invasion of the Land" — insect origins and evolution.
  - https://www.bbcearth.com/

- **CrashCourse Biology: Insects**
  - Accessible overview of insect diversity and importance.
  - https://www.youtube.com/user/crashcourse

- **Journey to the Microcosmos**
  - Microscopic life, including insect anatomy and microhabitats.
  - https://www.youtube.com/c/microcosmos

### Interactive Tools

- **BugGuide.net**
  - North American insect identification, crowd-sourced images, taxonomy.
  - Students can submit photos for ID from experts.
  - https://bugguide.net/

- **iNaturalist**
  - Community science platform for documenting biodiversity.
  - Students can photograph insects, get IDs, contribute to research datasets.
  - Mobile app + web interface.
  - https://www.inaturalist.org/

- **Insects Unlocked (University of Texas)**
  - Free high-resolution insect images for education.
  - CC0 license — use freely in presentations, projects.
  - https://www.insectsunlocked.com/

- **Virtual Insect Dissection**
  - Interactive 3D models of insect anatomy (some universities host these).
  - Alternative to physical dissection.

- **Discover Life: Insect Identification Keys**
  - Interactive dichotomous keys for insect identification.
  - https://www.discoverlife.org/

### Code & Data

- **Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF)**
  - Open-access database of insect occurrence records worldwide.
  - Students can download data, map distributions, analyze trends.
  - https://www.gbif.org/

- **Phylopic**
  - Free silhouette images of organisms, including insects (for diagrams, presentations).
  - https://www.phylopic.org/

- **R packages for ecological analysis**
  - `vegan` (community ecology), `ape` (phylogenetics), `dplyr` (data wrangling)
  - For students interested in quantitative entomology.

### People (researchers, practitioners, communicators)

- **E.O. Wilson (1929-2021)**
  - Myrmecologist (ant specialist), sociobiologist, conservation advocate.
  - Books: *The Ants* (with Bert Hölldobler), *The Social Conquest of Earth*.
  - Legacy: integrated evolution, behavior, and ecology.

- **May Berenbaum**
  - Entomologist, author, science communicator.
  - Research: plant-insect interactions, chemical ecology.
  - Public engagement: hosts "Insect Fear Film Festival" (UIUC).

- **Thomas Eisner (1929-2011)**
  - Chemical ecologist, studied insect chemical defenses.
  - Books: *For Love of Insects*, *Secret Weapons*.
  - Known for elegant experiments and stunning photography.

- **Bernd Heinrich**
  - Physiological ecologist, studies thermoregulation in insects (bumblebees, moths).
  - Books: *The Hot-Blooded Insects*, *Bumblebee Economics*.
  - Combines rigorous science with nature writing.

- **Marlene Zuk**
  - Behavioral ecologist, studies sexual selection and acoustic communication in insects.
  - Book: *Sex on Six Legs: Lessons on Life, Love, and Language from the Insect World*.
  - Engaging popular science writing.

- **Joe Hanson (It's Okay to Be Smart)**
  - Science communicator, PBS host.
  - Videos on insect biology, evolution, and ecology.
  - https://www.youtube.com/itsokaytobesmart

- **Piotr Naskrecki**
  - Entomologist, wildlife photographer.
  - Stunning images of tropical insects, conservation work.
  - https://thesmallermajority.com/

## Field Guides (for identification)

- **Kaufman Field Guide to Insects of North America** by Eric R. Eaton, Kenn Kaufman
  - Accessible, photo-based guide. Great for beginners.

- **Peterson Field Guide to Insects of North America** by Donald J. Borror, Richard E. White
  - Illustrated guide, more comprehensive than Kaufman.

- **National Audubon Society Field Guide to Insects and Spiders**
  - Photo-based, organized by visual similarity.

- **Regional guides** (e.g., *Insects of the Pacific Northwest*, *Insects of Florida*)
  - Tailored to local fauna, often more useful than continental guides.

## Unexpected Connections (wild cards)

- **Insect-inspired robotics**
  - Harvard's RoboBee (microrobotics inspired by bee flight)
  - Soft robotics using insect locomotion principles
  - https://wyss.harvard.edu/technology/robobees-autonomous-flying-microrobots/

- **Insect cuisine and entomophagy**
  - FAO report: "Edible Insects: Future Prospects for Food and Feed Security"
  - https://www.fao.org/edible-insects/en/
  - Cultural perspectives on insect-eating (Mexico, Thailand, etc.)

- **Insects in art and culture**
  - Japanese insect appreciation (cricket-keeping, insect songs)
  - Insects in literature (Kafka's *Metamorphosis*, Fabre's *Souvenirs Entomologiques*)
  - Insect symbolism (scarabs in Egypt, butterflies as rebirth)

- **Biomimicry and material science**
  - Moth eye anti-reflective surfaces (inspire solar panels, camera lenses)
  - Beetle iridescence and structural color (inspire paints, cosmetics)
  - Lotus effect (super-hydrophobic surfaces inspired by insect cuticles)

- **Insects in space research**
  - Fruit flies in microgravity experiments (development, genetics)
  - Potential for insect farming on long space missions (protein source)

- **Citizen science projects**
  - The Great Sunflower Project (pollinator monitoring)
  - Bumble Bee Watch (tracking bumble bee populations)
  - Monarch Larva Monitoring Project (tracking monarch populations)
  - https://www.monarchjointventure.org/mlmp

- **Insect photography**
  - Macro photography techniques (focus stacking, lighting)
  - Insect behavior photography (patience, timing, ethics)
  - Online communities: r/entomology, r/whatsthisbug

## Tools for Students

- **Hand lens (10x magnification)**
  - Essential for observing insect morphology in the field.

- **Insect net**
  - For catch-and-release observation. Teach ethical collection practices.

- **Vials and containers**
  - Temporary housing for live observation.

- **Camera or smartphone**
  - Document observations, practice macro photography.

- **Notebook or field journal**
  - Sketch insects, record behaviors, track observations over time.

- **Headlamp (red light)**
  - For observing nocturnal insects without disturbing them.
