# Glaciology — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

1. **Ice Physics & Material Properties**
   - Crystal structure, rheology, deformation mechanisms
   - Thermal properties, density, porosity
   - Ice-air, ice-water, ice-rock interfaces

2. **Glacier Dynamics**
   - Mass balance, accumulation vs ablation
   - Ice flow mechanisms (internal deformation, basal sliding)
   - Glacier classification (temperate, polythermal, polar)
   - Surge dynamics and instabilities

3. **Ice Sheet Modeling**
   - Conservation equations (mass, momentum, energy)
   - Shallow ice approximation (SIA)
   - Ice stream dynamics
   - Numerical methods and computational approaches

4. **Climate Interactions**
   - Glacier-climate feedbacks
   - Ice core records and paleoclimate
   - Sea level contributions
   - Albedo and surface energy balance

5. **Observational Methods**
   - Remote sensing (InSAR, altimetry, gravimetry)
   - Field techniques (GPR, ice cores, GPS)
   - Mass balance measurements

6. **Ice Sheets & Ice Shelves**
   - Antarctic and Greenland ice sheets
   - Ice shelf dynamics and buttressing
   - Marine ice sheet instability
   - Calving processes

## Key Sources

### Textbooks
- **Physics of Glaciers** by Cuffey & Paterson — comprehensive standard text
- **The Physics of Glaciers** by Hooke (4th ed.) — accessible introduction
- **Glaciers and Glaciation** by Benn & Evans — field-oriented approach

### Online Courses
- MIT OCW 12.475 — Seminar in Geophysics: Dynamics of Glaciers and Ice Sheets
- University of Alaska Fairbanks — Glaciology course materials
- FUTURELEARN — Glaciers and Ice Sheets in a Changing Climate (University of Edinburgh)

### Research Organizations
- International Glaciological Society (IGS)
- NSIDC (National Snow and Ice Data Center)
- NASA Cryospheric Sciences Lab
- British Antarctic Survey

### Interactive Tools & Data
- GLIMS (Global Land Ice Measurements from Space)
- Ice sheet models: PISM, Elmer/Ice, ISSM
- WorldView ice imagery
- CryoSat-2 and ICESat-2 data portals
