# Glaciology — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

- **The Physics of Glaciers (4th edition)** by Kurt Cuffey & W.S.B. Paterson — The definitive comprehensive textbook. Mathematically rigorous, covers all major topics from ice physics to ice sheet modeling. Best for intermediate-to-advanced students with calculus background. Excellent for lessons 8, 14-18.
  - https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/physics-of-glaciers/E6B8D18B17F58429C0E675437A7EB3B7

- **The Physics of Glaciers** by Roger LeB. Hooke (4th edition) — More accessible alternative to Cuffey & Paterson. Good physical intuition, less mathematical density. Suitable for intermediate level throughout.

- **Glaciers and Glaciation (2nd edition)** by Douglas Benn & David Evans — Field-oriented approach with exceptional photography and diagrams. Emphasizes observational methods and geomorphology. Excellent visual support for lessons 1-6, 20-21.
  - https://www.routledge.com/Glaciers-and-Glaciation/Benn-Evans/p/book/9781444135169

- **MIT OpenCourseWare 12.475** — Seminar in Geophysics: Dynamics of Glaciers and Ice Sheets. Graduate-level course materials including lecture notes, problem sets. Strong on ice sheet modeling (lessons 13-18).
  - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/12-475-seminar-in-geophysics-dynamics-of-glaciers-and-ice-sheets-spring-2009/

- **Antarctic Glaciers** — Educational website by Bethan Davies. Excellent accessible explanations, diagrams, and case studies. Free resource covering glacier processes, thermal regimes, Antarctic ice sheets. Use throughout.
  - https://www.antarcticglaciers.org/

## Supplementary

### Videos & Lectures

- **AGU Fall Meeting Cryosphere Sessions** — Annual conference talks; cutting-edge research. Search YouTube for "AGU cryosphere" + topic.
  - https://www.youtube.com/user/AGUvideos

- **Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory Lectures** — Public lectures on climate and glaciology. Accessible to general audience.
  - https://www.ldeo.columbia.edu/

- **NASA's Dynamic Planet: Glaciers and Ice Sheets** — Educational video series showing satellite observations of ice change.
  - https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/cgi-bin/search.cgi?value=glaciers

- **NSIDC Educational Videos** — Short explainers on cryosphere topics (mass balance, ice cores, sea ice).
  - https://nsidc.org/learn/videos

- **Richard Alley's Ice Ages and Climate Lecture Series** — Penn State course videos; engaging presentation style.
  - https://www.e-education.psu.edu/earth107/

### Interactive Tools

- **GLIMS (Global Land Ice Measurements from Space)** — Global glacier inventory with satellite imagery viewer. Explore glacier change over time.
  - https://www.glims.org/maps/glims

- **NSIDC Data Portal** — Access real satellite data (ICESat-2, CryoSat-2, MODIS snow cover).
  - https://nsidc.org/data

- **Polar Ice Sheet Models Intercomparison Project (PISM)** — Open-source ice sheet model with tutorials. Run simple simulations.
  - https://pism.io/

- **Ice Sheet System Model (ISSM)** — JPL's ice sheet modeling framework with documentation and tutorials.
  - https://issm.jpl.nasa.gov/

- **Elmer/Ice** — Open-source finite element ice flow model.
  - http://elmerfem.org/elmerice/

- **NASA Worldview** — Satellite imagery viewer; explore Greenland and Antarctic ice sheets in near-real-time.
  - https://worldview.earthdata.nasa.gov/

- **USGS Repeat Photography Project** — Compare historical and modern glacier photos; dramatic visual evidence of retreat.
  - https://www.usgs.gov/centers/norock/science/repeat-photography-project

### Code & Datasets

- **PISM tutorials on GitHub** — Step-by-step ice sheet modeling examples.
  - https://github.com/pism/pism

- **ISSM examples** — MATLAB/Python scripts for ice sheet simulations.
  - https://issm.jpl.nasa.gov/documentation/tutorials/

- **WGMS (World Glacier Monitoring Service)** — Global glacier mass balance database. Download CSV data for analysis.
  - https://wgms.ch/data_databaseversions/

- **IMBIE (Ice Sheet Mass Balance Inter-comparison Exercise)** — Ice sheet mass balance estimates from multiple studies. Download data.
  - http://www.imbie.org/

- **Ice Core Data Portal (NOAA NCEI)** — Access to ice core paleoclimate datasets.
  - https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/paleoclimatology/ice-core

- **CryoCloud** — JupyterHub environment for cryosphere data analysis. Pre-configured Python environment.
  - https://cryointhecloud.com/

## Unexpected Connections (for wild cards)

- **Europa's ice shell** — Planetary glaciology; tidal flexing and subsurface oceans. Connects to lesson 14 (ice sheet physics applies to moons).
- **Ice stalactites (brinicles)** — Underwater ice formation in polar seas; stunning videos available.
- **Glacial lake outburst floods** — Catastrophic drainage events; links glaciology to hydrology and natural hazards.
- **Snowball Earth hypothesis** — Global glaciation 600 Mya; connects ice-albedo feedback (lesson 24) to deep-time climate.
- **Antarctic subglacial lakes** — Lake Vostok and others; isolated ecosystems, astrobiology implications.
- **Ice core gas bubbles and atmospheric chemistry** — Direct samples of ancient air; connections to atmospheric science.
- **Glacial earthquakes** — Ice quakes from calving and stick-slip motion; seismology meets glaciology.
- **Ice acoustics** — Sound propagation in ice; relevance to sensor networks and marine mammal communication.
- **Diamond dust (ice crystal precipitation)** — Atmospheric ice nucleation; connections to cloud physics.
- **Glacial isostatic adjustment** — Post-glacial rebound; solid Earth response to ice loading/unloading.
