# Cellular Automata — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Books

- **Stephen Wolfram, "A New Kind of Science"** (2002) — comprehensive treatment of CA, Wolfram classification, computational universality. Dense but foundational. Available free online: [https://www.wolframscience.com/nks/](https://www.wolframscience.com/nks/)

- **Andrew Ilachinski, "Cellular Automata: A Discrete Universe"** (2001) — thorough textbook covering theory, applications, and implementation. Best technical introduction for intermediate learners.

- **Elwyn Berlekamp, John H. Conway, Richard K. Guy, "Winning Ways for Your Mathematical Games, Vol. 2"** (1982) — includes Conway's own analysis of Game of Life. Classic source.

- **Paul Rendell, "Turing Machine Universality of the Game of Life"** (2016) — detailed construction of a Turing machine in Life. For computational theory deep dive.

### Papers

- **John Conway, "The Game of Life"** — originally popularized in Martin Gardner's Scientific American column (October 1970). Foundational reading.

- **William Gosper, "Exploiting Regularities in Large Cellular Spaces"** (1984) — describes the Hashlife algorithm. Advanced but illuminating.

- **Matthew Cook, "Universality in Elementary Cellular Automata"** (2004) — proof that Rule 110 is Turing-complete. Shows universality in simplest CA.

### Online Courses

- **MIT OpenCourseWare — 6.006 Introduction to Algorithms** — includes cellular automata modules: [https://ocw.mit.edu/](https://ocw.mit.edu/)

- **Complexity Explorer (Santa Fe Institute)** — free courses on complexity science, emergence, and CA: [https://www.complexityexplorer.org/](https://www.complexityexplorer.org/)

- **Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Cellular Automata** — philosophical and theoretical foundations: [https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/cellular-automata/](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/cellular-automata/)

## Interactive Tools

### Simulators

- **Golly** — fast, powerful, open-source CA simulator. Supports Game of Life, Wolfram rules, and many other CA types. Essential tool: [https://golly.sourceforge.net/](https://golly.sourceforge.net/)

- **copy.sh/life** — web-based Game of Life with fast rendering and pattern library: [https://copy.sh/life/](https://copy.sh/life/)

- **playgameoflife.com** — interactive tutorial and simulator with guided lessons: [https://playgameoflife.com/](https://playgameoflife.com/)

- **LifeViewer** — web-based pattern viewer, used in LifeWiki embeds: [https://lazyslug.com/lifeviewer/](https://lazyslug.com/lifeviewer/)

- **Wolfram MathWorld CA demonstrations** — interactive demos of elementary CA: [https://mathworld.wolfram.com/CellularAutomaton.html](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/CellularAutomaton.html)

### Pattern Libraries

- **LifeWiki** — comprehensive database of Game of Life patterns, rules, and discoveries. The go-to reference: [https://conwaylife.com/wiki/Main_Page](https://conwaylife.com/wiki/Main_Page)

- **Game of Life Pattern Collection** — curated patterns organized by type: [https://conwaylife.com/patterns/](https://conwaylife.com/patterns/)

## People to Follow

- **John Horton Conway (1937-2020)** — inventor of Game of Life, profound mathematician. His legacy lives in the patterns.

- **Stephen Wolfram** — created Wolfram classification, wrote "A New Kind of Science". Active on CA research and complexity.

- **Bill Gosper** — discovered the first glider gun, invented Hashlife algorithm. Pioneer of Life research.

- **Paul Rendell** — built Turing machines in Life, demonstrated computational universality practically.

- **Nathaniel Johnston** — researcher on CA, maintains many online resources and tools.

- **Adam P. Goucher** — active Life researcher, discovered many advanced patterns (Gemini spaceship, Sir Robin, etc.).

- **The LifeWiki Community** — active forums and Discord for pattern hunters and CA enthusiasts: [https://conwaylife.com/forums/](https://conwaylife.com/forums/)

## Unexpected Connections

### Art and Music

- **"Life: A Work in Progress"** — art installation using Game of Life: exhibits the aesthetic of emergence

- **Cellular automata music generation** — CA rules used to compose algorithmic music: [https://teropa.info/blog/2016/07/28/javascript-systems-music.html](https://teropa.info/blog/2016/07/28/javascript-systems-music.html)

- **Processing/p5.js CA art** — generative art community using CA for visuals

### Science

- **Alan Turing's morphogenesis** — reaction-diffusion systems related to CA for pattern formation in biology

- **Lattice gas automata** — CA models of fluid dynamics (HPP, FHP models)

- **Wolfram Physics Project** — speculative theory that universe is a CA: [https://www.wolframphysics.org/](https://www.wolframphysics.org/)

- **Traffic flow modeling** — Nagel-Schreckenberg model uses CA to simulate highway traffic

### Computer Science

- **Hashlife algorithm** — exploits memoization and symmetry for exponential speedup

- **Reversible CA** — Critters, Margolus neighborhood, implications for physics

- **Quantum cellular automata** — extension to quantum states

### Philosophy

- **Computational irreducibility** — Wolfram's claim that some systems require simulation, no shortcuts

- **Artificial life** — CA as models of life, evolution, self-replication

- **Digital physics** — speculative idea that reality is fundamentally computational

## Recommended Learning Path

1. **Start with Golly** — download and run it immediately. Play before reading.
2. **Read LifeWiki basics** — get oriented in the terminology and pattern types.
3. **Watch Numberphile Conway videos** — get inspired by the creator himself.
4. **Implement your own Life** — code a simple version from scratch (any language).
5. **Explore Wolfram MathWorld** — understand 1D elementary CA and classification.
6. **Dive into patterns** — spend time in the pattern library, discover favorites.
7. **Read Wolfram's NKS (selectively)** — chapters on CA, skip the controversial parts if desired.
8. **Watch Turing machine videos** — see computational universality in action.
9. **Join the forums** — engage with the community, ask questions, share discoveries.
10. **Build something** — custom rule, pattern finder, visualization tool — make it yours.
