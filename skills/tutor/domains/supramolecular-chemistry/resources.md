# Supramolecular Chemistry — Self-Assembly: Resources

## Primary Sources (Textbooks and Foundational References)

### Comprehensive Textbooks
- **Steed, J. W. & Atwood, J. L. "Supramolecular Chemistry" (3rd ed., Wiley, 2022)**
  - The standard reference for supramolecular chemistry
  - Covers all interaction types, host-guest chemistry, crystal engineering, and applications
  - Excellent for intermediate learners — good balance of theory and examples
  - https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Supramolecular+Chemistry%2C+3rd+Edition-p-9781119657453

- **Lehn, J.-M. "Supramolecular Chemistry: Concepts and Perspectives" (VCH, 1995)**
  - Nobel Prize winner's vision of the field
  - More conceptual than Steed & Atwood, less comprehensive
  - Good for understanding the "why" and philosophy of supramolecular chemistry
  - Classic but older (1995) — best for historical perspective

- **Schneider, H.-J. & Yatsimirsky, A. "Principles and Methods in Supramolecular Chemistry" (Wiley, 2000)**
  - Strong focus on thermodynamics and quantitative methods
  - Excellent for understanding binding constants, cooperativity, and measurement techniques
  - More technical than Steed & Atwood — good for students who want deeper physical chemistry

### Specialized Topics
- **Philp, D. & Stoddart, J. F. "Self-Assembly in Natural and Unnatural Systems" (Angew. Chem. Int. Ed., 1996)**
  - Classic review article connecting biological and synthetic self-assembly
  - https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.199609361

- **Whitesides, G. M. & Grzybowski, B. "Self-Assembly at All Scales" (Science, 2002)**
  - Broad perspective on self-assembly from molecules to galaxies
  - Excellent for seeing universal principles across scales
  - https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1071063

- **Aida, T., Meijer, E. W., & Stupp, S. I. "Functional Supramolecular Polymers" (Science, 2012)**
  - Modern applications-focused review
  - Good for seeing where the field is going (materials, biomedical)
  - https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1205962

## Interactive Tools and Visualizations

### Molecular Visualization
- **ChemTube3D** — 3D structures of molecules and assemblies
  - Excellent for seeing host-guest complexes, capsules, coordination geometries
  - http://www.chemtube3d.com/

- **Protein Data Bank (PDB)** — biological self-assembly examples
  - Search for: capsids, chaperonins, ferritin (protein cages)
  - https://www.rcsb.org/

- **Cambridge Structural Database (CSD)** — small-molecule crystal structures
  - Search for supramolecular assemblies (requires institutional access)
  - https://www.ccdc.cam.ac.uk/solutions/csd-system/components/csd/

- **PyMOL** (free open-source version)
  - For visualizing structures downloaded from PDB or CSD
  - https://pymol.org/

- **Jmol / JSmol** — web-based molecular viewer
  - Can embed in web pages, good for interactive lessons
  - http://jmol.sourceforge.net/

### Computational Chemistry
- **WebMO** — web-based molecular modeling
  - Can build structures and run simple calculations
  - https://www.webmo.net/demoserver/cgi-bin/webmo/login.cgi

- **Avogadro** — free molecular editor and visualization
  - Build structures, optimize geometries
  - https://avogadro.cc/

- **ChemDoodle** — drawing and visualization tool
  - Good for creating publication-quality molecular diagrams
  - https://www.chemdoodle.com/

### Thermodynamics Calculators
- **BindFit** — binding constant calculator from NMR or UV-Vis titration data
  - http://supramolecular.org/

- **ITC data analysis tools** (various, often provided by instrument manufacturers like MicroCal)

- **Colloidal self-assembly** — particles organizing through depletion forces

### Computer Science

### Art and Design

## Recommended Learning Pathway

For an intermediate student working through this curriculum:

1. **Start with** Steed & Atwood textbook (chapters 1-3 for foundations)
2. **Supplement with** ChemTube3D for 3D visualization
3. **Watch** TMP Chem videos for qualitative understanding of interactions
4. **Read** Whitesides & Grzybowski (Science 2002) for big-picture perspective
5. **Practice with** BindFit calculator for quantitative binding analysis
6. **Explore** PDB and CSD for real molecular structures
7. **Follow** recent papers in *Chem. Soc. Rev.* for state-of-the-art applications

This combination balances **conceptual understanding** (Lehn, Whitesides), **practical knowledge** (Steed & Atwood, Schneider), **visual learning** (ChemTube3D, PyMOL), and **current research** (journals, reviews).
