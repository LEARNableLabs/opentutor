# Synthetic Biology — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Foundations & Principles
- Engineering principles applied to biology (abstraction, standardization, modularity)
- BioBricks and genetic parts standardization
- Central dogma engineering (DNA → RNA → Protein manipulation)
- Regulatory circuits and feedback loops

### 2. Genetic Circuit Design
- Logic gates in biology (AND, OR, NOT, NOR)
- Toggle switches and oscillators
- Feedback and feedforward circuits
- Transcriptional and post-transcriptional regulation

### 3. Genome Engineering
- CRISPR-Cas systems for precise editing
- Genome synthesis and minimal genomes
- Orthogonal systems and genetic isolation
- Directed evolution and protein engineering

### 4. Metabolic Engineering
- Pathway design and optimization
- Flux balance analysis
- Enzyme engineering and cofactor recycling
- Biofuel and chemical production

### 5. Applications & Systems
- Biosensors and diagnostics
- Therapeutic applications (engineered cells, phage therapy)
- Biomanufacturing and industrial applications
- Environmental applications (bioremediation)

### 6. Tools & Methods
- DNA assembly methods (Gibson, Golden Gate, SLIC)
- Modeling and simulation (deterministic and stochastic)
- High-throughput screening and selection
- Design-build-test-learn cycle

### 7. Ethics & Safety
- Biosafety and biosecurity considerations
- Dual-use concerns
- Regulation and governance
- Societal implications

## Research Gaps Identified

For intermediate learners:
- Need strong foundation in molecular biology mechanisms before circuit design
- Mathematical modeling requires comfort with ODEs and stochastic processes
- Hands-on lab exposure ideal but often unavailable (simulations can help)
- Ethics integration throughout, not just at the end
- Real-world case studies to connect theory to applications
