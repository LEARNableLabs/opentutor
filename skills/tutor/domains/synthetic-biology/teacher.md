# Synthetic Biology — Domain Teaching Config

## Exercise Types
This domain uses a mix of delivery formats:
- **concept-focused mini-lessons** — 9 lessons (32%)
- **real-world application challenges** — 5 lessons (18%)
- **review and consolidation sessions** — 4 lessons (14%)
- **Socratic questions** — 4 lessons (14%)
- **curated resource exploration** — 3 lessons (11%)
- **teach-back exercises (student explains)** — 3 lessons (11%)

Primary format: concept-focused mini-lessons.

## Resource Types
This domain has strong coverage in: textbooks, video lectures, interactive tools, academic papers, code repositories, online courses. Prioritize these when recommending resources.

## Difficulty Curve
Balanced progression — steady difficulty increase with regular consolidation.

Distribution: 32% accessible (1-2), 39% standard (3), 29% challenging (4-5).

Difficulty peaks:
- Day 8: "What makes a genetic toggle switch flip between states?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 9: "How do you build a biological clock that oscillates?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 10: "Why do genetic circuits fail in real cells?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 11: "Can you model your circuit with differential equations?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 14: "What can you do beyond cutting: base editing and prime editing?" (difficulty 4)

## Domain Hooks
This field covers synthetic biology, with applications across theory and practice.

## Common Failure Modes
Students often struggle with the foundational concepts before building to more advanced material. Reinforce basics before progressing.
