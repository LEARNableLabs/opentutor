# Synthetic Biology — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks & Books
- **"Synthetic Biology: A Primer" by Paul S. Freemont et al.** — Excellent intermediate-level textbook covering foundations through applications. Well-structured for self-study.
- **"Engineering the Biosphere: How Synthetic Biology Can Shape Our World" by Ron Weiss et al.** — Application-focused, great for motivation and real-world context.
- **"Synthetic Aesthetics: Investigating Synthetic Biology's Designs on Nature"** — Interdisciplinary perspective connecting art, design, and synthetic biology. Good for rabbit holes.

### Key Papers (Foundational)
- **Endy (2005)** "Foundations for engineering biology" *Nature* 438, 449–453 — https://www.nature.com/articles/nature04342
  - Seminal paper laying out engineering principles for biology
- **Canton et al. (2008)** "Refinement and standardization of synthetic biological parts and devices" *Nature Biotechnology* 26, 787–793 — https://www.nature.com/articles/nbt1413
  - BioBricks standards and the parts registry
- **Gardner et al. (2000)** "Construction of a genetic toggle switch in Escherichia coli" *Nature* 403, 339–342 — https://www.nature.com/articles/35002131
  - First synthetic genetic toggle switch
- **Elowitz & Leibler (2000)** "A synthetic oscillatory network of transcriptional regulators" *Nature* 403, 335–338 — https://www.nature.com/articles/35002125
  - The repressilator: first synthetic oscillator

### Academic Courses (Online)
- **MIT 20.020** — Introduction to Biological Engineering Design — https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/20-020-introduction-to-biological-engineering-design-spring-2009/
  - Full lecture notes, assignments, readings. Excellent foundation.
- **MIT 20.305** — Introduction to Biological Engineering — https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/biological-engineering/
  - More advanced, but great supplementary material
- **Stanford BioE 44** — Fundamentals of Synthetic Biology (materials less publicly available, but syllabus is findable)

### iGEM (International Genetically Engineered Machine)
- **iGEM Main Site** — https://igem.org/
  - Annual competition, educational resources, community
- **iGEM Parts Registry** — http://parts.igem.org/
  - Searchable database of BioBricks with characterization data
- **iGEM Resources** — https://2020.igem.org/Resources
  - Getting started guides, protocols, tutorials

### Interactive Tools & Software

#### Design & Simulation
- **Benchling** — https://benchling.com
  - Industry-standard molecular biology design software. Free academic tier. Sequence design, primer design, CRISPR guides.
- **iBioSim** — https://github.com/MyersResearchGroup/iBioSim
  - Genetic circuit modeling and simulation (ODEs, stochastic). Open-source.
- **TinkerCell** — http://www.tinkercell.com/
  - Visual circuit design and simulation
- **Cello** — http://cellocad.org/
  - Automated design of genetic circuits from logic specifications
- **SBOLDesigner** — https://github.com/SynBioDex/SBOLDesigner
  - Visual design using SBOL (Synthetic Biology Open Language) standard

#### Metabolic Modeling
- **COBRApy** — https://opencobra.github.io/cobrapy/
  - Python package for constraint-based metabolic modeling (FBA)
- **Escher** — https://escher.github.io/
  - Visualization of metabolic pathways and flux distributions
- **KEGG** — https://www.genome.jp/kegg/pathway.html
  - Database of metabolic pathways (reference for pathway design)

#### Sequence Analysis
- **SnapGene** — https://www.snapgene.com/
  - DNA sequence visualization and cloning (free viewer, paid full version)
- **ApE (A Plasmid Editor)** — https://jorgensen.biology.utah.edu/wayned/ape/
  - Free, lightweight plasmid editor
- **Geneious** — https://www.geneious.com/
  - Comprehensive sequence analysis (paid, but powerful)

## Databases & Repositories

### Genetic Parts & Plasmids
- **Addgene** — https://www.addgene.org/
  - Nonprofit plasmid repository. Huge collection, excellent educational resources.
- **JBEI ICE** — https://ice.jbei.org/
  - Inventory of Composable Elements; parts registry and design repository
- **SynBioHub** — https://synbiohub.org/
  - Repository for sharing synthetic biology designs in SBOL format

### Protein & Pathway Data
- **UniProt** — https://www.uniprot.org/
  - Protein sequences and annotations
- **BRENDA** — https://www.brenda-enzymes.org/
  - Enzyme functional data; essential for metabolic engineering
- **MetaCyc** — https://metacyc.org/
  - Database of metabolic pathways from all domains of life

### Literature
- **arXiv q-bio** — https://arxiv.org/archive/q-bio
  - Preprints in quantitative biology, including synthetic biology
- **bioRxiv** — https://www.biorxiv.org/
  - Preprint server for biology; fast access to latest research
- **Semantic Scholar (Synthetic Biology)** — https://www.semanticscholar.org/
  - AI-powered paper search and recommendations
