# Philosophy of Probability and Statistics — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Interpretations of Probability
- **Frequentist interpretation** — probability as long-run relative frequency
- **Bayesian interpretation** — probability as degree of belief
- **Propensity interpretation** — probability as physical disposition
- **Logical interpretation** — probability as logical relation between propositions
- **Subjectivist vs objectivist** debates

### 2. Foundations of Statistical Inference
- Frequentist inference (Neyman-Pearson, Fisher)
- Bayesian inference and prior selection
- Likelihood principle
- Sufficiency and ancillarity
- Role of models and assumptions

### 3. Causality and Correlation
- Difference between correlation and causation
- Causal graphs and DAGs (Pearl)
- Randomized controlled trials
- Observational studies and confounding
- Counterfactuals

### 4. Statistical Practice and Philosophy
- P-values and their interpretation
- Multiple testing and the replication crisis
- Statistical significance vs practical significance
- Null hypothesis significance testing (NHST) critiques
- Publication bias and researcher degrees of freedom

### 5. Bayesian Epistemology
- Dutch book arguments
- Coherence and rational belief
- Prior elicitation and sensitivity
- Objective vs subjective Bayesianism
- Reference priors

### 6. Decision Theory
- Expected utility theory
- Loss functions and risk
- Minimax and admissibility
- Sequential decision-making
