# Fermentation Science — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

1. **Biochemistry of Fermentation** — Glycolysis, anaerobic respiration, energy production, metabolic pathways
2. **Microbial Players** — Lactic acid bacteria (LAB), Saccharomyces and other yeasts, molds, mixed cultures
3. **Alcoholic Fermentation** — Beer brewing, wine making, spirits, yeast metabolism
4. **Lactic Acid Fermentation** — Kimchi, sauerkraut, pickles, yogurt, cheese, enrichment and selection
5. **Other Fermentation Types** — Acetic acid (vinegar, kombucha), mixed fermentation
6. **Food Safety & Quality** — pH control, spoilage prevention, starter cultures, temperature management
