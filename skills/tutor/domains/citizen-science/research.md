# Citizen Science Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

1. **Foundations & History**
   - Evolution from amateur naturalism to structured participatory research
   - Theoretical frameworks (Mode 2 knowledge production, post-normal science)
   - Ethics and power dynamics in participatory work

2. **Project Design & Implementation**
   - Research question formulation with communities
   - Co-design methodologies
   - Data collection protocols and quality assurance
   - Technology platforms and tools

3. **Participant Engagement & Motivation**
   - Recruitment and retention strategies
   - Learning outcomes and science literacy
   - Psychological motivations (contribution, learning, social connection)
   - Community building and communication

4. **Data Quality & Validation**
   - Volunteer data reliability
   - Training and calibration methods
   - Statistical approaches to data quality
   - Expert validation vs. consensus models

5. **Impact & Outcomes**
   - Scientific outputs (publications, discoveries)
   - Policy influence and conservation outcomes
   - Educational and social impacts
   - Equity and inclusion in participation

6. **Emerging Trends**
   - AI-assisted classification and validation
   - Mobile and sensor technologies
   - Extreme citizen science (community-led research)
   - Global South perspectives and decolonization
