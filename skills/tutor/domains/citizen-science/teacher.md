## Common Failure Modes
1. **"Citizen science is a single method"** — Students often treat it as one thing, missing the vast spectrum from contributory data collection to fully co-created research. The Shirk framework (lesson 4) is critical for distinguishing modes, but students may still default to "volunteers collect data, scientists analyze" as the only model. Push them to explore **co-created** examples early (community mapping, environmental justice projects) to break this mental model.

2. **"Data quality is the main challenge"** — While data quality gets lots of attention, the harder problems are often **recruitment, retention, and equitable benefit**. Students may overemphasize technical validation methods and underestimate the social infrastructure needed to sustain projects. Lessons 12-13 and 19-23 address this, but requires deliberate attention to power and partnership.
