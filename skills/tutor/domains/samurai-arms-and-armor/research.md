## Major Subtopics

### 1. Japanese Sword Metallurgy
- **Tamahagane steel production** — traditional smelting from iron sand (satetsu), tatara furnace operation
- **Folding and layering** — eliminating impurities, carbon distribution, creating jihada (grain patterns)
- **Differential hardening (yakire)** — clay coating, quenching, creating the hamon line
- **Blade geometry and structure** — shinogi-zukuri, kissaki, five elements of blade quality

### 2. Armor Construction and Materials
- **Yoroi evolution** — o-yoroi (great armor), do-maru, tosei-gusoku
- **Materials and lacing** — kozane (small plates), odoshi (lacing techniques), lacquer preservation
- **Component construction** — kabuto (helmet), do (cusp), kote (armored sleeves), suneate (shin guards)
- **Aesthetic and status signaling** — mon (family crests), ornamentation, color symbolism

### 3. Weapons Arsenal
- **Blade family** — katana, wakizashi, tanto, tachi (evolution of mounting and use)
- **Pole weapons** — yari (spear), naginata (glaive), historical battlefield role
- **Ranged weapons** — yumi (asymmetric longbow), construction and kyudo tradition
- **Battlefield vs. ceremonial** — functional differences, Edo period formalization

### 4. Martial Culture and Training
- **Bushido development** — warrior code evolution, Zen influence, Hagakure and Budoshoshinshu texts
- **Ryu (schools)** — transmission of techniques, kata practice, legitimate lineage (soke)
- **Social hierarchy** — samurai class privileges, sword-bearing rights, honor system
- **Peacetime transformation** — Edo period shift from combat to cultural practice

### 5. Historical Evolution
- **Heian period (794-1185)** — tachi dominance, cavalry warfare
- **Kamakura-Muromachi (1185-1573)** — katana emergence, infantry combat
- **Sengoku period (1467-1615)** — mass warfare, tosei-gusoku development, firearms introduction
- **Edo period (1603-1868)** — formalization, symbolic role, artisan specialization
- **Meiji restoration** — sword ban, preservation movement, modern legacy
