## Common Failure Modes
1. **"Left-brain people are logical, right-brain people are creative"** — This pop-psychology oversimplification is pervasive. While hemispheric specialization is real (left typically handles language/sequential processing, right handles spatial/holistic processing), most cognitive functions recruit both hemispheres. Creativity, logic, and personality are not lateralized in this simplistic way. Correct by showing actual fMRI data of complex tasks lighting up both sides.

2. **"Handedness perfectly predicts language hemisphere"** — Students often assume right-handers have left-hemisphere language and left-handers have right-hemisphere language. Reality: ~95% of right-handers AND ~70% of left-handers have left-hemisphere language. Crossed dominance (atypical handedness-language mapping) is common. Show the actual prevalence data and discuss why the correlation is weaker than expected.
