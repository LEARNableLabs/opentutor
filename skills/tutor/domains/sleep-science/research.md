# Sleep Science — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Sleep Fundamentals
- Sleep stages (NREM 1-3, REM)
- Sleep architecture and cycling
- EEG/polysomnography measurement
- Sleep homeostasis

### 2. Circadian Biology
- Suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN) and master clock
- Two-process model (homeostatic drive + circadian rhythm)
- Zeitgebers (light, temperature, social cues)
- Chronotypes and individual differences

### 3. Sleep Neurobiology
- Brain regions: hypothalamus, thalamus, brainstem, cortex
- Neurotransmitters: adenosine, GABA, orexin/hypocretin, melatonin
- Neural circuits for wake/sleep switching
- Glymphatic system

### 4. Functions of Sleep
- Memory consolidation (declarative, procedural, emotional)
- Metabolic regulation and restoration
- Immune function
- Emotional regulation
- Brain waste clearance

### 5. Sleep Across Development
- Infant and childhood sleep patterns
- Adolescent circadian shifts
- Aging and sleep changes
- Sleep in non-human animals

### 6. Sleep Disorders
- Insomnia (acute and chronic)
- Sleep apnea (obstructive and central)
- Narcolepsy and hypersomnia
- Parasomnias (sleepwalking, night terrors, REM behavior disorder)
- Circadian rhythm disorders

### 7. Sleep & Health
- Cardiovascular disease
- Metabolic syndrome and diabetes
- Mental health (depression, anxiety, PTSD)
- Neurodegenerative diseases (Alzheimer's, Parkinson's)
- Cancer and immune function

### 8. Applied Sleep Science
- Sleep deprivation effects (acute and chronic)
- Shift work and jet lag
- Sleep hygiene and CBT-I
- Pharmacological interventions
- Societal implications (school start times, work schedules)

## Pedagogical Approach for Intermediate Level
Given the intermediate level:
- Assume basic biology and neuroscience background (cell biology, basic neuroanatomy)
- Provide mechanistic explanations (not just descriptions)
- Include both basic science and clinical/applied content
- Balance molecular/cellular level with systems/behavioral level
- Incorporate recent research findings (last 10-15 years)
- Connect to real-world health and policy implications
