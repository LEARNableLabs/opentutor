# Sleep Science — Domain Teaching Config

## Exercise Types
This domain uses a mix of delivery formats:
- **concept-focused mini-lessons** — 12 lessons (43%)
- **Socratic questions** — 5 lessons (18%)
- **real-world application challenges** — 4 lessons (14%)
- **review and consolidation sessions** — 4 lessons (14%)
- **curated resource exploration** — 2 lessons (7%)
- **teach-back exercises (student explains)** — 1 lessons (4%)

Primary format: concept-focused mini-lessons.

## Resource Types
This domain has strong coverage in: textbooks, video lectures, interactive tools, academic papers, code repositories, online courses. Prioritize these when recommending resources.

## Difficulty Curve
Balanced progression — steady difficulty increase with regular consolidation.

Distribution: 43% accessible (1-2), 43% standard (3), 14% challenging (4-5).

Difficulty peaks:
- Day 9: "What brain regions orchestrate the switch between wake and sleep?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 11: "What neurotransmitters are the conductors of the sleep orchestra?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 23: "What causes sudden sleep attacks in narcolepsy?" (difficulty 4)
- Day 27: "Does sleep protect against Alzheimer's disease?" (difficulty 4)

## Domain Hooks
This field covers sleep science, with applications across theory and practice.

## Common Failure Modes
Students often struggle with the foundational concepts before building to more advanced material. Reinforce basics before progressing.

## Vocabulary
Key terms for this domain: sleep stages, NREM sleep, REM sleep, sleep architecture, ultradian rhythms, sleep cycles, stage progression, polysomnography, EEG, EMG, EOG, sleep homeostasis, adenosine, sleep pressure, suprachiasmatic nucleus (and 71 more).
