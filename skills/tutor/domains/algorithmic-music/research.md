# Research Summary — Algorithmic and Generative Music

## Overview

Algorithmic and generative music encompasses computational approaches to music composition, performance, and sound design. The field bridges computer science, mathematics, music theory, and digital signal processing.

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Algorithmic Composition Techniques
- **Markov chains** — probabilistic state-based generation
- **L-systems** — grammar-based fractal structures applied to music
- **Cellular automata** — emergent patterns from simple rules (Conway's Game of Life for music)
- **Genetic algorithms** — evolutionary approaches to composition
- **Constraint-based systems** — rule-driven composition

### 2. Stochastic and Probabilistic Methods
- Random walks and Brownian motion
- Probability distributions for parameter control
- Noise types (white, pink, 1/f) in musical contexts
- Perlin noise for smooth parameter evolution

### 3. Live Coding and Real-time Systems
- Live coding paradigms and languages (TidalCycles, Sonic Pi, SuperCollider)
- Pattern languages and temporal recursion
- Just-in-time compilation for performance
- Network music and collaborative coding

### 4. Grammars and Formal Systems
- Generative grammars (Chomsky hierarchy applied to music)
- Lindenmayer systems for melodic and rhythmic structures
- Context-free and context-sensitive musical grammars

### 5. Historical and Conceptual Foundations
- Early pioneers: Iannis Xenakis (stochastic music), John Cage (chance operations)
- Brian Eno's generative systems and ambient music
- David Cope's EMI (Experiments in Musical Intelligence)
- Modern AI/ML approaches (neural networks, transformers)

### 6. Tools and Environments
- Text-based: SuperCollider, ChucK, Csound
- Visual: Max/MSP, Pure Data, Reaktor
- Live coding: TidalCycles, Sonic Pi, Hydra
- Web-based: Tone.js, Web Audio API

### Textbooks and References
- *The Computer Music Tutorial* by Curtis Roads (comprehensive reference)
- *Algorithmic Composition: Paradigms of Automated Music Generation* by Gerhard Nierhaus

- TidalCycles (tidalcycles.org) — pattern-based live coding

- Tone.js (tonejs.github.io) — web audio framework for interactive music
