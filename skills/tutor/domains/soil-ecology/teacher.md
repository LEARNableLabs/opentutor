## Common Failure Modes
1. **"Soil is inert substrate that just holds plants up"**
   - **Why:** Cultural narratives treat soil as "dirt," not as ecosystem
   - **Correction:** Start with biodiversity scale (teaspoon = billions of organisms). Show how soil creates itself through biological activity.

2. **"Healthy soil = lots of NPK fertilizer"**
   - **Why:** Industrial ag equates fertility with chemical inputs
   - **Correction:** Distinguish nutrient availability from soil health. Show how biology regulates nutrient release.

3. **"Tilling is necessary to aerate soil and prepare seedbeds"**
   - **Why:** Conventional practice; short-term benefits mask long-term damage
   - **Correction:** Compare tilled vs no-till aggregate stability. Show how biology creates better structure than machines.

4. **"Fungi are just decomposers"**
   - **Why:** Missing the mycorrhizal partnership and network functions
   - **Correction:** Emphasize symbiosis first, decomposition second. Wood wide web metaphor.
