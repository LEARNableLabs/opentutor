# Soil Ecology and Regenerative Agriculture — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Soil Biology and the Soil Food Web
- Microbial communities (bacteria, archaea, fungi, protozoa)
- Mycorrhizal networks and plant-fungal symbiosis
- Soil fauna (nematodes, arthropods, earthworms)
- Decomposition and nutrient cycling
- Microbial ecology and community dynamics

### 2. Soil Health and Function
- Soil structure and aggregation
- Water infiltration and retention
- Organic matter dynamics
- Nutrient availability and cycling (N, P, K, micronutrients)
- Soil chemistry fundamentals (pH, CEC, organic acids)

### 3. Regenerative Agriculture Practices
- Cover cropping and green manures
- No-till and reduced tillage systems
- Crop rotation and polycultures
- Composting and organic amendments
- Holistic planned grazing (rotational grazing)
- Agroforestry and silvopasture
- Integration of livestock

### 4. Ecosystem Services and Carbon
- Carbon sequestration in soils
- Climate change mitigation potential
- Water cycle regulation
- Biodiversity conservation
- Soil erosion prevention

### 5. Assessment and Monitoring
- Soil health indicators and tests
- Visual soil assessment
- Biological indicators
- Long-term monitoring frameworks
- Economic and ecological metrics

## Content Strategy

This intermediate curriculum assumes basic biology and chemistry knowledge. It balances:
- **Conceptual depth** — mechanisms of soil processes, not just practices
- **Practical application** — how to implement and monitor regenerative techniques
- **Systems thinking** — connections between soil health, climate, economics, biodiversity
- **Evidence-based** — peer-reviewed research plus practitioner experience
- **Engagement** — real-world case studies, hands-on assessment methods
