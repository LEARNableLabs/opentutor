# Soil Ecology and Regenerative Agriculture — Resources

## Primary Textbooks and Courses

### Textbooks
- **The Nature and Properties of Soils** (Brady & Weil, 15th ed.) — comprehensive soil science textbook, covers physical, chemical, and biological properties. Gold standard for intermediate-level soil science.
  - https://www.pearson.com/

- **Soil Biology and Ecology** (Eldor Paul, 2014) — focused on biological aspects, food webs, microbial ecology. Perfect depth for intermediate students.
  - https://www.wiley.com/

- **Teaming with Microbes** (Jeff Lowenfels & Wayne Lewis) — accessible introduction to soil food web. Great gateway for gardeners transitioning to deeper understanding.
  - https://www.timberpress.com/

- **Dirt to Soil** (Gabe Brown, 2018) — practitioner perspective on regenerative transition. Case study of Brown's Ranch. Strong on systems thinking.
  - https://www.chelseagreen.com/

- **Growing a Revolution** (David Montgomery, 2017) — journalist perspective on global regenerative agriculture movement. Accessible, inspiring, evidence-based.
  - https://wwnorton.com/

### Online Courses
- **Cornell Soil Health** — free resources and training modules
  - https://soilhealth.cals.cornell.edu/

- **Soil Food Web School** (Elaine Ingham) — microbial ecology courses, microscopy training
  - https://www.soilfoodweb.com/

- **Kiss the Ground Education** — free curriculum on regenerative agriculture
  - https://kisstheground.com/education/

- **Savory Institute** — holistic management and grazing courses
  - https://savory.global/

## Practical Engagement Activities

### DIY Soil Assessments
- **Jar Test** — soil texture analysis with water and a jar
  - https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/Internet/FSE_DOCUMENTS/nrcs142p2_053260.pdf

- **Slake Test** — aggregate stability in water
  - https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/resources/guides-and-instructions/soil-health-assessment

- **Earthworm Count** — dig 1 cubic foot, count worms (healthy = 10+)
  - https://rodaleinstitute.org/

- **Infiltration Test** — pour water, time how fast it soaks in
  - https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/

### Hands-On Projects
- **Compost Building** — hot composting, C:N balancing, temperature monitoring
  - https://rodaleinstitute.org/science/articles/compost-101/

- **Cover Crop Trial** — plant a cocktail mix, observe root diversity and biomass
  - https://www.sare.org/publications/managing-cover-crops-profitably/

- **Microscopy** — observe soil microbes (requires microscope, staining)
  - https://www.soilfoodweb.com/

- **Farm Visit** — visit a regenerative farm, interview farmer, assess soil
  - Find farms via Regeneration International or Rodale network

## Tools and Equipment

- **Basic Soil Test Kit** — pH, NPK, organic matter
  - Widely available: $20-50

- **Shovel and Bucket** — for visual assessment, sampling
  - Standard gardening tools

- **Microscope** (optional, advanced) — observe soil biology
  - 400x minimum for bacteria/fungi

- **Soil Moisture Meter** — track water holding capacity changes
  - $10-30

- **Penetrometer** — measure soil compaction
  - $50-200

- **Soil Test Labs** — send samples for comprehensive analysis
  - https://understandingag.com/
  - https://www.ars.usda.gov/
  - Local university extension services
