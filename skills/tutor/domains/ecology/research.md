# Research Summary — Ecology and Population Dynamics

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Population Ecology Fundamentals
- Population structure (age, size, distribution)
- Demographic parameters (birth rate, death rate, survivorship)
- Life tables and life history strategies
- Metapopulation dynamics

### 2. Population Growth Models
- Exponential growth (unlimited resources)
- Logistic growth (carrying capacity)
- Density-dependent vs density-independent factors
- Allee effects and critical thresholds
- Stochastic models and extinction risk

### 3. Species Interactions
- Competition (intra- and interspecific)
- Predator-prey dynamics (Lotka-Volterra models)
- Parasitism and disease
- Mutualism and commensalism
- Coevolution

### 4. Community Ecology
- Community structure and organization
- Ecological succession (primary and secondary)
- Species diversity and evenness
- Keystone species and ecosystem engineers
- Food web complexity and stability

### 5. Energy Flow and Nutrient Cycling
- Trophic levels and pyramids
- Energy transfer efficiency
- Biogeochemical cycles (carbon, nitrogen, phosphorus)
- Decomposition and nutrient limitation

### 6. Applied and Conservation Ecology
- Habitat fragmentation and corridor design
- Invasive species dynamics
- Conservation strategies (SLOSS debate, reserve design)
- Climate change impacts on populations and communities
- Restoration ecology

## Key Sources

### Textbooks
- **Molles & Sher, "Ecology: Concepts and Applications"** (8th ed.) — comprehensive intermediate text with strong applied focus
- **Gotelli, "A Primer of Ecology"** — mathematical approach, excellent for models
- **Begon, Townsend & Harper, "Ecology: From Individuals to Ecosystems"** — classic comprehensive text
- **Rockwood, "Introduction to Population Ecology"** — focused treatment of population dynamics

### Open Educational Resources
- **MIT OCW 1.018 Ecology I: The Earth System** — systems approach, quantitative
- **Khan Academy Biology: Ecology and the Environment** — accessible intro modules
- **Nature Education Knowledge Project: Ecology** — peer-reviewed modules with current research

### Interactive Tools
- **NetLogo Models Library** — agent-based simulations of population dynamics, predator-prey, competition
- **EcoLab** — population dynamics simulator
- **SERC Teaching Quantitative Skills in the Geosciences** — datasets and activities

### Video Resources
- **Crash Course Ecology** (YouTube) — 12-episode series covering major topics
- **Bozeman Science: Ecology** — targeted videos on specific concepts
- **HHMI BioInteractive** — data visualization tools and case studies

### Research and Current Topics
- **Ecological Society of America** — teaching resources, data sets, career info
- **British Ecological Society** — journals and public outreach
- **Annual Review of Ecology, Evolution, and Systematics** — synthesis articles

## Available Datasets
- **LTREB (Long Term Research in Environmental Biology)** — NSF-funded long-term datasets
- **eBird** — citizen science bird population data
- **Global Population Dynamics Database** — time series for hundreds of species
- **NEON (National Ecological Observatory Network)** — standardized ecological data across sites

## Assessment Approach
Intermediate level assumes:
- Biology fundamentals (cell biology, genetics, evolution)
- Basic calculus (derivatives, exponential functions)
- Comfort with quantitative reasoning
- Interest in both theory and application

Focus on:
- Building intuition for mathematical models (not just equations)
- Connecting theory to real-world case studies
- Developing systems thinking
- Preparing for research or applied work in ecology
