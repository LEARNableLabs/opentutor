## Common Failure Modes
1. **"Mathematics was invented by ancient Greeks and developed in Europe"**
   - **Why students believe this**: Eurocentric education, emphasis on Greek proof tradition
   - **Correction**: Explicitly teach Babylonian, Egyptian, Indian, Chinese, and Islamic contributions. Show how decimal system, zero, algebra, algorithms all came from non-European sources. The "Western" mathematical tradition is a synthesis of global knowledge.

2. **"Historical mathematicians knew less than we do, so their work is simple"**
   - **Why students believe this**: Hindsight bias, modern notation makes old problems look trivial
   - **Correction**: Show original notation and methods. Let students struggle with geometric algebra or Roman numerals. Emphasize that brilliance is relative to available tools.
