## Common Failure Modes
1. **"Darker roast = stronger coffee"**
   - **Why students get this wrong**: Darker roasts *taste* bolder (bitter, heavy body), which reads as "strong." But strength is about concentration (TDS), not flavor intensity.
   - **How to correct**: Clarify that "strong" has a technical definition (mass of dissolved coffee per volume of water). Darker roasts are actually *less* dense due to moisture loss, so you need *more* grounds by volume to hit the same strength. Run a side-by-side brew with equal masses of light vs. dark roast and measure TDS.

2. **"Washed coffee is lighter than natural coffee"**
   - **Why students get this wrong**: They confuse processing method with roast degree. Washed coffees are often roasted lighter *by choice* (to showcase clarity and acidity), but "washed" ≠ "light roast."
