# Research Summary — Differential Geometry and General Relativity

## Major Subtopics

1. **Differential Geometry Foundations**
   - Manifolds, tangent spaces, vector fields
   - Differential forms, exterior calculus
   - Curves and surfaces, geodesics

2. **Tensor Calculus**
   - Tensor algebra and tensor fields
   - Covariant derivatives, parallel transport
   - Lie derivatives and Killing vectors

3. **Riemannian Geometry**
   - Metric tensors, connections
   - Curvature tensors (Riemann, Ricci, scalar)
   - Geodesic deviation

4. **Einstein's Field Equations**
   - Stress-energy tensor
   - Einstein tensor and cosmological constant
   - Weak field approximation

5. **Applications and Solutions**
   - Schwarzschild solution (black holes)
   - Kerr solution (rotating black holes)
   - Friedmann-Robertson-Walker cosmology
   - Gravitational waves

## Key Sources

### Textbooks
- **Liang & Zhou - Differential Geometry and General Relativity (2023)** — Graduate text with 5 chapters on differential geometry, 4 on GR fundamentals
- **Dray - Differential Forms and the Geometry of General Relativity** — Accessible approach using differential forms instead of heavy index notation
- **Carroll - Lecture Notes on General Relativity** — Widely used comprehensive notes covering differential geometry through cosmology
- **Misner, Thorne, Wheeler - Gravitation** — The "big black coffee table" reference, 1279 pages, geometrically focused

### Course Materials
- **MIT OCW 8.962 General Relativity** — Graduate course covering Einstein's theory, differential geometry, experimental tests, black holes, cosmology
- **Stanford General Relativity Lectures** — 10 lectures (17.5 hours) on gravity, geometry, Riemannian/curved space, Minkowski spacetime
- **University of Warwick PX436** — Syllabus and course notes available

### Interactive Tools
- **GeodesicViewer** — Interactive tool for exploring geodesics in curved spacetimes
- **Visualizing General Relativity** (michaelmoroz.github.io) — Web-based geodesic raytracing
- **OGRePy** — Python package for symbolic tensor calculations
- **EinsteinPy** — Python for simulating geodesics in various spacetime geometries
- **OpenRelativity (MIT Game Lab)** — Educational visualizations using games

## Notes for Intermediate Level

Students at this level typically have:
- Strong multivariable calculus background
- Linear algebra (vector spaces, transformations)
- Some exposure to special relativity helpful but not required

The challenge is building geometric intuition while maintaining mathematical rigor. The differential forms approach (Dray) may be more accessible than traditional index-heavy presentations. Interactive visualization tools are crucial for understanding curved spacetime.
