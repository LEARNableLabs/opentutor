# Nuclear Physics and Nuclear Engineering — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### Nuclear Physics Fundamentals
- **Nuclear structure** — nucleus composition, nuclear models (liquid drop, shell model), binding energy
- **Radioactive decay** — alpha, beta, gamma decay; decay chains; half-life calculations
- **Nuclear reactions** — fission, fusion, cross-sections, Q-values, conservation laws
- **Nuclear forces** — strong force, weak force, nucleon-nucleon interactions
- **Nuclear spectroscopy** — energy levels, gamma ray spectroscopy, detectors

### Nuclear Engineering Applications
- **Reactor physics** — criticality, neutron moderation, reactor kinetics, control
- **Reactor design** — thermal reactors (PWR, BWR, CANDU), fast reactors, advanced concepts
- **Fuel cycle** — uranium enrichment, fuel fabrication, reprocessing, waste management
- **Radiation protection** — dose calculation, shielding, safety systems, ALARA principles
- **Medical applications** — PET, radiotherapy, isotope production
- **Industrial applications** — NDT, radiography, gauges, food irradiation

## Key Sources

### Academic Textbooks
- **"Introduction to Nuclear Engineering"** by Lamarsh & Baratta — standard undergraduate text, covers reactor physics and engineering applications
- **"Nuclear Physics: Principles and Applications"** by John Lilley — accessible treatment of nuclear physics fundamentals
- **"Radiation Detection and Measurement"** by Knoll — comprehensive detector physics and measurement techniques
- **"Nuclear Reactor Analysis"** by Duderstadt & Hamilton — advanced reactor theory

### Online Courses
