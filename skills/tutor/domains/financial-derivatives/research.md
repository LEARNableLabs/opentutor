# Financial Derivatives — Research Summary

## Major Subtopics

### 1. Derivatives Fundamentals
- Definition, purposes (hedging, speculation, arbitrage)
- Forward vs. futures contracts
- No-arbitrage principle and forward pricing
- Market participants and mechanics

### 2. Futures Markets
- Contract specifications and standardization
- Marking to market and margin requirements
- Basis risk and convergence
- Hedging strategies with futures
- Cross-hedging and optimal hedge ratios

### 3. Options Theory
- Call and option payoffs and profit diagrams
- Put-call parity
- Factors affecting option prices
- Binomial option pricing model
- Risk-neutral valuation

### 4. Black-Scholes Model
- Derivation and assumptions
- Option Greeks (delta, gamma, theta, vega, rho)
- Implied volatility
- Applications and limitations

### 5. Interest Rate Swaps
- Plain vanilla swap mechanics
- Valuation using zero curves
- Day count conventions
- Credit risk in swaps

### 6. Advanced Topics
- Exotic options (barriers, digitals, Asians)
- Volatility derivatives (VIX futures)
- Credit derivatives basics
- Real-world risk management

## Key Sources

### Textbooks
- **Hull, John C.** *Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives* (11th ed.) — industry standard, comprehensive coverage, intermediate-advanced level
- **McDonald, Robert L.** *Derivatives Markets* — excellent pedagogical approach with clear explanations
- **Shreve, Steven E.** *Stochastic Calculus for Finance* (Vol. I & II) — for mathematical foundations
