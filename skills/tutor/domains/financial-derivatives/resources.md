# Financial Derivatives — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks

- **Hull, John C.** *Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives* (11th edition)
  - Coverage: Comprehensive — forwards, futures, options, swaps, exotics, numerical methods
  - Level: Intermediate to advanced
  - Why: Industry standard. Clear explanations, good examples, DerivaGem software
  - Best for: Lessons 1-26 (entire curriculum)
  - Website: https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/options-futures-and-other-derivatives/P200000005938

- **McDonald, Robert L.** *Derivatives Markets* (3rd edition)
  - Coverage: Futures, options, swaps, with excellent intuition-building
  - Level: Intermediate
  - Why: More accessible than Hull, better pedagogical flow, great problem sets
  - Best for: Lessons 1-22 (foundations through Black-Scholes)
  - Website: https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/derivatives-markets/P200000005856

- **Shreve, Steven E.** *Stochastic Calculus for Finance* (Vol I: Binomial Model; Vol II: Continuous-Time Models)
  - Coverage: Rigorous mathematical treatment of derivative pricing
  - Level: Advanced (reference for curious students)
  - Why: Best binomial model exposition (Vol I), foundation for Black-Scholes derivation (Vol II)
  - Best for: Deep dives after Lessons 15 and 18
  - Website: https://www.springer.com/series/3866

## Unexpected Connections

### Cross-Discipline Links

**1. Options and Evolution**
- Biological organisms face option-like decisions: invest resources now (exercise) or wait for more information (hold)
- Optimal foraging theory parallels optimal exercise boundaries
- Real options in evolutionary strategy
- **Resource**: "Real Options in Evolutionary Biology" papers

**2. Derivatives and Weather/Climate**
- Weather derivatives for hedging temperature, rainfall, snowfall
- Hurricane bonds (cat bonds)
- Climate risk and long-dated derivatives
- **Resource**: https://www.cmegroup.com/trading/weather/

**3. Options in Venture Capital and Startups**
- Startup equity = call option on company value with strike = liquidation preference
- Explains risk-seeking behavior, pivot decisions, timing of exit
- Employee stock options as compensation and incentive alignment
- **Resource**: Entrepreneurial finance textbooks, Paul Graham essays

**4. Game Theory and Options**
- Option to delay = value of information in sequential games
- Strategic real options in competitive markets
- Connection to dynamic programming
- **Resource**: Dixit & Pindyck, *Investment Under Uncertainty*

**5. Prediction Markets**
- Binary options on election outcomes, box office revenues, climate targets
- Implied probabilities from option prices = market forecast
- Efficient markets hypothesis meets political science
- **Resource**: https://www.predictit.org/, https://polymarket.com/

**6. Quantum Finance**
- Path integrals in quantum mechanics ↔ path integrals in option pricing
- Feynman-Kac formula bridges physics and finance
- Uncertainty principle analogies
- **Resource**: Baaquie, *Quantum Finance*

**7. Machine Learning and Option Pricing**
- Neural networks for approximating option prices
- Reinforcement learning for optimal hedging
- Deep learning for implied volatility surfaces
- **Resource**: Recent quant finance papers (arXiv, SSRN)

**8. Historical Derivatives Markets**
- Tulip mania (1630s) — early options on bulbs
- Rice futures in Dojima, Japan (1700s)
- Shows derivatives are ancient, not modern financial engineering
- **Resource**: Economic history papers on early derivatives

**9. Derivatives in Art and Literature**
- Michael Lewis, *The Big Short* — CDS and mortgage derivatives
- Adam McKay film adaptation — dramatizes 2008 crisis
- Shows derivatives in popular culture
- **Resource**: Book and film, both excellent

**10. Space Derivatives**
- Proposed options on asteroid mining rights
- Launch insurance as put options
- Long-dated uncertainty meets frontier markets
- **Resource**: Space economics papers (speculative but fun)

## Wild Cards for Engagement

- **GameStop (2021)**: Options gamma squeeze, market structure failure
- **Negative Oil Prices (April 2020)**: WTI futures at -$37, storage constraints
- **LTCM Collapse (1998)**: When Nobel Prize winners' models fail
- **Barings Bank Failure (1995)**: Rogue trader Nick Leeson, futures losses
- **Portfolio Insurance and 1987 Crash**: How delta hedging amplified the crash
- **Jérôme Kerviel (Société Générale, 2008)**: €4.9B loss from unauthorized futures trades

These cases make risk management visceral and show why institutional details matter.
