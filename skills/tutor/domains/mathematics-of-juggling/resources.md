# Mathematics of Juggling — Resources

## Primary Sources (for lesson content)

### Textbooks and Academic Books

- **Burkard Polster, "The Mathematics of Juggling"** (Springer, 2003)
  - https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/b98883
  - https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Mathematics_of_Juggling.html?id=YCARBwAAQBAJ
  - Comprehensive first textbook covering combinatorics, graph theory, knot theory, mechanics, differential equations, control theory, and robotics in juggling context. Ideal for intermediate to advanced students. Covers siteswap rigorously and extends to advanced topics.

### Survey Articles and Papers

- **Burkard Polster, "The Mathematics of Juggling" (survey article)**
  - https://www.qedcat.com/articles/juggling_survey.pdf
  - Shorter, more accessible version of the textbook. Excellent for getting the big picture before diving deep.

- **Ron Graham, "Juggling Mathematics and Magic"** (UCSD)
  - https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~ronspubs/13_05_juggling.pdf
  - Co-founder of juggling mathematics (with Claude Shannon) explains connections between combinatorics, magic tricks, and juggling patterns.

- **Glenn Sun, "Mathematics of Juggling"** (UCLA Math Circle, 2022)
  - https://circles.math.ucla.edu/circles/lib/data/Handout-3697-3294.pdf
  - Advanced course handout covering ladder diagrams, siteswap, state diagrams, and proofs. Well-structured for intermediate students.

- **Elementary Introduction to Juggling** (UCSD)
  - https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~jeggers/Resources/juggling.pdf
  - Accessible introduction with clear diagrams and examples.

### Course Materials

- **JUG Curriculum** (St. Louis Community College)
  - https://www.jug.net/wt/jug.htm
  - Unusual interdisciplinary curriculum including Juggleometry (geometry of juggling), UFTOJ (Unified Field Theory of Juggling), and rhythmic analysis.

- **Stephen Hardy, "The Mathematics of Juggling" (handouts, 2016)**
  - https://doctorhardy.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/07/mathofjugglinghandouts.pdf
  - Lecture notes with worked examples and exercises.

## People (researchers and practitioners)

- **Claude Shannon** (AT&T Bell Labs, MIT) — Pioneer of information theory who also developed juggling theory in the 1980s. Famous for juggling while riding a unicycle on a tightrope.
- **Ron Graham** (UCSD, AT&T Bell Labs) — Mathematician famous for Graham's number and combinatorial optimization. World-class juggler who co-developed juggling mathematics with Shannon.
- **Burkard Polster** (Monash University) — Author of the first comprehensive textbook on mathematics of juggling. Known as "Mathologer" on YouTube.
- **Colin Wright** — Mathematician and juggler who authored influential papers on juggling theory and pattern enumeration.
- **Allen Knutson** (Cornell) — Mathematics professor and former juggling world record holder. Combines expertise in algebra and physical performance.
- **Paul Klimek** — Inventor of siteswap notation (Santa Cruz, 1981).
- **Bruce "Boppo" Tiemann, Joel David Hamkins, Bengt Magnusson** (Caltech undergraduates, 1985) — Independently developed siteswap notation; discovered pattern 441 mathematically before it was juggled.

## Unexpected Connections (for wild cards)

- **Change ringing** (English bell ringing) — Uses permutation groups and Cayley graphs nearly identical to juggling state diagrams. See group theory chapters in Polster's book.
- **Knot theory** — Ring juggling patterns trace out knots in 3D space. Juggling becomes physical notation for knot diagrams.
- **Robotics and control theory** — MIT and CMU juggling robots use state-space control and feedback derived from juggling mathematics. Links to Lagrangian mechanics and trajectory optimization.
- **Symbolic dynamics** — Advanced connection between juggling sequences and shift spaces in dynamical systems theory.
- **Graph coloring and scheduling** — Passing patterns for multiple jugglers map to graph coloring problems and resource allocation.
- **Music and rhythm theory** — Rhythmic patterns in music share mathematical structure with juggling beat patterns. See Peter Jan Beek's work on rhythmic motion.

## Historical Context

- **1980s**: Claude Shannon develops "A Mathematical Theory of Juggling" (unpublished but circulated).
- **1981**: Paul Klimek invents siteswap notation independently.
- **1985**: Caltech undergraduates Tiemann, Hamkins, and Magnusson develop siteswap independently; discover pattern 441 mathematically before it was physically juggled.
- **1985-1990**: Siteswap notation spreads through the juggling community; pattern databases begin to form.
- **2003**: Burkard Polster publishes the first comprehensive textbook, "The Mathematics of Juggling" (Springer).
- **2010s-present**: Online simulators, mobile apps, and interactive tools make juggling mathematics accessible to wide audiences. Robotics applications emerge.
