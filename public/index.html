The method

Personal tutoring is the most effective form of education ever measured.

It’s also the least scalable. OpenTutor is the tutor that changes that: it adapts to every learner, never tires, and turns conversation into compounding habit.

01

Deliberate Practice

Daily adaptive challenges that target your learning edge. Each lesson is a short Socratic conversation: your tutor asks before it explains.

02

Spaced Repetition

Data-driven recall scheduling — never forget what matters. Concepts that tripped you up come back in later lessons for a retest.

03

Learning Anywhere

Learn in your browser, in Telegram, or inside the AI agent you already use.

04

Effortless Consistency

Lessons take a few minutes, and your tutor keeps your place. On Telegram, it brings you each day’s lesson.

OpenTutor uses AI. It can make mistakes—check important facts and explore the sources alongside your lessons.
